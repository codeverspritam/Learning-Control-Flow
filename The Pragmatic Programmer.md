### **Programming Ka Sahi Tarika**

Seedhi baat yeh hai ki, yeh book aapko batati hai ki aise tareeqe se program kaise karein jise aap follow kar sakein. Aapko lagega ki yeh koi mushkil kaam nahi hona chahiye, lekin yeh hai. Kyun? Ek wajah yeh hai ki saari programming books programmers ne nahi likhi hoti hain. Bahut si books language designers ya un journalists dwara compile ki jati hain jo unke saath milkar unke naye creations ko promote karte hain. Wo books aapko batati hain ki kisi programming language mein **baat** (talk) kaise karein—jo yakeenan (certainly) zaroori hai, lekin wo ek programmer ke kaam ka sirf ek chhota sa hissa hai.

Ek programmer programming language mein baat karne ke alawa aur kya karta hai? Khair, yeh ek gehra mudda (deeper issue) hai. Zyada-tar programmers ko yeh samjhane mein mushkil hogi ki wo karte kya hain. Programming ek aisa kaam hai jo barikiayon (details) se bhara hai, aur un barikiayon ka hisaab rakhne ke liye focus ki zaroorat hoti hai. Ghante beet jate hain aur code nazar aane lagta hai. Jab aap upar dekhte hain toh wahan wo saare statements hote hain. Agar aap dhyan se na sochein, toh aapko lagega ki programming bas ek language mein statements type karna hai. Aap galat honge, bilkul, lekin bookstore ke programming section mein dekh kar aap yeh nahi bata payenge.

### **Think About Your Work**

_The Pragmatic Programmer_ mein, Dave aur Andy hamein batate hain ki aise tareeqe se program kaise karein jise hum follow kar sakein. Wo itne samajhdaar (smart) kaise bane? Kya wo bhi dusre programmers ki tarah details par focus nahi karte? Jawab yeh hai ki unhone is baat par **dhyan diya** (paid attention) ki wo kaam karte waqt kya kar rahe hain—aur phir unhone use behtar karne ki koshish ki.

Tasavvur (imagine) karein ki aap ek meeting mein baithe hain. Shayad aap soch rahe hain ki yeh meeting hamesha chalti rahegi aur isse behtar hota ki aap programming kar rahe hote. Dave aur Andy soch rahe honge ki wo meeting kyun kar rahe hain, aur hairan (wondering) ho rahe honge ki kya kuch aur hai jo wo meeting ki jagah kar sakte hain, aur yeh faisla kar rahe honge ki kya us cheez ko automate kiya ja sakta hai taaki meeting ka kaam future mein apne aap ho jaye. Phir wo use kar dalenge.

Dave aur Andy bilkul isi tarah sochte hain. Wo meeting unhe programming karne se rok nahi rahi thi. Wo meeting **hi** programming thi. Aur wo aisi programming thi jise behtar banaya ja sakta tha. Main jaanta hoon ki wo aisa sochte hain kyunki yeh tip number do hai: **Think About Your Work** (Apne kaam ke baare mein sochein).

### **Pragmatic Approach: Ek Gold Mine**

Sochiye ki yeh log kuch saalon tak isi tarah soch rahe hain. Jaldi hi unke paas solutions ka ek sangrah (collection) hoga. Ab imagine karein ki wo apne kaam mein un solutions ko kuch aur saalon tak istemal karte hain, aur unhe hata (discarding) dete hain jo bahut mushkil hain ya jo hamesha results nahi dete. Khair, yahi approach toh **pragmatic** (vyavharik/practical) hone ki definition hai. Ab imagine karein ki wo ek ya do saal aur lagakar un solutions ko likhte hain. Aap sochenge, _Yeh jankari toh ek sone ki khaan (gold mine) hogi._ Aur aap sahi honge.

Authors hamein batate hain ki wo kaise program karte hain. Aur wo hamein aise tareeqe se batate hain jise hum follow kar sakein. Lekin is baat mein aapki soch se kahin zyada gehrai hai. Mujhe samjhane dein.

### **Pattern Language aur QWAN**

Authors ne software development ki koi badi theory pesh karne se parhez (avoid) kiya hai. Yeh khush-kismati (fortunate) hai, kyunki agar wo aisa karte toh unhe har chapter ko apni theory defend karne ke liye todna-marodna (warp) padta. Aisa warping aksar physical sciences mein hota hai, jahan theories ya toh laws ban jati hain ya khamoshi se hata di jati hain. Dusri taraf, programming mein bahut kam laws hain. Isliye "wanna-be laws" ke gird buni gayi programming advice likhne mein toh acchi lag sakti hai, lekin practice mein nakamyab (fails) ho jati hai. Yahi wajah hai ki bahut si methodology books galat sabit hoti hain.

Maine is problem ko barah (dozen) saalon tak study kiya hai aur mujhe sabse zyada umeed ek device mein mili jise **pattern language** kehte hain. Short mein, ek _pattern_ ek solution hai, aur pattern language solutions ka ek aisa system hai jo ek dusre ko mazboot (reinforce) banata hai. In systems ki talaash ke liye ek poori community ban gayi hai.

Yeh book tips ke collection se kahin zyada hai. Yeh bhed ki khaal mein ek **pattern language** hai (pattern language in sheep's clothing). Main aisa isliye keh raha hoon kyunki har tip experience se nikali gayi hai, thos advice (concrete advice) ke roop mein kahi gayi hai, aur ek system banane ke liye dusron se judi hui hai. Yahi wo khoobiyan (characteristics) hain jo hamein ek pattern language seekhne aur follow karne mein madad karti hain. Wo yahan bhi usi tarah kaam karti hain.

Aap is book ki advice follow kar sakte hain kyunki yeh concrete hai. Aapko yahan koi dhundli (vague) baatein nahi milengi. Dave aur Andy seedha aapke liye likhte hain, jaise ki har tip aapke programming career ko urja-vaan (energizing) banane ki ek aham strategy (vital strategy) ho. Wo ise simple banate hain, ek kahani sunate hain, halka-phulka (light touch) andaaz rakhte hain, aur phir un sawalon ke jawab dete hain jo aapke try karne par saamne aayenge.

Aur bhi bahut kuch hai. Jab aap das ya pandrah tips padh lenge, toh aapko is kaam mein ek extra dimension nazar aane lagega. Hum ise kabhi-kabhi **QWAN** kehte hain, jo _Quality Without A Name_ ka short form hai. Is book ki ek apni philosophy hai jo dheere-dheere aapke hosh (consciousness) mein utregi aur aapki apni soch ke saath mil jayegi. Yeh updesh (preach) nahi deti. Yeh bas batati hai ki kya kaam karta hai. Lekin batane ke dauran bahut kuch aur bhi samajh aata hai. Yahi is book ki khoobsurti hai: Yeh apni philosophy ko embody karti hai, aur wo bhi bina kisi dikhawe (unpretentiously) ke.

Toh yeh rahi aapke saamne: programming ki poori practice ke baare mein ek asaan, padhne-layak aur istemal-layak book. Maine bahut der tak bataya ki yeh kyun kaam karti hai. Shayad aapko sirf isse matlab hai ki yeh kaam karti hai. Yeh karti hai. Aap khud dekh lenge.

— **Ward Cunningham**

## **Preface**

Yeh book aapko ek behtar programmer banne mein madad karegi.

Chahe aap ek akele developer (lone developer) hon, kisi bade project team ka hissa hon, ya ek consultant jo ek saath kai clients ke saath kaam karte hon—yeh book aapko, ek vyakti (individual) ke taur par, behtar kaam karne mein madad karegi. Yeh book theoretical nahi hai—hum practical topics par dhyan dete hain, aur is baat par ki aap apne anubhav (experience) ko zyada behtar faisle (informed decisions) lene ke liye kaise istemal karein. Shabd _pragmatic_ Latin ke _pragmaticus_ se aaya hai—jiska matlab hai "business mein kushal" (skilled in business)—jo khud Greek shabd se nikla hai, jiska arth hai "karna" (to do). Yeh "karne" ke baare mein ek book hai.

Programming ek kala (craft) hai. Sabse simple level par, iska matlab hai computer se wo karwana jo aap chahte hain (ya jo aapka user chahta hai). Ek programmer ke taur par, aap thode listener hain, thode advisor, thode tasmash-been (interpreter), aur thode dictator. Aap un pakad mein na aane wali zarooraton (elusive requirements) ko capture karne ki koshish karte hain aur unhe express karne ka aisa raasta nikaalte hain taaki ek machine unke saath insaf (justice) kar sake. Aap apne kaam ko document karne ki koshish karte hain taaki dusre use samajh sakein, aur ise is tarah engineer karte hain taaki dusre is par aage kaam kar sakein. Aur sabse badi baat, aap yeh sab project ki ghadi ki lagatar tik-tik (relentless ticking) ke khilaf karte hain. Aap har roz chhote chamatkar (miracles) karte hain.

Yeh ek mushkil kaam hai.

Bahut se log aapko madad ki peshkash (offering) kar rahe hain. Tool bechne wale (vendors) apne products ke chamatkaron ka prachar (tout) karte hain. Methodology gurus wada karte hain ki unki techniques results ki guarantee deti hain. Har koi claim karta hai ki unki programming language sabse acchi hai, aur har operating system har mumkin pareshani ka jawab hai.

Zaroor, isme se kuch bhi sach nahi hai. Koi asaan jawab nahi hain. "Sabse accha" (best) solution jaisi koi cheez nahi hoti, chahe wo koi tool ho, language ho, ya operating system. Sirf aise systems ho sakte hain jo kisi khaas halaat (circumstances) mein zyada mozu (appropriate) hon.

Yahan pragmatism kaam aata hai. Aapko kisi khaas technology se judna (wedded) nahi chahiye, balki aapka background aur experience itna bada hona chahiye ki aap khaas situations mein acche solutions chun sakein. Aapka background computer science ke basic principles ki samajh se banta hai, aur aapka experience practical projects ki ek wide range se aata hai. Theory aur practice milkar aapko mazboot banate hain.

Aap apne approach ko maujooda halaat aur mahaul (environment) ke hisab se adjust karte hain. Aap project ko mutasir (affecting) karne wale sabhi factors ki ahmiyat (importance) ko parkhte hain aur apne experience ka istemal karke sahi solutions nikaalte hain. Aur aap yeh kaam lagatar karte hain jaise-jaise kaam aage badhta hai. **Pragmatic Programmers** kaam ko poora karte hain, aur use acchi tarah karte hain.

### **Who Should Read This Book?**

Yeh book un logo ke liye hai jo zyada prabhavshali (effective) aur zyada productive programmers banna chahte hain. Shayad aap frustrated mehsoos karte hain ki aap apni kshamta (potential) tak nahi pahunch pa rahe hain. Shayad aap un colleagues ko dekhte hain jo tools ka istemal karke aapse zyada productive lagte hain. Ho sakta hai aapki maujooda job purani technologies istemal karti ho, aur aap jaanna chahte hain ki naye ideas ko apne kaam mein kaise apply kiya jaye.

Hum yeh dawa (pretend) nahi karte ki hamare paas saare (ya zyada-tar) jawab hain, aur na hi hamare saare ideas har situation mein apply hote hain. Hum bas itna keh sakte hain ki agar aap hamare approach ko follow karenge, toh aap tezi se experience hasil karenge, aapki productivity badhegi, aur aapko poore development process ki behtar samajh hogi. Aur aap behtar software likhenge.

### **What Makes a Pragmatic Programmer?**

Har developer anokha (unique) hota hai, apni alag khoobiyon (strengths) aur kamzoriyon (weaknesses), pasand aur napasand ke saath. Waqt ke saath, har koi apna khud ka personal environment taiyar (craft) karega. Wo environment programmer ki shaksiyat (individuality) ko utni hi shiddat se dikhayega jitna ki uske hobbies, kapde ya haircut. Halanki, agar aap ek Pragmatic Programmer hain, toh aap mein neeche di gayi kai khoobiyan (characteristics) hongi:

- **Early adopter/fast adapter:** Aapke paas technologies aur techniques ke liye ek parakh (instinct) hai, aur aapko cheezein try karna pasand hai. Jab aapko kuch naya diya jata hai, toh aap use jaldi samajh lete hain aur apne baaki knowledge ke saath jod (integrate) lete hain. Aapka atma-vishwas (confidence) experience se paida hota hai.
- **Inquisitive (Jigyasu):** Aap sawal puchne ke aadi hain. _Yeh badhiya hai—aapne yeh kaise kiya? Kya aapko us library ke saath koi problem aayi? Yeh BeOS kya hai jiske baare mein maine suna hai? Symbolic links kaise implement kiye jate hain?_ Aap chhote-chhote facts ke ek "pack rat" hain (ikattha karne wale), jinmein se har ek saalon baad kisi faisle (decision) ko mutasir kar sakta hai.
- **Critical thinker:** Aap shayad hi kabhi cheezon ko bina facts jaane maan lete hain. Jab colleagues kehte hain "kyunki yeh aise hi kiya jata hai," ya koi vendor aapki saari problems ka solution dene ka wada karta hai, toh aapko usme ek chunauti (challenge) ki boo aati hai.
- **Realistic:** Aap har us problem ki asli fitrat (underlying nature) ko samajhne ki koshish karte hain jiska aap saamna karte hain. Yeh realism aapko ek accha ehsas deta hai ki cheezein kitni mushkil hain, aur unme kitna waqt lagega. Yeh samajhna ki kisi process mein mushkil aayegi ya use poora hone mein waqt lagega, aapko tike rehne ki himmat (stamina) deta hai.
- **Jack of all trades:** Aap technologies aur environments ki ek badi range se waqif (familiar) hone ki koshish karte hain, aur naye developments ke saath up-to-date rehne ke liye mehnat karte hain. Halanki aapki maujooda job aapko ek specialist banne par majboor kar sakti hai, lekin aap hamesha naye areas aur nayi chunautiyon ki taraf badhne ke qabil (able) honge.

Humne sabse basic characteristics ko aakhir ke liye rakha hai. Saare Pragmatic Programmers unhe share karte hain. Wo itne basic hain ki unhe tips ke taur par kaha ja sakta hai:

> **Tip 1: Care About Your Craft**
> Hum mehsoos karte hain ki software develop karne ka tab tak koi matlab nahi hai jab tak aap use acchi tarah karne ki parwah (care) nahi karte.

> **Tip 2: Think! About Your Work**
> Ek Pragmatic Programmer banne ke liye, hum aapko chunauti de rahe hain ki aap jo kar rahe hain, use karte waqt uske baare mein sochein. Yeh maujooda tareeqon ka sirf ek baar ka hisaab (audit) nahi hai—yeh aapke har faisle ka, har roz, aur har development par lagatar chalne wala ek mulyankan (critical appraisal) hai. Kabhi bhi "auto-pilot" par kaam na karein. Lagatar sochte rahein, real-time mein apne kaam ko parkhte (critiquing) rahein. Purana IBM corporate motto, **THINK!**, Pragmatic Programmer ka mantra hai.

Agar yeh aapko bahut mehnat wala kaam lagta hai, toh aap _realistic_ hone ki khoobi dikha rahe hain. Isme aapka kaafi keemti waqt lagega—wo waqt jo shayad pehle se hi bahut zyada pressure mein hai. Iska inaam (reward) us kaam ke saath aapka zyada active involvement hai jise aap pasand karte hain, kai tarah ke subjects par mastery ka ehsas, aur lagatar sudhaar (continuous improvement) ki khushi. Lambe samay (long term) mein, aapka waqt ka investment wasool ho jayega kyunki aap aur aapki team zyada efficient ban jayenge, aisa code likhenge jise maintain karna aasan ho, aur meetings mein kam waqt bitayenge.

### **Individual Pragmatists, Large Teams**

Kuch logo ko lagta hai ki badi teams ya complex projects mein vyaktigat (individuality) ke liye koi jagah nahi hai. Wo kehte hain, "Software construction ek engineering discipline hai, jo tab toot jati hai agar team ke members apne faisle khud lene lagein."

Hum isse sehmat (disagree) nahi hain.

Software ka nirman (construction) ek engineering discipline _hona chahiye_. Halanki, yeh vyaktigat karigari (individual craftsmanship) ko nahi rokta. Middle Ages ke dauran Europe mein banaye gaye bade cathedrals ke baare mein sochein. Har ek ko banane mein hazaaron logon ka saalon ka waqt laga, jo kai dashakon (decades) tak phaila hua tha. Jo sabak (lessons) seekhe gaye, wo builders ke agle set ko diye gaye, jinhone apni upalabdhiyon (accomplishments) se structural engineering ko aage badhaya. Lekin carpenters, patthar tarashne wale (stonecutters), nakkashi karne wale (carvers), aur sheeshe ka kaam karne wale (glass workers) sab karigar (craftspeople) the, jo engineering ki zarooraton ko is tarah samajhte the ki ek aisi cheez taiyar ho jo sirf mechanical construction se kahin upar (transcended) ho. Unka apne vyaktigat yogdaan (individual contributions) par vishwas hi tha jisne un projects ko zinda rakha:

> "Hum jo sirf patthar tarashte hain, hamein hamesha cathedrals ka sapna dekhna chahiye."
> — **Quarry worker's creed**

Ek project ke overall structure ke andar hamesha individuality aur craftsmanship ke liye jagah hoti hai. Yeh khaas taur par sach hai agar hum software engineering ki maujooda halat ko dekhein. Aaj se sau saal baad, hamari engineering shayad utni hi purani (archaic) lage jitni medieval cathedral builders ki techniques aaj ke civil engineers ko lagti hain, jabki hamari karigari (craftsmanship) ka samman (honor) tab bhi kiya jayega.

### **It's a Continuous Process**

> England ke Eton College mein aaye ek tourist ne mali (gardener) se pucha ki usne ghaas (lawns) ko itna perfect kaise banaya. "Yeh asaan hai," usne jawab diya, "Bas har subah os (dew) ko jhad dein, har dusre din unhe kaatein (mow), aur hafte mein ek baar roll karein."
> "Bas itna hi?" tourist ne pucha.
> "Bilkul," mali ne jawab diya. "Aisa 500 saal tak karein aur aapke paas bhi ek accha lawn hoga."

Bade lawns ko rozana thodi dekhbhal ki zaroorat hoti hai, aur bade programmers ko bhi. Management consultants baaton mein aksar shabd _kaizen_ ka istemal karte hain. "Kaizen" ek Japanese term hai jo lagatar kai chhote sudhaar (small improvements) karne ke concept ko capture karta hai. Ise Japanese manufacturing mein productivity aur quality mein hone wale bade faydon ki ek mukhya wajah maana jata tha aur poori duniya mein ise copy kiya gaya. Kaizen vyaktiyon (individuals) par bhi apply hota hai. Har roz, apne paas maujood skills ko nikhara (refine) karein aur apne khazane (repertoire) mein naye tools jodein. Eton lawns ke ulta, aapko kuch hi dino mein results dikhne lagenge. Saalon baad, aap hairan reh jayenge ki kaise aapka experience phala-phoola hai aur aapki skills grow hui hain.

### **What's in a Name?**

> "Jab main kisi shabd (word) ka istemal karta hoon," Humpty Dumpty ne thode hiqarat bhare (scornful) lehze mein kaha, "iska wahi matlab hota hai jo main chunta hoon—na usse zyada, na usse kam."
> — **Lewis Carroll, Through the Looking-Glass**

Is book mein jagah-jagah aapko jargon (takniki shabd) milenge—ya toh bilkul acche English words jinhe bigad kar (corrupted) kuch technical matlab de diya gaya hai, ya phir khaufnaak banawati shabd (horrendous made-up words) jinhe language se dushmani rakhne wale computer scientists ne matlab diye hain. Pehli baar jab hum inme se kisi jargon word ka istemal karte hain, toh hum use define karne ki koshish karte hain, ya kam se kam uske matlab ka ishara (hint) dete hain. Halanki, hamein yakeen hai ki kuch shabd choot gaye honge, aur dusre, jaise _object_ aur _relational database_, itne common hain ki unhe define karna boring hoga. Agar aapka saamna kisi aise term se hota hai jo aapne pehle nahi dekha, toh kripya use skip na karein. Use dhoondne ke liye waqt nikaalein, shayad Web par, ya shayad kisi computer science textbook mein. Aur, agar aapko mauka mile, toh hamein e-mail bhej kar shikayat (complain) karein, taaki hum agle edition mein uski definition jod sakein.

Yeh sab kehne ke baad, humne computer scientists se badla lene ka faisla kiya. Kabhi-kabhi, concepts ke liye bilkul acche jargon words maujood hote hain, jinhe humne ignore karne ka faisla kiya hai. Kyun? Kyunki maujooda jargon aam taur par kisi khaas problem domain ya development ke kisi khaas phase tak mehdood (restricted) hota hai. Halanki, is book ki basic philosophies mein se ek yeh hai ki jo techniques hum recommend kar rahe hain wo universal hain: misaal ke taur par, modularity code, designs, documentation aur team organization sab par apply hoti hai. Jab hum bade context mein conventional jargon word ka istemal karna chahte the, toh wo confusing ho jata tha—hum us term ke purane bojh (baggage) ko door nahi kar pa rahe the. Jab aisa hua, toh humne apne khud ke terms ijaad (inventing) karke language ki girawat (decline) mein apna yogdaan diya.
