# Introduction: Sun Wu and his Book (SUN WU AUR UNKI KITAAB)

Ssu-ma Ch`ien ne Sun Tzu ki nimnlikhit biography di hai:

> Sun Tzu Wu Ch`i State ke nivaasi the. Unki *ART OF WAR* ne unhe Wu ke King Ho Lu ke dhyan mein laya. Ho Lu ne unse kaha: "Maine aapke 13 chapters ko dhyan se padha hai. Kya main sainikon ko sambhalne ke aapke siddhant ko ek chhote test ke liye prastut kar sakta hoon?"   Sun Tzu ne jawab diya: "Aap kar sakte hain."   Ho Lu ne poocha: "Kya yeh test mahilaon par laagu kiya ja sakta hai?"   Jawab phir se haan mein tha, isliye Palace se 180 ladies ko baahar lane ka intazam kiya gaya. Sun Tzu ne unhe do companies mein baanta, aur har ek ke head par King ki favorite concubines mein se ek ko rakha. Phir unhone un sabhi ko apne haath mein bhaale (spears) lene ke liye kaha, aur unhe is tarah sambodhit kiya: "Main maan leta hoon ki aap aage aur peeche, daayein haath aur baayein haath ke beech ka antar jaanti hain?"   Ladkiyon ne jawab diya: Haan.   Sun Tzu ne aage kaha: "Jab main kehnta hoon "Eyes front," toh aapko seedhe aage dekhna chahiye. Jab main kehta hoon "Left turn," toh aapko apne baayein haath ki taraf muh karna chahiye. Jab main kehta hoon "Right turn," toh aapko apne daayein haath ki taraf muh karna chahiye. Jab main kehta hoon "About turn," toh aapko apni peeth ki taraf poori tarah ghoomna chahiye."   Phir se ladkiyon ne sehamati di. Command ke shabdon ko is tarah samjhaye jaane ke baad, unhone drill shuru karne ke liye halberds aur battle-axes ko kram mein set kiya. Phir, dhol (drums) ki aawaz par, unhone order diya "Right turn." Lekin ladkiyan sirf hansne lagin. Sun Tzu ne kaha: "Agar command ke shabd saaf aur alag nahi hain, agar orders ko achhi tarah se nahi samjha gaya hai, toh general doshi hai."   Toh unhone unhe phir se drill karwana shuru kiya, aur is baar order diya "Left turn," jispar ladkiyan ek baar phir hansne lagin. Sun Tzu: "Agar command ke shabd saaf aur alag nahi hain, agar orders ko achhi tarah se nahi samjha gaya hai, toh general doshi hai. Lekin agar uske orders saaf HAIN, aur sainik phir bhi aagya ka palan nahi karte hain, toh yeh unke officers ki galati hai."   Aisa kehkar, unhone dono companies ke leaders ka sar kalam (beheaded) karne ka order diya. Ab Wu ka king ek unche pavilion ke top se yeh scene dekh raha tha; aur jab usne dekha ki uski favorite concubines ka execution hone wala tha, toh woh bahut ghabra gaya aur tezi se nimnlikhit message neeche bheja: "Hum ab apne general ki sena ko sambhalne ki kshamta se poori tarah santusht hain. Agar hum in do concubines se vanchit (bereft) ho jaate hain, toh hamare khane aur peene ka swad khogayega. Hamari ichha hai ki unka sar kalam na kiya jaye."   Sun Tzu ne jawab diya: "Ek baar apne Majesty ka commission praapt karne ke baad unki forces ka general banne ke liye, His Majesty ke kuch orders hain jinhe, us kshamta mein kaam karte hue, main swikar karne mein a-saksham hoon."   Tadanusaar, unhone dono leaders ka sar kalam karwaya, aur turant unke sthaan par leaders ke roop mein kram mein agle pair ko install kiya. Jab yeh kar diya gaya, toh drill ke liye ek baar phir dhol bajaya gaya; aur ladkiyan sabhi evolutions se guzari, daayein ya baayein mudte hue, aage badhte hue ya peeche ghoomte hue, ghutne tekte hue ya khade hote hue, poori sateekta aur precision ke sath, ek aawaz bhi nikalne ki himmat nahi ki. Phir Sun Tzu ne King ke paas ek messenger bheja yeh kehte hue: "Aapke sainik, Sire, ab uchit roop se drilled aur anushasit hain, aur aapke majesty ke inspection ke liye taiyar hain. Unhe kisi bhi upayog mein laya ja sakta hai jo unka sovereign chahta ho; unhe aag aur paani se guzarne ke liye kahein, aur woh aagya ka ulanghan nahi karenge."   Lekin King ne jawab diya: "Hamare general ko drilling band karne dein aur camp wapas lautne dein. Jahan tak hamari baat hai, Hamari sena ka inspection karne neeche aane ki koi ichha nahi hai."   Ispar Sun Tzu ne kaha: "King sirf shabdon ke shauqeen hain, aur unhe kaarnamon mein translate nahi kar sakte."   Uske baad, Ho Lu ne dekha ki Sun Tzu ek aisa vyakti tha jo sena ko sambhalna jaanta tha, aur aakhirkar use general niyukt kiya. West mein, usne Ch`u State ko haraya aur Wu ki capital Ying mein apna raasta banaya; north mein usne Ch`i aur Chin ke States mein darr paida kiya, aur feudal princes ke beech apna naam phelaya. Aur Sun Tzu ne King ki taqat mein hissa liya.

Sun Tzu ke baare mein khud Ssu-ma Ch`ien ke paas is chapter mein humein batane ke liye bas itna hi hai. Lekin woh unke descendant, Sun Pin ki biography dene ke liye aage badhte hain, jo unke prasiddh purvaj ki maut ke lagbhag ek sau saal baad paida hue the, aur apne samay ke ullekhniya military genius bhi the. Historian unke baare mein bhi Sun Tzu ke roop mein baat karta hai, aur unke preface mein hum padhte hain: "Sun Tzu ke pair kaat diye gaye the aur phir bhi unhone war ki art par charcha karna jaari rakha." Toh aisa lagta hai ki, "Pin" unki apangta (mutilation) ke baad unhe diya gaya ek nickname tha, jab tak ki naam ka hisaab dene ke liye story ka aavishkar na kiya gaya ho. Unke career ki charam ghatna, unke dhokhebaaz rival P`ang Chuan ki karari haar, Chapter V. ss. 19, note mein sankhipt roop se sambandhit milegi.

Bade Sun Tzu par wapas laut-te hain. Unka zikr *SHIH CHI* ke do doosre passages mein kiya gaya hai: --

> Apne shasan ke teesre saal mein [512 B.C.] Wu ke king, Ho Lu ne Tzu-hsu [yaani Wu Yuan] aur Po P`ei ke sath maidan pakda, aur Ch`u par hamla kiya. Usne Shu shehar par kabza kar liya aur do prince ke beton ko maar daala jo pehle Wu ke generals the. Phir woh Ying [capital] par ek hamle ka chintan kar raha tha; lekin general Sun Wu ne kaha: "Sena thak chuki hai. Yeh abhi sambhav nahi hai. Humein intazar karna chahiye".... [Aage ki kamyab ladai ke baad,] "navvein saal mein [506 B.C.], King Ho Lu ne Wu Tzu-hsu aur Sun Wu ko sambodhit karte hue kaha: "Pehle, aapne ghoshna ki thi ki hamare liye Ying mein pravesh karna abhi sambhav nahi tha. Kya samay ab pak gaya hai?" Dono mardon ne jawab diya: "Ch`u ka general Tzu-ch`ang, laalchi aur paapi hai, aur T`ang aur Ts`ai dono ke princes usse nafrat karte hain. Agar Your Majesty ne bada hamla karne ka faisla kiya hai, toh aapko T`ang aur Ts`ai ko jeetna hoga, aur phir aap kamyab ho sakte hain." Ho Lu ne is salah ka palan kiya, [paanch pitched battles mein Ch`u ko haraya aur Ying mein march kiya.]

Yeh sabse haal ki date hai jis par Sun Wu ke baare mein kuch bhi record kiya gaya hai. Woh apne patron ke baad zinda rahe lagte nahi hain, jinaki maut 496 mein ek ghaav ke prabhavon se hui thi.

Ek aur chapter mein yeh passage aata hai:

> Is samay se aage, kai prasiddh sainik ek ke baad ek ubhare: Kao-fan, jise Chin State dwara niyukt kiya gaya tha; Wang-tzu, Ch`i ki seva mein; aur Sun Wu, Wu ki seva mein. In mardon ne war ke siddhanton ko viksit kiya aur unpar roshni daali.

Yeh kaafi saaf hai ki Ssu-ma Ch`ien ko kam se kam ek aitihasik hasti ke roop mein Sun Wu ki vaastavikta par koi shak nahi tha; aur ek apvaad ko chhodkar, jis par abhi dhyan diya jayega, woh vicharadhin daur par sabse mahatvapurna authority hain. Isliye, *WU YUEH CH`UN CH`IU* jaise karya ke baare mein zyada kehna zaroori nahi hoga, jise 1st century A.D. ke Chao Yeh dwara likha gaya maana jata hai. Yeh attribution thoda sandeh-aspad hai; lekin agar yeh doosri tarah se hota, toh bhi unka vivran kam mulya ka hota, kyunki yeh *SHIH CHI* par aadharit hai aur romantic details ke sath vistarit hai. Sun Tzu ki story, jis mulya ke liye hai, chapter 2 mein milegi. Isme note karne yogya akele naye points hain: (1) Sun Tzu ko pehle Wu Tzu-hsu dwara Ho Lu ko recommend kiya gaya tha. (2) Unhe Wu ka nivaasi kaha jata hai. (3) Unhone pehle ek ekant jeevan jee tha, aur unke samkalin unki kshamta se an-jaan the.

Nimnlikhit passage *Huai-nan Tzu* mein aata hai: "Jab sovereign aur ministers dimaag ki vipreetata dikhate hain, toh ek Sun Tzu ke liye bhi dushman ka samna karna asambhav hota hai." Yeh maanate hue ki yeh karya asli hai (aur abhi tak ispar koi shak nahi kiya gaya hai), hamare paas yahan Sun Tzu ke liye sabse shuruati seedha sandarbh hai, kyunki Huai-nan Tzu ki maut 122 B.C. mein hui thi, *SHIH CHI* ke duniya mein aane se kai saal pehle.

Liu Hsiang (80-9 B.C.) kehte hain: "Wajah ki Sun Tzu ne 30,000 mardon ke head par 200,000 ke sath Ch`u ko haraya yeh hai ki baad wale a-nanushasit the."

Teng Ming-shih ne humein soochit kiya ki surname "Sun" Duke Ching of Ch`i [547-490 B.C.] dwara Sun Wu ke grandfather ko diya gaya tha. Sun Wu ke father Sun P`ing, Ch`i mein Minister of State bane, aur Sun Wu khud, jinaka style Ch`ang-ch`ing tha, T`ien Pao ke rishtedaaron dwara utprerit kiye ja rahe vidroh ke kaaran Wu bhaag gaye. Unke teen beta the, jinmein se doosra, jiska naam Ming tha, Sun Pin ka father tha. Is vivran ke anusar phir, Pin Wu ka grandson tha, jo ki, is baat par vichar karte hue ki Wei par Sun Pin ki jeet 341 B.C. mein haasil hui thi, chronological roop se asambhav ke roop mein kharij kiya ja sakta hai. Teng Ming-shih dwara yeh data kahan se praapt kiye gaye the mujhe nahi pata, lekin beshak unpar koi bhi bharosa nahi kiya ja sakta.

Ek aakarshak document jo Han period ke ant se bacha hua hai woh chhota preface hai jo Great Ts`ao Ts`ao, ya Wei Wu Ti dwara Sun Tzu ke unke edition ke liye likha gaya tha. Main ise poori tarah se doonga: --

> Maine suna hai ki praacheen log apne faayde ke liye dhanush aur teer (bows and arrows) ka upayog karte the. *SHU CHU* "eight objects of government" mein "army" ka zikr karta hai. *I CHING* kehta hai: "'army' dridhta aur nyay ko darshati hai; anubhavi leader ke paas achha bhagya hoga." *SHIH CHING* kehta hai: "King apne krodh mein shaandar dhang se utha, aur usne apne sainikon ko sangathit kiya." Yellow Emperor, T`ang the Completer aur Wu Wang sabhi ne apni peedhi ko sahayata dene ke liye bhaale aur battle-axes ka upayog kiya. *SSU-MA FA* kehta hai: "Agar ek aadmi doosre ko nishchit uddeshya se maarta hai, toh woh khud uchit roop se mara ja sakta hai." Woh jo sirf yuddh-parak upaayon par nirbhar karta hai use khatam kar diya jayega; woh jo sirf shantipurna upaayon par nirbhar karta hai woh nasht ho jayega. Iske udaharan ek taraf Fu Ch`ai hain aur doosri taraf Yen Wang. Military maamlon mein, Sage ka kanoon niyamit roop se shanti banaye rakhna hai, aur apni forces ko tabhi aage badhana hai jab avsar ki zaroorat ho. Woh armed force ka upayog tab tak nahi karega jab tak zaroorat dwara majboor na kiya jaye.
> Maine yuddh aur ladai ke vishay par kai kitaabein padhi hain; lekin Sun Wu dwara compose kiya gaya karya un sabhi mein sabse gehra hai. [Sun Tzu Ch`i state ke nivaasi the, unka vyaktigat naam Wu tha. Unhone Ho Lu, King of Wu ke liye 13 chapters mein *ART OF WAR* likha. Iske siddhanton ko mahilaon par test kiya gaya tha, aur unhe baad mein ek general banaya gaya tha. Unhone west ki taraf ek sena ka netritv kiya, Ch`u state ko kuchla aur capital Ying mein pravesh kiya. North mein, unhone Ch`i aur Chin ko darr mein rakha. Unke samay ke ek sau saal aur usse zyada baad, Sun Pin rahe. Woh Wu ke descendant the.] Deliberation aur planning ke unke treatment mein, field mein utarne mein tezi ka mahatva, dharana ki spashtata, aur design ki gehrai mein, Sun Tzu nindatmak criticism ki pahonch se pare khade hain. Lekin mere samkalin unke aadeshon ke poore matlab ko pakadne mein asafal rahe hain, aur chhote details ko abhyas mein laate hue jismein unka karya prachur hai, unhone iske aavashyak tatva ko nazar-andaz kar diya hai. Yeh wahi motive hai jisne mujhe poore karya ki ek moti vyakhya ko rekhankit karne ke liye prerit kiya hai.

Upar di gayi cheez mein note karne yogya ek cheez yeh spasht bayan hai ki 13 chapters vishesh roop se King Ho Lu ke liye compose kiye gaye the. Iska samarthan I. ss. 15 ke aantarik sabhoot dwara kiya jata hai, jismein saaf lagta hai ki kisi ruler ko sambodhit kiya gaya hai.

*HAN SHU* ke bibliographic section mein, ek entry hai jisne kaafi charcha ko janam diya hai: "82 *P`IEN* (ya chapters) mein Wu ke Sun Tzu ke works, 9 *CHUAN* mein diagrams ke sath."

Yeh saaf hai ki yeh sirf Ssu-ma Ch`ien ko jaane jaane wale 13 chapters, ya woh nahi ho sakte jo hamare paas aaj hain. Chang Shou-chieh Sun Tzu ke *ART OF WAR* ke ek edition ka sandarbh dete hain jismein "13 chapters" ne pehla *CHUAN* banaya, yeh jodte hue ki iske alawa do doosre *CHUAN* bhi the. Isne ek theory samne layi hai, ki in 82 chapters ka bada hissa Sun Tzu ke doosre lekhon se bana tha — hum unhe apocryphal kahenge — *WEN TA* ke samaan, jiska Nine Situations se nipatne wala ek specimen *T`UNG TIEN* mein surakshit hai, aur doosra Ho Shin ke commentary mein. Yeh sujhav diya jata hai ki Ho Lu ke sath apne interview se pehle, Sun Tzu ne sirf 13 chapters likhe the, lekin baad mein apne aur King ke beech sawaal aur jawab ke roop mein ek prakar ka exegesis compose kiya. Pi I-hsun, *SUN TZU HSU LU* ke lekhak, *WU YUEH CH`UN CH`IU* se ek quotation ke sath iska samarthan karte hain: "Wu ke King ne Sun Tzu ko bulaya, aur unse war ki art ke baare mein sawaal poocha. Har baar jab unhone apne karya ka ek chapter prastut kiya, King ke paas unki tareef karne ke liye shabd kam pad gaye." Jaise woh ishara karte hain, agar poora karya upar bataye gaye fragments ke samaan paimane par expounded kiya gaya tha, toh chapters ki kul sankhya kaafi hone se nahi chuk sakti thi. Phir Sun Tzu ko shrey diye gaye kai doosre treatises ko shamil kiya ja sakta tha. Yeh tathya ki *HAN CHIH* 82 *P`IEN* ke alawa Sun Tzu ke kisi karya ka zikr nahi karta hai, jabki Sui aur T`ang bibliographies "13 chapters" ke alawa doosron ke titles deti hain, ek achha sabhoot hai, Pi I-hsun sochte hain, ki yeh sabhi 82 *P`IEN* mein shamil the. *WU YUEH CH`UN CH`IU* dwara supply kiye gaye details ki sateekta par apna vishwās tikaaye bina, ya Pi I-hsun dwara cite kiye gaye kisi bhi treatises ki asliyat ko swikar kiye bina, hum is theory mein rahasya ka ek sambhavid samadhan dekh sakte hain. Ssu-ma Ch`ien aur Pan Ku ke beech Sun Tzu ke jaadui naam ke tehat forgeries ki ek samriddh fasal ke ugne ke liye kaafi samay tha, aur 82 *P`IEN* original karya ke sath ek sath milaye gaye inka ek sangrahit edition ka pratinidhitv kar sakta hai. Yeh bhi sambhav hai, halanki kam sambhavna hai, ki unmein se kuch pehle ke historian ke samay mein maujood the aur unke dwara jaan-boojhkar nazar-andaz kiye gaye the.

Tu Mu ka anuman ek aise passage par aadharit lagta hai jo batata hai: "Wei Wu Ti ne Sun Wu ki Art of War ko ek sath piroya," jo badle mein Ts`ao King ke preface ke aakhri shabdon ki galatfahmi ka parinaam ho sakta hai. Yeh, jaise Sun Hsing-yen ishara karte hain, sirf yeh kehne ka ek namra tareeqa hai ki unhone ek vyakhyatmak paraphrase banaya, ya doosre shabdon mein, is par ek commentary likhi. Kul milakar, is theory ko bahut kam swikriti mili hai. Is tarah, *SSU K`U CH`UAN SHU* kehta hai: "*SHIH CHI* mein 13 chapters ka zikr dikhata hai ki woh *HAN CHIH* se pehle astitva mein the, aur baad ke accretions ko original karya ka hissa nahi maana jana chahiye. Tu Mu ke daave ko nischit roop se sabhoot ke roop mein nahi liya ja sakta."

Toh yeh maanane ke har kaaran hain ki 13 chapters Ssu-ma Ch`ien ke samay mein vyavaharik roop se waise hi maujood the jaise humare paas ab hain. Ki karya tab achhi tarah se jaana-maana tha woh humein itne shabdon mein batate hain. "Sun Tzu ke 13 Chapters aur Wu Ch`i ki Art of War do aisi kitaabein hain jinhe log aam taur par military maamlon ke vishay par sandarbhit karte hain. Dono vyapak roop se distributed hain, toh main yahan unpar charcha nahi karunga." Lekin jaise-jaise hum aage peeche jaate hain, gambhir kathinaaiyan ubharne lagti hain. Mukhya tathya jiska samna karna hai woh yeh hai ki *TSO CHUAN*, sabse bada samkalin record, general ya writer ke roop mein Sun Wu ka koi zikr nahi karta hai. Is a-sahaj sthiti ko dekhte hue yeh swabhavik hai ki kai scholars ko na sirf *SHIH CHI* mein diye gaye Sun Wu ki story par shak karna chahiye, balki aadmi ke astitva par bhi saaf taur par skeptik dikhna chahiye. Is case ke is paksh ka sabse shaktishali prastutikaran Yeh Shui-hsin dwara nimnlikhit disposition mein paaya jana hai: --

> Ssu-ma Ch`ien ke history mein bataya gaya hai ki Sun Wu Ch`i State ke nivaasi the, aur Wu dwara niyukt kiye gaye the; aur Ho Lu ke shasan mein unhone Ch`u ko kuchla, Ying mein pravesh kiya, aur ek bade general the. Lekin Tso ke Commentary mein koi Sun Wu bilkul nahi dikhta hai. Yeh sach hai ki Tso ke Commentary mein woh sab kuch hone ki zaroorat nahi hai jo doosre histories mein hai. Lekin Tso ne Ying K`ao-shu, Ts`ao Kuei, Chu Chih-wu aur Chuan She-chu jaise mamooli aam logon aur hired ruffians ka zikr karna nahi chhoda hai. Sun Wu ke case mein, jinaki shohrat aur uplabdhiyan itni shaandar thin, omission kaafi zyada spasht hai. Phir se, details diye gaye hain, unke uchit kram mein, unke samkalin Wu Yuan aur Minister P`ei ke baare mein. Kya yeh vishwasniya hai ki sirf Sun Wu ko hi chhod diya gaya hoga?
> Literary style ke mamle mein, Sun Tzu ka karya *KUAN TZU*, *LIU T`AO*, aur *YUEH YU* ke ek hi school se sambandhit hai aur "Spring and Autumn" ke ant ya "Warring States" period ki shuruat ke aaspas rehne wale kisi private scholar ka production ho sakta hai. Yeh story ki unke siddhanton ko vaastav mein Wu State dwara laagu kiya gaya tha, sirf unke followers ki taraf se badi baat ka parinaam hai.
> Chou dynasty ke phalte-phoolte daur se lekar "Spring and Autumn" ke samay tak, sabhi military commanders statesmen bhi the, aur baahari abhiyanon ko sanchalit karne ke liye professional generals ka class tab maujood nahi tha. Yeh "Six States" ke period tak nahi tha ki yeh riwaaj badla. Ab yadyapi Wu ek a-sabhya State tha, kya yeh kalpanik hai ki Tso ne is tathya ko un-recorded chhod diya hoga ki Sun Wu ek bade general the aur phir bhi koi civil office nahi sambhala? Humein jo bataya gaya hai, isliye, Jang-chu aur Sun Wu ke baare mein, authentic matter nahi hai, balki theorising pundits ki laaparwah rachna hai. Vishesh roop se mahilaon par Ho Lu ke prayog ki story uttejit roop se behuda aur a-vishwasniya hai.

Yeh Shui-hsin Ssu-ma Ch`ien ko yeh kehte hue pratinidhitv karte hain ki Sun Wu ne Ch`u ko kuchla aur Ying mein pravesh kiya. Yeh poori tarah se sahi nahi hai. Isme koi shak nahi hai ki reader ke dimaag par chhoda gaya prabhav yeh hai ki unhone kam se kam in kaarnamon mein hissa liya. Tathya mahatvapurna ho sakta hai ya nahi; lekin *SHIH CHI* mein kahin bhi spasht roop se nahi bataya gaya hai ki Sun Tzu Ying ko lene ke avsar par general the, ya ki woh wahan gaye bhi the. Iske alawa, jaise hum jaante hain ki Wu Yuan aur Po P`ei dono ne expedition mein bhaag liya, aur yeh bhi ki iski kamyabi kaafi hadd tak Ho Lu ke chhote bhai Fu Kai ke dash aur enterprise ke kaaran thi, yeh dekhna aasan nahi hai ki ek aur general ne usi campaign mein itni mukhya bhoomika kaise nibhayi hogi.

Sung dynasty ke Ch`en Chen-sun ke paas note hai: --

> Military writers Sun Wu ko apni art ke father ke roop mein dekhte hain. Lekin yeh tathya ki woh *TSO CHUAN* mein nahi dikhte hain, yadyapi unke baare mein kaha jata hai ki unhone Wu ke Ho Lu King ke tehat seva ki, ise a-nishchit banata hai ki woh vaastav mein kis period se sambandhit the.

Woh yeh bhi kehte hain: --

> Sun Wu aur Wu Ch`i ke works asli praacheen kal ke ho sakte hain.

Yeh dhyan dene yogya hai ki Yeh Shui-hsin aur Ch`en Chen-sun dono, Ssu-ma Ch`ien ke history mein dikhne wale Sun Wu ke vyaktitva ko kharij karte hue, us karya ko paramparik roop se aavantit date ko swikar karne ki taraf jhukte hain jo unke naam ke tehat guzarta hai. *HSU LU* ke lekhak is antar ki sarahana karne mein asafal rehte hain, aur parinaamswarup Ch`en Chen-sun par unka kadwa hamla vaastav mein apna nishana chook jata hai. Phir bhi woh do points mein se ek banate hain, jo nischit roop se hamare "13 chapters" ki uchi praacheenta ke paksh mein batate hain. "Sun Tzu," woh kehte hain, "Ching Wang [519-476] ke daur mein rahe honge, kyunki unhe Chou, Ch`in aur Han dynasties ke baad ke works mein baar-baar plagiarised kiya gaya hai." Is sambandh mein do sabho se besharm offenders Wu Ch`i aur Huai-nan Tzu hain, dono apne samay mein mahatvapurna aitihasik hastiyan hain. Pehle wale Sun Tzu ki alleged date ke sirf ek century baad rahe, aur unki maut 381 B.C. mein hone ke liye jaani jaati hai. Yeh unhi ke liye tha, Liu Hsiang ke anusar, ki Tseng Shen ne *TSO CHUAN* deliver kiya, jo unhe iske author dwara sonpa gaya tha. Ab yeh tathya ki *ART OF WAR* se quotations, swikrut ya doosri tarah se, alag-alag epochs ke itne saare authors mein paaye jaane hain, un sabhi ke pehle ek bahut majboot sthapit karta hai, -- doosre shabdon mein, ki Sun Tzu ka treatise 5th century B.C. ke ant tak pehle se hi astitva mein tha. Sun Tzu ki praacheenta ka aage ka sabhoot unke dwara upayog kiye jaane wale kai shabdon se jude archaic ya poori tarah se aprachalit arthon dwara pradan kiya jata hai. Inki ek list, jise shayad vistarit kiya ja sakta hai, *HSU LU* mein di gayi hai; aur yadyapi kuch vyakhyaayein sandeh-aspad hain, mukhya argument isse shayad hi prabhavit hota hai. Phir se, yeh nahi bhoolna chahiye ki Yeh Shui-hsin, pratham shreni ke ek scholar aur critic, jaan-boojhkar 13 chapters ke style ko fifth century ke shuruati hisse se sambandhit hone ki ghoshna karte hain. Yeh dekhte hue ki woh vaastav mein khud Sun Wu ke astitva ko galat sabit karne ke prayas mein shamil hain, hum yakin rakh sakte hain ki unhone karya ko baad ki date mein aavantit karne mein hichkichahat nahi ki hoti agar unhone imaandari se vipreet vishwās nahi kiya hota. Aur yeh vishesh roop se ek aise point par hai jahan ek padhe-likhe Chinaman ka judgment sabse zyada vazan uthayega. Doosra aantarik sabhoot dhoondhna door nahi hai. Is tarah XIII. ss. 1 mein, zameen-kabze ki praacheen pranali ka ek spasht ishara hai jo Mencius ke samay tak pehle hi guzar chuka tha, jo ise ek sudhre hue roop mein phir se zinda hote dekhne ke liye utsukh the. Akeli warfare jo Sun Tzu jaante hain woh vividh feudal princes ke beech chalayi jaane wali hai, jismein armored chariots ek bada hissa nibhate hain. Unka upayog Chou dynasty ke ant se pehle poori tarah se khatam ho gaya lagta hai. Woh Wu ke ek aadmi ke roop mein bolte hain, ek aisa state jo 473 B.C. jitni shuruat mein hi astitva mein rehna band ho gaya tha. Ispar main abhi dhyan doonga.

Lekin ek baar karya ko 5th century ya usse pehle ka sandarbhit karein, aur iske bona fide production ke alawa kuch aur hone ke chances samajhdari se ghat jaate hain. Forgeries ka bada daur 473 ke kaafi baad tak nahi aaya. Ki ise 473 ke turant baad ke daur mein forge kiya gaya hoga yeh vishesh roop se a-sambhav hai, kyunki koi bhi, ek niyam ke roop mein, khoye hue kaaran ke sath khud ko pehchanning mein tezi nahi dikhata. Jahan tak Yeh Shui-hsin ke theory ki baat hai, ki author ek literary recluse the, woh mujhe poori tarah se a-manya lagta hai. Agar Sun Tzu ke maxims ko padhne ke baad ek cheez doosri se zyada spasht hai, toh woh yeh hai ki unka saar vyaktigat observation aur anubhav ke ek bade sangrah se distilled kiya gaya hai. Woh na sirf ek paidaishi strategist ke dimaag ko darshate hain, jo generalisation ki ek durlabh kshamta se samppan hai, balki apne samay ki military sthitiyon se gehre se parichit ek practical soldier ke dimaag ko bhi. Is tathya ke baare mein kuch na kehne par ki in sayings ko Chinese history ke sabhi sabse bade captains dwara swikar aur endorse kiya gaya hai, woh taazgi aur imaandari, teevrata aur common sense ka ek combination offer karte hain, jo is idea ko poori tarah se kharij karta hai ki unhe study mein krutrim roop se banaya gaya tha. Agar hum swikar karte hain, phir, ki 13 chapters "CH`UN CH`IU" period ke ant ke aaspas rehne wale ek military man ke genuine production the, kya hum, *TSO CHUAN* ki khamoshi ke baavjood, Ssu-ma Ch`ien ke vivran ko iski poornata mein swikar karne ke liye baadhy nahi hain? Sober historian ke roop mein unki uchi pratishtha ko dekhte hue, kya humein yeh maanane mein hichkichana nahi chahiye ki Sun Wu ki biography ke liye unhone jo records khinche the woh galat aur a-vishwasniya the? Jawab, mujhe darr hai, negative mein hona chahiye. *SHIH CHI* mein batayi gayi story ke anusar chronology ke liye abhi bhi ek gambhir, agar fatal nahi, aapatti hai, jise, jahan tak mujhe pata hai, abhi tak kisi ne ishara nahi kiya hai. Sun Tzu mein do passages hain jismein woh samkalin maamlon ki taraf ishara karte hain. Pehla VI. ss. 21 mein hai: --

> Yadyapi mere anuman ke anusar Yueh ke sainik sankhya mein hamare apne se zyada hain, woh jeet ke maamle mein unhe koi faayda nahi pahonchayega. Main tab kehta hoon ki jeet haasil ki ja sakti hai.

Doosra XI. ss. 30 mein hai: --

> Yeh poochhne par ki kya ek sena ko SHUAI-JAN ki nakal karne ke liye majboor kiya ja sakta hai, mujhe jawab dena chahiye, Haan. Kyunki Wu ke aadmi aur Yueh ke aadmi dushman hain; phir bhi agar woh ek hi boat mein ek nadi paar kar rahe hain aur ek toofan mein phans jaate hain, toh woh ek doosre ki sahayata ke liye aayenge jaise baaya haath daayein ki madad karta hai.

Yeh do paragraphs composition ki date ke sabhoot ke roop mein atyadhik moolyavan hain. Woh karya ko Wu aur Yueh ke beech sangharsh ke daur ko aavantit karte hain. Itna Pi I-hsun dwara dekha gaya hai. Lekin jo abhi tak dhyan se bacha hai woh yeh hai ki woh Ssu-ma Ch`ien ke narrative ki credibility ko bhi gambhir roop se kamzor karte hain. Jaise humne upar dekha hai, Sun Wu ke sambandh mein di gayi pehli positive date 512 B.C. hai. Woh tab ek general ke roop mein bole jaate hain, Ho Lu ke confidential adviser ke roop mein kaam karte hue, taaki us monarch se unka alleged introduction pehle hi ho chuka tha, aur beshak 13 chapters pehle hi likhe gaye hone chahiye. Lekin us samay, aur uske kai saalon baad tak, 506 mein Ying ke capture tak, Ch`u aur Yueh nahi, Wu ka bada vanshanugat dushman tha. Do states, Ch`u aur Wu, aadhi sadi se zyada samay se lagatar yuddh mein the, jabki Wu aur Yueh ke beech pehla yuddh sirf 510 mein ladagaya tha, aur tab bhi Ch`u ke sath teevra sangharsh ke beech mein sandwich kiya gaya ek chhota interlude se zyada nahi tha. Ab Ch`u ka 13 chapters mein bilkul zikr nahi hai. Praakritik nishkarsh yeh hai ki woh ek aise samay mein likhe gaye the jab Yueh Wu ka mukhya antagonist ban gaya tha, yaani, Ch`u dwara 506 ki badi apmaan ko sahne ke baad. VI. ss. 21 se upar quote kiya gaya sentence mujhe shayad hi aisa lagta hai jo jeet ke poore flush mein likha ja sakta tha. Yeh iske bajaye ishara karta lagta hai ki, kam se kam moment ke liye, tide Wu ke khilaf mud gaya tha, aur ki woh sangharsh ka sabse badtar hissa pa rahi thi. Isliye hum nishkarsh nikal sakte hain ki hamara treatise 505 mein astitva mein nahi tha, jis date se pehle Yueh Wu ke khilaf koi ullekhniya kamyabi score karta hua nahi dikhta hai. Ho Lu ki maut 496 mein hui thi, toh agar kitaab unke liye likhi gayi thi, toh yeh 505-496 ke daur ke dauran honi chahiye, jab ladaiyon mein ek shanti thi, Wu ne apekshakrit Ch`u ke khilaf apne sarvottam prayas dwara thak chuka tha. Doosri taraf, agar hum Sun Wu ke naam ko Ho Lu se jodne wali parampara ko nazar-andaz karne ka chunav karte hain, toh yeh 496 aur 494 ke beech, ya sambhavid roop se 482-473 ke daur mein roshni dekh sakta tha, jab Yueh ek baar phir ek bahut hi gambhir khatra ban raha tha.

Hum kaafi hadd tak yakin mahsoos kar sakte hain ki author, jo bhi rahe hon, apne din mein kisi bade kad ke aadmi nahi the. Is point par *TSO CHUAN* ka negative testimony *SHIH CHI* se jude kisi bhi authority ke tukde ko kaafi hadd tak pichhaad deta hai, agar ek baar iske doosre tathy galat sabit ho jaate hain. Sun Hsing-yen, halanki, mahan commentary se unke naam ke omission ko samjhane ka ek kamzor prayas karte hain. Woh Wu Tzu-hsu the, woh kehte hain, jinhone Sun Wu ke kaarnamon ka saara shrey liya, kyunki baad wale (ek alien hone ke naate) ko State mein koi office inaam mein nahi diya gaya tha.

Phir Sun Tzu legend kaise shuru hua? Yeh ho sakta hai ki kitaab ki badhti hui shohrat ne uske author ko dheere-dheere ek prakar ka artificial renown pradan kiya. Yeh bilkul sahi aur uchit mahsoos kiya gaya ki war ki science mein itna achhi tarah se nipun vyakti ke paas unke shrey mein solid uplabdhiyan bhi honi chahiye. Ab Ying ka capture bina kisi shak ke Ho Lu ke shasan mein arms ka sabse bada kaarnama tha; isne aaspas ke sabhi states par ek gehra aur tikau prabhav chhoda, aur Wu ko uski taqat ke chhote samay ke charam tak uthaya. Isliye, samay beetne ke sath, usse zyada praakritik kya ho sakta tha ki strategy ke swikrut master, Sun Wu ko popular roop se us campaign ke sath pehchana jaye, pehle shayad sirf is arth mein ki unke dimaag ne ise kalpana ki aur plan kiya; baad mein, ki yeh vaastav mein unke dwara Wu Yuan, Po P`ei aur Fu Kai ke sath milkar chalaaya gaya tha?

Yeh saaf hai ki Sun Tzu ki zindagi ke outline ko bhi phir se construct karne ka koi bhi prayas lagbhag poori tarah se anuman par aadharit hona chahiye. Is aavashyak proviso ke sath, main kahunga ki woh shayad Ho Lu ke accession ke samay Wu ki seva mein pravesh kiye the, aur prince ke shasan ke pehle hisse ko mark karne wali teevra military activity ke dauran, sirf ek subordinate officer ki kshamta mein hi sahi, anubhav ik इकट्ठा kiye the. Agar woh bilkul bhi general bane, toh woh nischit roop se upar bataye gaye teenon ke sath barabar ke darje par kabhi nahi the. Woh nischit roop se Ying ke investment aur occupation mein maujood the, aur agle saal Wu ke achanak pataan ke gawah bane. Is critical juncture par Yueh ka hamla, jab uska rival har taraf se pareshan tha, unhe ashwast karta lagta hai ki yeh upstart kingdom woh bada dushman tha jiski taraf ab se har prayas ko nirdeshit karna padega.

Sun Wu is tarah ek achhi tarah se seasoned warrior the jab woh apni prasiddh kitaab likhne baithe, jo mere hisaab se Ho Lu ke shasan ke shuruat ke bajaye ant ki taraf aayi honi chahiye. Mahilaon ki story sambhavid roop se usi samay ke aaspas hone wali kisi asli ghatna se ugi ho sakti hai. Jaise ki hum kisi bhi srot se iske baad Sun Wu ke baare mein aur nahi sunte hain, unke apne patron ke baad zinda rehne ya Yueh ke sath mauth-sangharsh mein bhaag lene ki kam hi sambhavna hai, jo Tsui-li mein disaster ke sath shuru hua tha.

Agar yeh inferences lagbhag sahi hain, toh us bhagya mein ek nishchit irony hai jisne aadesh diya ki China ka shanti ka sabse prasiddh aadmi war par uske sabse bade writer ke samkalin hona chahiye.

## The Text of Sun Tzu (Sun Tzu Ka Text)

Mujhe Sun Tzu ke text ke itihaas ke baare mein zyada ik इकट्ठा karna kathin laga hai. Shuruati authors mein hone wale quotations yeh dikhane ke liye jaate hain ki "13 chapters" jinke baare mein Ssu-ma Ch`ien bolte hain mool roop se waise hi the jaise ab extant hain. Hamare paas iske liye unka shabd hai ki woh unke din mein vyapak roop se circulate kiye gaye the, aur sirf afsos kar sakte hain ki unhone us kaaran unpar charcha karne se parhez kiya.

Sun Hsing-yen apne preface mein kehte hain: --

> Ch`in aur Han dynasties ke dauran Sun Tzu ki *ART OF WAR* military commanders ke beech aam upayog mein thi, lekin aisa lagta hai ki unhone ise rahasyamayi arth ke karya ke roop mein treat kiya, aur pichhli peedhi ke labh ke liye ise expound karne ke liye un-ichhuk the. Is tarah yeh hua ki Wei Wu ispar commentary likhne wale pehle the.

Jaise humne pehle hi dekha hai, yeh maanane ka koi samajhdar kaaran nahi hai ki Ts`ao Kung ne text ke sath chhedchaad ki. Lekin text khud kaafi aksar itna aspasht hota hai, aur us samay se aage hone wale editions ki sankhya itni badi hai, vishesh roop se T`ang aur Sung dynasties ke dauran, ki yeh hairani ki baat hogi agar kai corruptions andar pravesh karne mein kamyab na hue hon. Sung period ke beech ki taraf, jis samay tak Sun Tzu par sabhi mukhya commentaries astitva mein thin, ek nishchit Chi T`ien-pao ne 15 *CHUAN* mein "Sun Tzu with the collected commentaries of ten writers" entitled ek karya publish kiya. Ek aur text tha, Ta-hsing ke Chu Fu dwara prastut kiye gaye variant readings ke sath, jiske paas us daur ke scholars ke beech supporters bhi the; lekin Ming editions mein, Sun Hsing-yen humein batate hain, yeh readings kisi na kisi kaaran se ab circulation mein nahi daale gaye the. Is tarah, 18th century ke ant tak, kshetra ke akele kabze mein text Chi T`ien-pao ke edition se nikala gaya ek tha, yadyapi us mahatvapurna karya ki koi actual copy zinda bachi nahi jaani jaati thi. Yeh, isliye, Sun Tzu ka text hai jo 1726 mein printed bade Imperial encyclopedia ke War section mein dikhta hai, *KU CHIN T`U SHU CHI CH`ENG*. Mere paas maujood ek aur copy jo ki vyavaharik roop se wahi text hai, thode variations ke sath, woh hai jo "Eleven philosophers of the Chou and Ch`in dynasties" [1758] mein shamil hai. Aur Capt. Calthrop ke pehle edition mein printed Chinese saaf taur par ek aisa hi version hai jo Japanese channels se hokar nikala hai. Toh cheezein tab tak waisi hi rahin jab tak Sun Hsing-yen [1752-1818], ek vishisht antiquarian aur classical scholar, jinhone Sun Wu ka actual descendant hone ka daava kiya, ne Hua-yin temple ki library ke ek visit par Chi T`ien-pao ke lambe samay se khoye hue karya ki ek copy ko achanak dhoondha. Isse juda hua Cheng Yu-Hsien ka *I SHUO* tha, jiska zikr *T`UNG CHIH* mein kiya gaya tha, aur use bhi nasht hua maana jata tha. Yeh wahi hai jise Sun Hsing-yen "original edition (ya text)" ke roop mein nirdisht karte hain — ek thoda bhramak naam, kyunki yeh kisi bhi tarah se hamare samne Sun Tzu ke text ko uski pristine purity mein prastut karne ka daava nahi kar sakta. Chi T`ien-pao ek laaparwah compiler the, aur apne din mein current thode debased version ko reproduce karne se santusht the lagte hain, bina tab uplabdh sabse shuruati editions ke sath ise collate karne ki takleef uthaye. Kismat se, naye dhoondhe gaye karya se bhi purane Sun Tzu ke do versions abhi bhi extant the, ek Tu Yu ke Constitution par bade treatise *T`UNG TIEN* mein dafan, doosra isi tarah *T`AI P`ING YU LAN* encyclopedia mein enshrined. Dono mein poora text paaya jana hai, bhale hi fragments mein toota hua, doosre matter ke sath milaya hua, aur kai alag-alag sections par bikhra hua. Is baat par vichar karte hue ki *YU LAN* humein 983 saal tak wapas le jata hai, aur *T`UNG TIEN* lagbhag 200 saal aur aage, T`ang dynasty ke beech tak, Sun Tzu ke in early transcripts ke mulya ko kam nahi aankaa ja sakta. Phir bhi unka upayog karne ka idea kisi ko tab tak nahi aaya jab tak Sun Hsing-yen ne, Government instructions ke tehat kaam karte hue, text ka ek poora recension nahi kiya. Yeh unka apna vivran hai: --

> Sun Tzu ke text mein unke editors dwara aage badhaye gaye kai mistakes ke kaaran, Government ne order diya ki [Chi T`ien-pao ka] praacheen edition upayog kiya jana chahiye, aur text ko poore mein sodhit aur correct kiya jana chahiye. Aisa hua ki Wu Nien-hu, Governor Pi Kua, aur Hsi, second degree ke ek graduate, sabhi ne khud ko is adhyayan mein samarpit kiya tha, shayad isme mujhe peeche chhodte hue. Tadanusaar, maine military men ke liye ek textbook ke roop mein poore karya ko blocks par cut karwaya hai.

Yahan sandarbhit teenon vyakti saaf taur par Sun Hsing-yen ke commission se pehle Sun Tzu ke text par vyast the, lekin humein unke dwara vaastav mein poore kiye gaye karya ke baare mein shak mein chhoda gaya hai. Kisi bhi maamle mein, naya edition, jab aakhirkar produce kiya gaya, Sun Hsing-yen aur sirf ek co-editor Wu Jen-shi ke naamon mein dikha. Unhone "original edition" ko apna aadhar banaya, aur purane versions ke sath dhyan se tulna karke, saath hi extant commentaries aur jaankari ke doosre sroton jaise *I SHUO* ke zariya, kai sandeh-aspad passages ko restore karne mein kamyab hue, aur kul milakar, jise swikar kiya jana chahiye use Sun Tzu ke original karya ke sabse paas ka approximation ke roop mein turn out kiya jise hum kabhi paane ki sambhavna rakhte hain. Yeh wahi hai jise ab se "standard text" kaha jayega.

Maine jis copy ka upayog kiya hai woh 1877 dated reissue se sambandhit hai. Yeh 6 *PEN* mein hai, jo 83 *PEN* mein 23 early philosophical works ke ek achhi tarah se printed set ka hissa banata hai. Yeh Sun Hsing-yen ke ek preface ke sath khulta hai (is introduction mein vyapak roop se quote kiya gaya), Sun Tzu ki zindagi aur performances ke traditional view ko vindicating karte hue, aur iske paksh mein sabhoot ko aakarshak roop se concise fashion mein sum up karte hue. Iske baad unke edition ke liye Ts`ao Kung ka preface aata hai, aur *SHIH CHI* se Sun Tzu ki biography, dono upar translated. Phir aate hain, pehle, Cheng Yu-hsien ka *I SHUO*, author ke preface ke sath, aur agla, Pi I-hsun dwara compiled *SUN TZU HSU LU* entitled historical aur bibliographical jaankari ka ek chhota miscellany. Jahan tak karya ke body ki baat hai, har alag sentence ke baad text par ek note aata hai, agar zaroorat ho, aur phir isse sambandhit vividh commentaries, chronological order mein arranged. Inpar hum ab ek-ek karke sankhipt roop se charcha karne ke liye aage badhenge.

## The Commentators (Commentators / Vyakhya-kar)

Sun Tzu commentators ki ek ashadharan roop se lambi vishisht roll ka daava kar sakte hain, jo kisi bhi classic ko sammaanit karegi. Ou-yang Hsiu is tathya par tippani karte hain, yadyapi unhone tale poora hone se pehle likha tha, aur ise kaafi chaturai se yeh kehkar samjhate hain ki war ke artifices, a-santusht hone ke kaaran, isliye tareeqon ki ek badi vividhta mein treatment ke prati sanvedansheel hone chahiye.

1. TS`AO TS`AO or Ts`ao Kung**, baad mein Wei Wu Ti [A.D. 155-220] ke roop mein jaane gaye. Isme shayad hi koi shak ki jagah hai ki Sun Tzu par sabse shuruati commentary vaastav mein is ashadharan aadmi ke pen se aayi thi, jiski *SAN KUO CHIH* mein biography ek romance ki tarah padhi jaati hai. Unke operations ke scale mein duniya ne dekhe sabse bade military geniuses mein se ek, aur Napoleonic, woh vishesh roop se unke marches ki adbhut tezi ke liye prasiddh the, jisne line mein abhivyakti paayi hai "Ts`ao Ts`ao ki baat karo, aur Ts`ao Ts`ao pratyaksh ho jayenge." Ou-yang Hsiu unke baare mein kehte hain ki woh ek bade captain the jinhone "Tung Cho, Lu Pu aur do Yuan, father aur son ke khilaf apni taqat ko naapa, aur un sabhi ko haraya; jispar unhone Han ke Empire ko Wu aur Shu ke sath baanta, aur khud ko king banaya. Yeh record kiya gaya hai ki jab bhi ek far-reaching campaign ki eve par Wei dwara war ki council aayojit ki jaati thi, unke paas unke saare calculations taiyar hote the; un generals ne jinhone unka upayog kiya unhone das mein se ek ladai nahi khoyi; woh jinhone kisi vishesh maamle mein unke vipreet kaam kiya unhone apni senayein incontinently beaten aur bhaagte hue dekhein." Sun Tzu par Ts`ao Kung ke notes, austere brevity ke models, us sakht commander ke itne poori tarah se characteristic hain jise history ke roop mein jaana jata hai, ki unhe ek mamooli *LITTERATEUR* ke karya ke roop mein kalpana karna vaastav mein kathin hai. Kabhi-kabhi, vaastav mein, atyadhik compression ke kaaran, woh shayad hi samajhne yogya hain aur text se kam commentary ki zaroorat mein nahi khade hain.
2. **MENG SHIH.** Commentary jo is naam ke tehat hum tak aayi hai aapekshik roop se meager hai, aur author ke baare mein kuch nahi jaana jata hai. Yahan tak ki unka vyaktigat naam bhi record nahi kiya gaya hai. Chi T`ien-pao ka edition unhe Chia Lin ke baad rakhta hai, aur Ch`ao Kung-wu bhi unhe T`ang dynasty ko aavantit karte hain, lekin yeh ek galati hai. Sun Hsing-yen ke preface mein, woh Liang dynasty [502-557] ke Meng Shih ke roop mein dikhte hain. Doosre unhe 3rd century ke Meng K`ang ke sath pehchanenge. Unhe ek karya mein "Five Commentators" mein se aakhri ke roop mein naam diya gaya hai, doosre Wei Wu Ti, Tu Mu, Ch`en Hao aur Chia Lin hain.
3. **LI CH`UAN** 8th century ke military tactics par ek jaane-maane writer the. Unke works mein se ek ka vartaman din tak lagatar upayog kiya gaya hai. *T`UNG CHIH* unke dwara likhe gaye "Lives of famous generals from the Chou to the T`ang dynasty" ka zikr karta hai. Ch`ao Kung-wu aur *T`IEN-I-KO* catalogue ke anusar, unhone Sun Tzu ke text ke ek variant ka palan kiya jo ab extant se kaafi alag hai. Unke notes zyadatar chhote aur point par hain, aur woh aksar Chinese history se anecdotes dwara apni tippaniyon ko darshate hain.
4. **TU YU** (died 812) ne Sun Tzu par ek alag commentary publish nahi ki, unke notes *T`UNG TIEN* se liye gaye the, jo Constitution par encyclopedic treatise tha jo unka life-work tha. Woh kaafi hadd tak Ts`ao Kung aur Meng Shih ke dohraav hain, jiske alawa yeh vishwās kiya jata hai ki unhone Wang Ling aur doosron ke praacheen commentaries par khincha. *T`UNG TIEN* ke vishesh arrangement ke kaaran, unhe context se alag, har passage ko uske merits par samjhana padta hai, aur kabhi-kabhi unka apna explanation Ts`ao Kung ke sath mel nahi khata, jinhe woh hamesha pehle quote karte hain. Yadyapi "Ten Commentators" mein se ek ke roop mein sakhti se nahi gina jana chahiye, unhe Chi T`ien-pao dwara unki sankhya mein joda gaya tha, unke grandson Tu Mu ke baad galat tareeqe se rakha gaya tha.
5. **TU MU** (803-852) shayad ek poet ke roop mein sabse behtareen dhang se jaane jaate hain — T`ang period ki shaandar galaxy mein bhi ek chamakdaar tara. Hum Ch`ao Kung-wu se seekhte hain ki yadyapi unke paas war ka koi practical anubhav nahi tha, woh vishay par charcha karne ke atyadhik shauqeen the, aur iske alawa *CH`UN CH`IU* aur *CHAN KUO* eras ke military history mein achhi tarah se padhe-likhe the. Unke notes, isliye, dhyan dene yogya hain. Woh bahut prachur hain, aur historical parallels se bhare hue hain. Sun Tzu ke karya ka saar is tarah unke dwara summarize kiya gaya hai: "Benevolence aur justice ka abhyas karein, lekin doosri taraf artifice aur expedient measures ka poora upayog karein." Unhone aage ghoshna ki ki Sun Tzu ki maut ke baad se beete hazaar saalon ke sabhi military triumphs aur disasters, jaanch karne par, unki kitaab mein maujood maxims ko har vishesh maamle mein uphold aur corroborate karte hue paaye jaayenge. Ts`ao Kung ke khilaf Tu Mu ka thoda spiteful charge pehle hi kahin aur vichar kiya ja chuka hai.
6. CH`EN HAO** Tu Mu ke samkalin rahe lagte hain. Ch`ao Kung-wu kehte hain ki woh Sun Tzu par ek naya commentary likhne ke liye prerit hue the kyunki ek taraf Ts`ao Kung ka bahut aspasht aur subtle tha, aur doosri taraf Tu Mu ka bahut long-winded aur diffuse tha. Ou-yang Hsiu, 11th century ke beech mein likhte hue, Ts`ao Kung, Tu Mu aur Ch`en Hao ko Sun Tzu par teen mukhya commentators kehte hain, aur dekhte hain ki Ch`en Hao lagatar Tu Mu ki kamiyaon par hamla kar rahe hain. Unka commentary, yadyapi merit se khali nahi hai, unke purvajon ke neeche rank hona chahiye.
7. **CHIA LIN** T`ang dynasty ke tehat rahe hone ke liye jaane jaate hain, kyunki Sun Tzu par unka commentary *T`ang Shu* mein zikr kiya gaya hai aur baad mein usi dynasty ke Chi Hsieh dwara Meng Shih aur Tu Yu ke sath phir se publish kiya gaya tha. Yeh thode scanty texture ka hai, aur quality ke point par bhi, shayad gyarah mein se sabse kam moolyavan hai.
8. **MEI YAO-CH`EN** (1002-1060), aam taur par unke "style" ke roop mein Mei Sheng-yu ke naam se jaane jaate hain, Tu Mu ki tarah, vishishtta ke ek poet the. Unka commentary bade Ou-yang Hsiu dwara ek laudatory preface ke sath publish kiya gaya tha, jismein se hum nimnlikhit chun sakte hain: --

> Baad ke scholars ne Sun Tzu ko galat padha hai, unke shabdon ko distort karte hue aur unhe apne ek-tarfa drishtikon ke sath square banane ki koshish karte hue. Is tarah, yadyapi commentators ki kami nahi rahi hai, sirf kuch hi task ke barabar sabit hue hain. Mere dost Sheng-yu is galati mein nahi gire hain. Sun Tzu ke karya ke liye ek critical commentary pradan karne ki koshish mein, woh is tathya ko dhyan se nahi khote hain ki yeh sayings internecine warfare mein shamil states ke liye intended thin; ki author teen praacheen dynasties ke sovereigns ke tehat prevailing military sthitiyon se chintit nahi hai, na hi Minister of War dwara prescribed nau punitive measures se. Phir se, Sun Wu ne diction ki brevity se pyaar kiya, lekin unka matlab hamesha gehra hota hai. Chahe vishay sena ko march karwana ho, ya sainikon ko sambhalna ho, ya dushman ka anuman lagana ho, ya jeet ki forces ko control karna ho, ise hamesha vyavasthit roop se treat kiya jata hai; sayings sakht logical sequence mein ek sath bandhe hue hain, halanki ise un commentators dwara obscured kar diya gaya hai jo shayad unke matlab ko pakadne mein asafal rahe hain. Apne commentary mein, Mei Sheng-yu ne in critics ke sabhi sakht pakshpaaton ko hata diya hai, aur khud Sun Tzu ke asli matlab ko baahar lane ki koshish ki hai. Is tarah, ulhan ke baadal bikhair diye gaye hain aur sayings saaf kiye gaye hain. Main ashwast hoon ki vartaman karya teen bade commentaries ke sath side by side hand down hone ke yogya hai; aur aage aane wali peedhiyon ke paas mere dost Sheng-yu ka dhanyavaad karne ka lagatar kaaran hoga us kaafi sabhi ke liye jo woh sayings mein paate hain.

Dosti ki exuberance ke liye kuch allowance banate hue, main is favorable judgment ko endorse karne ki taraf jhukta hoon, aur nischit roop se unhe merit ke order mein Ch`en Hao ke upar rakhunga.

9. **WANG HSI**, Sung dynasty ke bhi, unki kuch interpretations mein nischit roop se original hain, lekin Mei Yao-ch`en se kaafi kam judicious, aur kul milakar ek bahut vishwasniya guide nahi hain. Woh Ts`ao Kung ke sath apne commentary ki tulna karne ke shauqeen hain, lekin tulna unke liye aksar flattering nahi hoti. Hum Ch`ao Kung-wu se seekhte hain ki Wang Hsi ne Sun Tzu ke praacheen text ko revise kiya, lacunae ko bhara aur galatiyon ko correct kiya.
10. **HO YEN-HSI** Sung dynasty ke. Is commentator ka vyaktigat naam Cheng Ch`iao dwara *TUNG CHIH* mein upar bataye anusar diya gaya hai, jo twelfth century ke beech ke aaspas likha gaya tha, lekin woh *YU HAI* mein simply Ho Shih ke roop mein dikhte hain, aur Ma Tuan-lin ne Ch`ao Kung-wu ko yeh kehte hue quote kiya ki unka vyaktigat naam an-jaan hai. Cheng Ch`iao ke bayan par shak karne ka koi kaaran nahi lagta, doosri tarah se main ek anuman lagane aur unhe ek Ho Ch`u-fei ke sath pehchanne ki taraf jhuka hota, jo war par ek chhote treatise ke lekhak the, jo 11th century ke baad ke hisse mein rahe the. Ho Shih ka commentary, *T`IEN-I-KO* catalogue ke shabdon mein, yahan-wahan "helpful additions" rakhta hai, lekin mukhya roop se dynastic histories aur doosre sroton se adapted form mein liye gaye prachur extracts ke liye ullekhniya hai.
11. **CHANG YU.** List ek aise commentator ke sath khatam hoti hai jo shayad koi badi originality ka nahi hai, lekin lucid exposition ki adbhut powers se samppan hai. Unka commentary Ts`ao Kung ke commentary par aadharit hai, jinaki terse sentences ko woh masterly fashion mein expand aur viksit karne mein kamyab hote hain. Chang Yu ke bina, yeh kehna surakshit hai ki Ts`ao Kung ke commentary ka kaafi hissa apni pristine obscurity mein cloaked raha hota aur isliye mulya-heen. Unka karya Sung history, *T`UNG K`AO*, ya *YU HAI* mein zikr nahi kiya gaya hai, lekin yeh *T`UNG CHIH* mein ek niche paata hai, jo unhe "Lives of Famous Generals" ke lekhak ke roop mein bhi naam deta hai.

Yeh kaafi ullekhniya hai ki aakhri-naam wale chaaron ko itne chhote samay ke andar phalna-phoolna chahiye tha. Ch`ao Kung-wu ise yeh kehkar account karte hain: "Sung dynasty ke shuruati saalon ke dauran Empire ne shanti ka ek lamba daur anand liya, aur mardon ne war ki art ka abhyas karna band kar diya. Lekin jab [Chao] Yuan-hao ka vidroh aaya [1038-42] aur frontier generals baar-baar haraye gaye, toh Court ne war mein kushal mardon ke liye sakht jaanch ki, aur military topics sabhi uche officials ke beech vogue ban gaye. Isliye yeh hai ki hamari dynasty mein Sun Tzu ke commentators mukhya roop se us daur se sambandhit hain."

In gyarah commentators ke alawa, kai doosre hain jinka karya hum tak nahi pahoncha hai. *SUI SHU* chaar ka zikr karta hai, yaani Wang Ling (Tu Yu dwara aksar Wang Tzu ke roop mein quoted); Chang Tzu-shang; Wei ke Chia Hsu; aur Wu ke Shen Yu. *T`ANG SHU* Sun Hao ko jodta hai, aur *T`UNG CHIH* Hsiao Chi ko, jabki *T`U SHU* ek Ming commentator, Huang Jun-yu ka zikr karta hai. Yeh sambhav hai ki inmein se kuch upar bataye gaye Chi T`ien-pao aur Chi Hsieh ki tarah doosre commentaries ke collectors aur editors rahe hon.

## Appreciations of Sun Tzu (Sun Tzu Ki Sarahana)

Sun Tzu ne China ke kuch sabse bade mardon ke dimaagon par ek potent fascination ka abhyas kiya hai. Prasiddh generals mein jinhe utsah ke sath unke pannon ka adhyayan karne ke liye jaana jata hai, Han Hsin (d. 196 B.C.), Feng I (d. 34 A.D.), Lu Meng (d. 219), aur Yo Fei (1103-1141) ka zikr kiya ja sakta hai. Ts`ao Kung ka opinion, jo Chinese military annals mein sabse uche sthaan ke liye Han Hsin ke sath dispute karta hai, pehle hi record kiya ja chuka hai.

Isse bhi zyada ullekhniya, ek tareeqe se, Su Hsun (Su Tung-p`o ke father) jaise poori tarah se literary mardon ka testimony hai, jinhone military topics par kai essays likhe, jin sabhi ne apni mukhya prerna Sun Tzu se li. Unka nimnlikhit chhota passage *YU HAI* mein surakshit hai: --

> Sun Wu ka kehna, ki war mein koi jeetne ka nishchit nahi kar sakta, sach mein bahut alag hai jo doosri kitaabein humein batati hain. Wu Ch`i Sun Wu ke hi stamp ke aadmi the: un dono ne war par kitaabein likhin, aur woh popular speech mein "Sun and Wu" ke roop mein jude hue hain. Lekin war par Wu Ch`i ke remarks kam vazani hain, unke rules zyada rough aur zyada crudely stated hain, aur plan ki wahi unity nahi hai jo Sun Tzu ke karya mein hai, jahan style terse hai, lekin matlab poori tarah se baahar laya gaya hai.

Nimnlikhit Cheng Hou dwara "Impartial Judgments in the Garden of Literature" se ek extract hai: --

> Sun Tzu ke 13 chapters na sirf sabhi military men ki training ke staple aur base hain, balki scholars aur men of letters ke sabse dhyan se dhyan ko bhi majboor karte hain. Unke sayings terse hain phir bhi elegant, saral hain phir bhi gehre, perspicuous aur atyadhik practical. *LUN YU*, *I CHING* aur bada Commentary, saath hi Mencius, Hsun K`uang aur Yang Chu ke writings jaise works, sabhi Sun Tzu ke level se neeche girte hain.

Chu Hsi, ispar tippani karte hue, criticism ke pehle hisse ko poori tarah se swikar karte hain, yadyapi woh aadaraniya classical works ke sath bold tulna ko na-pasand karte hain. Is prakar ki bhasha, woh kehte hain,

> "ek ruler ke un-relenting warfare aur reckless militarism ki taraf jhukav ko encourage karti hai."

## Apologies for War (Yuddh Ki Apologies / Safai)

Earth par sabse bade shanti-priya rashtra ke roop mein China ke baare mein sochne ke aadi hone ke naate, hum is khatre mein hain ki yeh bhool jayein ki war ke sabhi phases mein unka anubhav bhi aisa raha hai jiska koi modern State mukabla nahi kar sakta. Unki lambi military annals us point tak wapas phali hui hain jahan woh samay ke dundh mein khogaye hain. Unhone Great Wall banayi thi aur Danube par pehla Roman legionary dekhe jaane se sadiyon pehle apni frontier ke sath ek vishaal standing army banaye rakhe hue the. Praacheen feudal States ke lagatar collisions, centralization of government ke baad Huns, Turks aur doosre invaders ke sath grim conflicts, itni saari dynasties ke overthrow ke sath aane wale terrific upheavals, un count-less rebellions aur minor disturbances ke alawa jo ek-ek karke jale aur phir bujh gaye, yeh kehna shayad hi bahut zyada hoga ki arms ka clash Empire ke kisi na kisi hisse mein kabhi resound karna band nahi hua hai.

Utna hi ullekhniya illustrious captains ka succession hai jin par China shaan ke sath ishara kar sakta hai. Sabhi deshon ki tarah, sabse bade unke history ke sabse fateful crises par ubharne ke shauqeen hain. Is tarah, Po Ch`i us period mein vishisht khade hain jab Ch`in baaki independent states ke sath apne aakhri sangharsh mein pravesh kar raha tha. Ch`in dynasty ke breakup ke baad ke stormy years Han Hsin ke transcendent genius dwara illuminated hain. Jab Han ki House badle mein apne pataan ki taraf ladkhada rahi hai, Ts`ao Ts`ao ki badi aur baleful figure scene par dominate karti hai. Aur T`ang dynasty ki sthapna mein, man dwara haasil kiye gaye sabse shaktishali tasks mein se ek, Li Shih-min (baad mein Emperor T`ai Tsung) ki superhuman energy Li Ching ki shaandar strategy dwara seconded ki gayi thi. In generals mein se kisi ko bhi Europe ke military history mein sabse bade naamon ke sath tulna se darne ki zaroorat nahi hai.

Is sabke baavjood, Chinese sentiment ka bada body, Lao Tzu se neeche tak, aur vishesh roop se Confucianism ke standard literature mein reflected, lagatar pacific aur kisi bhi roop mein militarism ke atyadhik virodhi raha hai. Principle par warfare ka bachao karne wala koi bhi literati paana itna a-samanya kaam hai, ki maine kuch passages ko ik इकट्ठा karne aur translate karne ko worth while socha hai jismein un-orthodoxy view ko upheld kiya gaya hai. Nimnlikhit, Ssu-ma Ch`ien dwara, dikhata hai ki Confucius ke unke ardent admiration ke baavjood, woh phir bhi kisi bhi keemat par shanti ke advocate nahi the: --

> Military weapons Sage dwara hinsa aur krurta ko saja dene, pareshani ke samayon ko shanti dene, kathinaaiyon aur khatron ko hatane, aur sankat mein pade logon ki sahayata karne ke liye upayog kiye jaane wale saadhan hain. Apni veins mein khoon aur apne head par horns wala har jaanwar ladega jab uspar hamla kiya jayega. Man kitna zyada aisa karega, jo apne breast mein pyaar aur nafrat, khushi aur gusse ki faculties ko dharan karta hai! Jab woh khush hota hai, toh uske andar sneh ki bhavna jaagti hai; jab gussa hota hai, toh uska poisonous sting play mein laya jata hai. Yeh praakritik kanoon hai jo uske astitva ko govern karta hai.... Toh hamare samay ke un scholars ke baare mein kya kaha jayega, sabhi bade mudon se andhe, aur relative values ki kisi sarahana ke bina, jo sirf "virtue" aur "civilization" ke baare mein apne stale formulas ko bhonk sakte hain, military weapons ke upayog ki ninda karte hue? Woh nischit roop se hamare desh ko impotence aur dishonor aur uske uchit heritage ke nuksan taraf le jaayenge; ya, kam se kam, woh hamla aur vidroh, territory ka tyag aur aam kamzori layenge. Phir bhi woh stubbornness se apne liye sthaan ko modify karne se inkaar karte hain. Sach yeh hai ki, jaise parivar mein teacher ko rod ko nahi chhodna chahiye, aur punishments ko State mein dispense nahi kiya ja sakta, waise hi military chastisement ko Empire mein abeyance mein girne ki anumati kabhi nahi di ja sakti. Koi sirf itna keh sakta hai ki yeh taqat kuch dwara samajhdari se, doosron dwara bewakoofi se exercise ki jayegi, aur ki arms dharan karne walon mein se kuch vafadar honge aur doosre vidrohi.

Agla piece Sun Tzu par unke commentary ke liye Tu Mu ke preface se liya gaya hai: --

> War ko punishment ke roop mein paribhashit kiya ja sakta hai, jo government ke functions mein se ek hai. Yeh Chung Yu aur Jan Ch`iu dono Confucius ke disciples ka profession tha. Aaj kal, trials aayojit karna aur litigation sunna, offenders ko jail mein daalna aur market-place mein flogging dwara unka execution karna, sabhi officials dwara kiya jata hai. Lekin vishaal senayon ko chalaana, fortified cities ko neeche phenkna, mahilaon aur bachhon ko qaid mein khinchti hui le jana, aur traitors ka sar kalam karna -- yeh bhi kaam hai jo officials dwara kiya jata hai.   Rack aur military weapons ke objects mool roop se ek hi hain. Flogging ki saja aur war mein heads cutting off ke beech koi aantarik antar nahi hai. Kanoon ke chhote infractions ke liye, jinse aasaani se nipata jata hai, sirf thodi matra mein force niyukt karne ki zaroorat hoti hai: isliye military weapons aur wholesale decapitation ka upayog. Dono mamlon mein, halanki, end in view dushth logon se chhutkara paana hai, aur achhe logon ko comfort aur relief dena hai....   Chi-sun ne Jan Yu se poocha, kehte hue: "Kya aapne, Sir, adhyayan dwara apni military aptitude haasil ki hai, ya yeh innate hai?" Jan Yu ne jawab diya: "Yeh adhyayan dwara haasil ki gayi hai." "Yeh kaise ho sakta hai," Chi-sun ne kaha, "yeh dekhte hue ki aap Confucius ke disciple hain?" "Yeh ek tathya hai," Jan Yu ne jawab diya; "mujhe Confucius dwara padhaya gaya tha. Yeh uchit hai ki bade Sage ko civil aur military dono functions exercise karne chahiye, halanki pakka hone ke liye fighting ki art mein meri instruction abhi tak bahut door nahi gayi hai."   Ab, "civil" aur "military" ke beech is sakht antar ka author kaun tha, aur har ek ko action ke ek alag sphere tak seemit karne wala, ya kis dynasty ke kis saal mein ise pehle introduced kiya gaya tha, mere kehne se zyada hai. Lekin, kisi bhi maamle mein, yeh hua hai ki governing class ke sadasya military topics par vistarit karne se kaafi darte hain, ya sirf ek sharmnak dhang se aisa karte hain. Agar koi vishay par charcha karne ke liye kaafi bold hain, toh unhe turant coarse aur brutal propensities ke eccentric individuals ke roop mein set down kar diya jata hai. Yeh ek ashadharan instance hai jismein, reasoning ki sheer kami ke zariya, mard dukhdaai roop se buniyaadi siddhanton ko kho dete hain.   Jab Duke of Chou Ch`eng Wang ke tehat minister the, unhone ceremonies ko regulate kiya aur music banaya, aur scholarship aur learning ki arts ka satkar kiya; phir bhi jab River Huai ke barbarians ne vidroh kiya, woh sally forth hue aur unhe saja di. Jab Confucius ne Duke of Lu ke tehat office sambhala, aur Chia-ku mein ek meeting bulayi gayi, unhone kaha: "Agar pacific negotiations pragati mein hain, toh warlike preparations pehle se hi ki jaani chahiye thin." Unhone Ch`i ke Marquis ko rebuked aur shamed kiya, jo unke neeche cowered hue the aur hinsa ki taraf badhne ki himmat nahi ki. Kaise kaha ja sakta hai ki in do bade Sages ko military maamlon ka koi gyaan nahi tha?

Humne dekha hai ki bade Chu Hsi Sun Tzu ko ucha aadar dete the. Woh Classics ki authority ko bhi appeal karte hain: --

> Hamare Master Confucius ne, Wei ke Duke Ling ko jawab dete hue kaha: "Maine kabhi armies aur battalions se jude maamlon ka adhyayan nahi kiya hai." K`ung Wen-tzu ko jawab dete hue, unhone kaha: mujhe buff-coats aur weapons ke baare mein instruct nahi kiya gaya hai." Lekin agar hum Chia-ku mein meeting ki taraf mudte hain, toh hum paate hain ki unhone Lai ke mardon ke khilaf armed force ka upayog kiya, taaki Ch`i ka marquis overawed ho jaye. Phir se, jab Pi ke nivaasiyon ne vidroh kiya, unhone apne officers ko unpar hamla karne ka order diya, jispar woh haraye gaye aur ulhan mein bhaag gaye. Unhone ek baar shabd kahe: "Agar main ladta hoon, toh main jeetta hoon." Aur Jan Yu ne bhi kaha: "Sage civil aur military dono functions exercise karta hai." Kya yeh ek tathya ho sakta hai ki Confucius ne war ki art mein kabhi adhyayan ya instruction praapt nahi ki? Hum sirf itna keh sakte hain ki unhone apni teaching ka vishay banne ke liye armies aur fighting se jude maamlon ko vishesh roop se nahi chuna.

Sun Tzu ke editor, Sun Hsing-yen, isi tarah ke strain mein likhte hain: --

> Confucius ne kaha: "Main military maamlon mein unversed hoon." Unhone yeh bhi kaha: "Agar main ladta hoon, toh main jeetta hoon." Confucius ne ceremonies ko order kiya aur music ko regulate kiya. Ab war State ceremonial ke paanch classes mein se ek banata hai, aur ise study ki ek independent branch ke roop mein treat nahi kiya jana chahiye. Isliye, "unversed in" shabdon ko yeh matlab lene ke liye liya jana chahiye ki aisi cheezein hain jo ek inspired Teacher bhi nahi jaanta. Jinhe sena ka netritv karna hai aur stratagems devise karne hain, unhe war ki art seekhni chahiye. Lekin agar koi Sun Tzu jaise achhe general ki services ko command kar sakta hai, jise Wu Tzu-hsu dwara niyukt kiya gaya tha, toh ise khud seekhne ki koi zaroorat nahi hai. Isliye Confucius dwara joda gaya remark: "Agar main ladta hoon, toh main jeetta hoon."
> Vartaman din ke mard, halanki, Confucius ke in shabdon ko unke sankuchit arth mein jaan-boojhkar interpret karte hain, jaise ki unka matlab tha ki war ki art par kitaabein padhne yogya nahi thin. Andhi dridhta ke sath, woh Chao Kua ke udaharan ko adduce karte hain, jo apne father ki kitaabon par bina kisi uddeshya ke pore karte the, is sabhoot ke roop mein ki saari military theory vyarth hai. Phir se, yeh dekhte hue ki war par kitaabon ko plans design karne mein opportunism, aur spies ke conversion jaisi cheezon se lena-dena hai, woh hold karte hain ki art a-naitik hai aur sage ke a-yogya. Yeh log is tathya ko nazar-andaz karte hain ki hamare scholars ke adhyayanon aur hamare officials ke civil administration ko bhi efficiency tak pahonchne se pehle steady application aur abhyas ki zaroorat hoti hai. Praacheen log vishesh roop se mamooli novices ko apne kaam ko botch karne ki anumati dene se chary the. Weapons baneful hain aur fighting perilous; aur vyarth jab tak ki ek general lagatar abhyas mein na ho, use battle mein doosre mardon ki lives ko hazard nahi karna chahiye. Isliye yeh aavashyak hai ki Sun Tzu ke 13 chapters ka adhyayan kiya jaye.
> Hsiang Liang apne nephew Chi ko war ki art mein instruct karte the. Chi ko iske general bearings mein art ka ek rough idea mila, lekin woh apne adhyayanon ko unke uchit outcome tak nahi badhayenge, parinaam yeh hua ki woh aakhirkar haraye gaye aur overthrown hue. Unhone ehsaas nahi kiya ki war ke tricks aur artifices verbal computation से pare hain. Sung ke Duke Hsiang aur Hsu ke King Yen ko unke misplaced humanity dwara destruction mein laya gaya tha. War ki treacherous aur underhand prakriti avsar ke anusar guile aur stratagem ke upayog ko zaroori banati hai. Confucius dwara ek extorted oath ka ulanghan karne, aur unke disguise mein Sung State chhodne ka ek record par case hai. Kya hum phir recklessness se Sun Tzu par sach aur imaandari ko disregard karne ka aarop laga sakte hain?

## Bibliography (Granth-Soochi)

Sun Tzu ke baad, war par nimnlikhit sabse purane Chinese treatises hain. Har ek par notes mukhya roop se *SSU K`U CH`UAN SHU CHIEN MING MU LU*, ch. 9, fol. 22 sqq से khinche gaye hain.

1. **WU TZU**, 1 *CHUAN* ya 6 chapters mein. Wu Ch`i (d. 381 B.C.) dwara. Ek genuine karya. Dekhein *SHIH CHI*, ch. 65.
2. **SSU-MA FA**, 1 *CHUAN* ya 5 chapters mein. 6th century B.C. ke Ssu-ma Jang-chu ko galat tareeqe se aavantit. Iski date, halanki, shuruati honi chahiye, kyunki teen praacheen dynasties ke riwaaj unke pannon ke andar lagatar milne hain. Dekhein *SHIH CHI*, ch. 64.
*SSU K`U CH`UAN SHU* (ch. 99, f. 1) tippani karta hai ki war par sabse purane teen treatises, *SUN TZU*, *WU TZU* aur *SSU-MA FA*, aam taur par bolte hue, sirf sakhti se military cheezon se chintit hain -- troops produce karne, ik इकट्ठा karne, train karne aur drill karne ki art, aur expedient measures, plans lay karne, goods ke transport aur sainikon ko sambhalne ke sambandh mein correct theory -- baad ke works ke sath majboot contrast mein, jismein war ki science ko aam taur par metaphysics, divination aur magical arts ke sath milaya jata hai.
3. LIU T`AO**, 6 *CHUAN*, ya 60 chapters mein. 12th century B.C. ke Lu Wang (ya Lu Shang, T`ai Kung ke roop mein bhi jaane jaate hain) ko aavantit. Lekin iska style Three Dynasties ke era se sambandhit nahi hai. Lu Te-ming (550-625 A.D.) karya ka zikr karta hai, aur chhah sections ke headings ko enumerates karta hai taaki forgery Sui dynasty se baad ki na ho sake.
4. **WEI LIAO TZU**, 5 *CHUAN* mein. Wei Liao (4th cent. B.C.) ko aavantit, jinhone prasiddh Kuei-ku Tzu ke tehat adhyayan kiya. Karya mool roop se 31 chapters mein raha lagta hai, jabki hamare paas jo text hai usme sirf 24 hain. Iska matter mool mein paryapt roop se sound hai, yadyapi strategical devices Warring States period ke logon se kaafi alag hain. Ise jaane-maane Sung philosopher Chang Tsai dwara ek commentary pradan kiya gaya hai.
5. **SAN LUEH**, 3 *CHUAN* mein. Huang-shih Kung ko aavantit, ek legendary personage jinhone ek bridge par ek interview mein Chang Liang (d. 187 B.C.) ko ise bestow kiya tha kaha jata hai. Lekin yahan phir se, style Ch`in ya Han period se dating wale works ka nahi hai. Han Emperor Kuang Wu [25-57 A.D.] zahir taur par unke ek proclamations mein isse quote karta hai; lekin vicharadhin passage ko baad mein insert kiya gaya ho sakta hai, karya ki genuineness ko sabit karne ke liye. Hum bahut door nahi honge agar hum ise Northern Sung period [420-478 A.D.], ya thoda pehle sandarbhit karte hain.
6. **LI WEI KUNG WEN TUI**, 3 sections mein. T`ai Tsung aur unke bade general Li Ching ke beech ek dialogue ke roop mein likha gaya, ise aam taur par baad wale ko shrey diya jata hai. Competent authorities ise ek forgery maante hain, yadyapi author war ki art mein saaf taur par achhi tarah se nipun tha.
7. **LI CHING PING FA** (pehle wale ke sath uljhaya na jaye) 8 chapters mein ek chhota treatise hai, jo *T`ung Tien* mein surakshit hai, lekin alag se publish nahi kiya gaya hai. Yeh tathya *SSU K`U CH`UAN SHU* se iske omission ko samjhata hai.
8. **WU CH`I CHING**, 1 *CHUAN* mein. Legendary minister Feng Hou ko aavantit, Han dynasty ke Kung-sun Hung (d. 121 B.C.) dwara exegetical notes ke sath, aur prasiddh general Ma Lung (d. 300 A.D.) dwara eulogised kiya gaya tha kaha jata hai. Phir bhi iska sabse shuruati zikr *SUNG CHIH* mein hai. Yadyapi ek forgery, karya achhi tarah se ek sath rakha gaya hai.

Is baat par vichar karte hue ki Chu-ko Liang ko hamesha se kitna ucha popular estimation mein rakha gaya hai, war par unke pen ko shrey diye gaye ek se zyada karya paana hairani ki baat nahi hai. Aise hain (1) *SHIH LIU TS`E* (1 *CHUAN*), *YUNG LO TA TIEN* mein surakshit; (2) *CHIANG YUAN* (1 *CHUAN*); aur (3) *HSIN SHU* (1 *CHUAN*), jo Sun Tzu se wholesale chori karta hai. Inmein se kisi ke paas bhi genuine maane jaane ka thoda sa bhi daava nahi hai.

# I. LAYING PLANS (I. YOJNAAYEIN BANANA)

[Ts`ao Kung, is chapter ke title ke Chinese ke arth ko paribhashit karte hue, kehte hain ki yeh general dwara apne aasthayi upayog ke liye chune gaye temple mein chintan ko sandarbhit karta hai, ya jaise humein kehna chahiye, unke tent mein. Dekhein ss. 26.]

1. Sun Tzu ne kaha: War ki art State ke liye atyadhik mahatvapurna hai.
2. Yeh zindagi aur maut ka maamla hai, ya toh suraksha ka ya vinash ka raasta. Isliye yeh jaanch ka ek aisa vishay hai jise kisi bhi keemat par nazar-andaz nahi kiya ja sakta.
3. War ki art, phir, paanch sthir karakon (constant factors) dwara sanchalit hoti hai, jinhe kisi dwara maidaan mein praapt sthitiyon ko tay karne ki koshish karte waqt, apne chintan mein dhyan mein rakha jana hai.
4. Yeh hain: (1) The Moral Law; (2) Heaven; (3) Earth; (4) The Commander; (5) Method and discipline.

[Aage aane wali baaton se aisa lagta hai ki Sun Tzu ka "Moral Law" se matlab samanjasya (harmony) ka ek siddhant hai, jo apne naitik pehlu mein Lao Tzu ke Tao se alag nahi hai. Koi ise "morale" dwara render karne ke liye lubhaya ja sakta hai, agar ise ss. 13 mein ruler ka ek attribute na maana gaya hota.]

5, 6. MORAL LAW logon ko apne ruler ke sath poori tarah se anukool hone ka kaaran banta hai, taaki woh apni zindagiyon ki parwah kiye bina, kisi bhi khatre se un-dar kar unka palan karenge.

[Tu Yu ne Wang Tzu ko yeh kehte hue quote kiya: "Niyamit abhyas ke bina, officers battle ke liye ik इकट्ठा hote waqt nervous aur a-nirnayak honge; niyamit abhyas ke bina, general sankat ke paas hone par wavering aur irresolute hoga."]

7. HEAVEN raat aur din, thand aur garmi, samay aur mausam ko darshata hai.

[Commentators, mujhe lagta hai, yahan do shabdon ka fuzool ka rahasya banate hain. Meng Shih Heaven ke "hard aur soft, waxing aur waning" ko sandarbhit karte hain. Wang Hsi, halanki, yeh kehne mein sahi ho sakte hain ki jisaka matlab hai "Heaven ki general economy," jismein paanch tatva, chaar mausam, hawa aur baadal, aur doosre ghatnaayein shamil hain.]

8. EARTH mein dooriyan, badi aur chhoti; khatra aur suraksha; khula maidaan aur sankuchit raaste; zindagi aur maut ke chances shamil hain.
9. COMMANDER wisdom, sincerity, benevolence, courage aur strictness ke sadgunon ko darshata hai.

[Chinese ke paanch cardinal sadgun hain (1) humanity ya benevolence; (2) uprightness of mind; (3) self-respect, self-control, ya "proper feeling;" (4) wisdom; (5) sincerity ya good faith. Yahan "wisdom" aur "sincerity" ko "humanity ya benevolence" se pehle rakha gaya hai, aur do military sadgunon "courage" aur "strictness" ko "uprightness of mind" aur "self-respect, self-control, ya 'proper feeling'" ke liye substitute kiya gaya hai.]

10. METHOD AND DISCIPLINE dwara sena ko iske uchit sub-divisions mein sangathit karna, officers ke beech rank ke graduations, un raaston ka rakh-rakhav jinase sena tak supplies pahonch sakti hain, aur military expenditure ke control ko samjha jana hai.
11. Yeh paanch heads har general ko parichit hone chahiye: woh jo unhe jaanta hai woh jeetega; woh jo unhe nahi jaanta woh asafal hoga.
12. Isliye, apne chintan mein, jab military sthitiyon ko tay karne ki koshish kar rahe hon, toh unhe is tarah se ek tulna ka aadhar banne dein: --
13. (1) Do sovereigns mein se kaun Moral law se samppan hai?

[Yaani, "apne praja ke sath samanjasya mein hai." Cf. ss. 5.]

(2) Do generals mein se kiske paas sabse zyada kshamta hai?

(3) Heaven aur Earth se praapt labh kiske sath hain?

[Dekhein ss. 7,8]

(4) Kis taraf anushasan sabse sakhti se laagu kiya jata hai?

[Tu Mu Ts`ao Ts`ao (A.D. 155-220) ki adbhut story ki taraf ishara karte hain, jo itne sakht disciplinarian the ki ek baar, khadi fasalon ko nuksan ke khilaf apne khud ke sakht kanoonon ke anusar, unhone apne ghode ko makki ke khet mein shying karne ki anumati dene ke liye khud ko mrityudand sunaya! Halanki, apna sar khone ke bajaye, unhe apne baal kaatkar apne nyay ke ahasas ko santusht karne ke liye mana liya gaya tha. Vartaman passage par Ts`ao Ts`ao ki apni tippani vishisht roop se chhotisi hai: "jab aap koi kanoon banate hain, toh dekhein ki iska ulanghan na ho; agar iska ulanghan hota hai toh apradhi ko maut ke ghat utar diya jana chahiye."]

(5) Kaun si sena zyada majboot hai?

[Bhaautik roop se ke sath-sath naitik roop se bhi. Jaise Mei Yao-ch`en ise rakhte hain, swatantra roop se rendered, "ESPIRIT DE CORPS aur 'big battalions'."]

(6) Kis taraf officers aur men zyada uche roop se trained hain?

[Tu Yu ne Wang Tzu ko yeh kehte hue quote kiya: "Niyamit abhyas ke bina, officers battle ke liye ik इकट्ठा hote waqt nervous aur a-nirnayak honge; niyamit abhyas ke bina, general sankat ke paas hone par wavering aur irresolute hoga."]

(7) Kis sena mein inaam aur saja dono mein zyada nirantarata hai?

[Kis taraf is baat ki sabse absolute nishchitata hai ki yogyata ko uchit roop se inaam diya jayega aur galat kaam ko sankhipt roop se saja di jayegi?]

14. In saat vicharon ke zariya main jeet ya haar ka purvanuman laga sakta hoon.
15. Jo general meri salah sunta hai aur uspar kaam karta hai, woh jeetega: --aise vyakti ko command mein rakha jaye! Jo general meri salah nahi sunta aur na hi uspar kaam karta hai, woh haar ka samna karega: --aise vyakti ko kharij kiya jaye!

[Is paragraph ka roop humein yaad dilata hai ki Sun Tzu ka treatise vishesh roop se unke patron Ho Lu, Wu State ke king ke labh ke liye compose kiya gaya tha.]

16. Meri salah ke labh par dhyan dete hue, aam kanoonon se aage aur pare kisi bhi sahayak paristhitiyon ka bhi faayda uthaayein.
17. Jaise paristhitiyan anukool hongi, waise hi kisi ko apne plans ko modify karna chahiye.

[Sun Tzu, ek practical soldier ke roop mein, "bookish theoric" mein se koi nahi rakhenge. Woh humein yahan abstract siddhanton par apna vishwās na tikaane ki chetawani dete hain; "kyunki," jaise Chang Yu ise rakhte hain, "jabki strategy ke mukhya kanoonon ko sabhi ke labh ke liye kaafi saaf taur par bataya ja sakta hai, aapko actual warfare mein ek anukool sthiti surakshit karne ki koshish mein dushman ki kriyaon dwara nirdeshit hona chahiye." Waterloo ki ladai ki eve par, cavalry ko command karne wale Lord Uxbridge, Duke of Wellington ke paas gaye agle din ke liye unke plans aur calculations kya the yeh jaan-ne ke liye, kyunki, jaise unhone samjaya, woh achanak khud ko Commander-in-chief pa sakte the aur ek critical moment mein naye plans banane mein a-saksham hote. Duke ne shanti se suna aur phir kaha: "Kal pehle hamla kaun karega -- main ya Bonaparte?" "Bonaparte," Lord Uxbridge ne jawab diya. "Khair," Duke ne jaari rakha, "Bonaparte ne mujhe apne projects ka koi idea nahi diya hai; aur kyunki mere plans unpar nirbhar karenge, aap mujhse yeh batane ki umeed kaise kar sakte hain ki mere kya hain?" [1]]

18. Saari warfare dhokhe (deception) par aadharit hai.

[Is pithy aur gehre kehne ki sachai ko har soldier dwara swikar kiya jayega. Col. Henderson ne humein bataya ki Wellington, itne saare military qualities mein bade, vishesh roop se us ashadharan skill dwara vishisht the jiske sath unhone apni movements ko chhipaya aur dost aur dushman dono ko dhokha diya.]

19. Isliye, jab hamla karne mein saksham hon, toh humein a-saksham lagna chahiye; jab apni forces ka upayog kar rahe hon, toh humein nishkriya lagna chahiye; jab hum paas hon, toh humein dushman ko yeh vishwās dilana chahiye ki hum door hain; jab door hon, toh humein use yeh vishwās dilana chahiye ki hum paas hain.
20. Dushman ko lubhane ke liye baaits (chara) pakdein. A-vyavastha (disorder) ka naatak karein, aur use kuchal dein.

[Chang Yu ko chhodkar sabhi commentators kehte hain, "Jab woh a-vyavastha mein ho, toh use kuchal dein." Yeh maanana zyada praakritik hai ki Sun Tzu abhi bhi war mein deception ke upayog ko darsha rahe hain.]

21. Agar woh sabhi points par surakshit hai, toh uske liye taiyar rahein. Agar woh uchi taqat mein hai, toh usse bachein (evade).
22. Agar aapka opponent gusse wale swabhav (choleric temper) ka hai, toh use chidhane ki koshish karein. Kamzor hone ka naatak karein, taaki woh ahankaari ho sake.

[Tu Yu dwara quoted Wang Tzu kehte hain ki achha tactician apne adversary ke sath waise hi khelta hai jaise billi chuhe ke sath khelti hai, pehle kamzori aur nishkriyata ka naatak karti hai, aur phir achanak uspar japat ti hai.]

23. Agar woh aaram kar raha hai, toh use koi aaram mat do.

[Yeh shayad matlab hai halanki Mei Yao-ch`en ke paas note hai: "jab hum aaram kar rahe hon, dushman ke khud ko thakaane ka intazar karein." *YU LAN* ke paas "Use lubhaayein aur use thakaayein" hai.]

Agar uski forces ekrat (united) hain, toh unhe alag karein.

[Kam plausble woh vyakhya hai jise zyadatar commentators dwara pasand kiya gaya hai: "Agar sovereign aur subject anukool mein hain, toh unke beech vibhajan daalein."]

24. Uspar wahan hamla karein jahan woh a-tai-yaar ho, wahan pratyaksh hon jahan aapki umeed na ho.
25. Yeh military devices, jeet ki taraf le jaane wale, pehle se ujagar nahi kiye jaane chahiye.
26. Ab jo general ladai jeetta hai woh ladai lade jaane se pehle apne temple mein kai calculations karta hai.

[Chang Yu humein batate hain ki praacheen samay mein ek aise general ke upayog ke liye ek temple ko alag rakhna riwaaj tha jo maidaan mein utarne wala tha, taaki woh wahan campaign ke apne plan ko vistarit kar sake.]

Jo general ladai haarta hai woh pehle se bahut kam calculations karta hai. Is tarah kai calculations jeet ki taraf le jaate hain, aur kam calculations haar ki taraf: kitna zyada koi calculation nahi bilkul! Is point par dhyan dene se hi main purvanuman laga sakta hoon ki kaun jeetne ya haarne ki sambhavna rakhta hai.

[1] Sir W. Fraser dwara "Words on Wellington".

# II. WAGING WAR (II. YUDDH LADNA)

[Ts`ao Kung ke paas note hai: "Jo yuddh ladna chahta hai use pehle keemat ginni chahiye," jo humein is khoj ke liye taiyar karta hai ki chapter ka vishay woh nahi hai jiski hum title se umeed kar sakte hain, balki mool roop se tareeqon aur saadhanon (ways and means) par vichar hai.]

1. Sun Tzu ne kaha: War ke operations mein, jahan maidaan mein ek hazaar swift chariots, utne hi heavy chariots, aur ek laakh mail-clad soldiers hon,

["Swift chariots" halke bane hue the aur, Chang Yu ke anusar, hamle ke liye upayog kiye jaate the; "heavy chariots" zyada bhaari the, aur suraksha ke uddeshyon ke liye design kiye gaye the. Li Ch`uan, yeh sach hai, kehte hain ki baad wale halke the, lekin yeh shayad hi sambhav lagta hai. Shuruati Chinese warfare aur Homeric Greeks ke beech ki taamsheelon ko note karna aakarshak hai. Har maamle mein, war-chariot mahatvapurna karak tha, kyunki isne woh nucleus banaya jiske aaspas foot-soldiers ki ek nishchit sankhya ko samoohit kiya gaya tha. Yahan di gayi sankhyaon ke sambandh mein, humein soochit kiya jata hai ki har swift chariot ke sath 75 footmen the, aur har heavy chariot ke sath 25 footmen, taaki poori sena ek hazaar battalions mein baanti jaye, har ek mein do chariots aur ek sau mard shamil hon.]

unhe ek hazaar LI tak le jaane ke liye paryapt rasad (provisions) ke sath,

[1 mile mein 2.78 modern LI hote hain. Lambai Sun Tzu ke samay se thodi alag rahi ho sakti hai.]

ghar par aur front par hone wala kharcha, mehmanon ka satkar, gond (glue) aur paint jaise chhote saman, aur chariots aur armor par kharcha ki gayi rakam shamil karke, prati din ek hazaar ounces silver ke kul tak pahonchega. Ek laakh mardon ki sena khadi karne ki keemat aisi hoti hai.

2. Jab aap actual fighting mein shamil hote hain, agar jeet aane mein lambi hai, toh mardon ke weapons dull ho jaayenge aur unka utsah (ardor) dhima pad jayega. Agar aap kisi shehar ka gheraav karte hain, toh aap apni taqat ko thaka denge.
3. Phir se, agar campaign lamba khinchta hai, toh State ke resources is dabaav ke barabar nahi honge.
4. Ab, jab aapke weapons dull ho jaate hain, aapka utsah dhima pad jata hai, aapki taqat thak jaati hai aur aapka khazana kharch ho jata hai, toh doosre chieftains aapki atyadhik sthiti (extremity) ka faayda uthane ke liye ubhar aayenge. Tab koi aadmi, chahe kitna bhi samajhdar kyun na ho, un parinaamon ko rokne mein saksham nahi hoga jo zaroor honge.
5. Is tarah, yadyapi humne war mein bewakoofana tezi (stupid haste) ke baare mein suna hai, chaturai ko kabhi bhi lambe vilamb (long delays) ke sath juda hua nahi dekha gaya hai.

[Yeh concise aur kathin sentence commentators mein se kisi dwara bhi achhi tarah se nahi samjaya gaya hai. Ts`ao Kung, Li Ch`uan, Meng Shih, Tu Yu, Tu Mu aur Mei Yao-ch`en ke paas is ashay ke notes hain ki ek general, swabhavik roop se bewakoof hone par bhi, tezi ki sheer taqat ke zariya jeet sakta hai. Ho Shih kehte hain: "Tezi bewakoofana ho sakti hai, lekin kisi bhi tarah yeh energy aur khazane ke kharche ko bachati hai; lambe operations bahut chatur ho sakte hain, lekin woh apne sath musibat laate hain." Wang Hsi yeh tippani karke kathinai se bachte hain: "Lambe operations ka matlab hai sena ka boorha hona, daulat ka kharch hona, ek khali khazana aur logon ke beech dukh; asli chaturai aisi musibaton ke hone se bachati hai." Chang Yu kehte hain: "Jab tak jeet haasil ki ja sakti hai, chaturai bhari deri ke muqable bewakoofana tezi pasandida hai."

Ab Sun Tzu kuch nahi kehte, sivaay shayad ishare se, ki bina vichar ki gayi tezi chalak lekin lambe operations se behtar hai. Woh jo kehte hain woh kaafi zyada surakshit hai, yaani ki, jabki speed kabhi-kabhi a-samajhdarana ho sakti hai, deri kabhi bhi bewakoofana ke alawa kuch nahi ho sakti -- agar sirf isliye kyunki iska matlab rashtra ke liye gareebi hai. Sun Tzu dwara yahan uthaye gaye point par vichar karte waqt, Fabius Cunctator ka classic udaharan apariharyata roop se dimaag mein aayega. Us general ne jaan-boojhkar Rome ke endure karne ki kshamta ko Hannibal ki alag-thalag sena ke khilaf naapa, kyunki use laga ki baad wale ko ek an-jaan desh mein lambe campaign se peedit hone ki zyada sambhavna thi. Lekin yeh poori tarah se ek vivadaaspad prashn hai ki kya unke tactics lambe daur mein kamyab sabit hue hote. Unka ulatna sach hai, Cannae ki taraf le gaya; lekin yeh sirf unke paksh mein ek negative anuman sthapit karta hai.]

6. Aisa koi udaharan nahi hai jahan kisi desh ko lambi warfare se labh hua ho.
7. Yeh sirf wohi hai jo war ki buraiyon se poori tarah se parichit hai jo ise aage badhane ke labhdayak tareeqe ko poori tarah se samajh sakta hai.

[Yaani, tezi ke sath. Sirf wohi jo lambe yuddh ke vinashkari prabhavon ko jaanta hai ise ant tak lane mein tezi ke atyadhik mahatva ko realize kar sakta hai. Sirf do commentators is vyakhya ka paksh lete lagte hain, lekin yeh context ke logic mein achhi tarah se fit baithta hai, jabki yeh rendering, "Jo war ki buraiyon ko nahi jaanta woh iske labhon ki sarahana nahi kar sakta," saaf taur par arth-heen hai.]

8. Kushal soldier doosra levy nahi uthata, na hi uske supply-wagons do baar se zyada load kiye jaate hain.

[Ek baar war declare ho jaane par, woh reinforcements ka intazar karne mein keemti samay barbaad nahi karega, na hi woh naye supplies ke liye apni sena ko wapas bheje ga, balki bina kisi vilamb ke dushman ki sarhad ko paar karta hai. Yeh sifarish karne ke liye ek sahasik policy lag sakti hai, lekin sabhi bade strategists ke sath, Julius Caesar se lekar Napoleon Bonaparte tak, samay ka mulya -- yaani, apne opponent se thoda aage hona -- numerical superiority ya commissariat ke sambandh mein sabse behtareen calculations dono se zyada maayne rakhta hai.]

9. Ghar se apne sath war material laayein, lekin dushman par forage (charai) karein. Is tarah sena ke paas apni zarooraton ke liye paryapt khana hoga.

[Yahan "war material" ke roop mein translate kiya gaya Chinese shabd shabdash "upayog ki jaane wali cheezein" ka matlab rakhta hai, aur iska matlab sabse vyapak arth mein hai. Isme rasad ko chhodkar sena ka saara saamaan (impedimenta) shamil hai.]

10. State khazane ki gareebi ke kaaran sena ko door se contributions dwara banaye rakha jata hai. Door se sena ko banaye rakhne mein yogdan dene se log gareeb ho jaate hain.

[Is sentence ki shuruat agle ke sath uchit roop se balance nahi karti hai, yadyapi spasht roop se aisa karne ke liye intended hai. Iske alawa, arrangement itna a-sahaj hai ki main text mein kisi corruption ka sandeh kiye bina nahi reh sakta. Chinese commentators ko kabhi aisa nahi lagta ki arth ke liye koi sodhan zaroori ho sakta hai, aur humein wahan se koi madad nahi milti. Sun Tzu ne logon ki gareebi ke kaaran ko darshane ke liye jin Chinese shabdon ka upayog kiya unka saaf taur par kisi aisi pranali se sandarbhit hai jiske dwara kisaanon ne apne makki ke yogdan seedhe sena ko bhej diye. Lekin unpar is tarah se sena ko banaye rakhne ka bojh kyun padna chahiye, sivaay iske ki State ya Government aisa karne ke liye bahut gareeb hai?]

11. Doosri taraf, sena ki nikatta ke kaaran keematein upar jaati hain; aur uchi keematein logon ke tatva (substance) ko bahaye jaane ka kaaran banti hain.
12. Jab unka tatva bahaya jata hai, toh kisaan bhaari exactions dwara peedit honge.

13, 14. Tatva ke is nuksan aur taqat ki thakavat ke sath, logon ke ghar poori tarah se khali ho jaayenge, aur unki aamdani ka teen-dasvaan hissa (3/10) nasht ho jayega;

[Tu Mu aur Wang Hsi sehamat hain ki logon se unki aamdani ka 3/10 nahi, balki 7/10 chheen liya jata hai. Lekin ise hamare text se nikalna kathin hai. Ho Shih ke paas ek characteristic tag hai: "PEOPLE ko State ka aavashyak hissa, aur FOOD ko logon ka heaven maane jaane par, kya yeh sahi nahi hai ki authority mein maujood logon ko dono ka mulya dena chahiye aur saavdhan rehna chahiye?"]

jabki toote hue chariots, thake hue ghodon, breastplates aur helmets, bows aur arrows, spears aur shields, protective mantles, draught-oxen aur heavy wagons ke liye sarakari kharche iski kul aamdani ka chaar-dasvaan hissa (4/10) honge.

15. Isliye ek samajhdar general dushman par forage karne ka point banata hai. Dushman ke rasad ka ek cartload apne khud ke bees (20) ke barabar hota hai, aur isi tarah uske charai ka ek PICUL apne khud ke store se bees (20) ke barabar hota hai.

[Kyunki front par ek cartload transport karne ki prakriya mein bees cartloads khap jaayenge. Ek PICUL 133.3 pounds (65.5 kilograms) ke barabar naap ki ek unit hai.]

16. Ab dushman ko maarne ke liye, hamare mardon ko gusse mein uttejit kiya jana chahiye; taaki dushman ko harane se labh ho sake, unke paas unke inaam hone chahiye.

[Tu Mu kehte hain: "Sainikon ko dushman ko harane ka labh dikhane ke liye inaam zaroori hain; is tarah, jab aap dushman se spoils capture karte hain, toh unka upayog inaam ke roop mein kiya jana chahiye, taaki aapke sabhi mardon ke paas ladne ki teevra ichha ho, har ek ke apne hisaab par."]

17. Isliye chariot fighting mein, jab das ya doosre chariots le liye gaye hon, toh unhe inaam diya jana chahiye jinhone pehla liya. Hamare apne jhande dushman ke jhandon ke liye substitute kiye jaane chahiye, aur chariots ko hamare sath milakar aur sahyog mein upayog kiya jana chahiye. Bandi banaye gaye sainikon ke sath dhyalu vyavhaar kiya jana chahiye aur rakha jana chahiye.
18. Ise kaha jata hai, apni khud ki taqat ko badhane ke liye jeete hue dushman ka upayog karna.
19. Yuddh mein, phir, apna bada uddeshya jeet hone dein, lambe campaigns nahi.

[Jaise Ho Shih tippani karte hain: "War koi aisi cheez nahi hai jiske sath khilvaad kiya jaye." Sun Tzu yahan mukhya sabak ko dohraate hain jise yeh chapter laagu karne ke liye intended hai.]

20. Is tarah yeh jaana ja sakta hai ki senayon ka leader logon ke bhagya ka faisla karne wala hai, woh aadmi jispar yeh nirbhar karta hai ki rashtra shanti mein hoga ya khatre mein.
