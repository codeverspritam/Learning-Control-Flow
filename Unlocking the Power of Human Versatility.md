### _(Prastavana)_

## Marking 500 Years Since the Death of Leonardo Da Vinci, the Archetypical Polymath

### _(Leonardo Da Vinci, Asali Bahumukhi Pratibha [Polymath] Ki Death Ke 500 Saalon Ka Chinhankan)_

---

Leonardo, jo _uomo universale_ (universal man yaani sarvabhaumik mard) hain, zyaada tar logon ke dimaag mein ek polymath (bahumukhi pratibha) ka mukhya roop hain.

Painting (chitrashala), sculpture (moortikala), architecture (vaastukala), stage design, music (sangeet), military aur civil engineering, mathematics (ganit), statics (sthitivigyan), dynamics (gativigyan), optics (prakashiki), anatomy (sharir-rachna), geology (bhoogarbhavigyan), botany (vanaspativigyan) aur zoology (jantuvigyan) — Leonardo ne inmein se zyaada tar cheezon ko ek aise level par jaatri rakkha jo in subjects ki kisi bhi history mein zikr ke layak (warrants mention) hai. Inmein se dheron fields ke professionals Leonardo mein khud ko dekhte hain, aur unhein apna ideal (aadarsh) claim karte hain.

Yeh poori tarah se sateek (appropriate) hai ki Waqās Ahmed ki kitab _The Polymath_ ke cover par Leonardo ka **Vitruvian Man** hona chahiye—woh phhaile hue haath-pairon wali aakriti (outstretched figure) jo ek square aur circle mein samahit hai, jo Vitruvius ki _The Ten Books on Architecture_ par aadharit hai. Yeh insani praniyon, duniya aur brahmand (cosmos) ki mukhya ekta (essential oneness) ke liye Leonardo ka ek visual bhajan (visual hymn) hai. Ise aksar vigyapanon (advertising) mein aur kahin aur mauqavadi tareeqe (opportunistically) se kisi aam cheez ko ek dikhne wali gehrai (apparent profundity) pradan karne ke liye use kiya jata hai. Yahan, halanki, yeh Ahmed ke prayas (endeavour) ke center mein sthit hai.

Yeh dekhte hue ki hum ab dimaagi aur practical prayasoon ko kaise classify aur compartmentalise (alag-alag hisson mein baantte) hain, hum Leonardo ki diversity (vividhata) ko dekhne ki taraf jhukte hain. Unhone ekta (unity) dekhi. Woh ekta physical world ke buniyaadi sangathan (fundamental organisation) ki thi, jo 'mathematics ke kanoonon, yaani sankhya aur maap (number and measure) — jise arithmetic aur geometry kaha jata hai, jo poori truth ke sath discontinuous aur continuous qualities se deal karte hain' ke aalingan (embrace) mein aati thi. Nature mein roopon ki dheron vividhata ke piche niyamit kanoonon (natural law) ke context mein is baare mein koshish aur sanyog wale kanoonon ka ek set tha ki kaise roop (form) kaam (function) ke mutabik fit baithta hai. In universal laws ko light ke vyavahar (behaviours of light), solids aur fluids ki motion (gati), insani body ki mechanics aur har us phenomenon se extrapolate (nishkarsh nikaala) kiya ja sakta tha jisme action involve ho, chahe ek process ke roop mein ya ek result ke roop mein. Ek example ke roop mein, unhone paani ki vortex motion (bhavar gativigyan) ko baalon ke ghumrale hone (curling of hair) jaise same kanoonon ke expression ke roop mein dekha. Hum ab pehle wale ko dynamics ke sahare assign karte hain, doosre wale ko statics ke sahare. Unhone un boundaries ke paar dekha jise hum ab knowledge ki branches ko alag karne ke liye use karte hain. Leonardo ki ekta (unity) ki meri personal khoj meri haal hi ki _Living with Leonardo_ mein vichar ki gayi hai, jo ek aishi personal journey ke baare mein batati hai jo science ki ek degree ke sath shuru hui aur brahmand ke sabse keemti art ke piece, _Saviour of the Cosmos (Salvator Mundi)_, par peak hui.

Leonardo ki mathematical polymathy ek vishisht tarah ki thi, lekin main sochta hoon yeh possible hai ki zyaada tar polymaths apni diversity mein usse zyaada unity dekhte hain jitna hum aasaani se discern (samajh) sakte hain. Woh rishton, analogies (samantaon), commonalities, affinities, relevancies, piche chhupi causalities (kaaranon), aur structural unities ko dekhne mein mazeed behtar hote hain. Hamari modern world mein ek artist ke liye ek professional engineer ke roop mein kaam karna o' course difficult hai — ek aishi cheez jise Renaissance ke dauran sweekar (accepted) kiya jata tha, bhale hi asadharn (uncommon) ho. Aaj ka koi bhi polymath un karrwai se surakshit professional boundaries ke baare mein aware hue bina nahi reh sakta jinhein cross karne ki zarurat hai. Institutional structures, jinhein nineteenth century mein sabse diligently khada kiya gya tha, koi shakk nahi chhodte ki yeh boundaries kahan hain. Unhein outsiders ko baahar rakhne aur insiders ko andar rakhne ke liye design kiya gya hai. Professional knowledge ka vishal dher specialists ke status ko certify karta hai, jise jargon (takniki sabdon) ke janglon aur acronyms (sankshep naamon) ke barricades dwara support milta hai. Modern disciplines ki oonchi mang asli hain lekin woh har us insaan ke khilaf protective ramparts (suraksha deewar) ke roop mein bhi serve karti hain jo isse belong nahi karta.

Modern societies mein polymathy ke paas halka-pan (shallowness) aur amateurism (gair-pyshevarpan) ka risk rehta hai. Hum us kalank (stigma) ke baare mein aware hain ki ek polymath ek ‘jack of all trades and a master of none’ (har kaam mein haath aazmane wala lekin kisi mein maharat na rakhne wala) hota hai. Lekin iska ek purana phhaila hua version bhi hai, ‘A jack of all trades is a master of none, but often better than a master of one.’ Arts aur sciences mein dheron bade innovations tab utthe jab baahri wisdom (outside wisdom) ko ek aishi discipline par asar daalane ke liye laya gya jo apne khud ke criteria mein santusht (complacent) ho chuki thi. DNA ke dauran Biology ko physicists aur chemists ke aane se reformed kiya gya tha. Copernicus ki sixteenth-century ki kranti (revolution) navachar-purna moolyankan (innovatory observation) jitna hi beauty ke concepts dwara driven thin. 1905 mein Einstein ne space, time aur energy ke ek vision ke baare mein kushal ikhtesaar (eloquent brevity) ke sath likha, jo tab ke sanghatit modern astronomy aur physics mein kya sahi aur galat tha iska ek comprehensive review lene ke bajaye radical intuition (buniyaadi antardrishti) par founded tha. Woh ek insider the jo baahar khade hone mein safal rahe.

Doosre ke territory (kshetra) par bina sahi respect aur namrata (humility) ke jeet haasil karne mein o' course ek danger hai. Main ise Leonardo ke studies ke sath dekhta hoon. Modern professionals, maan lijiye engineering mein, assume karte hain ki woh apne is visheshadhikar-prapt aur narrow-focused knowledge ke zariye Leonardo ko samajhne ki problem ko solve kar sakte hain, Leonardo ko modern world mein ‘apne samay se aige ka mard’ (a man ahead of his time) ke roop mein badal (transposing) dete hain. Nateejah distortion (vikriti) hota hai. Usse milti-julti cheez tab sthit hui jab artist David Hockney ne claim kiya ki painters ne nature ke apne depiction ke asar ke liye lambe samay se optical devices ka use kiya hai — ek aisa idea jiske sath meri kaafi sympathy hai. Isne modern optics ke experts, khhasakar lenses ke, ke liye door open kar diya, jinke paas shuruati optical instruments ke nature aur us samay picture-making ka business kaisa tha ismein mushqil se koi interest tha. Past (ateet) ka charitra nirdharit karte waqt, hamein present (vartaman) ke ghamand (arrogance) ke prati alert rehne ki zarurat hai.

Asali polymathy mein incorrigible ambition (na-sudharne yogya mahatvakanksha), draddhta (undeterability), imagination (kalpana), openness (khulapan), aur humility (namrata) ka ek unique aur asambhav mishran (improbable blend) involve hota hai. Yeh Leonardo ke daur jaisa same nahi ho sakta. Phier bhi kisi cheez ko aise dekhne ka niyamit kanoon jaise yeh kuch aur ho — ise uske normal conceptual place ke alava kahin aur belong karte hue dekhna — ab pehle se kahin mazeed vital (mahatva-purna) hai agar hamein aapas ki samajhdaari ki un cultures ko nurture karna hai jo insani race ke survival (bachne) ke liye zaroori hain.

**Martin Kemp**

_Emeritus Professor of the History of Art, Oxford University_

# Preface

### _(Prastavana)_

---

> _A mind that is stretched by new experiences can never go back to its old dimensions.—Oliver Wendell Holmes Sr._
> _(Ek dimaag jo naye anubhavon [new experiences] dwara kheenchta hai, woh kabhi bhi apne purane aakar [old dimensions] mein wapas nahi ja sakta.)_

---

Mera interest ek optimal life (sabse behtar life) ki khoj mein hai. Yaani, ek aisa dimaag viksit karne aur ek aishi life ka anubhav karne mein jo jitni rich ho sakti hai utni ho. So, jabki yeh book sochne ka ek naya tareeqa prastav-it (proposes) karti hai, yeh insaan hone ka ek naya tareeqa, jeene ka ek naya tareeqa bhi set karti hai—us tareeqe se different jise aapke liye 'normal' ke roop mein un taakaton dwara set kiya gya hai jo behtar jaan'ne ka dava karti hain. Yeh majooda paradigm (dhache) se soul (aatma) ko baahar nikaalne (extraction) ki mang karti hai, ek astral projection (shari-rik seema se baahar ka vichaar) ki tarah, aur history aur possibilities (sambhavnaon) ke kshetran ka ek visit. Is sabki shuruat ek conscious (sachet), mindful life jeene se honi chahiye, dimaag ko mazeed niyamit roop se aur mazeed effectively tarike se sochne ke liye switch on karke—objects aur unke connections ke baare mein, poore (whole) aur vishisht (particular) ke baare mein, philosophical (darshanik) aur practical ke baare mein—taaki aap woh sab ban sakein jo aap ban sakte hain: complete aap (the complete you).

Mujhe ise likhne ke liye koi commission nahi diya gya tha. Yeh hal hi tak poori tarah se ek personal intellectual odyssey (vyaktigat boddhik yaatra) thi, jab mujhe realize hua ki yeh itni mahatva-purna cheez ban gayi hai jise world ke sath share na karna galat hoga. As such, ispar kaam tarah-tarah ke anubhavon—physical, emotional, intellectual, spiritual aur doosre—ke beech buna (woven) gya tha, jisme se har ek ne mujhe is majooda topic par ek unique insight (sateek samajh) di.

Aakhirkar, aap wahi hain jo aap anubhav (experience) karte hain. Har ek vichaar, emotion aur knowledge ka bahaav samay ke sath kisi bhi di gayi cheez par aapke perspective (nazariye) ko ya toh tuchh roop se ya mool roop (subtly or fundamentally) se impact karega. Yeh sirf ek intuitive speculation (antardrishti ka andaza) nahi hai, balki ek neuroscientific fact (vaigyanik tathya) hai. Aapki badalti hui nature aapke connectome mein manifest (prakat) hoti hai—woh complex, hamesha badalta hua circuitry jo har ek brain ke liye unique hai. As such, main is book ko research aur write karne mein proactive aur reactive dono tha. Maine intermittent periods (beech-beech ke samay) ke alava, khud ko is subject mein poori tarah 'immerse' (doobaya) nahi kiya. Complete immersion se us tarah ki narrow-minded specialisation (choti-soch wali visheshagyata) ka khatra hota jise yeh book antatah challenge karne ki koshish karti hai. Is baare mein conscious hote hue, maine ek five-year ke period mein apni life ke baaki saarre facets (pahlon) se knowledge ko apne dimaag mein pehle se majood material ke sath fuse, clash aur connect hone diya. Hence main ant tak apne approach, structure, content aur conclusion ko lekar open raha.

Maine Butterfly Effect ko action mein dekha kyunki har ek vichaar, idea ya fact ne pichele vicharon ki position aur nature (ya existence tak) ko fundamentally badal diya. Main ideas aur opinions ki dynamic fluidity (gatisheel badlav) ko samajhne laga. Maine ek thesis ke sath shuruat nahi ki thi aur baad mein tar-ko se use sahi theerane (post-rationalise) ki koshish nahi ki, jaisa ki samanya tareeqa hai. Instead, shuruat se ant tak, yeh book ek exploratory adventure (khoj-bhara carnama) thi, jisne kisi bhi diye gaye point par, dimaag aur world ke baare mein asadharn—kabhi-kabar transformational (badlav-kari)—insights zahir kiye.

Pichle five years ne mujhe meri poori school-university life ke mukable kahin mazeed 'an education' (ek shiksha) pradan ki hai. Isi period ke dauran, apne late twenties mein, maine is book ka zyaada tar hissa likha. Main is vishal zimmedari se kabhi anjaan (unconscious) nahi tha jo is subject par English bhasha mein sabse pehli book hone ke sath aayi thi. In pages mein main jo insights include kar raha hoon, woh is tarah anubhav-atmak (experiential) aur boddhik (intellectual) sroton ke ek bade dher se hain; jisme prophets, sages (rishiyon), scientists, historians, philosophers, artists, polymaths aur—scripture (shastron) ke zariye—khud God ke sabd shamil hain. Ek young, limited dimaag hone ke kaaran, main aishi wisdom (buddhimatee) par gehrai se rely karta hoon—mera prayas ise curate, synthesise aur communicate karne ka tha.

Jaise-jaise woh book ko padhenge, kuch critics mere inquiry ke method ko kisi aishi cheez se identify (pehchan'ne) ke liye utsaahit ho sakte hain jisse woh familiar hain taaki woh 'jaan sakein main kahan se aa raha hoon'—kya woh ek traditional postmodernist hain, ya shayad ek Nietzschean perspectivist hain? Kya woh Daoist Zhuangzi ya Jainist Anekantavada ki philosophy employ kar rahe hain? Kya woh Ibn Khaldun ya Al Ghazali ke school se hain? Javab simultaneous (ek sath) roop se upar diye gaye sabhi hain, aur koi nahi bhi. Postmodernism ki tarah, mera vichaar pakadne ke liye koi aasan jaanwar nahi hai, lekin usse different, yeh Truth ya Ultimate Reality (Antim Satya) ki possibility ko sweekar karta hai aur ise pursue aur experience karne ke tarah-tarah ke tareeqon ko recognize karta hai. Is tareeqe se, main doosri cultures aur world views ko mukhya roop se dimaagi kalpanayein (mere mental constructs) mein ghatakar unka parochh roop se apmaan nahi kar raha hoon, jaisa ki zyaada tar postmodernists, orientalists aur materialists karte hain. Indeed agar yeh book mahatva ka kuch bhi karti hai, toh yeh logon ko ideas (aur sach mein individuals) ko pehle se majood categories ke automatic members ke roop mein dekhne ke bajaye unke apne adhikaar mein hybrid, nuanced (baareek), multi-faceted constructs ke roop mein dekhne ke liye encourage karti hai.

Is topic ki ek asli exploration ke liye, iske adbhut nuances (baareekiyo) ko dekhte hue, main jaanta تھا ki ek historian hone jitna hi ek futurist (bhavishya-vakta) hona, ek rationalist hone jitna hi ek mystic (rahsyavadi) hona, ek scholar hone jitna hi ek storyteller hona, ek thinker hone jitna hi ek doer (karne wala) hona mahatva-purna tha. Maine jin methods aur tools ko zaoori mehsus kiya unhein recruit (shamil) kiya. Is book ka raison d’être (jeene ka maqsad) vichaar ko uksana (provocation of thought) utna hi hai jitna ki action ke liye ek pukaar (call to action). So main aapse, reader se, guzarish karta hoon ki aap khud ko us world mein immerse kar lein jise book aapke liye create karne ki koshish karti hai, iske content par seriously vichar (reflect) karein, ise apne majooda knowledge mein integrate karein, apni khud ki life mein iski applicability aur relevance ka moolyankan (assess) karein, jiske zariye ise long-term memory mein store kiya ja sake, future ki soch mein use ke liye ready. Yeh internalisation (aantrikikaran) ki process hai, jiske bina knowledge apne sabse mahatva-purna role: dimaag ko rich banane, mein fail ho jata hai.

Is book ne mujhe nishchitta se ek blueprint diya hai us thode se hisse ke liye jo mere future ka bacha ho sakta hai. Jo koi bhi mujhe jaanta hai woh dekh sakta hai ki main apni life ko in pages mein outlined soch aur lifestyle ke mutabik jeeta hoon; alag-alag kshetran mein mere kaam ne ise likhne ki process ko influence bhi kiya hai aur isse influence hua bhi hai. Lekin yeh ab mere baare mein nahi hai. Isse kahin mazeed mahatva-purna aapqi self-actualisation (aatm-siddhi) ki yaatra shuru karne ki taiyari hai. Aur agar yeh book us taiyari mein ek atom ke vajan (atom’s weight) jitna bhi contribute karti hai, toh saara credit God ka hai aur sirf kamiyan meri apni hain.

Main achhi tarah aware hoon ki is book ko publish karne ke baad main apni life ke alalag points par, different insights ke sath, ek different mard hokar ispar niyamit wapas aa sakta hoon. Agar main Mandarin seekh kar, ek Samoan clan (kabeele) ke sath reh kar, zoology ka study kar ke, lute (veena) bajana seekh kar aur ek triathlon mein compete kar ke is book par dobara aata, toh main sure hoon mere insights different hote, agar mazeed evolved na hote toh. Jis point par mere paas add ya amend (badlav) karne ke liye mukhya roop se zyaada hoga, main is kaam ko revise karne, ya ek alag volume mein iske ideas par build karne ke liye dekh sakta hoon—ya shayad koi mujhse behtar qualified mujhe yeh sammaan dega.

Is subject ki poori complexity yeh sanket deti hai ki iski investigation ek mission accomplished (khatam hua mission) hone ke bajaye ek jaatri rakhne wali khoj (ongoing pursuit) honi chahiye. Jaisa ki Leonardo da Vinci ne kaha tha: _‘art is never finished, only abandoned’ (kala kabhi khatam nahi hoti, sirf chhod di jaanti hai)._ Usi dharra par—bhale hi kisi bhi tarah se same authority ke sath nahi, aur na hi ek moment ke liye ise art ka ek kaam maante hue—main ise filhal ke liye jaane de raha hoon.

**Waqās Ahmed**

**October 2018**

# Chapter 1 — Introduction

### _(Adhyay 1 — Parichay)_

---

She was black. And poor. Still she rose remarkably from a life of discrimination and abuse in 1950s America to become a key figure in the civil rights movement. She was at the heart of the struggle, a prominent campaign organiser who worked for both Martin Luther King Jr and Malcolm X prior to each of their assassinations. Even after the historic Civil Rights Act of 1964, this young radical would continue to be at the forefront of the fight for social justice and women’s rights.
_(Woh black thin. Aur poor thin. Phir bhi woh 1950s ke America mein bhedbhav aur utpeedan [discrimination and abuse] ki life se asadharn roop se uthin aur civil rights movement mein ek mukhya vyaktitv banin. Woh is sangharsh ke dil mein thin, ek prominent campaign organiser jo unqi assassinations se pehle Martin Luther King Jr aur Malcolm X dono ke liye kaam karti thin. 1964 ke historic Civil Rights Act ke baad bhi, yeh young radical social justice aur women’s rights ki fight mein sabse aige [forefront] rehna jaatri rakkhegi.)_

Her continuing interest in social causes led her to take a job as a globe-trotting journalist, first for _The Arab Observer_ in Cairo and then the _Ghanaian Times_ in Accra. Extensive travels allowed her to satisfy her linguistic curiosity, and she would come to know a variety of European, Middle Eastern and West African languages. By the end of her life, she was considered an eminent historian of African-American affairs, with 30 honorary doctorates and a professorship at a major American university.
_(Social causes mein unke jaatri rehne wale interest ne unhein ek globe-trotting journalist [duniya ghoomne wali patrakar] ke roop mein job lene par lagaya, pehle Cairo mein 'The Arab Observer' ke liye aur phir Accra mein 'Ghanaian Times' ke liye. Vishal travels ne unhein apni linguistic curiosity [bhashayi jigyasa] ko santusht karne ki izazat di, aur woh tarah-tarah ki European, Middle Eastern aur West African languages jaan'ne lagin. Apni life ke ant tak, unhein African-American affairs ki ek eminent historian maana jata tha, jinke naam 30 honorary doctorates aur ek badi American university mein professorship thi.)_

Politics, journalism, history aur bhashaon mein kamayi (accomplishment) ek parichit, bhale hi prabhavshali, career route hai. Lekin kya ho agar main aapko bataon ki wahi same young lady ek professional Calypso dancer, ek Tony Award-nominated theatre actress aur ek acclaimed film director bhi thin jinhone sanyog se ek Pulitzer Prize-nominated screenplay bhi likha tha? Aur yeh saari accomplishments woh bhi nahi hain jiske liye woh sabse zyaada famous hain.

Aakhirkar, unhein ek literary giant (sahit-yak diggaj) ke roop mein jaana jata tha—apne naam par fiction aur nonfiction ke 30 bestselling titles ke sath ek outstandingly popular aur critically acclaimed poet, playwright aur novelist. Unhone poetry ke kai volumes publish kiye, jiske liye unhein Pulitzer Prize ke liye nominate kiya gya tha aur woh khhasakar African American women ke beech popular the, jinhein modern history ke mukhya events ko mark karne ke liye bhi recite kiya gya hai jaise ki ek U.S. president ki inauguration (shapath grahan), Michael Jackson ki death aur United Nations ki 50th anniversary. Lekin unqi autobiography hai—jo kai volumes mein publish hui—jo tark roop se unka sabse mahatva-purna kaam hai, aur beeswi century mein African American experience ki samajh mein ek mukhya contribution maana jata hai. Aur haanh, hum abhi bhi usi same lady ke baare mein baat rahe hain!

Unqi accomplishments ke aadar mein, maine is book ke liye ek interview ki guzarish karte hue unse sampark (contacted) kiya. Mere dukh ke liye, uske thode samay baad hi unka nidhan (passed away) ho gya. Hamare beech mahan Maya Angelou ke roop mein jaani jaane wali, yeh poetess, playwright, author, singer, composer, dancer, actor, filmmaker, journalist, polyglot, historian aur activist multifaceted human (bahu-mukhi insaan) ki ek aishi nasal (breed) thin jo ab chinta-janak roop se vilupt (extinct) hone ke danger mein hai: **polymath (bahu-shrut / har-fan-maula).**

Yeh book insani potential (potanta) ke poore vikas (full realisation) ke baare mein hai. As such, yeh dimaag ki ek kranti (revolution of the mind) ki mang karti hai, jiska netritva insaan ki ek yugon purani prajaati (species) dwara kiya jata hai jise polymaths ke roop mein jaana jata hai, jise kabhi-kabar (halanki galat roop se) “Renaissance men” ke roop mein refer kiya jata hai. Unhein define karne ka sabse concise (chota) tareeqa yeh hai:

> Exceptional versatility (asadharn bahu-mukhi pratibha) wale insaan, jo multiple, seemingly unrelated fields (tarah-tarah ke, dikhne mein gair-sambandhit kshetran) mein excel (shreshthata haasil) karte hain.

Yeh upari definition (superficial definition) hai. Different tareeqe se kahein toh, polymaths multi-dimensional minds (bahu-bhaami dimaag) hain jo iske sabse complete, rounded sense mein optimal performance aur self-actualisation (aatm-siddhi) ko pursue karte hain. Aishi ek mindset rakhne ke kaaran, woh life bhar ki specialisation (visheshagyata) ko reject karte hain aur bajaye iske tarah-tarah ke objectives ko pursue karne ka rukh karte hain jo dekhne wale ko disparate (alag) lag sakte hain—simultaneous (ek sath) ya succession (ek ke baad ek) mein; vichaar aur/ya action ke zariye. Unke minds aur lives ki na-nakal-yogya complexity (inimitable complexity) hi unhein uniquely human banati hai. As such, unhone hamare past ko shape kiya hai aur hamare future ke owner honge. Yeh book explains karti hai kaise.

Bhale hi koi standard Homo sapiens se ek vishisht neurobiological farq ka tark dene kabil ho (hum ab jaante hain ki vyavahar, personality aur brain ke size aur structure ke beech ek correlation hai), is context mein ek ‘species’ ya ‘breed’ ka reference mukhya roop se metaphorical (alankar-ik) hai. Toh kaun sach mein ek polymath ke roop mein qualify hota hai? Halanki kai versatile log hain jo different fields mein—alag-alag hadd tak aur mixed success ke sath—operate karte hain, woh point jahan versatile operator, ya dabbling dilettante (shaukiya taur par haath marne wala), ek true polymath banta hai, woh har ek field mein attained accomplishment ya mastery ke level par depend karta hai jise poori tarah se fields ke saarre variety ke sath liya jaye.

Hum nineteenth-century ke badnaam Italian adventurer Giacomo Casanova ka case lete hain. 25 saal ki umra tak, unka ek court lawyer, Venetian army mein soldier, San Samuele theatre ke liye violinist, professional gambler, Venetian noblemen ke physician aur Rome mein clergyman ke roop mein chota lekin lively career pehle hi reh chuka tha. Europe ke chaaron taraf Grand Tour par scandal, imprisonment (jail), escape (bhaagne) aur social climbing ke ek period ke baad, unhone Parisian nobility ke beech ek alchemist ke roop mein ek reputation haasil ki, French government ke liye ek spy (jasoos) ban gaye, European governments ko lottery schemes bechin aur phir apne aakhiri saal Bohemia mein Count Waldstein ki fambly mein ek librarian ke roop mein bitaye, jahan unhone woh illustrious memoirs (shandaar sansmaran) likhe jo unke naam ko womanising (aashiq-bazi) ka paryayvachi bana denge. Kaisi ek colorful life thi! Lekin kya Casanova ke illustrious pursuits unhein ek polymath ke roop mein qualify karte hain? Ya woh kam pad gaye, khud ko zyaada hi phhailakar (spreading himself too thin) aur zyaada tar fields mein jisme unhone operate kiya koi asli contribution dene mein fail rahe?

Ya phir mid-twentieth century mein ek lambe career ke dauran serial impostor (bhes badalne wala badmaash) ke roop mein tarah-tarah ke identities dharne wale American fraudster Ferdinand Waldo Demara ka kya. Zaroori qualifications ke bina, unhone dhokhe se (lekin sach mein successfully) ek ship’s doctor, ek civil engineer, ek sheriff’s deputy, ek assistant prison warden, applied psychology ke ek doctor, ek hospital orderly, ek lawyer, ek child-care expert, ek Benedictine aur Trappist monk, ek newspaper editor, ek cancer researcher aur ek teacher ke roop mein kaam kiya. Yahan bada variety wala ek mard tha; lekin kya unhone ek polymath kahe jaane ke liye breadth ke sath-sath zaroori depth (gehrai) bhi pradarshit ki?

Technically, polymath mukhya roop se kam se kam teen seemingly unrelated fields mein excel karta hai (‘poly’ do se mazeed hone ke kaaran). Lekin reality mein, yeh suggest karna ki koi kisi vishisht field mein ‘excel kar chuka hai’ ya ‘accomplished hai’ ek relative statement hoga. Accomplishment—theek khushi, success aur intelligence ki tarah—tarah-tarah ke forms mein aati hai, aur hone ki ek mukhya roop se subjective state hai. Accomplishment ke niyamit pradarshan (conventional manifestations), halanki, mukhya roop se inmein se kisi ek ya combination ko include karte hain: critical acclaim (shameekshako ki tareef), popular recognition, financial success, works ka publication ya exhibition, qualification ya award, demonstrated skill aur experience. Lekin kevel ek profession ya academic discipline ke terms mein accomplishment ka moolyankan karna bhi polymath ko dekhne ka ek kaafi insular (sankuchit) aur limited tareeqa hai. Ek human being apni ‘profession’ ya field se kahin mazeed hai. Many-sidedness (bahu-pakshiyata) tarah-tarah ke forms mein aati hai. So true polymath ke paas ek aishi tarah ka mind aur approach hota hai jo kahin mazeed substantial (mukhya) aur holistic (samagra) hai, jaisa hum baad mein explore karenge.

Kisi bhi case mein, kisi ko ‘polymath’ ke label ko zyaada hi loosely chaaron taraf phenkne se saavdhani baratni chahiye; sirf multitalented hone aur ek genuine polymath hone ke beech ek farq hai, theek jaise intelligent hone aur ek proven genius hone ke beech ek farq hai. Ek multitalented individual zaroori roop se un talents ka use ya unhein nateejah (fruition) tak nahi pahuncheyata un fields mein accomplished karne ke liye jo har ek talent se correspond karte hain. That said, kam hi log realize karte hain ki yeh term different types ke individuals ke ek dher par apply hota hai, unhein include karte hue jinhein pehle polymaths ke roop mein na socha gya ho.

Saarre cases mein prerequisite (pehli shart), jaisa pehle zikr kiya gya hai, ek ‘exceptional cross-domain versatility’ hai, lekin sabse mahan, sabse influential, sabse self-actualised polymaths mukhya roop se self-seeking, holistically minded, connection-forming humans hain jinki pehchan ek boundless curiosity (seema-heen jigyasa), outstanding intelligence aur adbhut creativity (rachnatmakta) se hoti hai.

O' course, har ek human multifarious potential (tarah-tarah ki potanta) ke sath paada hota hai. Why, phir, parents, schools aur employers insists karte hain ki hum apne kai talents aur interests ko restrict karein; ki hum sirf ek mein ‘specialise’ karein? Hamein ek myth (jhootha niyam) becha gya hai, ki ‘specialise’ karna hi truth, identity, ya yahan tak ki ek livelihood (ajiwika) ko pursue karne ka akela raasta hai. Yet specialisation kuch nahi balki ek purana system hai jo ignorance (bhav-heenata), exploitation (shoshan) aur disillusionment (niraasha) ko paala deta hai aur creativity, opportunity aur progress ko rokta (thwarts) hai.

World ke sabse mahan historians, futurists, philosophers aur scientists ke sath exchanges ki ek series ke baad, yeh book history ke ek narrative (katha) aur future ke liye ek aishi vision ko ek sath banti hai jo unwarranted ‘hyperspecialisation’ (gair-wajib hadd se zyaada visheshagyata) ke is majooda system ko disrupt (todna) karne ki koshish karti hai. Indeed, yeh khulasa karti hai ki true specialist actually ek polymath hi hota hai.

Sochne aur hone ka ek aur raasta majood hai. Ek aishi approach ke zariye jo philosophical aur practical dono hai, hum aapqi innate polymathic state (janmjaat polymathic state) ko dobara dhoondhne aur unlock karne ki taraf ek cognitive journey (dimaagi yaatra) set karenge. Aige badhte hue, yeh book hamari education aur professional structures mein ek cultural revolution (saanskritik kranti) se kam kuch prastavit nahi karti, jiske zariye har kisi ko multiple ways mein khud ko express karne aur apni many-sided potential ko poora karne ke liye encourage kiya jaye. Na keval yeh individual satisfaction ko enhance karta hai, balki aisa karke, ek aishi conscious aur creative society ko facilitate karta hai jo twenty-first-century ki challenges ki complexity ko address karne ke liye highly motivated aur well equipped dono ho.

Reader ko us journey par le jaane ke liye, yeh book ek bahut vishisht structure (structure) ko follow kareg-i. Shuruat karne ke liye, hamein yeh samajhne ki zarurat hai ki different societies mein aur different samay par, polymaths hamesha exist karte the aur indeed world history mein sabse influential figures mein se kuch the, jinhone modern world ko shape karne mein mukhya bhoomika nibhai. Yeh khhasakar mahatva-purna hai, kyunki aaj hum ek highly specialised society mein jeete hain jo polymath ko, sath hi uske existence ki kisi bhi memory ko discourage (lagbhag suppress) karti hai. Jabki yeh status quo kuch chune hue logon ko suit karta hai (jo control ke ek tool ke roop mein specialisation ka use karke divide aur conquer karne mein happy hain), yeh insani fulfilment, intellectual freedom aur societal progress ki keemat par aata hai.

Sabse mahatva-purna baat yeh hai ki, sapiens (manav-jaati) simply vanished ho jayenge jab tak ki hum dimaag ko is tareeqe se cultivate na karein jo hamein _Project Earth_ ke liye indispensable (lazmi) banaye. Machine intelligence aur so-called technological singularity ke mandrane (aur nuclear, environmental aur economic catastrophes ki toh baat hi chodo jo mazeed paas hain) ke sath, world ke paas polymath ke ek revival (punar-jaagran) ko dekhne ke alava kam hi chandra (choice) bacha hai, kyunki multifaceted, complex, creative, versatile aur inimitable human ki sirf yahi prajaati hai jiska ek highly complex, automated, super-intelligent future mein koi value ya relevance hoga.

Toh kya kiya jaye? Pehle, hamein apne minds ko polymath ki tarah sochne aur operate karne kabil banane ke liye recondition karna hoga, un timeless traits aur methods ko adopt karte hue jinhein history ke dauran count-less polymaths dwara pradarshit kiya gya hai. Hamein phir un polymaths ko identify karna hoga jo abhi bhi zinda hain yeh lessons seekhne ke liye ki hum apni khud ki polymathic potential ko kaise unleash karein aur system dwara hamari upar force ki gayi hyperspecialisation ka virodh kaise karein.

Finally, hamein khud system ko badalna dhoondhna hoga—iski majooda sanskriti, educational curricula aur pedagogy (shikshan-shastra), social structures, sansthaon, kaam ke environment aur sach mein iski samanya worldview ko—aur ise ek aise system se replace karna hoga jo polymathic minds ko breeds aur encourages kare aur polymaths ki ek nayi global generation ko andar laye. Sirf yeh optimally functioning, highly creative, self-actualised minds hi future ki stewardship (dekh-bhaal) le sakte hain aur humanity ko ek progressive tomorrow ki taraf steer kar sakte hain. Yeh ek aishi kranti ki mang karta hai jo cognitive aur cultural dono ho; aane wale chapters aapqi consciousness (chetna) ko jagane ka aim rakhte hain, taaki aap bhi ismein join ho sakein.

Aaiye hum shuruat se hi clear rahein: polymaths kisi exclusive club, order ya society ke members nahi hain—har ek human ke paas ek banne ka potential hai. In fact, ‘banna’ (becoming) shayad ‘wapas lautna’ (reverting) ke mukable kam sateek hai. Hum sab inherently multifaceted beings (mool roop se bahu-pakshiye prani) hain aur childhood ke dauran saaf taur par is disposition (jhukaav) ko pradarshit karte hain; hum adulthood tak us tareeqe se rehte hain ya nahi, yeh cultural, educational, political aur economic influences ke ek cornucopia (dher) dwara nirdharit kiya jata hai. So individual ke liye, ek polymath hona mukhya roop se aapke primordial self (mool-swaroop) ke prati true hona hai; yeh ek doosre tareeqe se sote hue dimaag (slumbering mind) ki glimmering potential ko unlock karna hai. Us process ka pehla hissa ek fundamental fact ko compute aur internalise karna hai: ki polymaths ek timeless people (samay se pare ke log) hain.

# Chapter 2 — A Timeless People

### _(Adhyay 2 — Samay Se Pare Ke Log)_

---

Polymath (bahu-shrut / har-fan-maula) utna hi purana hai jitna ki khud Homo sapiens. Shuruati insani life ki parivartan-sheel nature (capricious nature), jisme insani knowledge limited thi phir bhi challenges aur opportunities itne vishal the, ne asadharn versatility (bahu-mukhi pratibha) aur creativity ki mang ki hogi. Zoologist aur ethnologist Desmond Morris ne apni popular book _The Naked Ape_ mein confirm kiya ki insaan nature se sabse zyaada non-specialised (gair-visheshagya), adaptive aur mauqaparast (opportunistic) animal hai. So yeh hairat-dar nahi hai ki agrani world historian Felipe Fernández-Armesto ne yeh prastavit (postulated) kiya ki:

> “Aap jitna piche jaayenge utni hi mazeed polymathy majood thi, kyunki kaafi haal tak (sub specie aeternitatis), domains (kshetra) undefined the aur kisi ek field mein expertise kisi doosre field mein interest ko nahi rokti thin . . . ek expert stargazer (tara-mandal dekhne wala) ek healer (vaidya) ya hunter (shikari) ya jo kuch bhi woh kabil lagta/lagti ho, ban sakta tha.”

Aishi shuruati societies mein, zyaada tar humans ne ise apna business banaya tha ki woh **‘practical generalists’ (vyavaharik samanyavadi)** banein—yaani, knowledge aur skills ka ek aisha bada dher haasil karein jiska unqi survival (jeevit rehne) ke liye ek practical value ho. Iska aksar matlab hota tha ki ek hi person ke paas ek botanist ya physician ki knowledge ho (yeh jaan'ne ke liye kaun se plants harm karte hain, heal karte hain ya khane yogya hain), ek hunter ki skills hon (apne aur apni fambly ke liye prabandh karne ke liye), ek architect ya engineer ki creativity ho (sahi materials ka use karke sahi jameen par ek safe house ya shelter banana) aur ek artist ka dimaag ho (games, shows aur visual artwork ke zariye apni fambly ya community ko entertain aur enlightened karne ke liye). Wahan labor ka koi batwara (division of labour) nahi tha—har koi woh sab kuch tha jo woh ban sakta tha.

O' course, har ek ke paas apni vishisht strengths aur jhukaav (inclinations) hote the, jinhein fambly, community ya kabeele ke khaatir recognize, encourage aur draw kiya jata था. Ek functionalist society ka vikas nishchitta se hua. Lekin life bhar ki micro-specialisation (baareek visheshagyata) ki sanskriti ka koi saboot nahi milta. Traditional societies se aige badhte hue, polymath shuruati civilisations ke nirmaan aur parinaam-swaroop sthit 'high culture' (uccha sanskriti) ke liye lazmi (integral) tha jo ancient history ki mahan artistic aur scientific accomplishments ke liye zimmedar thin.

Egypt aur Central America ke pyramids, Iraq ke ziggurats (staged tower temples), Crete mein Knossos ka palace, mainland Greece mein Mycenae ka qila aur Indus par Harappa aur Mohenjo-daro ke grid-plan se bane sheharon jaise mahan ancient edifices (vibhuti-purna imaraton) par vichar karte hue, popular writer aur historical investigator Graham Hancock ka vishwas hai ki us samay ke dauran banaye gaye mahan monuments khud is baat ka saboot hain ki unke architects polymaths the:

> “Agar aap Ancients (prachin logon) ki achievements ko dekhein, toh aap payenge ki sirf polymaths hi unhein create kar sakte the. Bhale hi hamare paas concerned individuals ki biographies na hon, hum unke handiwork (karigari) se yeh nishkarsh [deduce] nikaal sakte hain ki yeh sankuchit specialists ki koi team nahi thi balki tarah-tarah ke disciplines mein bahu-bhaami roop se kabil logon ka ek group tha.”

Iska **Imhotep** se behtar koi udaharan nahi hai, jo Saqqara ke step pyramid ke architect the aur history ke records mein sabse pehle polymath hain. Zyaada tar historians agree karte hain ki Imhotep legendary King Djoser (Egypt ki Third Dynasty ke shayad sabse behtar jaane jaane wale pharaoh) ke sam-kaalin (contemporary) the. Imhotep ek sadharan nagrik (commoner) the jinhone ek liberal education haasil ki thi aur unke biographer ke mutabik:

> “Wah ek erudite (viddvan), versatile mard ke roop mein bade hue, ek tarah ke Aristotelian genius, jinhone saari knowledge ko apna prant [province] bana liya.”

Unka genius, aisa lagta hai, jaldi hi identify aur reward kiya gya kyunki woh ranks mein uthte gaye aur aakhirkar khud King Djoser ka dhayan attract kiya. King ke sath isi ghanishth rishte ne Imhotep ko flexibility aur resources dono pradan kiye taaki woh apne interests ke variety ko explore kar sakein aur apne talents ke dher ko pradarshit kar sakein.

Unqi potential se prabhavit hokar, Djoser ne Imhotep ko apna trusted minister, ya vizier (wazir) appointed kiya. Isi bhoomika mein woh sabse zyaada phhale-phhoole (flourished), involve karte hue tarah-tarah ke cross-disciplinary duties ko. Unqi jurisdiction ‘state ke alalag departments par phhaili thin,’ jisme ‘Judiciary, Treasury, War (Army aur Navy), Interior, Agriculture, aur General Executive’ shamil the. Yeh ek tarah ki twenty-first-century ki prime ministerial ya chief executive bhoomika thi, aur historians dwara Imhotep ki economy, foreign relations aur public engagement ke kshetran mein skilled statesmanship (kushalta) ke kai udaharan diye gaye hain.

Imhotep ke is naye sthan aur power ne unhein stately duties se pare ki activities ko pursue karne ki izazat di. Unqi polymathic urge ne unhein unke sabse mahan talents ki taraf push kiya: architecture, medicine, spirituality, science, poetry aur philosophy. Ek engineer aur architect ke roop mein, Imhotep ne kuch phenomenal breakthroughs (asadharn khoj) kiye. Poore kshetra mein unke works jaldi samne aane ke sath, Imhotep apni buildings mein columns (khambhon) ka use karne wale sabse pehle logon ke roop mein jaane gaye. Patthar ke sath design, compose aur kaam karne ki unqi prabhavshali ability (unhone Saqqara ke kshetra mein kai buildings banayi thin) ne unhein King Djoser ke liye Saqqara step pyramid design karne ka ek ambitious project jitwaya. Isne Imhotep ko na keval ek architect ke roop mein, balki ek sculptor, astronomer aur inventor ke roop mein bhi apni abilities pradarshit karne ka opportunity diya. Unhone Djoser Pyramid ko is tarah ke vishal paimane ki duniya ki sabse pehli completely stone-dressed building ke roop mein design kiya. Nateejah ek staggering 200-foot-tall patthar ka pyramid tha jisne us samay ki architectural duniya mein ek kranti laadi aur aane wali Egyptian dynasties ke liye ek niyam (precedent) set kar diya. Furthermore, Robert Bauval jaise researchers dwara ise iske chhupaye gaye celestial alignments (akashiye sanrekhano) ke kaaran ‘patthar mein ek astronomical manual’ ke roop mein maana jata hai.

Ek physician ke roop mein, Imhotep ki achievements ko barabar, agar mazeed nahi toh, groundbreaking recognize kiya jata hai. Unhein 200 se mazeed diseases ko identify aur cure karne aur medicine par dheron treatises likhne ke liye jaana jata hai. Unhein papyrus scroll ke invention ka credit diya jata hai aur unqi pehchan us roop mein ki gayi hai jo baad mein _Edwin Smith papyrus_ ke roop mein jaana gya—ek medical treatise jo asadharn roop se alaukik vicharon (supernatural notions) se mukt hone ke liye vishisht hai, aur jisme mukhya anatomical observations, ailments aur cures ki ek series shamil hai, jisme wounds (zakhamon) ke liye honey (shahad) ka use aur bleeding rokne ke liye raw meat (kacche maans) ka use shamil hai. Ek skilled aur innovative physician ke roop mein Imhotep ki reputation ne unqi death ke baad hafton tak unhein demigod (ardh-devta) ka sthan dilane mein ek lazmi bhoomika nibhai. Physician Sir William Osler ne kaha ki Imhotep hi asli:

> “Father of Medicine (Chikitsha ke Pita) the . . . purani prachinta ke dhundhlapan se saaf baahar nikalne wale physician ke sabse pehle vyaktitv.”

Medical profession mein Imhotep ki legacy ko _Hippocratic oath_ (ek kasam jo saarre physicians practice karne par lete hain) ki shuruat mein dekha ja sakta hai jisme yeh Asclepius—woh devta jise Greeks Imhotep ke sath associate karte the—ko ek aishi devta ke roop mein refer karta hai jiski kasam khani hai.

Iske pare, Imhotep ne chief lector priest (uccha darje ke pujari) ke roop mein ek mahatva-purna bhoomika nibhai, permanent duties aur spiritual engagements jaise balidan ceremonies aur mummy funeral processions ke sath. Woh aksar events par king (kingdom ke ultimate high priest) ko represent karte the, ek aishi position jahan unhein sirf tabhi elevated kiya ja sakta tha jab woh sahi skills aur respect ke kabze mein hon. Imhotep ne philosophy aur poetry mein bhi works produce kiye. Unke ideas ko famous roop se poems mein refer kiya gya tha jaise ‘Maine Imhotep aur Hordedef ke sabd sune hain jinke discourses ke sath log itna bolte hain’ aur unhein tarah-tarah ke proverbs ka credit diya jata hai, jisme famous **‘Eat, drink and be merry, for tomorrow we may die’ (Khao, piyo aur aish karo, kyunki kal hum mar sakte hain)** shamil hai. Aishi diverse overachievement ki rareness ne us samay ke logon ko poori tarah se abhibhut (overwhelmed) kar diya tha—jinhone shayad ise unqi divinity (divinity) ke ek sanket ke roop mein dekha—aur woh pharaoh ki statue ke hisse ke roop mein darshaaye jaane wale aur divine status tak elevated kiye jaane wale akela sadharan nagrik (commoner) ban gaye.

Ek practicing physician, architect aur astronomer ke roop mein jinhone ek priest, inventor, poet, philosopher aur statesman ke roop mein Egyptian society aur culture mein tremendous contributions bhi kiye, Imhotep sabse pehle recorded polymaths mein se ek the. Unke tomb (qabr) ke inscription ke mutabik unke official titles mein shamil hain:

> _Egypt ke King ke Chancellor, Doctor, Upper Egypt ke King ke baad line mein sabse pehle, Great Palace ke Administrator, Vanshanugat Kulin (Hereditary Nobleman), Heliopolis ke High Priest, Builder, Chief Carpenter, Chief Sculptor, aur Maker of Vases in Chief._

## Patronage _(Sanrakshan)_

Imhotep ki tarah, polymaths ke paas history ke dauran zyaada tar cases mein ek platform raha hai—namid roop se ek social ya professional position ke form mein—jisne unhein multiple fields ko explore aur contribute karne ke opportunities pradan kiye. Unki job intrinsically unhein polymathic hone ki izazat deti thi (ya kabhi-kabar required karti thin). Ek aishi platform ka ek niyamit udaharan jo social history ke ek bade hisse ke liye exist karta raha hai woh **courtier (darbaari)** ki position hai. Yeh intellectuals aur artists the jo royal courts mein advisers, administrators ya entertainers ke roop mein act karte the. Na keval ek vibrant aur bhadkeele court ne polymaths ko attract kiya, balki isne ek aisha environment bhi create kiya jo multitalented ko actively paala deta aur encourage karta tha. Office mein mahatva-purna positions diye jaane par, yeh courtiers apne monarch ke patronage (sanrakshan) par rely karte the. Unqi har-tarah ki brilliance ya toh ek janmjaat attribute thi jise patron dwara favor aur encourage kiya jata tha, ya different fields se sambandhit tarah-tarah ke projects ke arbitrary commissioning ke zariye sanyog se mukt (fortuitously unleashed) hui thin.

Western history mein, aishi court culture ka archetype Renaissance Italy mein exist karta tha. Yahan, Baldassare Castiglione ne apni _Book of the Courtier_ mein ‘perfect courtier’ ke ideal aur status ka outline nikaala: _“Uske [courtier] paas har ek reasonable cheez mein unhein [unke princes ko] serve karne ki knowledge aur ability hogi.”_ Importantly, royal patrons ek hi individual ko tarah-tarah ke gair-sambandhit tasks ke liye commission karne se darte nahi the, kyunki individual ki samagra ability par trust unke liye ‘efficiency,’ ‘specialisation’ ya division of labour se kahin mazeed mahatva-purna tha. Is context mein, courtier ki position hi thi jisne polymath ko establishment ke dil mein phhalne-phhoolne (thrive) ki izazat di.

Yeh us mard ke case mein tha jise world ka sabse celebrated polymath maana jata hai: **Leonardo da Vinci**. Is mahan maestro ne ek Florentine guild mein ek painting aur engineering apprentice ke roop mein shuruat ki, aur dono fields mein sabse agrani practitioners mein se ek ban gaye. Unqi paintings mein se kam se kam do—_Mona Lisa_ aur _The Last Supper_—sabse mahan masterpieces mein se maani jaanti hain. Doosri works, jaise _Virgin at the Rocks_ aur haal hi mein unke naam ki gayi _Salvator Mundi_, jo unke signature _sfumato_ style mein painted thin, ko bhi art critics dwara aishi paintings ke roop mein hailed kiya jata hai jo Renaissance art ko define karti hain. Ek sculptor ke roop mein, unhone famous lekin badkismat _Sforza Horse_ ko design aur build kiya. Unhone ek royal stage aur costume designer, ek event organizer aur interior designer ke roop mein bhi serve kiya aur kam se kam do royal weddings ke dauran active the. Unhein Cesare Borgia ke liye ‘family architect aur general engineer’ name kiya gya tha aur unhone Mario de Guiscardi ki suburban villa ko design kiya. Pisa ke siege ke dauran unhone Signoria of Florence aur Lord of Piombino ke liye topographical sketches, military machines ke designs aur fortifications banaye aur Ottoman invasion ka virodh karne ke prayasoon ke dauran Venetian Republic ke liye ek military engineer ke roop mein bhi kaam kiya.

Ek life-bhar ke note-taker, Leonardo ke notes ka poora collection ek hadd se pare wide-ranging—bhale hi seemingly sporadic (kahin-kahin)—thesis roop karta hai jisme philosophy, optics, geometric perspective, anatomy aur aviation ke kshetran mein investigations shamil hain. Unqi notebooks mein se sirf kuch hi bach saktin hain, lekin Leonardo par world ki sabse agrani authority, Martin Kemp, ke mutabik, unhone academic length ki lagbhag 50 books roop ki hotin—universal knowledge ka ek complete body jo vishisht roop se aakarshak visual form mein prastut kiya gya hai.

Unhone University of Pavia mein anatomy ke professor ke sath anatomical studies aur mathematician Fra Luca Pacioli ke sath mathematical studies jhele. Unhone Lombardi ki valleys aur Lake Iseo ke hydraulic aur geological studies kiye aur apni khud ki flying machine devised ki aur insani flight mein experiments conducted kiye. Ultimately, ek painter, sculptor, musician, stage aur costume designer, inventor, anatomist, aviator, engineer, military strategist aur cartographer ke roop mein excel karte hue, Leonardo archetypical polymath the. Kemp ne calculate kiya ki agar unse aaj ek ‘Leonardo assemble’ karne ko kaha jaye, toh unhein **13 different professionals** ki zarurat hogi!

Sforza aur Borgia jaise patrons Leonardo ko un kshetran mein se kisi mein bhi explore aur create karne ki izazat dene mein happy the jisme unhein laga ki woh contribute kar sakte hain. Unka aim simply apni reign ko glorify aur protect karna tha, aur agar ek multitalented individual us aim ki taraf bahu-bhaami roop se help kar sakta tha, toh use encourage kiya jata tha. Aishi polymathy Renaissance Europe mein nishchitta se widespread thi, aur isne Leibniz ko _House of Hanover_ mein aur Copernicus ko _court of Warmia_ mein contributions dene ki izazat di. Chaaron taraf world mein, chahe woh Persian hakeem, West African griots, Chinese junzi, ya Mayan itz’at ke form mein ho, multipurpose, polymathic courtier ki tradition premodern history ke zyaada tar eras mein exist karti thin.

Jabki scholarship ko historically royal patronage dwara unke courts mein support kiya jata tha, doosre bodies jaise religious institutions, ‘mystery schools,’ secular academic universities aur amateur societies ne history ke different points par polymath ko support karne mein ek mukhya bhoomika nibhai. Khhasakar shuruati Middle Ages ke dauran (jise aksar ‘Golden Age of God’ ke roop mein refer kiya jata hai) humne kai theologians—Christian priests, Jewish rabbis, Hindu aur Buddhist punditas aur Muslim ’alims—ko apne samay ke sabse widely learned individuals bante dekha; Christian world mein Bede, Albertus Magnus, Konrad of Megenberg, Psellos aur Bar Hebraeus jaise log saaf udaharan the, aur East mein Abhinavagupta, Chavundarya, Sankardeva, Dikshita aur Zanabazar. Chunki Islamic civilisation is samay ke dauran khhasakar well developed thi, Muslim theologians ne kisi bhi doosre group se pare ek intellectual versatility pradarshit ki. Al-Ghazali, al-Razi aur al-Tusi jaise log religion ke scholars utne hi the jitne woh physicians, poets aur philosophers the. Polymathic culture Islamic world mein itna embedded tha ki Maimonides, Hunayn ibn Ishaq aur Abraham ibn Ezra jaise Christian aur Jewish polymaths bhi iske tehat phhale-phhoole.

## Laymen _(Aam Log)_

Kisi qualified historian ya ‘conspiracist’ ka nishkarsh nikaalna nahi lagta yeh dekhne ke liye ki power, wealth, influence aur isliye knowledge (chahe esoteric ho ya exoteric) insaan ke jaan'ne wale lagbhag har ek society mein hamesha kuch chune hue logon tak hi confined rahi hai. Aur knowledge ke yahi custodians the jinhone, intellectual resources ki prachurta ke sath-sath professional flexibility ke ek bade darje tak apni exclusive access ke kaaran, aksar apne tarah-tarah ke talents aur interests ko explore karne ki sabse badi propensity (jhukaav) rakkhi. History mein recorded polymaths ka ek bada hissa is tarah apne samay ki elite class se aaya hai—ya kam se kam aakhirkar ismein shamil hua hai. In other words, scholars aur artists samanya roop se sirf tabhi mukhyata mein uthe hain jab unke ruling princes dwara unhein izazat di gayi. Politicians, economists, writers, artists, lawyers aur soldiers ko sirf tabhi excel karne ki permission thi agar unka kaam status quo ke sath accord karta aur ise prastut karta tha. So history ke dauran, yeh mukhya roop se kings, emperors, caliphs, dictators aur, indeed, governments ke blessing aur patronage ke sath tha ki multiple talented individuals ko unqi poori poornata mein phhalne-phhoolne ka licence pradan kiya gya hai.

Bhale hi vishal roop se aisa hi case raha ho, yeh, halanki, poori tarah true nahi hai. Ek aise daur mein jahan ‘great man’ historical narrative ka dbaav hai, koi ‘people’s’ histories (logon ki history) mein jaanch karne ke liye majboor hai taaki aam bheed ke beech learnedness aur polymathy ki hadd ka pata lagaya ja sake. Intellectual development ki taddpan social ya financial standing se mel nahi khati—yeh ek universal insani trait hai. Un count-less unsung ‘practical generalists’ (upar dekhein) ke alava jo insani history ke dauran har ek culture mein exist rahe honge (khhasakar traditional societies mein), hamein yeh bhi sweekar karna chahiye ki intellectual erudition ya ‘encyclopaedic generalism’ working class ke kai logon ke beech hamesha prevalent tha. Yeh hairan karne wala nahi hona chahiye; knowledge se vanchit rehne par, lay people (aam log) ise sabse utsaahit roop se taraste aur pursue karte hain. Kuch log propaganda, excessive kaam ke ghante aur khaali distractions dwara conditioned hote hain lekin zyaada tar ko unqi erudition ki prabhavshali variety ke terms mein underestimate kiya jata hai. Iske sanket poore sahitya mein bikhare hain, agar koi sirf dekhe toh.

American writer Henry Thoreau ne famous roop se yaad kiya ki rural America mein unka samna ek aise kisaan se hua jiske paas khet jotte (ploughing the field) waqt uski back pocket mein Homer ki _Iliad_ ka ek Greek version tha. China mein, Sung period ke ek writer Fung Ta-tsung ne note kiya ki _“har ek peasant (kisaan), artisan (karigar) aur merchant apne son ko padhna seekhata hai.”_ Usne aige record kiya ki _“yahan tak ki herdsmen (charwahe) aur wives jo khet mein kaam kar rahe apne husbands ke liye khana laanti hain woh bhi ancient times ke mardon ke poems recite kar sakti hain.”_

Jonathan Rose ne apne seminal work, _The Intellectual Life of the British Working Classes_, mein khulasa kiya ki intellectual freedom ki desire aur pursuit exclusively ek ‘bourgeois’ (pujivadi) concept nahi thi, aur yeh ki British working class ya ‘the masses’ tarah-tarah ke subjects ke baare mein usse kahin mazeed well read the jitna unhein traditionally credit diya jata hai. Unhone tarah-tarah ke udaharan pradan kiye, jisme sixteen-century ke Reformation ke baad artisans dwara theological aur literary works publish karne se lekar eighteen-century ke Scotland mein weavers (bunkaron) ke beech ek autodidactic (self-learning) culture viksit hone tak shamil hai. Ab celebrated painter, poet, political activist aur religious commentator **William Blake**, for instance, is artisan tradition se un thode logon mein se ek the jinhein baad mein ek polymath ke roop mein recognize kiya gya. Past ke kai polymaths history ke pages mein kho gaye hain, simply kyunki unhein un logon dwara recognition ya ‘in alignment’ ke layak nahi maana gya jo histories commission karte hain.

## Women _(Aurat-Jaati)_

Women ke baare mein kya? Kyun hamare paas ‘Renaissance man’ ka koi popular female version nahi hai? Unfortunately, history ke dauran knowledge ka monopolisation (ekadhikaar) gender ke sath utna hi true hai jitna yeh class ke sath hai. Chauvinism (purush-shradha) ki ek lambi history ke nateejah roop unke male counterparts ke samne outrageously kam recorded female polymaths hain. Yeh mard hain jo hamare records mein ‘polymaths’ aur ‘Renaissance men’ ke roop mein niyamit samne aate hain. Iska ek kaaran yeh hai ki jabki kai female polymaths exist kartiin thin unhein records se omit kar diya gya ya un logon dwara simply overlooked kar diya gya jinhone history likhi aur record ki (jo vishal roop se mard the). Yeh isliye hai kyunki insani history ke zyaada tar (bhale hi saarre nahi) societies kaafi hadd tak male-dominated rahi hain.

Jabki yeh true hai ki saaloon se women ki polymathic achievements ko ignore (ya simply chhupane) karne ke liye mukhya roop se historians zimmedar hain, badkismat reality yeh barkarar rehti hai ki public sphere mein sach mein bahut kam female polymaths exist kartiin thin. Iska unqi polymathise karne ki ability ya propensity se kam lena-dena hai (in fact opposite probably case tha) aur mazeed unke samay ke restrictive societies dwara unpar imposed ki gayi cultural norms aur barriers se talluk hai.

Kemetic royalty, Enlightenment Europe ki bluestockings, shuruati Islam ki Al-Muhaddithat aur Tang China ki courtesans jaise kuch exceptions ke sath, women ko modern age se pehle intellectual aur professional kshetran mein shamil shaz-o-nadir hi kiya jata था. Jabki male courtier ko, for instance, traditionally well mannered, multitalented, widely educated aur cultured ke roop mein respect kiya jata tha, uski female counterpart, courtesan—jabki kai cases mein barabar cultured aur multitalented hone ke bavajood—ko automatically (aur aksar galat roop se) sexual promiscuity (chalu-pan) ke sath associate kar diya jata tha. Japanese geisha, jisme se kai artistic polymaths thin, is gender bias ke udaharan hain.

Modern Hollywood same phenomenon se jhelta hai. Mardon ki versatility ka swagat kiya jata hai, jabki women ko aksar sex appeal ke mutabik branded kar diya jata hai. **Hedy Lamarr**, for instance, jo 1940s mein sabse popular Hollywood film actresses mein se ek thin, ek talented inventor bhi thin. Unhone torpedoes ko jam hone se rokne ke liye ek frequency-hopping system devised kiya, jiska use aaj bhi Bluetooth devices mein kiya jata hai. Academy Award winner **Natalie Portman** ek mathematics prodigy bhi thin. Aise kai udaharan majood hain.

Grassroots par wapas jaate hue, women ne traditionally insani history ke ek bade hisse mein ek domestic (gharelu) life jeei hai aur isliye multiple fields mein—ek ki toh baat hi chodo—society, scholarship aur culture mein contributions dena unke liye kam possible hua. Cultural critic aur feminist scholar Gayatri Chakravorty Spivak ne ise explain kiya:

> “Polymathy ki sambhavna women ke liye available nahi rahi hai, kyunki women ko hairan karne wale roop se private persons ke roop mein define kiya gya hai. Yahan tak ki main, jiska India mein ek highly educated aur cultured fambly mein ek relatively liberal upbringing hua tha, vishwas karti thin ki women ke paas academic personalities nahi hotin. Jinhone ek field mein achieve kiya woh, Derrida ko quote karein toh, ‘honorary males’ ban gayin. Agar woh aige badhkar polymathic ho bhi jaanti hai, toh ideologic issues aur baaki cheezon ke kaaran doosri auraton dwara usse ghrina ki jaanti hai. Yeh ek bahut sad cheez hai.”

Yahan tak ki domestic life mein bhi, female ki polymathic hone ki ability ka nonetheless paryapt roop se pradarshit kiya gya hai, child rearing (bacche paalna), food preparation, educating, entertaining, farming aur processing jaise tarah-tarah ke tasks ke beech juggle karte hue. Traditional South Asian culture mein, for instance, **ghargrasti (mukhya roop se 'house management' ke roop mein translate hota hua)** apne aap mein ek aisha multifaceted role hai jisme tarah-tarah ke cognitive aptitudes, strands of knowledge aur emotional aur intellectual attributes ke beech effective roop se switching ki zarurat hoti hai. Inmein khana banana aur cleaning, ghar ke finances handle karna, bacchon ko paalna aur educate karna, social relationships ko skilfully manage karna, fambly ki care karne mein draddh rehna, guests ko appropriately entertain karna aur kai doosre tasks ke beech apna khud ka appearance maintain rakhna shamil raha ho sakta hai. Indeed, tarah-tarah ke studies ab yeh dikhane samne aaye hain ki women actually mardon ke mukable behtar multitaskers aur adjustors hain. _“Epistemological shifting [jo polymath dwara required hai] ki ability aur practice women ke beech exist karti hai,”_ Spivak kehti hain, _“lekin ise public sphere mein enter karne ki izazat nahi di gayi hai.”_

Modern period se pehle ki women, khhasakar Europe mein, social, professional aur intellectual life ke zyaada tar forms se marginalised thin. Enlightenment khud ajeeb roop se male dominated tha. Yeh, halanki, British philosopher Alfred North Whitehead dwara sweekar kiya gya tha ki us period ke dauran un-educated, well-travelled auratein actually aksar apne husbands se mazeed cultured, wiser aur better-informed thin, kyunki Industrial Revolution ke baad male education mukhya roop se ek vishisht job ke liye unhein prepare karne ke bajaye inert (disconnected, bekar) ideas ka installation tha bajaye world ko samajhne ke.

Kai female polymaths apne husbands ya lovers ke shadow (saaye) mein jeein. Yeh khhasakar French Enlightenment ke dauran true था, jab kai intellectuals ki wives aur lovers aksar unke salon hostesses, translators ya researchers ke roop mein serve kartiin thin, lekin jinhone kai cases mein apne khud ke adhikaar mein mukhya contributions kiye. **Marie Lavoisier**, French nobleman aur chemist Antoine Lavoisier ki wife, ek linguist, chemist aur artist thin jinhone apne husband ki books ko translate karne ke sath-sath unhein illustrated bhi kiya. Woh unke researcher ke roop mein unke sath travel bhi kartiin thin aur salons chalati thin jahan woh Benjamin Franklin jaise doosre prominent figures ko entertain kartiin thin. Lekin woh ek polymath ke roop mein acknowledged kiye jaane ke liye zyaada hi female thin.

**Émilie du Châtelet** Voltaire ki lover thin, jinhone ek baar unke baare mein kaha tha ki woh _“ek mahan mard hain jinki akeli galti ek aurat hona tha.”_ Whon ek mathematician, physicist aur translator thin jinhone Bible ka ek critical analysis bhi likha, apne debts pay off karne ke liye financial derivatives ka ek system viksit kiya, philosophy aur linguistics par tarah-tarah ke discourses likhe aur female education ke samarthan mein ek activist banin. Yet unhein abhi bhi mukhya roop se ‘Voltaire’s lover’ ke roop mein jaana jata hai.

Bhale hi chaaron taraf world se acknowledged aur acclaimed female scholars, artists aur leaders ke dheron udaharan hain, publically multiple domains mein accomplishments rakhne ke liye bahut kam jaani gayi hain. Ban Zhao, Lubna of Cordoba, Hildegard von Bingen, Anna Maria von Schurman, Maria Agnesi aur Florence Nightingale—jinhein hum agle chapter mein explore karenge—jaise udaharan rare hain. Jaise hi women ne modern era mein professionally public sphere mein enter karna shuru kiya, unhein mardon se dugni hard work karni padi, aur specialisation aur single-field focus ko khud ko kabil prove karne ke raaste ke roop mein dekha gya.

Europe mein ‘bluestocking’ (ya ‘learned lady’) ka idea sirf late eighteen century mein samne aaya. Women’s academic institutions chaaron taraf world mein sirf baad ke nineteen aur shuruati twentieth centuries mein uth khade hue, jab United States mein _Seven Sisters colleges_, U.K. mein _Girton aur Bedford colleges_, Japan mein _Tsuda College_, aur India mein _Lahore College for Women_ sabse pehle established kshetran mein se ban gaye, jaise naye co-ed universities thin jaise _University of Chicago_ aur _London School of Economics_. Aur isliye, unfortunately, aap is book ke ek bade hisse mein male polymaths par ek disproportionate focus payenge, bhale hi yeh notion genders ke paar barabar applicable (aur sach mein pertinent) ho.

## The ‘Other’ _(Doosre / Gair-Western)_

Women aur laymen ki tarah, Anglo-European ke alava doosri societies ke polymaths ko history ke pages se neglect ya omit kiya gya hai. Yahan tak ki sabse behtar historians bhi hamesha kuch cultural aur political biases ke patra rahe hain—chahe deliberate ho ya unintentional—iska matlab hai ki unke ‘world history’ ke versions ek bahut hi one-dimensional perspective tak restricted hain. Reality yeh hai ki zyaada tar history texts ya toh us samay ki ruling authorities dwara directly commission kiye gaye the ya majooda doctrine se indirectly influenced the.

Pichle 400 saalon mein, for instance, non-Western events, logon, culture aur thought ke zyaada tar historical references usse aaye hain jise Edward Said ne famous roop se **‘Orientalist’ perspective** ke roop mein refer kiya hai—yaani, ya toh cherry-picked (chuna hua), downplayed, ya ‘Other’ ke baare mein derogatory stereotypes aur galat generalisations par consist. Said ne is tendency ko pradarshit karne ke liye Western sahitya se dheron udaharan refer kiye, jo ek vachitrah superiority cult (shreshthata ke brahm) ke vikas ke kaaran samne aaya.

‘Western civilisation’ ka notion—Greek thought, Roman law aur Anglo-Saxon adventurism ke uttaradhikari ke roop mein—Renaissance ke baad ‘progress ke liye central force’ ke roop mein propagated kiya gya tha aur phir Enlightenment se Europe mein elite dwara ‘the classics’ ke roop mein padha aur study kiya gya. Saari doosri (aksar grander) civilisations jaise Indian, Islamic, Babylonian, Chinese, Egyptian aur Mayan ko is tarah degraded aur generalised kar diya gya, collectively ‘Oriental’ category mein girte hue, jabki Native Indian, Pacific Island, African aur Aboriginal societies ‘savages’ (ganwaru) ki category mein gir gayin. Western ‘historical method’—apni sibling ‘scientific method’ ki tarah—ne is tarah chaaron taraf world mein indigenous historians dwara history dohrane ke saarre doosre forms aur methods ke samne superiority assume kar li. Nateejatan, bahut kam Western historians ne tarah-tarah ke cultures aur histories ko apne khud ke historical world narrative mein genuinely aur proportionately integrate kiya. Yeh bias, o' course, hamesha exclusively West tak hi simit nahi raha hai—yeh history ke har ek epoch mein kisi bhi dominant society ya empire ka ek feature raha hai, ek hadd tak ya doosre tak. Har ek major empire ke paas khud ko world ka center manne ka ghamand (hubris) tha.

Ideas jo West ke baare baahar se originate hote hain aur majooda paradigm ke sath accord nahi karte unhein aksar ‘pseudo-history,’ ‘pseudo-science’ ya ‘philosophical mumbo-jumbo’ (andhavishwas) ke roop mein derided kiya jata hai. Aur jabki chaaron taraf world se kuch aur mahan works hamare paas laye gaye hain, yahan tak ki sabse behtar translations bhi aksar in works ke true essence aur cultural nuances ko capture karne mein kam pad jaanti hain. Kuch works ko specifically ‘anglicise’ (English-jaisa) karne ke prayas sirf cultures ke beech samajh ki kami ko reinforced karne ka kaam karte hain. Aaj ke sabse ‘educated’ log bhi isliye aksar chaaron taraf world mein exist karne wale perspectives ke variety mein se sirf ek mein hi educated hain—woh mukhya roop se knowledge ki surface ko scratch karte hain.

Kisi ko sirf aaj ke popular science aur history books (yahan tak ki ‘bestsellers’) ko dekhna hai yeh realize karne ke liye ki yeh reality kitni gehrai se entrenched (jami) hai. Africa, Asia, Latin America, Caribbean ya Pacific Islands ki sabse popular histories abhi bhi vishal roop se Europeans ya anglo-educated intellectual elites dwara likhi jaanti hain—baad wale woh hain jo, Frantz Fanon ke mutabik, **“culturally colonised” (saanskritik roop se gulam)** ho chuke hain. In reasons se, ek asli ‘world’ history compile karne ke zyaada tar prayas—chahe ideas ka ho, logon ka, ya events ka—hopelessly inadequate barkarar rehte hain aur perspective mein vishal roop se Eurocentric hain.

Aur yahi kaaran hai niyamit recorded history mein highlighted zyaada tar polymaths white, male Anglo-European elites hote hain, jaise sirf wohi multitalented, encyclopaedic genius ke kabil the. Yeh sirf history nahi hai—jab bhi modern ‘intellectuals’ polymaths ko refer karte hain woh lagbhag hamesha Ancient Greece, Renaissance Italy, ya Victorian England par hi concentrate karte hain. Kya yeh excusable hai?

Non-white polymaths hamesha prachur roop (abundance) mein the. Hum sirf eighteenth aur nineteenth centuries ko lete hain, jab European colonial powers ne practically zyaada tar world par govern kiya. Kitne Europeans Ecuadorian **Eugenio Espejo** ke baare mein jaante hain, jo Spanish occupation ke dauran Latin America ke sabse learned indigenous logon mein se ek the aur unhone ek lawyer, physician, journalist, theologian, economist, political satirist aur educational reformer ke roop mein excel kiya? Ya **Sol Plaatje**, modern South Africa ke sabse mahan black polymaths mein se ek, jinhone language, literature, politics aur journalism mein contributions diye aur apne country ki politics, culture aur society par ek gehra aur lasting impact chhoda. Indian physician **José Gerson da Cunha** ke baare mein kya, jinhone history, numismatics (mudra-shastra), archaeology, linguistics aur medicine sahit tarah-tarah ke subjects par kai papers aur 20 books likhin? Nishchhit roop se Philippine activist **Epifanio de los Santos** nahi, jo ek astoundingly versatile scholar aur essayist the, lekin ek talented musician aur artist hone ke sath-sath renowned politician, lawyer and journalist bhi the.

Yeh hairat-dar nahi hai, jaisa hum baad mein dekhenge, ki is period ke dauran sabse mahan polymaths mein se kuch classism ya colonialism ke khilaf apni mukti ki ladaiyon (struggles for freedom) mein instrumental the. Polymaths ke roop mein barabar—aur kai cases mein tark roop se mazeed—impressive hone ke bavajood, in ‘colonies se aaye indigenous folk’ ko unqi sahi appreciation se vanchit rakkha gya hai.

## Encouragement _(Protsahan)_

Ek aishi duniya mein jahan life ke saarre kshetran mein specialisation lagbhag compulsory hai, hamare liye aaj kalpana karna difficult hai ki insani history ke ek bade hisse ke liye, polymathy ko normal, yahan tak ki natural maana jata था. Aishi cultures, philosophies aur social structures ke dheron instances hain jinhone polymath ko encourage kiya—agar saarre logon ke liye nahi toh kam se kam ek chune hue class ke liye zoor.

Ancient Greece mein, for instance, polymathic philosophers exist karte the, freethinking enquirers jo fields aur disciplines se pare nikal jaate the. Woh ek question pursue karte, aur knowledge ke kisi bhi field ko explore karne ke liye taiyar hote jo ispar roshni daal sake. Unhein **atopos ya ‘unclassifiable’ (vargikaran se pare)** ke roop mein jaana jata tha. Yeh vahi the—Socrates, Plato aur Aristotle jaise log—jinhone us Greek philosophy ko define kiya jiske hum itne karzdaar hain. Roman Era ke dauran, _otium_ की sanskriti (intellectual ya artistic activities ko pursue karne ke liye politicians, lawyers, merchants aur soldiers dwara liya gya ‘leisure time’ / khaali samay) ne currency haasil ki aur ek multifaceted lifestyle ka vikas goal ban gya, khhasakar elite ke beech.

Islamic world mein, Quran ne logon ko iske message ke baare mein ponder, reflect aur sochne ke liye encourage kiya (aur in fact demand ki) (Arabic _tafakkur_, ya ‘to think,’ poore text mein niyamit mention kiya gya hai). Quran mein iske multiple references hain, jo different points par study, travel, scientific investigation, philosophical enquiry aur social dialogue ke zariye knowledge aur wisdom ke acquisition ko encourage karta hai. Yeh, cosmic unity ki taraf sanket karne wale fundamental Islamic concept _tawheed_ ke sath, aur iske prophet, Muhammad ki apni multifaceted life ke powerful example ne mahan Muslim polymaths (ya _Hakeem_) ki generations ko inspire kiya. Yahan tak ki Bertrand Russell ne bhi apni widely acclaimed lekin incredibly Eurocentric _History of Western Philosophy_ mein in shuruati Muslim scholars ko ‘generally encyclopaedic’ ke roop mein recognize karne ke alava koi chandra nahi paaya.

Sung China mein, bureaucrats (_Shih_) statesmen hone ke sath-sath artists aur scientists bhi the jo, idealist philosophers hone se bahut door, bade paimane par technology, culture aur society mein practical contributions dene se mazeed concerned the. Woh building, inventing aur creating ke sath-sath governing aur administering mein bhi interested the. Intellectual pursuits ke sath, _Shih_ ne aishi habits pradarshit kin aur hobbies cultivate kin jinhone na keval unke social status aur refinement ko mark kiya, balki unhein mazeed polymathic banaya. Woh vahi banna chahte the jise _I-Ching_ **junzi ya ‘perfect gentleman’** ke roop mein refer karta hai—woh jo, dheron righteous qualities ke kabze mein hone ke sath-sath, _liu yi_ ya **‘Six Arts’ (Rites, Music, Archery, Charioteering, Calligraphy aur Mathematics)** ko master karta hai. Yeh vahi sanskriti thi jisne Shen Kuo, Su Shi aur Su Song jaise phenomenal polymaths ko janm diya.

Eighteenth-century ke Japan mein Tokugawa shogunate ke tehat yuddh (warfare) ki kami ke kaaran, soldiers ke roop mein samurais ka role kam hua aur woh courtiers, bureaucrats aur administrators ban gaye. Ek niyamit udaharan **Yanagisawa Kien** the, jo, samurai ki is nayi sanskriti ko dekhte hue, finally apni kai ‘doosri sides’ ko unleash karne kabil hue. So ek accomplished painter aur calligrapher hone ke sath-sath, unhone un ‘Sixteen Noble Accomplishments’ ko master kiya jise ek samurai se expect kiya jata tha, jisme military arts (jaise swordplay, horsemanship aur archery), music (unhone flute aur harp bajaya) poetry, medicine, Buddhism aur tea ceremony shamil thin.

French Enlightenment ke dauran, intellectual specialisation ke commonplace hone se theek pehle, _virtuosi_ ‘free aur unconfined’ enquirers ke roop mein ubhar kar aaye, jaise freelance intellectuals ka ek group samne aaya jise _curieux_ ke roop mein jaana gya—ek classic udaharan **Montesquieu** the jinke many-sided interests unqi library aur unqi notebooks dono mein reflected the. Intellectual class ke alava, bourgeoisie ya ‘gentlemen’ se bhi music, fencing, philosophy aur dancing jaise multiple pursuits ko pursue aur master karne ki expect ki jaanti thin—ek custom jise Molière ke classic play _Le Bourgeois Gentilhomme_ mein famous roop se darshaya gya hai. Historian Andrew Robinson ne Enlightenment ko ‘undisputedly polymathic’ ke roop mein dekha; aur Comte de Buffon jaise figures ne is culture ko exemplify (prakat) kiya.

Britain mein usi period ke dauran, ek aishi hi nasal ko celebrate kiya gya. Art historian Kenneth Clark ne kaha _“Eighteenth-Century England was the paradise of the amateur; jisse mera matlab hai, un mardon se jo itne rich aur grand the ki jo chahein woh kar sakein, jo nevertheless aishi cheezein karte the jisme ek bade hisse ki expertise required hoti thi.”_ Unhein Philip Ball dwara apni book _Curiosity_ mein **‘professors of everything’ (har cheez ke professors)** ke roop mein refer kiya gya tha, aur inmein Christopher Wren jaise log shamil the, jinhone ek architect, physicist, astronomer aur mathematician ke roop mein excel kiya; William Herschel, jinhone ek astronomer, botanist, composer aur physicist ke roop mein excel kiya; aur William Petty, jinhone ek naval architect, physician, professor of music, chemist aur engineer ke roop mein excel kiya.

So ek aishi sanskriti (kam se kam elite ke beech) jo mind aur life ki many-sided potential ko encourage karti thi, history mein hamesha kisi na kisi shape ya form mein exist rahi dekhi ja sakti hai. History ke kai points par, polymaths ko acknowledged aur celebrate kiya gya tha ki woh kaun the. However, jaise hum aaj piche dekhte hain, unhi same polymaths ko hamari history books ke zariye hamari consciousness mein **‘specialists’** ke roop mein implanted kar diya gya hai. Yeh koi hairani nahi hai ki humne apni polymathic heritage se sampark kho diya hai.

## The Myth of the ‘Specialist’ _(‘Specialist’ Ka Bhram)_

Edwin Hubble athletics mein excel karte the, ek amateur boxer aur angler (machhuare) the, World War I ke dauran U.S. army mein soldier ke roop mein serve kiya, ek lawyer ke roop mein qualify hue, basketball coach kiya aur Spanish padhai isse pehle ki woh ultimately Nobel Prize-winning astronomer bane jinka naam mukhya _Hubble Telescope_ ko kiya gya tha. Nicolaus Copernicus ek clergyman, economist, painter, polyglot, diplomat, physician aur lawyer the iske sath-sath modern astronomy ke father banne ke jinki heliocentric theory (soorya-kandrit niyam) ne aane wali hafton tak astronomers ke brahmand ko dekhne ke tareeqe mein kranti laadi. Charles Dodgson ek successful photographer, mathematician, clergyman aur inventor the lekin hum unhein **Lewis Carroll** ke roop mein jaantey hain, short stories, poems aur novels ke writer, sabse famous _Alice in Wonderland_. Aur Anthony Burgess, _A Clockwork Orange_ ke author, British army mein ek major, ek linguist aur polyglot, teacher, musical composer aur tarah-tarah ke subjects par ek writer bhi the.

Bhale hi hum ise sweekar karein ya nahi, hum automatically world ke bade achievers ko unke concerned fields mein life-bhar ke specialists ke roop mein vichar karte hain. Hum expect karte hain ki woh apne kaam mein poori tarah se immersed rahe hain, kisi cheez ko unke primary subject ya activity se distract karne ki izazat na dete hue. Hum assume karte hain ki Nobel laureates, mahan scientists, writers aur artists, athletes, businessmen aur statesmen jo hain woh isliye bane kyunki unka apne field ke prati exclusive commitment (ek-matra samarpit-ta) tha. Hamein aksar bataya jata hai ki yeh complete, long-term immersion hi hai, jo aakhirkar unke creative breakthroughs ke liye allow karta hai. **Yeh ek flawed assumption (galat dharna) hai.**

Jaise koi kuch renowned ‘specialists’ ki lives ko study karta hai, aisa lagta hai ki unke paas aksar dheron avocational pursuits (pehle ke careers ya hobbies) hote hain jo kisi na kisi tareeqe se unke mukhya prayas ko influence karte hain—ya kai cases mein facilitate karte hain. Kai cases mein woh khud isse sweekar karte hain. Isliye yeh sawaal uthane ke layak hai ki kya woh jinhein hum automatically ‘specialists’ vichar karte hain actually specialists hain bhi ya nahi.

Typecasting (ek hi khanche mein dhalna) aur ek person ko ek exclusive identity ke sath attach karne ki yeh sanskriti history ko dekhne ke hamare limited tareeqe ko bhi affect karti hai. Yeh fact ki hum Copernicus aur Hubble ko sirf unqi astronomy ke liye aur Carroll aur Burgess ko sirf unqi fiction ke liye jaantey hain hamari majooda mentalities ka khulasa karta hai. Kyunki hamare minds, hamari lifestyles, hamari soch, hamari sanskriti, hamari societies itni specialised hain, hum automatically assume kar lete hain ki yeh hamesha se isi tarah raha hai. Hum history ke logon ke kai sides ko dismiss ya ignore kar dete hain, bajaye iske unhein ek vishisht achievement ke liye celebrate karte hain. Hum sirf kuch hi cases ko—jaise Leonardo, Franklin aur Aristotle—polymaths ke roop mein sweekar karte hain kevel yeh pradarshit karne ke liye ki woh ‘exceptional’ (abnormal) cases hain, practically ek different planet se aayi ek different species.

Truth iske poori tarah opposite hai: **specialisation niyam nahi balki exception (apvaad) hai.** Aise log hain jinke liye ek domain sach mein ek bada, exclusive pukaar (calling) hai: shayad Nusrat Fateh Ali Khan, Pele, Muhammad Ali, John Nash, Akira Kurosawa, Stephen Hawking, Pablo Picasso, Sigmund Freud, A.R. Rahman, Mozart, Rumi aur un jaise. Yeh exceptional cases hain—jinke liye yeh phrase apply ho sakta hai ‘woh ise karne ke liye hi paada hue the’—bhale hi unqi yaatrayan mein usse kahin mazeed tha jitna hum sweekar karne ki parwah karte hain.

In fact, koi world history se kisi bhi mahan figure ko select kar sakta hai—art, science, business, politics, kuch bhi—unki lives explore karein aur dekhein ki unke paas varied backgrounds, varied interests ya varied accomplishments hain ya nahi un kshetran ke alava jiske liye woh mukhya roop se jaane jaante hain. Hyper-specialists, aap payenge, rare hain. **Polymaths hi norm (niyam) hain.**

Twenty-first-century ke dimaag ke liye, jo is baat ko assume karne ke liye conditioned hai ki saarre humans specialists hain ya unhein hona chahiye, polymaths hamesha exist karte the isse sweekar karna hi kaafi difficult ho sakta hai, yeh toh chodo ki woh sach mein history ke prime shapers rahe hain. Is hypothesis (parikalpana) ko prove karne ke liye, aane wala section ‘great man’ historical narrative ko dekhne ka ek different tareeqa set karta hai.

# Chapter 3 — Hamari Duniya Ke Nirmaata

### _(Shapers of Our World)_

## Intro

Polymaths saaf taur par ek dilchasp prajaati hain।

Wo hamein dikhate hain ki agar insaan kisi vishisht shreni mein band hone se mana kar de, to wo kya karne kabil hain।

Wo insani potential ki bahu-pakshiyata ko sateek roop se darshate hain।

Lekin wo actually society mein kya value add karte hain?

Iska jawaab ek simple fact mein sthit hai: polymaths mein world history ke sabse vishal-prabhavi (influential) vyaktitv shamil hain।

Apne samay ke badlav laane walon (disruptors) ke roop mein, unhone human story mein mukhya roop se contribute kiya hai, modern world ko ek aise tareeqe se shape kiya hai jiske bina iski kalpana karna bhi na-mumkin hota।

Isse bhi zyaada, hum ye appreciate karna shuru karenge ki unka polymath hona hi wo mukhya fact tha jisne unqi mahan-ta ko define kiya।

World par unka impact positive maana jata hai ya nahi, ye ek saapeksh baat hai — mukhya point ye hai ki ye transformational tha।

Phir bhi world history ka pratyaksh-avlokan (surveying) kiye bina aur un history-makers mein se sirf kuch ko identify kiye bina aishi dharstata-purna (audacious) claim karna kitna bold hoga; wo jinhein hum automatically single-field specialists maante hain lekin jo actually many-sided aur multidimensional geniuses the।

Structure ke khaatir, in udaharanon ko leaders, king-makers, revolutionaries, intellectuals, educators, scientists, mystics, artists, entrepreneurs aur humanitarians mein segment kiya gya hai — sirf isliye kyunki inhi mukhya sroton (principle channels) ke zariye human story sabse zyaada impacted hui thi।

## Leaders (Neta)

Muhammad ibn Abdullah ek sadharan, anpadh mard the jo seventh-century Bedouin Arabia mein ek anaath charvahe se uthkar ek adarniya (respected) merchant bane।

Lekin 40 saal ki umra mein, unhein ek poori tarah different divya-pukaar (calling) mili aur unhone duniya badalne ka apna campaign shuru kiya।

Ek door-daraz (remote) cave mein saalo ki meditation ke baad, wo ek aise gyan-prakash (enlightenment) tak pahunche the, jo us wajah se sthit hua jise Muslims divine revelations maante hain।

Isne unhein na keval ek re-vered (pujya) spiritual guide banaya, balki ek soldier bhi jo ek krantikari sangharsh ke dauran apni army ko command karta tha, ek statesman jinhone ek naye state ko establish, govern aur expand kiya, ek lawmaker jinhone ek poori tarah naye kanooni-system (legal system) ko form aur implement kiya aur ek encyclopaedic thinker jo general spiritual guidance pradan karne ke sath-sath business, politics aur kanoon se lekar family, social relations aur swachhata (hygiene) jaise mazeed personal issues par bhi practical advice jaari karte the।

Aisa karke, Muhammad ne ek aishi movement shuru ki jo mahan civilisations ki sthapana ko inspire karegi, great art produce karegi aur science aur philosophy mein ut-krishth (tremendous) breakthroughs ko ut-prerit (catalyse) karegi।

World aur iske future par unka impact itna transformational tha ki gora alagtabadi (white separatist) scholar Michael H. Hart tak, apni mukhya (seminal) book _The One Hundred: A Ranking of History’s Most Influential Persons_ mein, Muhammad ko all time ke sabse vishal-prabhavi human ke roop mein list ke top par rank karne par majboor hue।

Quran — Muhammad dwara ucharit divine revelations ki kitab — Muslims dwara God ka sabd maani jaanti hai, aur kisi bhi map-dand se kai doosron dwara ek vishal-gyankosh-sambandhit (encyclopaedic) aur literary masterpiece।

Muhammad ke paas wo tha jise unke biographer Fethullah Gulen ek ‘universal knowledge’ ke roop mein refer karte hain jo kisi ko bhi suchit (inform) kar sakti thi — ‘7th-century ke gair-shikshit (ignorant) Bedouin se lekar world ke greatest modern intellectual’ tak।

Unqi multifaceted life, sima-pare (transcendent) wisdom aur war, law, diplomacy aur spirituality ke mamlon mein unqi leadership unhein Islamic world mein _al-Insan al-Kamil_ ya ‘the Universal Man’ ka title dilayegi।

Leaders — chahe wo political, military, business, movement ya sansthanik (organisational) hon — nischit roop se kisi bhi society mein sabse vishal-prabhavi abhineta (actors) mein se ek hote hain kyunki wo apne adhikaar-patra (mandate) ke nature ke mutabik aise decisions lete hain jo logon ki ek significant number ko affect karte hain।

Aur Muhammad ki tarah, history mein sabse vishal-prabhavi leaders maane jaane walon mein se kai in fact kisi na kisi type ke polymaths the।

Ismein koi surprise nahi hona chahiye।

Companies, charities, governments ya kisi bhi tarah ke bade sansthan, sangthan ya project jiske naturally kai aayam (dimensions) hote hain, unke leaders se mool-roop (intrinsically) se san-shleshak (synthesisers) hone ki required hoti hai।

Unse multiple factors aur nazariyo ka paripoorn (rounded) knowledge hone ki umeed ki jaanti hai jo sabse important decisions ko influence kar sakte hain।

Leaders par aise multiple dikhne mein gair-sambandhit par mool-roop se jude (intrinsically connected) factors ko dhyan mein rakhne ki zimmedari hoti hai।

Unqi job ka nature unse ye mang karta hai ki wo piche hatkar poori badi-tasveer (big picture) ko jaanchein (assess), sahkarya (synergies) ko evaluate karein aur zaroori kadiyon (dots) ko connect karein।

So ek great leader kevel ek bold decision-maker nahi hota, balki ek samagra-roop-se-suchit (holistically informed) decision-maker hota hai, jo sandarbh (context) ke mahatva ko samajhne aur nazariya (perspective) ka ek ehsaas rakhne kabil hota hai।

Some of history’s sabse vishal-prabhavi (main kehne ki dharstata karoon ‘greatest’) ruling monarchs khali utsaahit sanrakshak (enthusiastic patrons) banne se kahin aige nikal gaye।

Wo ek aise vishesh-adhikaar-purna (privileged) position mein the jahan wo bina kisi aarthik tangee (financial constraints) ya doosre restrictions ke apni interests aur talents ko pursue kar sakte the, jiska samna aarthik aur political swatantrata ke bina jeene wale aam log karte hain।

Kuch monarchs ne rajniti-parakh (statecraft) ke maqsad se apni activities ko diversify kiya, jabki doosron ne mukhya roop se personal interest ke taur par aur apni vaidhata ke sign ke roop mein multiple talents ko pradarshit karne ke liye activities li।

Aisa karke, unhone tarah-tarah ki talents pradarshit kin, aksar khud ko asadharn polymaths ke roop mein zahir (revealing) karte hue।

Ek mukhya udaharan Frederick II the, jo thirteenth century mein Holy Roman Emperor the aur jinhein widely _stupor mundi_ ya ‘wonder of the world’ ke roop mein refer kiya jata tha — ek aisa title jo unhone fifth crusade mein Jerusalem ko jitne ke alava bhi kai aur cheezon se kamaya tha (jise thirteenth-century Europe mein political achievement ka choti-bindu [pinnacle] maana jata tha)।

Frederick ek polyglot, poet aur linguist the jinhone bhasha-vigyan-sambandhit (philological) experiments conduct kiye bhasha ke evolution ko samajhne ke liye, falconry mein ek specialist the (jispar unhone ek treatise likha) aur 'Dark Ages' ke dauran kisi bhi European monarch ke mukable ek bemisaal (unparalleled) level par arts aur scholarship ke patron the।

Western world ke liye kam jaane-pehne Nezahualcóyotl the, wo fifteenth-century ke warrior-statesman jinhone Aztec Texcoco ke Golden Age ko ut-prerit (instigated) aur preside kiya — ek aisa period jisne rule of law, scholarship aur kalatmakta (artistry) ko ek aise level par laya jiska us kshetra ki doosri cultures par gehra impact pada।

Unhone power ke batware par based ek kanoon-sanhita (code of law) design kiya, jisne finance, war, justice aur culture ke councils ko create kiya।

Unke rule ke tehat, Texcoco Triple Alliance ke boddhik-kendra (intellectual centre) ke roop mein phhala-phhoola aur uske paas ek vishal library thi jo, dukh-daayi (tragically), Spanish jeet (conquest) ke dauran bach nahi saktin।

Unhone music ki ek academy bhi establish ki aur Mesoamerica ke saarre regions se aane wale yogya (worthy) entrants ka swagat kiya।

Wo ek philosophical movement ke father ke roop mein bhi jaane jaate hain, jiske followers ko Nahuatl language mein _tlamatinime_ refer kiya jata hai — ek aisa sabd jiska matlab hai ‘woh jo kuch jaanta hai’, jiska samanya anuvad ‘wise man’ kiya jata hai।

Unhon Texcoco mein ekeishwarvaad (monotheism) ka idea introduce karne ke liye bhi note kiya jata hai।

Nahuatl language ki kai sabse mahan poems unhein attribute ki jaanti hain, jinmein se kuch Mexico City ke National Museum of Archaeology ki deewaron par likhi hain।

Unhein prachin (pre-Hispanic) era ke mahan designers aur architects mein se ek maana jata hai aur kaha jata hai unhone Lake Texcoco ke taaza aur khare (brackish) paani ko alag karne ke liye _Albarrada de Nezahualcóyotl_ (Nezahualcóyotl ka bandh) design kiya tha — ek aisa system jo unqi death ke ek sadi (century) baad bhi use mein tha।

Unhone shehar ke kuch sabse prateek-atmak (iconic) sites ko commission kiya aur kai cases mein personally design kiya।

Unqi story Suleiman ‘the Magnificent’ se alag nahi hai, jinhone Ottoman Empire ki military, political aur economic power ke shikhhar (apex) ko preside kiya, aur personally samaj, shiksha, kar-vyavastha (taxation) aur criminal law se sambandhit legislative changes institute kiye।

Unke kanoon (canonical law ya Kanuns) ne unqi death ke baad sadiyon tak ke liye empire ke form ko fix kar diya।

Ek polyglot hone ke naate, wo five languages bolte the: Ottoman Turkish, Arabic, Bosnian, Chagatai (Turkish ki ek boli jo Uyghur se sambandhit hai) aur Persian।

Suleiman na keval apne aap mein ek vishisht (distinguished) poet aur sunar (goldsmith) the; balki wo culture ke ek mahan patron bhi the, jinhone Ottoman Empire ke artistic aur literary development ke golden age ko dhyan-purvak-dekha (overseeing), aur famously mahan architect Mimar Sinan ke sath sah-karya (collaborating) karke Islamic history ke kuch sabse shandaar architectural feats ko build kiya।

And then aise mazeed famous monarchs hain jinhein hum achhi tarah jaante hain lekin unqi leadership ke alava unqi interests aur accomplishments ko historians dwara (aur is tarah hamare minds mein bhi) aksar upari dikhave ke roop mein nichla-darja (downgraded) diya jata hai ya paridhee-ye-kalpana (peripheral fancies) ke roop mein kamdarja dekar chhod diya jata hai।

Akbar ‘the Great’, jo India ke sixteenth-century emperor the, unhone shashan ka ek mazboot (robust) system set up kiya, matrimonial alliances ke zariye empire ke andar ke guthon (factions) ke sath diplomacy pursued ki, fine art aur architecture ko badhava diya aur subcontinent par mukhya rannitik (strategic) ports aur cities ko include karne ke liye empire ko expand kiya।

Moreover, unhone ek poori tarah naye religion, Din-e-Ilahi, ke gathan (formation) ko coordinate kiya, jo empire ke saarre faiths, traditions aur world views ke elements ko ek-sath-milata (synthesise) tha।

Kam jaana-pehna ye fact hai ki Akbar ne continuous learning ki lifestyle pursued ki।

Anpadh (illiterate) hone ke bavajood, unhone khud ko alalag topics ke experts se ghera — aur aksar doosron se apne liye zor se books padhne ko kehte the।

Unhon ek soldier aur huntsman ke roop mein train kiya gya tha, aur aakhirkar wo ek durbhedya (formidable) warrior bane jo emperor ke roop mein apni battles ko khud lead karte aur ladte the।

Wah ek prathisthit artisan, armourer aur blacksmith bhi the aur carpentry aur lace-making mein excel karte then, lagatar naye skills ko seekhte, master aur tayaar (implementing) karte hue, jiske zariye unhone apne court ka samman earn kiya aur apne pratidwandi (adversaries) ke minds mein ek almost superhuman image create ki।

Unke English counterpart ke sath ek jaishi similarities dekhi ja sakti hain।

King Henry VIII apni six wives hone aur English Navy ko found karne ke liye famous hain, aur Roman Catholic Church se apne chamatkarik-alagao (ground-shattering detachment) ke liye badnaam hain।

But he was also a multitalented genius.
_(Lekin wo ek multitalented genius bhi the।)_

Unhone ek bhalaitait-bhaajak (jouster) aur hunter ke roop mein apne talents ka use apni athletic royal image ko viksit karne aur videshi dooton (foreign emissaries) aur rulers ko impress karne ke sath-sath vidrohon (rebellions) ko dabane (suppress) karne ki apni ability ko prasharit (convey) karne ke liye kiya।

Moreover, wo ek tivra-juari (avid gambler) aur dice player the, aur ek accomplished musician, author aur poet the; unka sabse parichit piece of music ‘Pastime with Good Company’ (The Kynges Ballade) hai।

Wah ek well-stocked library wale pehle well-educated English king bhi the, jinhone personally kai books ko tippani-yukt (annotated) kiya aur apna khud ka work likha aur publish kiya।

Kansha-purna (ambitious) monarchs ke paas aksar seekhne ka ek nirantar (perpetual) passion hota tha।

Seventeenth-century ki Queen Kristina of Sweden arts aur scholarship ki sabse prominent patronesses mein se ek thin।

Wah saarre gyan ki ek lifelong student thin, jinhein ek din mein 12 hours tarah-tarah ke subjects ko study karne mein bitane ke liye jaana jata tha।

Wah ek polyglot thin, jo sainik-kala (soldiery) ke art mein trained thin, jinhone Descartes ke sath philosophy ke mamlon par patra-achar (exchanged letters) kiya (aur in fact unhein ek tutor ke roop mein hire kiya), Leibniz ke sath astronomy study ki aur apni khud ki nakshtra-shala (observatory) set up ki।

India mein Malwa ke eleventh-century Paramar king, Raja Bhoja, ko historians dwara ek great statesman aur military genius ke roop mein recognize kiya jata hai, jinhone theology, poetry aur grammar se lekar engineering, architecture aur chemistry tak ke subjects par alalag treatises bhi likhe।

Ye polymaths monarchs the, lekin yahi same principle kuch asadharn modern ‘democratic’ leaders par bhi apply hota hai।

Thomas Jefferson ko aksar all time ke sabse mahan statesmen mein se ek ke roop mein gaurav-sahit (revered) jana jata hai।

Unke shuruati political career ne unhein ek congressman, doot (diplomat) [Minister to France], aur Secretary of State ke roop mein dekha।

Democratic Republican Party ko co-found karne ke baad, woh United States ke third President bane।

Most famously, Founding Fathers mein se ek ke roop mein, wo swatantrata-ghoshna (Declaration of Independence) ke mukhya author the।

Lekin politics se baahar unqi vividh (multifarious) accomplishments hi unhein baki ke 40-odd American presidents se alag khada karti hain।

So much so ki White House mein tarah-tarah ke Nobel laureates ko host karne wale ek dinner par, John F. Kennedy ne famously up-haas (joked) kiya tha: ‘Main sochta hoon ye talent ka, human knowledge ka sabse asadharn collection hai jise White House mein kabhi ek sath gather kiya gya ho — is sambhavit apvaad (possible exception) ke sath jab Thomas Jefferson akele dinner karte the।’

In addition to ek nish-tha-vaan lawyer hone ke, Jefferson ek farmer the jinka krishi-takneek (agricultural techniques) mein ek deep (enduring) interest tha, ek accomplished architect the jinhone Palladianism ko popularise karne mein help ki, ek srajan-shil (prolific) inventor the jinke designs mein ek revolving book stand aur ‘Great Clock’ shamil hain, ek polyglot the jo sahaj-roop (fluently) se five languages bolte the aur ek intellectual the jinhone apne samay ke mukhya vicharkon ke sath sampark (corresponded) kiya।

Did Jefferson’s many-sidedness contribute to his ability as a statesman?
_(Kya Jefferson ki many-sidedness ne statesman ke roop mein unqi ability mein contribute kiya?)_

Azeri intellectual Hamlet Isakhanli ke mutabik, ‘ek multifaceted person ke paas ek leader banne aur leadership mein safalta haasil karne ka mazeed bada chance hota hai।’

Prabandhan (management) psychologist Philip Tetlock apni research se ye nishkarsh nikaalte hain ki behtar nirnay (good judgement) ke sabse behtar scientific indicators aur single-minded specialisation ke beech ek ulta-sambandh (inverse relationship) hota hai।

Isaiah Berlin ki ‘fox and the hedgehog’ upma (analogy) se draw karte hue, wo contend karte hain ki fox — wo thinker jo dheron choti cheezein jaanta hai, alalag traditions ke ek mishrit srot (eclectic array) se draw karta hai, aur badalte events ke response mein tat-kaal-sudhaar (improvise) karne kabil hota hai — future ko predict karne mein us kanta-chuha (hedgehog) se mazeed successful hota hai jo sirf ek big cheez jaanta hai, ek hi tradition ke andar nistha-purvak mehnat karta hai aur spasht-na-hui (ill-defined) problems par nirdharit sutra (formulaic solutions) impose karta hai।

Tetlock ke thesis par comment karte hue, Oxford neuroscientist Anders Sandberg ne dobara confirm kiya, ‘manager manageable problems ko solve karta hai, ek leader a-gyat (unknown) problems ko solve karta hai। Leader ko probably ek tarah ka polymath hona padta hai। Main ek hedgehog leader ko lekar bahut chintit (worried) hounga।’

Indeed, world ke leading bhavishya-vaktaon (futurists) mein se ek Ray Kurzweil kisi bhi project mein leader ke polymathic hone ki avashyakta (need) par draddhta se jor (insists) karte hain।

‘Highly specialized fields ke experts ek team ka part ho sakte hain,’ wo mukt-kanth se sweekar (admits) karte hain, ‘lekin team leader ko multiple disciplines ko jodne (bridge) karne ki zarurat hoti hai।’

Ye Winston Churchill ki success ko bhi explain kar sakta hai, jinhein twentieth century ke sarvashrestha statesmen mein se ek ke roop mein celebrate kiya jata hai।

Unhone nau-sena-pradhan (First Lord of the Admiralty), vitta-mantri (Chancellor of the Exchequer), vanijya-board-adhyaksh (President of the Board of Trade) aur griha-mantri (Home Secretary) ko include karte hue uccha-stariya government positions ka ek diverse range hold kiya, isse pehle ki wo aakhirkar British history ke sabse adarniya prime ministers mein se ek bane।

Churchill ki accomplishments ek leader ke roop mein itni adarniya (revered) hain ki unhone aksar is fact ko dhak (overshadowed) diya hai ki wo ek accomplished soldier, artist aur scholar bhi the — unqi Queen ke mutabik ek ‘many-sided genius’।

Wah ek prolific writer bhi the jinhone 1953 mein saahitya (Literature) ke liye Nobel Prize jeeta; unhone ek novel, do historical biographies, sansmaran (memoirs) ke three volumes, kai histories aur ek yuddh-padd-lekhak (war correspondent) ke roop mein dheron newspaper articles likhe।

Ek oil painter ke roop mein unhone 100 se mazeed works produce kiye, zyaada tar prabhav-vadi (impressionist) landscapes, jinhein worldwide exhibit kiya gya hai — unhone painting par ek shodh-patra (treatise) tak likha tha।

Philosopher Roger Scruton — jo khud ek polymathic intellectual the — Churchill ki leadership ki mahan-ta ko is fact ke sahare rakhte hain ki wo ek polymath the: ‘. . . Main yahan Churchill ke baare mein sochta hoon, unqi history, literature, art, society ke tareeqon aur kanoon par pakad ke sath — jis sabne 1930s mein situation par ek kalpanatmak aur poora kabza (total grasp) haasil karne mein unqi help ki jab baki ki political class andhere mein ladkhada rahi thin।’

Wahan doosre udaharan bhi hain, dono।

America ke 26th president, Theodore Roosevelt, judo aur boxing mein excel karte the aur phir unhone 42 saal ki umra mein public office lene se pehle ek policeman, soldier, anveshak (explorer), farmer aur hunter ke roop mein chote-karya (stints) kiye aur aakhirkar America ke ab tak ke sarvashrestha leaders mein se ek bane।

Baad mein param-vidwan-neta (ultimate scholar-statesman) ke roop mein recognise kiye gaye, wo saarre American presidents mein se sabse mazeed published writers mein se ek bane jinki books history [nau-sena history ke sath-sath unqi vishal Winning of the West], nature aur travel saahitya [unqi Brazilian aur African sahason se kathaayein (tales)] aur philosophy jaise diverse subjects par thin।

‘Greatest South Africans of all time’ mein se ek ke roop mein ranked, Jan Smuts ko twentieth century ke mahan netaon mein se ek ke roop mein gaurav-sahit pukara (hailed) jata hai।

Unhone British government mein alalag cabinet posts hold kin, aur phir 1919 se 1924 tak aur 1939 se 1948 tak Union of South Africa ke Prime Minister rahe।

Wah First aur Second dono World Wars ko khatam karne wali shanti-sandhiyon (peace treaties) par sign karne wale akela person the।

Bahu-pakshiye (multilateral) level par, wo League of Nations aur iski successor, United Nations ke bhi ek important vishal-nirmaata (architect) the, jo aakhirkar dono organisations ke charters par sign karne wale akela person bane।

His success as a leader might have had something to do with the fact that he had an extremely diverse career.
_(Ek leader ke roop mein unqi safal-ta ka is fact se kuch lena-dena ho sakta hai ki unka ek behad vividhta-purna [extremely diverse] career tha।)_

## King-makers (Raja Banane Wale)

Weimar Romanticism movement ke roop mein jaane gaye movement ke mukhya abhinetaon (key figures) mein se ek, Johann Wolfgang von Goethe, ko world literature mein sarv-kaalik-mahan (all-time greats) mein se ek maana jata hai।

Lekin unke vishal-lok-priya (hugely popular) novels, plays aur poems ke addition mein, unhone biology, botany aur physics ki scientific dharraon (disciplines) mein bhi mukhya pragati (advances) kiye, jabki wo ek successful lawyer, courtier aur philosopher bhi rahe the।

Goethe ne apne career ki shuruat ek nax-sha-kaar (draughtsman) aur jala-rang-kalaakar (watercolourist) ke roop mein ki, aur ranga-shala (theatre) aur literature mein ek strong interest ko bhi badhava diya (nurturing)।

Unhone Leipzig mein kanoon (law) study karne ke liye enrol kiya, aakhirkar Frankfurt mein ek pramanit-adhikaari (licensee) ke roop mein certified hue aur dheron important cases par kaam kiya।

Wah phir Duke of Saxe-Weimar-Eisenach ke darbaar (court) mein ek adviser bane, jahan unhone France ke failed invasion aur Siege of Mainz ke dauran ek sainik-salaahkar (military adviser) ki position ko include karte hue kai positions hold kin।

## Revolutionaries (KRANTIKARI)

Establishment (prashasan ya sthapit vyavastha) ke andar alag-alag interests ko poora karna ek baat hai; lekin modern history ke kai polymaths (bahumukhi pratibha wale log) ne darasal iske khilaf ek bada virodh (**major resistance**) chalaaya, jiski aksar unhe ek bhaari keemat chukani padi. Paul Robeson apne samay ke sabse bade American footballers mein se ek the. Khel ke maidaan se door, woh Law School mein gehan padhai kar rahe the aur graduation ke baad unhone ek New York law firm mein apna career shuru kiya. Lekin ek athlete-lawyer hona hi kaafi nahi tha. Yeh yuva vyakti stage par zindagi bitane ke apne bachpan ke sapne ko poora karne ke liye dridh-sankalp (**determined**) tha. Padhai ke dauran kuch shows mein mehnat (hustle) karne ke baad, ek bass singer ke roop mein unki shohrat badhne lagi. Woh concert stage par Spirituals (America mein gulam banaaye gaye African logon dwara rache gaye dharmik geet) lane wale pehle logon mein se ek banenge aur _Ballad for Americans_ (ek American deshbhakti geet jo aaj bhi unki aawaz se juda hai) ke unke performance ne unhe ek national superstar bana diya.

Woh ek actor ke roop mein bhi barabar prabhavshali (talented) the aur _The Emperor Jones_ jaise naatakon mein unke shuruati performances ne unhe kaafi tareef dilayi. Unhone William Shakespeare ke _Othello_ mein title role nibhaya, jiske liye unhone 1945 mein Spingarn Medal jeeta. Unhone _Show Boat_ ke London aur Broadway productions mein kaam kiya, aur 1936 ke film version mein bhi acting ki. Film mein unka _Ol’ Man River_ ka gaan aaj bhi us geet ki sabse behtareen peshkash (interpretation) maana jata hai. Unhone British film producers ka dhyan apni taraf khincha, aur unka career tab nayi uchaiyon par pahonch gaya jab unhone _Song of Freedom_ aur _The Proud Valley_ samet kai British box office hit films mein lead roles nihaye.

Apni shaandar kamyabi ke baavjood, unhone aur zyada seekhne ki zaroorat mahsoos ki. Unhe khaas taur par bhashaon (languages) aur bhasha-vigyan (linguistics) mein interest tha. Isliye 1934 mein unhone London ke School of Oriental and African Studies mein admission liya, jahan unhone Mandarin, Swahili aur kai aanya African bhashaon par focus kiya. Ek ashadharan roop se tez seekhne wale (fast learner) hone ke naate, woh aakhirkar 20 se zyada bhashaon mein baatchit karne ke kabil (conversant) ho gaye — jisse woh twentieth century ke sabse pramukh polyglots (kai bhashayein bolne wale) mein se ek ban gaye.

African logon ke itihaas aur rajneeti ke unke adhyayan ne, unke nasli bhedbhav (racial segregation) ke vyaktigat anubhavon ke sath milkar, unhe United States mein racism (naslabad), colonialism (upniveshvad) aur classism (vargbad) ke sabse teekhe aalochakon (**trenchant critics**) mein se ek banne mein saksham banaya. Unhone duniya bhar ki yatra jaari rakhi — na sirf ek artist ke roop mein balki ab samajik nyay (social justice) ke ek abhiyan-karta (campaigner) ke roop mein bhi. Unke samajwadi (socialist) vicharon aur unki skin ke rang ka matlab tha ki unka apna desh unhe alag-thalag (isolate) kar dega, bhale hi woh modern American history ke sabse multitalented mardon mein se ek the.

Robeson har insaan ke poore aatm-siddhi (**complete self-actualisation**) mein vishwas rakhte the, chahe uski jaati, varg ya ling kuch bhi ho. Aur unhe laga ki yeh ek capitalist system ke tehat sambhav nahi hai. Asal mein, aam dharana ke vipreet, mool samajwadi soch ne ek aagyakari, sena-jaisi (regimented) janta ki kalpana nahi ki thi, balki samatavad (egalitarianism) ke ek aise system ki kalpana ki thi jo har vyakti ko apne anokhe tareeqe se chamakne ki buniyaad deta hai. Oscar Wilde ne apne prasiddh essay _The Soul of Man under Socialism_ mein is baat par zor diya ki private property par aadharit system kabhi bhi kisi vyakti ke poore vikas ya 'vyaktitva ki poori abhivyakti' (**full expression of personality**) ki anumati nahi de sakta. Unhone kaha ki 'kaam ke badle kiraya' (work for hire) ke darshan par aadharit system sirf aarthik roop se surakshit elite scholars aur artists ke ek chhote samooh ko hi poori tarah se phalne-phoolne ki anumati de sakta hai. Yeh ek aisa samaj banata hai jahan 'insaan apne wajood ke bajaye apni sampatti (possessions) mein doob jata hai'.

Asal mein, Marx khud ek 'bahu-pakshiya shiksha' (many-sided education) mein vishwas rakhte the aur unhone likha tha ki 'ek Communist samaj mein koi painter nahi hota balki sirf aise log hote hain jo doosri activities ke sath-sath painting mein shamil hote hain.' Shayad isi baat ne duniya bhar ke kai samajwadi krantikariyon ko, Robeson ki tarah, apne kai pehluon ko aazmane ke liye inspire kiya, lagbhag jaise ki yeh khud mein virodh (act of resistance) ka ek kaam tha.

Itihaas mein kai samajik-rajnitik krantiya (sociopolitical revolutions) hui hain, aksar ek satta-bhukha samooh doosre ki jagah leta hai. Lekin asli krantiya, jo gulamon, kisaanon, alpasankhyakon ya aupniveshik praja ka pratinidhitva karti hain, mool roop se shoshakon (oppressors) dwara unpar thope gaye system ko badalne ke liye shoshit logon dwara ek sangathit sangharsh (organised struggle) hain. Modern daur mein aksar diye jaane wale kamyab udaharanon mein American, Haitian, French, Cuban, Russian aur Iranian Revolutions shamil hain. Inka netritva radical hstiyon ne kiya, aamtaur par context ke aadhar par liberals, anti-colonial nationalists aur socialists ne.

Sabse prasiddh modern Western revolution late eighteenth century mein Paris mein hua tha, jiska netritva liberals ne niran-kush rajtantra (absolutist monarchy) ke khilaf kiya tha aur yeh Enlightenment ke swatantra-soch andolan se inspired tha. French Enlightenment ke do sabse pramukh vyakti na sirf apne aap mein krantikari the, balki ashadharan polymaths bhi the.

Voltaire ek bahumukhi writer the, jinhone lagbhag har saahityik roop mein rachnayein ki, jinmein plays, poems, novels, essays aur historical aur scientific works shamil hain. Unhone 20,000 se zyada patra (letters) aur 2,000 se zyada kitaabein aur pamphlets likhe. Woh us samay ke behadd sakht censor kanoonon ke khatre ke baavjood, nagrik aazadiyon (civil liberties) ke ek mukhar samarthak (outspoken advocate) the. Pierre Beaumarchais ko kai aitihasikaron dwara French Enlightenment ke sath-sath Revolution ke bhi mukhya patron mein se ek maana jata hai. Aisa isliye hai kyunki Beaumarchais ki zindagi — jo French Enlightenment ki aseemitata (boundlessness) ka udaharan thi — ek sahasik (adventurous) zindagi thi. Woh ek behtareen ghadi-saaz (watchmaker), French royal family ke musician, businessman, diplomat aur American Revolution ke dauran ek jaasoos (spy) the aur phir apne samay ke sabse kamyab naatak-karon (playwrights) mein se ek the.

Marxism ko Russia mein kranti ke aadhar ke roop mein apnaye jaane se pehle, isne Europe mein, khaas kar England mein prasiddhi haasil ki thi. William Morris, Marx ki soch se inspired aur unki beti Eleanor ke dost, England ke sabse bade samajwadi krantikari karyakartao mein se ek bane. Unhone apne career ki shuruat mid-eighteenth century mein ek Gothic revival architect ke roop mein ki, isse pehle ki woh apna dhyan interior design ki taraf modte. Unhone audyogik nirman (industrial manufacturing) ko kharij kar diya aur Arts and Craft Movement ki shuruat ki, paramparik haath ki karigari (hand craftsmanship) ki taraf wapsi aur artisan ke status ko artist ke barabar uthane ki maang ki. Woh wallpapers, ceramic tiles, stained glass, textiles aur doosre interior decorations ke liye ek pattern designer bane aur unhone textile weaving, dying, printing aur embroidery mein bhi mahaarat haasil ki. Woh ek maanyata-prapt oil painter bhi the, halanki unki akeli bachi hui easel painting _La Belle Iseult_ London ki Tate Gallery mein pradarshit hai.

Morris ne 15th century ke paramparik prakaashan tareeqon ki nakal karne ke liye typefaces aur book decorations bhi design kiye. Asal mein unhone apna khud ka publishing house, Kelmscott Press shuru kiya, jo apne chhote se daur mein desh ke sabse prasiddh specialist presses mein se ek ban gaya. Jin madhyakaaleen (medieval) lekhakon ko unhone publish kiya unse inspire hokar, Morris ne khud saahitya ki duniya mein zabardast yogdan diya. Woh Pre-Raphaelite kaviyon mein se pehle the, aur unhone apni poori zindagi mein kavita ke kai samgra (volumes of poetry) publish kiye. Ek novelist ke roop mein, Morris ne fantasy fiction genre ki shuruat ki, aur unke _News from Nowhere_ aur _The Wood beyond the World_ jaise novels ke baare mein kaha jata hai ki unhone James Joyce aur J.R.R. Tolkien jaise aage ke lekhakon ko prabhavit kiya. Jab 62 saal ki umar mein unka nidhan hua, toh unke doctor ne ghoshna ki ki kaaran 'sirf William Morris hona tha, aur das mardon se zyada kaam karna tha.' Morris ko apni zindagi ke ant tak dheere-dheere samajh aa gaya tha ki, unke prayason ke baavjood, unki zindagi mein kranti nahi hone wali thi. Unke novels unki utopian kalpana ko vyakt karne ka ek tareeqa the.

Kahin aur, samajwadi krantiya Russia, India aur Cuba aur duniya ke kai doosre hisson mein pak rahi thin. Aur unke apne polymaths the. Russian Alexander Bogdanov Russian Social Democratic Labour Party ke sath-sath proletarian cultural movement Proletkult ke founders mein se ek the. Lekin woh ek trained doctor (physician) aur psychiatrist bhi the, Bolshevik Revolution ke dauran ek rajneetik siddhantkar (political theorist) aur arthshastri (economist) the, Russian science fiction ke pioneer the (unka _Red Star_, Mars par ek utopia ke baare mein, modern Russian literature mein ek classic ban gaya) aur vigyan ke ek mahatvapurna darshanik aur pehle systems theorists mein se ek the (jo unke mukhya kaam _Tectology_ mein roopankit hai).

Indian Rahul Sankrityayan ne Bihar mein zameendaron ke khilaf kisaanon ke vidroh ka netritva kiya, unhe teen mauqon par jail bheja gaya aur woh apne samay ke pramukh samajwadi jeevnikaron (biographers) mein se ek the, jinhone Marx, Lenin, Stalin aur Mao Tse Tung par mukhya kaam poore kiye. Mahapundita (sabse gyaani) ke roop mein jaane jaane wale, woh ek hyper-polymath bhi the jinhone 100 se zyada kitaabein likhin jinmein sociology, literary history, philosophy, Buddhism, grammar, editing, folklore, science, drama aur politics samet kai vishay cover kiye gaye the.

1960 ke dashak ki Cuban Revolution ke leaders mein se ek, Che Guevara ko ek bestselling travel writer, ek yogya aur abhyas karne wale doctor, ek poojya Marxist philosopher, ek kushal sainik aur sainy siddhantkar aur aakhirkar ek shaktishali rajneeta aur kutneetijna (diplomat) hone ke baavjood shayad hi kabhi polymath kaha jata hai. Toh yeh hairan karne wala nahi hai ki U.S.-backed Bolivian authorities dwara 40 saal ka hone se pehle hi maare jaane ke baavjood, French philosopher Jean Paul Sartre ne unhe 'apne daur ka sabse poorna insaan' (**the most complete man of his age**) bataya.

Twentieth century ke shuruat tak, samrajyavadi adhikariyon (imperialist authorities) ko sthaniye karyakartaon se unke shasan ka kaafi virodh samna karna pada; aur unke beech se polymaths ki ek nayi nasal samne aayi. Unmein se kai zabardast roop se sarv-gun-sampanna (well rounded) the; har ek ashadharan roop se achha padha-likha tha (aamtaur par Western aur indigenous dono traditions mein), unke professional careers the, woh virodh ki kavita (resistance poetry) likhte the, darshanik vishwa-drishti (philosophical world views) viksit karte the, sainik ke roop mein ladte the, andolan-on ka netritva karte the aur aksar apni aupniveshik-virodhi (anti-imperialist) bhavnaon ko vyakt karne ke liye kala aur saahitya ka upayog karte the. Abhivyakti ke kai rachnatmak roop viksit karne ki zaroorat ne unhe polymath banne ke liye inspire (aur kuch mamlon mein majboor) kiya.

Filipino national hero José Rizal ek polymathic krantikari the jinki zindagi Spanish colonialists dwara 35 saal ki umar mein hi khatam kar di gayi thi. Shuruat mein kanoon mein interest lene ke baad, woh aakhirkar medicine ki padhai karne gaye, Universidad Central de Madrid se Licentiate in Medicine haasil kiya, aur ek ophthalmologist (aankhon ke doctor) bane. Kai log Rizal ko ek manav-vigyan vidwan (anthropological scholar) ke roop mein bhi describe karenge. Unhone Orient ki bhashaon aur sanskritiyon, jaise Chinese aur Japanese, ke sath-sath Filipinos par bhi vyapak research kiya. Bhashayein seekhne ki unki kshamta ashadharan thi — woh ek kaafi chhote samay ke andar 20 se zyada bhashaon mein fluent ho gaye.

Ek kavi ke roop mein, Rizal ne duniya bhar mein tareef haasil ki, 35 se zyada kavitaayein rachin, jinmein unki prasiddh _Ultimo Adios_ shamil hai. Lekin shayad sabse mahatvapurna unki kavita ne kai Filipinos ko Spanish colonialists ke khilaf aazadi ke liye khade hone prerit kiya. Unhone essay aur diary ke roop mein rajneetik kaam likhna shuru kiya, aur aakhirkar La Liga Filipina ki sthapna ki, jo ek krantikari sangathan tha jiske baad Katipunan aaya. Spain mein rehne ke dauran woh Spanish akhbar _La Solidaridad_ mein ek columnist bane, jismein unhone Filipino logon ke liye zyada aazadi aur bhrashtachar aur niyantran se mukt behtar shasan ki maang ki.

Rizal ek prabhavshali artist bhi the. Ek painter ke roop mein, unhone watercolor, oil aur crayon mein kai pieces banaye jinmein _Christ Crucified_, _Saturnina Rizal_ aur _Spanish Coat of Arms_ shamil hain, jismein cloth, seashells, paper aur canvas jaise alag-alag surfaces ka upayog kiya gaya. Unhone un jagahon ke, aitihasik smarakon ke aur apni zindagi ke logon ke kai sketches bhi banaye jahan unhone yatra ki thi.

Shayad aur bhi prabhavshali, halanki, ek sculptor ke roop mein Rizal ka shaandar output tha. Unke prasiddh _Triumph of Science over Death_ ne ek nagn mahila dwara mashal pakde hue European Dark Ages se Enlightenment ke uday ka pratinidhitva kiya. Unhone us samay ke kai mahatvapurna logon ke busts (ardha-moortiyan) bhi banaye, jinmein unke apne pita shamil the, wood, clay, wax aur terracotta mein kaam karte hue, aakhirkar 50 se zyada pieces banaye. Koi sochta hai ki unhe itni chhoti zindagi mein samay kab mila, lekin Rizal ek shauqeen hobbyist bhi the, chess, fencing, music (flute) aur pistol shooting mein kushalata ke sath haath aazmate the. Unhone cartography (nakshe banana) mein bhi hissa liya, Philippines ke kai ilaqon ke nakshe banaye jinmein Mindanao shamil hai. Kuch log Rizal ko 'ruralist' ke roop mein bhi sandarbhit kar sakte hain. Yeh krishi (agriculture) mein unke kaam ke kaaran hai — unke paas aise farms the jise unhone saalon tak manage kiya, jahan unhone vanaspati anusandhan (botanical research) kiya aur kai tarah ke paudhe ugaye.

Rizal saaf taur par multitalented the. Ek chhoti zindagi mein, woh ek kamyab doctor, artist, kavi, karyakarta, polyglot, hobbyist aur scholar bane, jinka kaam social aur physical sciences ke sath-sath arts tak phaela tha. Lekin unki ashadharan polymathy unke aupniveshik shoshakon dwara lagaye gaye system ke khilaf virodh ka ek roop thi.

Colonies mein unke apnon jaise hi sansthaayein banakar, 19th century mein imperialist authorities ne poori colonised duniya mein specialisation ki ek sanskriti phelayi. Colonial bureaucracies ne jahan zaroorat thi wahan specialists niyukt kiye aur sthaniye vicharkon aur artists ki bauddhika aazadi ko kaafi hadd tak baandh diya. Isliye, Rizal ki tarah, European imperialism ke ant ki taraf kai pramukh resistance figures adbhut roop se polymathic ke roop mein ubhare. South Asia mein, Muhammad Iqbal Pakistan ke 'intellectual founder' the, sath hi uske national poet bhi (jinhone kai bhashaon mein 12,000 se zyada chhand likhe). Lekin woh ek kamyab lawyer bhi the, unhone Urdu bhasha mein arthshastra (economics) par pehli kitaabon mein se ek likhi, aur unhe bade modern Islamic darshanikon mein se ek ke roop mein tareef milti hai — jo unhe woh banata hai jise Urdu mein _har-fun mawla_ ('master of every art') kaha jata hai.

Africa mein, Senegalese krantikari Cheikh Anta Diop ne ek physicist ke roop mein kamyabi haasil ki jinhone Einstein ki Theory of Relativity ko apni native Wolof bhasha mein translate kiya, Institut Fondamental d’Afrique Noire (IFAN) mein ek radiocarbon laboratory sthapit ki aur nuclear physics par ek kitaab likhi. Phir unhone genetics, linguistics, Egyptology, anthropology aur world history ke apne gyaan ko milakar apna groundbreaking masterpiece — _The African Origin of Civilization: Myth or Reality?_ — banaya, jismein unhone vyapak aitihasik aur vaigyanik vidwatta ke ek behtareen kaam ke zariya apni aupniveshik-virodhi rajneetik philosophy ka samarthan kiya. Phir, ek rajneetik siddhantkar ke roop mein, unhone _Black Africa: The Economic and Cultural Basis for a Federated State_ likha, jismein unhone tarka diya ki mahadweep aarthik roop se viksit aur aupniveshik gulami se poori aazadi tabhi haasil kar sakta hai agar woh ek akeli political entity ban jaye. Ek abhyas-karta (practitioner) bhi, pachees (twenty five) saalon se zyada ke dauran unhone teen political parties ki sthapna ki jo Senegal mein mukhya opposition bani.

## Intellectuals (BUDDHIMAN VAICHARIK)

India ke do sabse bade modern intellectuals ittefaq se baap aur betey the — dono ko D.D. Kosambi ke naam se jaana jata hai. Baap (Dharmananda) early-twentieth-century ke mathematician, Buddhist philosopher, Pali linguistics ke expert aur ek pramukh Marxist historian the. Shayad barabar, agar zyada nahi, toh utne hi prabhavshali unke betey, Damodar the, jinhone mathematics, language aur history mein bhi mahaarat haasil ki, lekin Buddhist philosophy par focus karne ke bajaye archaeology aur coins ke adhyayan (numismatics) mein interest rakha. Harvard mein padhai karne ke baad, Damodar Kosambi India wapas laute aur Banaras Hindu University, Aligarh Muslim University, Fergusson College Pune aur Tata Institute of Fundamental Research jaise pratishthit sansthaon mein mathematics padhaya. Unhone kai papers publish kiye aur ek world-class statistician aur geometrician bane, Chicago aur Princeton Universities mein lectures diye aur John von Neumann aur J. Robert Oppenheimer samet us samay ke top scientists ke sath sampark (liaison) banaye rakha.

Unhone apne badhte hue Marxist vicharon ke kaaran Indian aur American political aur corporate establishments ke sath matbhed (falling out) ka samna kiya, isliye unhone doosre academic interests par focus karne ka faisla kiya. Kosambi pehle se hi ek vilakshan polyglot (kai bhashayein bolne wale) the jinhone English, French, Italian aur German mein works publish kiye the aur kai South Asian bhashaon mein nipun (proficient) the. Sanskrit par unke focus ne Ancient Indian history ke adhyayan mein mahatvapurna safalta (breakthroughs) dilayi, aur unki kitaab _Culture and Civilisation in Ancient India_ ko Indian literature ka ek classic maana jata hai. Unhone baad mein numismatics par bhi ek mahatvapurna kaam publish kiya.

Kosambis dono Intellectual Polymath ke classic cases the — ek aisa vyakti jiska gyaan, vichar aur ideas na sirf kai alag-alag fields tak phaelte hain, balki iske alawa unmein se har ek mein mahatvapurna bauddhika yogdan (intellectual contributions) bhi dete hain. Woh generalists ke bajaye ‘multispecialists’ hain, jo ek zindagi ke dauran ya toh ek sath ya ek ke baad ek apne fields mein mahaarat haasil karte hain. Woh gyaan ke khoj-karta (discoverers), utpadak (producers) aur phelane wale (disseminators) hain aur paribhasha ke anusar kisi diye gaye vishay ya vishayon ke sandarbh mein kaam karne, padhne, chintan karne, kalpana karne ya sawaal poochhne aur jawab dene ke liye apni buddhi ka upayog karte hain. Intellectual, beshak, swabhavik roop se ek aisa vicharak hai jo — jaan-boojhkar ya unjaane mein — apne uddeshyeon ko haasil karne ke liye kriya (action) ke upar vichar (thought) ko prathmikta deta hai; aamtaur par kisi na kisi vidwatta-purna yogdan (scholarly contribution) ke zariya. Aamtaur par, woh philosophers, historians, scientists, social scientists, essayists, yahan tak ki novelists ke roop mein aate hain. Woh aamtaur par vishwa itihaas mein bade vicharon ke srot aur vikas-karta hote hain. Aur unmein se sabse behtareen aseemit vicharak (boundless thinkers) hote hain. Unka gyaan, vichar aur ideas na sirf kai alag-alag fields tak phaelte hain, balki woh iske alawa unmein se har ek mein mahatvapurna bauddhika yogdan bhi dete hain.

Polymathic intellectual ke liye sabse aam roop ek philosopher ka hai. Philosophy aaj allt-alag hisson mein baanti gayi hai (logic, metaphysics, ethics, wagerah) taaki uske andar specialists banaye ja sakein, lekin yeh ek sarv-samaveshi (all-encompassing), aseemit jaanch ke tareeqe ke sath-sath ek samagra (holistic), sarv-gun-sampanna vishwa-drishti ke roop mein shuru hui thi. Paramparik, premodern philosophers ne ek interdisciplinary, synthesising approach apnaya, apni philosophical world views ko behtar tareeqe se rachne ke liye kai tarah ke vishayon par sawaal uthaye.

Bertrand Russell dwara pehle sachi scholar-philosopher ke roop mein varnit, Aristotle ne Plato ki Academy mein shiksha paayi, aur baad mein unhe Macedon ki royal academy ke mukhya ke roop mein niyukt kiya gaya, jahan woh teen bhavishya ke rajaon ke vyaktigat tutor bane: Alexander the Great, Ptolemy aur Cassander. Athens mein 335 se 323 bce ke is daur ke dauran hi Aristotle ke baare mein maana jata hai ki unhone shayad sabse vividh bauddhika sahitya (intellectual oeuvre) paida kiya jise duniya ne kabhi dekha hai. Unki jaanch ki seema ashadharan thi. Physical sciences mein, unhone anatomy, astronomy, embryology, geography, geology, meteorology, physics aur zoology par adhyayan kiya aur observations likhe. Philosophy mein, unhone aesthetics, ethics, government, metaphysics, politics, economics, psychology, rhetoric aur theology par likha. Unhone education, videshi reeti-riwaaz, literature aur poetry par bhi adhyayan kiya aur mahatvapurna observations kiye. Unke sanyojit karya (combined works) Greek gyaan ka ek virtual encyclopaedia banate hain: unhone manav gyaan ke us samay astitva mein maujood lagbhag har kshetra mein yogdan diya, aur woh kai naye kshetron ke sansthapak (founder) the. British historian Brian Magee ne unke baare mein kaha tha ki ‘yeh sandeh-aspad hai ki kya kisi insaan ne kabhi utna jaana jitna unhone jaana’ aur Fernández-Armesto ne kaha ki woh ‘un kshetron ki mahaarat mein anokhe roop se seema-langhan karne wale (transgressive) the jinhe hum ab alag kshetra maante hain — aur unmein se kisi mein bhi unke prabhav ko shayad hi kisi ne paar kiya ho.’ Woh shayad intellectual polymath ke sabse shuruati udaharan the, ek aisa vyakti jisne gyaan ki gehrai aur vyapakta ko ek aise tareeqe se samanjasya mein laya jise dobara shayad hi dekha jayega.

Jabki polymathic philosopher sabse pehle Greeks dwara prateekit (typified) hua tha (Aristotle archetype the), 10th se 13th century ke Islamic philosophers ne polymathic enquiry ki apni parampara viksit ki. Ibn Sina (Latinised Avicenna), West mein sabse prasiddh Islamic philosophers mein se ek, ne 10 saal ki umar tak Quran ko yaad kar liya tha aur 21 saal ki umar tak Islamic learning ki saari shakhaon mein mahaarat haasil kar li thi. Samanid Emperor ke shahi chikitsak (court physician) ke roop mein, unhe ek shaandar library tak pahonch di gayi jisne unhe kai doosre kshetron mein apne adhyayan ko aage badhane mein saksham banaya. Iske baad jo bauddhika safar (intellectual odyssey) hua woh _Kitab al-Shifa_ mein parinath (culminated) hua, jo philosophy aur science ke kai tatvon ko samete hue gyaan ka ek sangrah (compendium) tha, jinmein logic, geometry, arithmetic, astronomy, music aur metaphysics shamil hain. Aise kisi bhi ek vyakti dwara banaye gaye is prakar ke karya mein ise kai logon dwara sabse bada aur sabse vividh karya maana jata hai.

Ibn Rushd (Latinised Averroes) ko Ibn Sina ka bauddhika uttaradhikari (intellectual successor) maana jata hai. Unhone duniya bhar mein prasiddh Cordoba University mein padhai ki, ek sath theology, law aur medicine ka adhyayan kiya. Unhe Cordoba mein mukhya nyayadhish (chief judge) niyukt kiya gaya aur samanaantar roop se Almohad prince Abu Ya’qub Yusuf ke shahi chikitsak ke roop mein seva ki, jahan unhone medicine mein ek zabardast vidwatta-purna yogdan diya, _al Kulliyat fi al Tibb_ (Generalities in Medicine), ek medical encyclopaedia. Court mein, Ibn Rushd ne khud ko ek anokhi sthiti mein paaya — Ibn Sina, Ibn Tufayl aur unse pehle ke doosron ki tarah — vyapak philosophical enquiry mein apni ruchi ko aage badhane mein saksham hone ki. Aristotelian philosophy ke vidwan, Greek philosophy ki al Ghazali ki pehle ki aalochana par unke jawab ko Islamic philosophy ke itihaas mein sabse mahatvapurna kaamon mein se ek ke roop mein saraha gaya. Nasir al-Din al-Tusi, 12th-century ke Persian ayatollah, ne theology, philosophy aur unke samay ke jaane-maane natural sciences ki lagbhag sabhi shakhaon mein krantikari yogdan diya; sabse vishesh roop se mathematics (geometry), chemistry (conversion of mass), biology (evolution) aur astronomy (planetary motion).

Kuch philosophical polymaths ko cultural critics bhi maana ja sakta hai. John Ruskin ne architecture par kai kitaabein likhin, Oxford mein poetry ke liye pratishthit Newdigate Prize jeeta, geology aur botany par papers likhe, Adam Smith ki _Wealth of Nations_ ki aalochana likhi, aur apni peedhi ke pramukh art critic aur historian bane — unka multi-volume masterpiece _Modern Painters_ aaj bhi modern times ke art criticism ke sabse behtareen kaamon mein se ek maana jata hai. Woh apne aap mein ek visual artist aur political thinker bhi the jinka art aur political dono halkon mein zabardast prabhav tha. Barabar kadd ke Ceylonese art historian Ananda Coomaraswamy the, jo Western artists ke dhyan mein Indian art ko lane wale pehle vyakti the. Coomaraswamy ek philosopher aur metaphysician bhi the jinhone ‘perennial philosophy’ ka samarthan kiya aur Traditionalist School ke founding fathers mein se ek bane. Ek mineralogist ke roop mein woh Ceylon ke Geological Survey mein shamil the. Woh Bengal cultural renaissance ke sath-sath swadeshi aazadi andolan ke ek pramukh sadasya the, ek asli polyglot jo Latin, Hindi, Icelandic, French, Pali, Greek, English, Sanskrit, Persian aur Chinese jaisi vividh bhashaon ko jaante the.

Intellectual polymaths ya toh aise scholars hote hain jo kai aas-paas se na jude vishayon mein shreshthta haasil karte hain (multidisciplinary), ya aise thinkers hote hain jo gyaan ke aapatat: alag-alag kshetron ko ek sath sanshleshit (synthesise) karte hain taaki unmein se ek ya adhik mein gambhir yogdan de sakein (interdisciplinary). Pehle udaharan ka ek achha udaharan American writer Isaac Asimov hain, jinhone aise books publish kiye jo Dewey Decimal library system ki sabhi 10 mukhya shreniyon (categories) mein aate the. Woh apni popular science books aur behadd aadaraniya science fiction novels ke liye sabse zyada jaane jaate hain. Unhone sciences ke sabhi kshetron (chemistry, mathematics, physics, astronomy, biology, computers, anatomy) par aur theology, literature, limericks aur DIY jaise vyapak vishayon par kitaabein likhin. Kul milakar unhone 500 se zyada kitaabein aur chhoti kahaniyan likhin, jinmein se kai unki mrityu ke baad publish hui.

Interdisciplinary scholars alag-alag kshetron se achhi tarah se parichit hote hain (yeh samajhte hue ki woh puzzle mein kaise fit baithte hain) balki har ek ki samajh mein vishisht yogdan dene ke bajaye. Yaani, woh prasangikaran (contextualise) karne ke liye sanshleshan (synthesise) karte hain, aur iska ulta bhi. Sabse behtareen social scientists mein se kuch ne, udaharan ke liye, gyaan ki kai dhararaon ko sanshleshit karne ki ek ashadharan kshamta ka pradarshan kiya hai. Karl Marx ne ‘capitalism ke kanoon’ (vishesh roop se _Das Kapital_ mein vishleshit) ko rachne ke liye economics, politics, sociology, history, literature aur psychology ke apne spasht roop se vyapak gyaan ka sahara liya; ek aisi parikalpana (hypothesis) jiska poori 20th century mein vishwa rajneeti par ek shaktishali prabhav padega. Marxist scholar David Harvey ke anusar: ‘Marx ke liye, naya gyaan radical roop se alag vaicharik khandon (conceptual blocs) ko lene, unhe ek sath ragadne aur ek krantikari aag banane se paida hota hai.’ Unke saaf taur par ideologically rival Adam Smith ke paas bhi physics, astronomy, law, history aur metaphysics (unhone har ek par mahatvapurna essays likhe) samet kai vishayon ka achha gyaan tha, jin sabhi ko unhone apni behadd prabhavshali aarthik philosophy ko rachan mein milaya jaisa ki _The Wealth of Nations_ mein vyakt kiya gaya hai. Astronomy jaise kuch prabhav turant saaf nahi lag sakte hain, lekin isne unki soch ko vaigyanik prasang (scientific context) aur dhancha zaroor pradan kiya hoga.

Jo log peshe ke roop mein lekhan (writing) ko apnate hain, unpar un vicharkon ki tulna mein kam paabandiyan hoti hain jo dharmik aur shaikshanik sansthaon se bandhe hote hain (halanki kai mamlon mein woh bhi apne publishers ki daya par hote hain). Bahumukhi lekhakon (versatile writers) ke paas alag-alag kshetron ko explore karne ki aazadi hoti hai, kabhi-kabhi alag-alag chhadm-naamon (pseudonyms) ke tehat. Modern era se pehle yeh aamtaur par nibandhkaros (essayists) ke roop mein aaye hain. European Renaissance ke dauran writer Giovanni Pico della Mirandola ki ek vidwan scholar ke roop mein pratishtha thi, jinki duniya mein maujood saare gyaan ko haasil karne aur milane ki khoj ne unhe Renaissance Humanism ki philosophy ke founding fathers mein se ek banaya — jiska charam unke krantikari essay _Oration on the Dignity of Man_ mein hua. English Renaissance writer Thomas Browne ne medicine, religion, philosophy, botany, archaeology, falconry, music aur mathematics jaise vividh vishayon par swatantra aur adhikarik roop se likha aur France ke unke samkalin Michel de Montaigne ne apne prasiddh essays ke sangrah ke zariya isi tarah ki bahumukhta aur vidwatta ka pradarshan kiya.

Fiction ke lekhak aksar ek behadd vyapak gyaan pradarshit karte hain jo unke dwara batayi jaane wali kahaniyon ke nirmaan ke liye aavashyak ho jata hai. Russian writer Leo Tolstoy ke apne novels mein (khaas kar _War and Peace_ mein) religion, history, warfare, politics, art, literature, economics, agriculture, society aur philosophy ke kushal sanshleshan (synthesis) ne unhe ab tak ke sabse prasiddh lekhakon mein se ek bana diya hai. Saahityik duniya mein aise hi doosre synthesisers mein Englishman Aldous Huxley aur Italian Umberto Eco shamil hain jinhone apne saahityik kaamon ke zariya kai vishayon ki ek ashadharan samajh ka pradarshan kiya hai, halanki unmein se kuch ne, Asimov ki tarah, apne aap mein kai kshetron mein apna yogdan diya hai.

Sabse behtareen ‘duniya ke itihaas’ un polymathic historians dwara likhe jaane ki pravritti rakhte hain jo alag-alag kshetron ko kamyabi ke sath curate karte hain — chahe woh bhaugolik, sanskritik, aarthik ya vaigyanik hon. 10th-century ke Arab historian Al-Mas’udi ne, udaharan ke liye, _The Meadows of Gold and Mines of Gems_ likha — vishwa itihaas ka ek kaalanukramik (chronological) hisaab jisne kai Islamic sciences ko milaya. 13th-century ke Tunisian historian Ibn Khaldun ne ab tak ke aitihasik sanshleshan (historical synthesis) ke sabse behtareen kaamon mein se ek ka nirmaan kiya — _Muqaddimah_. Mool roop se manav sabhyataon ke utthan aur patan ke kai pehluon par ek vishwakosh-samaan (encyclopaedic) thesis, isne philosophy, history, theology, economics, political theory, sociology, natural science aur geography ke mukhya tatvon ko kushalata se shamil kiya aur is prakriya mein ek naye shaikshanik vishay — social science — ka nirmaan kiya. Pulitzer Prize-winning historian Will Durant ka uddeshya history ke alag-alag pehluon ko ekrat karna tha, jise unhone mahsoos kiya ki 20th century tak yeh tezi se tukdon mein baant gaya tha. Yeh unke multi-volume historical masterpiece, _The Story of Civilization_ mein parinath hua, jismein woh ‘cheezon ko poore roop mein dekhne, samay mein itihaas ke zariya drishtikon, ekta aur samajh ko paane ki koshish karte hain. . .’ Jacob Bronowski ne apne prasiddh book aur TV documentary series ke zariya manav ke aitihasik vikas (‘Ascent of Man’) ko samjhane ke liye mathematics, physics, biology, philosophy aur literature ke apne pramanit gyaan ko ek sath laya.

## Educators (SHIKSHAK / SHIKSHA-VID)

47 bce mein Julius Caesar ne Marcus Terentius Varro ko — jinhe Quintilian dwara prasiddh roop se ‘Romans mein sabse zyada vidwan’ (the most learned of the Romans) ke roop mein quote kiya gaya tha — Rome ki public library ki dekh-rekh ke liye niyukt kiya. Isse pehle, Varro ne Rome aur Athens mein padhai ki aur Pompeii mein ek sainik aur rajneetijna ke roop mein seva ki. Unki _Nine Books of Disciplines_ baad ke encyclopaedists ke liye ek model bani aur organizing principles ke roop mein liberal arts ke unke upayog ke liye exemplary (udaharan-swaroop) thi, jinhe unhone grammar, rhetoric, logic, arithmetic, geometry, astronomy, musical theory, medicine aur architecture ke roop mein pehchana. Baad ke lekhakon ne Varro ki list ka upayog 'medieval schools ke saat classical liberal arts' ko define karne ke liye kiya.

Sirf ek mamooli generalist hone se santusht na hokar, Varro Roman scholarship mein apna khud ka yogdan dene ke liye utsuk the. Unhone aakhirkar 74 Latin works produce kiye jo gyaan ki ek behadd vyapak chaudai (breadth) ko cover karte the, jinmein se kai ne science, linguistics aur history mein ashadharan yogdan diya. Unka _Varronian Chronology_ ka sankalan (compilation) kaalanukramik itihaas (chronological history) mein ek mukhya kamyabi thi, jismein 700 se zyada ullekhniya Greeks aur Romans ki chhoti jeevniyan (biographies) shamil thin. _De Re Rustica_ mein, jo krishi (agriculture) par ek encyclopaedic book thi, Varro ne kalpana ki ki sthir paani (stagnant water) se hone wala sankraman (contagion) kisi vyakti ki health ko affect kar sakta hai — ek aisa siddhant jise baad mein sixteenth-century ke scientist Girolamo Fracastoro ko shrey diya gaya aur unke dwara vaigyanik roop se pramanit kiya gaya. _De lingua Latina_ (On the Latin Language) na sirf ek bhashayi karya ke roop mein balki kai tarah ke vishayon par moolyavan prasangik jaankari (incidental information) ke srot ke roop mein bhi prabhavshali hai.

Varro se pehle, praacheen duniya ke bade educators sant (sages) the jinhone apna gyaan anubhavatmak (experiential) aur adhyatmik/m metaphysical dono sroton se haasil kiya tha. Unmein se kai ke paas ek aam samajh (general wisdom) thi, jo teaching ke kai kshetron mein khud ko laagu karti thi. Confucius, udaharan ke liye, history, poetry, government, propriety, mathematics, music, divination aur sports ke ek encyclopaedic teacher the — jo khud Zhou Dynasty ke dauran 'Socrates ke andaaz mein, ek akele insaan ki university' (a one-man university) the. Is tarah woh rozmarra ki zindagi se jude kai praktikal maamlon par gyaan ke vishwasniya srot bane. Thiruvalluvar, praacheen India ke Tamil kavi, ne _Thirukural_ likha, jo naitik aur praktikal maxims ka ek prabhavshali set tha jismein Virtue (parivaar ke maamle, aatm-niyantran, vafadari), Wealth (rogar, mardangi, seekhna) aur Love (pyaar-jaatana, khoobsurati, antangta) ke maamle shamil the. Isi tarah, Greek philosopher Epicurus ne praktikal zindagi ke sabhi pehluon ke baare mein 'self-help' salah ke 300 karya likhe jinmein unka _On Love_, _On Justice_, _On Nature_ aur _On Human Life_ shamil hain.

Jaise hi praacheen itihaas mein bade santon ke hothon se gyaan (wisdom) nikla, gyaan (tathya-atmak aur anya) chhut-put roop se viksit aur phaelna shuru hua. Duniya bhar mein educators ka ek naya samooh, Varro ki tarah, phir encyclopaedias ke roop mein is gyaan ko ik इकट्ठा aur organize karne laga. Jaanne, sankalit karne aur share karne ke gun ke kaaran, yeh encyclopaedists paramparik roop se itihaas ke sabse prabhavshali educators mein se kuch rahe hain. Unki vidwatta ki prakriti aur gyaan ki sheere range is baat ki pushti karti hai ki sabse behtareen encyclopaedists vaastav mein polymaths the. Woh logon, jagahon, ghatnaon, vastuon aur doosri ghatnaon par vishisht roop se vyapak vividhta ke entries ko compile, edit (aur kabhi-kabhi khud yogdan) karte the.

Kai doosron ne is parampara ka palan kiya. Roman encyclopaedist Aulus Cornelius Celsus ne ek vyapak encyclopaedia compile kiya jismein Roman gyaan ke sabhi mukhya pehlu shamil the, jismein medicine, agriculture, warfare aur law par entries thin. Sirf ek volume, _De Medicina_, hum tak pahonch paaya hai, lekin Celsus ke samkalinon ki tippaniyan (commentaries) sujhati hain ki unhe us samay ke Roman jeevan se jude sabhi doosre maamlon ka gyaan tha. Tang Dynasty ke bureaucrat Du You ne _Tongdian_ ko compile karne mein 36 saal bitaye — ek 200-volume ka encyclopaedia jismein saara jaana-maana gyaan shamil tha, jise Food and commodity, Examination and advancement, Government offices, Rites, Music, Military, Penal law, Local administration aur Border defence mein baanta gaya tha.

Photius, Constantinople mein Eastern Orthodox Church ke 9th-century ke shaktishali patriarch, _Biblioteca_ ke apne sankalan ke liye sabse zyada jaane jaate the — jo 280 volumes ke classical authors ke uddharan (extracts) aur sankshiptikaran (abridgements) ka ek mahaan roop se vyapak sangrah tha. French polymath Vincent of Beauvais ne _Speculum Maius_ (ya ‘The Great Mirror’) banaya; jo Middle Ages mein sabse zyada padha jaane wala encyclopaedia tha. Yeh teen hisson mein Middle Ages ke saare gyaan ka ek compendium tha: _Speculum Naturale_ (natural sciences), _Speculum Doctrinale_ (practical knowledge) aur _Speculum Historiale_ (jaani-maani duniya ka itihaas).

Sung dynasty ke Chinese statesman aur encyclopaedist Li Fang ne Middle Ages ke doosre sabse zyada padhe jaane wale encyclopaedias ko compile kiya: _Four Great Books of Song_. Encyclopaedia ki parampara Middle Ages mein European Church scholarship ke andar vishesh roop se prachalit thi. Early Middle Ages ke sabse bade scholars mein se ek Saint Isidore of Seville ko is daur ke pehle jaane-maane encyclopaedia, _Etymologiae_ (lagbhag 630) ke lekhak ke roop mein vyapak roop se maanyata prapt hai, jismein unhone apne samay mein uplabdh sikhne ke ek bade hisse ko compile kiya, praacheen aur aadhunik dono. Yeh medieval libraries mein sabse popular compendium tha aur 1470 aur 1530 ke beech kam se kam 10 editions mein print hua tha, jo Renaissance mein bhi Isidore ki jaari popularity ko dikhata hai.

Islam ke pehle kuch sadiyon mein gyaan ke itne bade utpadan ke sath — praacheen Greek learning ke sanrakshan (preservation) ka zikr na bhi karein toh — iska zyadatar sankalan, sangathan (organisation) aur prasaar (dissemination) polymaths dwara kiya gaya tha. Egyptian Mamluk Al Nuwaiyri ne 9,000-page, 30-volume ka encyclopaedia compile kiya, jiska haal hi mein English mein _The Ultimate Ambition in the Arts of Erudition_ ke roop mein anuvaad kiya gaya hai. Isne 14th-century ke ek saahityakar (litterateur) ke drishtikon se jaani-maani duniya ko catalogue kiya. Entries medieval chaand-pooja karne wale cults, sexual aphrodisiacs aur baadlon ke tatva se lekar bhains ke doodh ke cheese aur flamingos ke ghosla banane ki aadatton tak pheli thin. Isi tarah, ek sadi baad, Egyptian scholar Al Siyuti ne medicine, language, law aur Islamic theology jaise vividh vishayon par lagbhag 500 kitaabein likhin, jo mool roop se 15th-century Cairo mein Islamic gyaan ki vividh shakhaon ko compile aur organize karti thin.

Sixteenth century mein, German Calvinist cleric Johann Heinrich Alsted ne do dashakon mein khud ek 7-volume ka encyclopaedia compile kiya. Aur phir French Enlightenment ke dauran, philosopher Denis Diderot ab tak ke sabse prasiddh encyclopaedias mein se ek, _Encyclopédie_ ke editor-in-chief the. Diderot ka apne masterpiece ke zariya uddeshya _pour changer la façon commune de penser_ tha, yaani manav gyaan ki ek badi matra ke sanshleshan (synthesis) ke zariya 'aam soch ke chehre ko badalna'. _Encyclopédie_ ko ab French Enlightenment philosophy ka ek mukhya pratinidhitva maana jata hai, aur Diderot ko khud parinaamswarup us daur ke mahatvapurna philosophers mein se ek ke roop mein pooja jata hai.

Lekin 1800s tak, uplabdh gyaan mein ghatatmak badhotari (exponential increase) ne vyaktigat encyclopaedists ki kshamtaon ko peeche chhod diya tha. _Encyclopaedia Britannica_ ke 1805 ke supplement ne swikar kiya ki 'koi bhi vyakti, chahe uski pratibha kitni bhi hairangez kyun na ho aur uska abhyas kitna bhi teevra kyun na ho, kabhi bhi ek chalta-phirta encyclopaedia (walking encyclopaedia) banne ki yukti-sangat umeed nahi kar sakta.' Aaj Wikipedia jaisi digitally communal encyclopaedias ke aagaman ne is karya ke liye vyaktigat polymaths par nirbharta ko visthapit (displace) kar diya hai. Lekin poore itihaas mein duniya bhar mein unka astitva hamesha gyaan ke sankalan aur prasaar mein mahatvapurna raha hai.

Jabki encyclopaedists is arth mein educators the ki unhone gyaan ko compile aur codify kiya, teachers aur professors is arth mein educators the ki woh (jabki kuch mamlon mein gyaan mein khud yogdan dete hue) academies, universities aur padhai ki doosri jagahon ke zariya vyapak duniya tak gyaan ke prabhavshali le-jaane-wale (deliverers) aur prasaarit karne wale (transmitters) the. Jaise encyclopaedias ne kitaabon ke roop mein kiya tha, academies aur universities ne jald hi gyaan aur uske teachers ko ek doosre se saaf taur par alag, khandon mein baante gaye vishayon (compartmentalised subjects) mein organize karna shuru kar diya. Lekin pehle ke kai academic educators ne vishayon ki ek vyapak range padhayi. Plato ne, apni academy mein, udaharan ke liye, philosophical enquiry ke ek sarvabhoomik (universal) tareeqe ka upayog kiya jisne unhe vishayon ki ek aisi range padhane (ya apne students ke sath jaanch karne) ke liye प्रेरित kiya jise aaj poori tarah se asambandhit (unrelated) maana jayega. Aur baad mein Romans ke tehat, Hypatia ne Alexandria mein Neoplatonic school ka netritva kiya jahan unhone mathematics, astronomy aur philosophy padhaya aur har ek par ek pramukh adhikari (leading authority) maani jaati thin.

Jabki pre-Renaissance teaching ka zyadatar kaam dharmathan (religious establishments) jaise ki mathon (monasteries) ke zariya kiya jata tha, samarpit universities duniya ke doosre hisson mein maujood rahin, jinmein West Africa bhi shamil hai. Yahan, Songhai Empire ne Mande dynasty ka sthaan liya aur 15th century tak vistarit hokar ab tak ke sabse bade Muslim empires mein se ek ban gaya — jo apne charam par 1.4 million square kilometres se zyada ko cover karta tha. Iske sabse pramukh intellectuals mein se ek, Ahmad Baba al Timbukti — law, theology, grammar aur anthropology ke vidwan — 32 saal tak duniya bhar mein prasiddh Sankore University ke mukhya the. Woh prasiddh roop se lagbhag 1,600 kitaabon ki ek vyaktigat library rakhne ke liye jaane jaate the — pre-Gutenberg Europe ke poore hisse mein published kitaabon ki kul matra shayad kam thi, yeh dekhte hue ek adbhut kaarnama. Is samay ke unke kuch saathi scholars ki tulna mein ise ‘ek namra sangrah’ (modest collection) maana jata tha. Adbhut roop se Timbuktu scholarship ke is daur ke dauran ik इकट्ठा ki gayi lagbhag 700,000 kitaabein bachi hui hain.

Eighteenth century mein, Maria Gaetana Agnesi kisi university mein pehli mahila professor banin. Unhe Pope Benedict XIV dwara pratishthit University of Bologna mein mathematics, natural philosophy aur physics ki chair par niyukt kiya gaya tha. Agnesi ek mathematician, physicist, philosopher, theologian aur polyglot thin jinhe kam umar mein hi Italian, French, Greek, Hebrew, Spanish, German aur Latin seekhne ki unki kshamta ke liye ek vilakshan pratibha (prodigy) ke roop mein maanyata mili thi. Unhone _Propositiones Philosophicae_ publish kiya, jo philosophy aur natural science par essays ki ek shrinkhla thi.

Eighteenth century ke baad Europe mein modern university ke uday ke sath, dharmanirpeksh (secular) academic thinkers modern educator ke adarsh udaharanon ke roop mein ubharne lage. Nineteenth-century ke scholar William Whewell modern Europe mein pehle poori tarah se ‘academic’ polymaths mein se ek the. Cambridge University mein sthit (Master of Trinity College ke roop mein), unhone theology, kai sciences, history, economics, law, architecture, education aur philosophy mein mahatvapurna yogdan diya. Early twentieth-century Britain mein ‘polymathic professor’ ko phir Bertrand Russell dwara prateekit kiya gaya, jinake politics, history, language, mathematics aur religion (mukhya roop se Christianity) ke sath-sath philosophy ke andar ki kai shakhaon ke gyaan ne — jise unke magnum opus _A History of Western Philosophy_ dwara sabse behtareen dhang se pradarshit kiya gaya — modern history mein pramukh sarv-gun-sampanna, vyapak philosophers mein se ek ke roop mein unki sthiti ko pakka kiya.

America mein, jisne Britain ke samaan hi ek uchha shiksha pranali viksit ki, Charles Sanders Peirce, mathematics, chemistry, meteorology, economics, logic, history aur linguistics ke professor aur philosopher, ko unke saathiyon dwara ‘American philosophers mein sabse original aur bahumukhi’ kaha gaya tha. Woh polymathic philosophers mein se aakhri logon mein se ek the, kyunki philosophy khud tab se ek vishay ke roop mein tezi se tukdon mein baant gayi thi aur specialised ho gayi thi. Machine computers ke uday ke sath, interdisciplinary thinking aur teaching social aur technological sciences ka kshetra ban gaya. Is context mein, Herbert Simon ne political science, economics, sociology, cognitive psychology aur computer science jaise vividh vishayon mein kai academic professorships sambhaalin. Unhone apne aap mein in vishayon mein se har ek mein ullekhniya yogdan diya. Woh political science ke professor the, unhone cognitive decision-making par ek krantikari granth (treatise) likha, artificial intelligence ke adhyayan mein ek pioneer bane, library science aur pedagogy mein yogdan diya aur 1978 mein Economics ke liye Nobel Prize jeeta.

## Mystics (SANT / RAHASYAWADI)

Rudolf Steiner ek philosopher aur scientist the jo Goethian Archives mein natural science ke editor bane. Unka sabse bada yogdan science, philosophy aur spirituality ka unka adbhut sanshleshan (synthesis) tha. Unhone apne vicharon ko — jinhe anthroposophy ke roop mein jaana gaya — life ke kai kshetron mein laagu kiya.

Unhone organic agriculture ki ek pranali (biodynamic) ki sthapna ki jo aaj organic farming ke abhyas mein vyapak ho gayi hai. Medicine mein unke kaam ne complementary medications aur supportive artistic aur biographic therapies ki ek vyapak range ke vikas ko janm diya. Ek architect ke roop mein unhone 17 buildings design kiye, jinmein se do ko architectural masterpieces maana jata hai. Woh ek painter bhi the jinke kaam ne Joseph Beuys aur doosre modern artists ko prabhavit kiya, aur ek naatak-kar (dramatist) the jinhone chaar mahatvapurna naatak likhe aur ek aisi acting system banayi jise prasiddh Russian director Mikhail Chekhov ko prabhavit karne ke liye jaana jata hai.

Anthroposophical ideas ko phelane ke liye, unhone Waldorf Schools ki sthapna ki — bachhon ko ek zyada samagra (holistic) tareeqe se shikshit karne ke liye design kiya gaya schools ka ek network. Woh interwar period ke dauran ek pramukh rajneetik karyakarta (political activist) bane (unhone _Toward Social Renewal_ likha, jo ek international bestseller bana) aur unke badhte hue samajik prabhav ne unhe Hitler ke sath takraav mein daal diya, jisne unhe (asafal roop se) badnam karne ki koshish ki. Steiner ki polymathy unki posthumous legacy (mrityu ke baad ki virasat) mein aur zyada pradarshit hoti hai. Art, architecture, agriculture, education, philosophy aur medicine ke alawa, unke vicharon ne banking aur politics jaise kshetron ko prabhavit karna jaari rakha hai.

Steiner duniya par ek bahu-aayami (multidimensional) prabhav daalne wale akele mystic se bahut door hain. Jonathan Black ki unki krantikari _Secret History of the World_ mein thesis yeh hai ki ek nishchit esoteric knowledge (guhya gyaan) itihaas ke kai sabse bade dimaagon ko baandhta hai. Interestingly, unke dwara mystics ke roop mein rekhankit kiye gaye kai log — Muhammad, da Vinci, Bernini, Carroll, Cicero, Voltaire, Copernicus, Franklin, Edison, Bacon, Goethe, Imhotep, Leibniz, Newton, Pythagoras aur Tolstoy — har ek ne kai (aapatat: asambandhit) kshetron mein shreshthta haasil ki aur aisa karke, itihaas banaya. Woh polymaths the. Sabse bade metaphysicians aur spiritual leaders mein se kuch — divine reality ko behtar tareeqe se samajhne ke liye gyaan ke vividh roopon par poochhtachh karne, unhe jodne aur sawaal uthane ki zaroorat se inspired — sabse zyada polymathic dimaag rakhne ki pravritti rakhte the.

Vishwa itihaas ke sabse prabhavshali mystics mein se kuch woh rahe hain jinhone physical world ko utni hi gehrai se samjha jitna ki metaphysical ko. Unhone theology ko utna hi aage badhaya jitna ki philosophy ko. Mystics life aur thought ke prati ek samagra (holistic) drishtikon rakhne ki pravritti rakhte hain, duniya ko ek doosre se juda hua aur ek hi srot se nikalta hua dekhte hain. Woh doosre (alien) kshetron ki khoj ko ullanghan (transgressions) nahi maante. Woh har cheez mein ek ekta (unity) dekhte hain.

Manavta ki pehli mukhya spiritual traditions mein se ek, Hinduism, beshak iska ek udaharan hai. Yeh Abhinavagupta mein prakat hota hai, 10th-century ke Indian mystic jinhone naatak, bhajan aur kavitaayein likhin, sangeet racha aur aestheticism, yoga aur theology par likha. Unhone aakhirkar Shaivism ki Hindu philosophy ka ek encyclopaedia (_Tantra̅lok_) banane ke liye apne works ko ek sath laya. Is drishtikon ne, Rabindranath Tagore ke anusar, 'jeeving soch ki us gehrai mein pravesh kiya jahan manav gyaan ki vividh dharaayein ek prakashman sanshleshan (luminous synthesis) mein milti hain.' 16th-century ke Assamese spiritual philosopher aur sant jinhe Mahapurush (Great One) Srimanta Sankardev ke roop mein jaana jata hai, ne Assamese logon ko bhayanak baad (severe flooding) se bachane ke liye ek dam (jo aaj bhi maujood hai) banaya. Lekin woh Tansen ke genre mein ek musician ke roop mein Assamese culture ko badalnes ke liye zyada jaane jaate hain jo 'apne raga-songs ke vibration se pedon se patte gira sakte the.' Ek kavi ke roop mein woh apne samay ke Kalidasa ke roop mein jaane jaate the, ek naatak-kar aur playwright jo open-air theatre ke pioneers mein se ek bane. Visual arts mein unka yogdan shaandar _Vrindavani Vastra_ tapestry shamil karta hai. 1920s ke Paris mein ek singer, musician, painter aur dancer ke roop mein khud ko sthapit karne ke baad, Frenchman Alain Daniélou duniya ke sabse bade sarv-gun-sampanna Western Indologists mein se ek banenge jinhone _Kama Sutra_ ka anuvaad kiya, _History of India_ likha, Tagore dwara unke music school ke director ke roop mein niyukt kiye gaye, West mein Indian classical music ko popular banaya aur, Yoga aur Shaivite philosophy par kai kitaabon ke gun dwara, Hindu tradition mein ek prasiddh spiritual authority bane.

Buddhism ke paas bhi prabhavshali polymathic mystics ka apna hissa hai. Zanabazar, Genghis Khan ke ek vanshaj, ek statesman aur Buddhist priest the jinhone Mongolia par Qing sovereignty ka samarthan kiya. Unhe West mein ‘Mongolian Michelangelo’ ke roop mein jaana jata hai, na sirf isliye kyunki woh desh ke sabse prasiddh sculptors mein se ek the (unke gilded bronze Buddha statues national treasures maane jaate hain) balki isliye bhi kyunki unka ilaqe ke art, architecture aur music par zabardast prabhav tha (unhone kai devotional songs rache). Ek scholar ke roop mein, unhone medicine, astronomy aur philology par vyapak roop se likha lekin Mongolian Buddhism ke apne reforms aur prasaar ke liye ek spiritualist ke roop mein sabse zyada jaane jaate hain. 19th-century ke Tibetan monk Jamgön Ju Mipham, jinhe Mahapandita (great man) kaha jata hai, 10 sciences mein mahaarat haasil karne ke liye prasiddh hain: arts aur crafts (bzo), health science (gso ba), language (sgra), logico-epistemology (tshad-ma), soteriology (nang don), poetry (snyan ngag), lexicology (mngon brjod), prosody (sdeb sbyor), dramaturgy (zlos gar) aur astrology (dkar rtsis). In alag-alag kshetron ke unke gyaan aur sanshleshan ne unhe Buddhist Nyingma philosophy mein ek krantikari yogdan dene ki anumati di. Ek soldier, public official, lawyer aur agriculturist ke roop mein ek ashadharan roop se vividh career ke baad, American Henry Steel Olcott ne 19th century ke ant mein Buddhism ke siddhanton ki khoj karne ke liye Sri Lanka (tab Ceylon) ki yatra ki, jahan woh ek dharmik prateek, ‘the White Buddhist’ banenge. Unhone kai Buddhist schools banaye, jinmein se kuch aaj pratishthit colleges hain. Ek vyapak level par, Madame Blavatsky ke sath milkar, unhone Theosophical Society ki sthapna ki, jo 19th century ke ant mein esoteric philosophy ke prasaar ke liye ek shaktishali andolan bana.

Judaism ke paas philosophy aur spirituality ka ek lamba itihaas hai, lekin shayad sabse bade Jewish polymaths Al-Andalus ki Islamic civilisation ke dauran phale-phoole. Abraham Ibn Ezra, 11th aur 12th centuries ke dauran Muslim Spain mein rehne wale ek rabbi, ek prasiddh exegist hone ke sath-sath Jewish philosophy ke itihaas mein sabse zyada aadaraniya hastiyon mein se ek the. Woh Arabic Muwashshah style mein ek prasiddh kavi bhi the, aur unki kavitaon ka ek sangrah baad mein Leon Weinberger dwara translate aur publish kiya gaya tha. Iske alawa, unhone astrology par saat granth (treatises), Hebrew grammar par saat aur teen mahatvapurna mathematical works likhe jinhone Europe mein Indian numerical systems ko import karne mein ek mukhya bhoomika nibhayi. Isi tarah, 11th-century Spain mein, theologian Moses Maimonides ne kai medical treatises produce kiye, astrology ki ek aalochana likhi aur mahatvapurna philosophical works produce kiye, mukhya roop se logic par, jo unke polymathic treatise _Guide to the Perplexed_ mein charam par pahoncha.

Christianity mein mystical tradition ne sach mein Hildegard von Bingen ke samay mein prasiddhi haasil ki, jo 11th century mein shuruat mein ek nun aur theologian ke roop mein ubharin. Woh 'visions' se inspired thin jisse unhone 100 se zyada letters, 72 songs, 70 poems aur vividh vishayon par 9 books produce kiye. Woh ek vishisht kavi, philosopher aur composer thin, jinhone botanical aur medicinal texts ki ek shrinkhla bhi likhi. Apni saathi nuns dwara Magistra ke roop mein chuni gayi, unhone Rupertsberg aur Eibingen ke mathon (monasteries) ki sthapna ki aur church mein mahilaon ke adhikaaron ka samarthan kiya. Thomas Aquinas, 13th-century ke Christian mystic aur monk, apne samay ke saare jaane-maane gyaan ko haasil karne ke liye jaane jaate hain, iska upayog unhone progressive Christian philosophy ko rachne ke liye kiya jisne baad mein kai Renaissance aur Enlightenment scholars ko prabhavit kiya. Haal hi mein, priest Pavel Florensky, jinhe unke biographer dwara ‘Russia ka anjaan da Vinci’ bataya gaya hai, ne philosopher aur mystic mein khud ko badalnes se pehle radical Christian Struggle Union ki sthapna ki. Unhone _The Pillar and Ground of the Truth_ likha, jo mathematics aur mysticism ka ek multidisciplinary exploration tha. Woh ek pramukh art critic bhi bane, art ki theory aur history ka adhyayan karke ancient Russian art par mahatvapurna works publish kiye. Mathematics mein unke shuruati background ne unhe sciences ko explore karne ke liye prerit kiya, aur unhone dielectrics aur electrodynamics par works publish kiye aur baad mein ek krantikari thesis _Imaginary Numbers in Geometry_ — Albert Einstein ki theory of relativity ka ek geometrical anuvaad, jo religion aur science ki anukoolta (compatibility) ko samjhane ki koshish karta hai. Ek chemist ke roop mein unhone prison mein iodine aur agar ke utpadan par bhi anusandhan (research) kiya, isse pehle ki unhe ek Soviet establishment dwara mrityudand diya jata jo dharmik virodhiyon (religious dissidents) ko utpeedit karta tha.

Islam mein mysticism Sufi tradition mein sabse behtareen dhang se prakat hota hai. 10th-century ke Persian mystic Al-Ghazali bina kisi vivad ke ab tak ke sabse vyapak roop se aadaraniya Sufis mein se ek hain. Unki pratishtha na sirf natural sciences, theology, mysticism, Western philosophy, grammar aur law (doosre kshetron ke beech) ke vyapak gyaan ke pradarshan se aati hai, balki usse bhi zyada mahatvapurna unke philosophical conclusions ke samarthan mein us gyaan ko synthesise karne ki unki kshamta se aati hai jaisa ki unke magnum opus _Ihya’ ‘Ulum al-Din_ (The Revival of the Religious Sciences) mein set kiya gaya hai. 13th century mein, Qutb al-Din al-Shirazi ek Islamic mystic the jinhone Islamic philosophy ke illumination school ki sthapna ki. Woh ek theologian the jinhone Qur’an par commentaries likhin, ek grammarian the jinhone Arabic syntax aur rhetoric par likha aur ek jurist the jinhone judge ke roop mein seva ki aur law par ek treatise likha. Ek scientist ke roop mein unhone Ibn Sina ke _Kolliyet_ par commentary ke sath-sath leprosy par ek karya likha, geometry par apne master ke kaam ko viksit kiya, heliocentrism ki sambhavna ko explore kiya aur astronomical tables par kaam kiya. Unke magnum opus, _The Pearly Crown_ mein natural, moral aur political philosophy ke sabhi pehlu shamil the. Lekin Shirazi sirf ek intellectual nahi the. Rumi ke samkalin, woh ek kavi aur musician bhi the. Woh ek kushal Rubab player the — aur chess ke ek master.

Insaan rational aur spiritual dono tarah ke jeev hain, lekin primary inclination chahe jo bhi ho, gyaan (chahe sansarik ho ya esoteric) hamesha ek lagatar khoj rehta hai. Asal mein is uddeshya ki taraf rational aur mystical dono tareeqon ka ek sath upayog kiya gaya hai, kabhi-kabhi (aur kuch daava karte hain ki zyada prabhavshali dhang se) ek sath. Praacheen Greek philosophers ne logos (logic) aur knosis (mysticism) dono ke upayog ke sath kai sawaalon ka peecha kiya, jismein Socratic Dialogues rational enquiry ka ek mukhya udaharan hai jo chetna ki ek badli hui sthiti (altered state of consciousness) ki taraf le gaya. Buddhism, jo paramparik roop se apni mystical aur moral essence ke liye jaana jata hai, _Ka̅la̅ma Sutta_, ya ‘Buddha’s Charter of Inquiry’ ka upayog karke mahatvapurna soch (critical thinking) ke zariya sachai ki khoj ko bhi encourage karta hai. Islam gyaan haasil karne ke auzar ke roop mein dil (qalb) aur dimaag (aql) dono ko rekhankit karta hai. Hindu philosophy ke mukhya parisar (core premises) mein se ek yeh hai ki hum jaan-na chahte hain. Asal mein gyaan God ki taraf chaar mukhya raaston mein se ek hai (jnana yoga), doosre pyaar, kaam aur psycho-physical training hain.

## Explorers (KHOJ-KARTA / AVISHKARAK)

Royal Geographical Society dwara samarthit, 19th-century ke sahasik yatri (adventurer) Richard Francis Burton ne kai krantikari expeditions (khoj yatraon) se guze, jinmein ek South Asian yatri ke bhes mein Hajj ki yatra karna, Somali Country aur Congo ki badi jheelon ki khoj karna aur Nile nadi ke ultimate srot ki khoj karna shamil tha. Unhone apni yatraon ke kai vritant (accounts) likhe jinmein _Wit and Wisdom from West Africa_, _A Personal Narrative of a Pilgrimage to Al-Medinah and Meccah_ aur _First Footsteps in East Africa_ shamil hain. Apni vyapak yatraon, bhashayi pratibha (linguistic talent) aur sanskritik ruchi ke parinaamswarup, Burton 19th century ke sabse bade polyglots mein se ek bane, jo reportedly Greek aur Urdu se lekar Swahili aur Hebrew tak 40 bhashaon aur boliyon mein baatchit karne mein nipun (conversant) the. Unhone apni bhasha kaushalon (language skills) ka upayog Hindi se _Kama Sutra_ aur Arabic se _Arabian Nights_ ka anuvaad karne ke liye kiya.

Commissioned explorer ke roop mein Burton ka career unki zindagi ke shuruat aur ant ki taraf ek sainik aur diplomat ke roop mein unke careers ke beech sandwich (daba hua) tha. Lekin woh ek prachur writer (prolific writer) bhi the jinhone, apne travel chronicles aur Oriental va Renaissance literature ke anuvaadon ke alawa, anthropological theses aur kavitaayein likhin jinmein prasiddh _Kasidah_ aur _The Perfumed Garden_ jaise kaam shamil hain. Iske alawa, woh ek expert fencer aur falconer the aur unhone dono par granth (treatises) likhe. ‘Koi bhi insaan ek sath sab kuch nahi ho sakta, chahe woh kitni bhi koshish kyun na kare’ unke biographer Byron Farwell ne likha, ‘lekin kisi bhi insaan ne Richard Francis Burton se zyada koshish nahi ki.’

Saari khoj (exploration) jigyaasa (curiosity) se paida hoti hai. Samraat-on (monarchs), samajon aur companio ne apne swarthon ke liye expeditions ko commission kiya hai. Us expedition par maujood explorer ke liye, halanki, prerna (motivation) sirf khoj (discovery) ki sambhavna hai. Woh kisi vishisht cheez ki khoj nahi kar rahe hain. Woh bas dekh rahe hain. Aur jab woh khoj karte hain, toh woh kuch naya khojna jaari rakhna chahte hain. Yahi wajah hai ki itihaas mein kai explorers mool roop se polymathic rahe hain.

Pehle bade English polymathic explorer Walter Raleigh ne Elizabethan court ke English Renaissance Man ko prateekit (typified) kiya. Mukhya roop se Queen ke charmer ke roop mein yaad kiye jaane wale, woh vaastav mein — apni zindagi ke alag-alag points par — ek kavi, explorer, sainik, historian, politician, merchant, spy aur kai vishayon par lekhak the. Aur phir jaise hi British empire 18th aur 19th centuries ke dauran sthapit aur vistarit hua, aur zyada scholar-adventurers ne alag-alag sanskritiyon ki khoj karne ke liye apni bahu-pratibhaon ka upayog kiya. James Atkinson Bengal mein Army of the Indus ke Superintending Surgeon the jahan unhe Order of the Dooranee Empire se sammaanit kiya gaya tha. Woh ek trained aur talented artist bhi the jinhone Afghanistan aur Punjab ke prabhavshali sketches aur paintings ki ek shrinkhla ke zariya apne sainy abhiyanon (military expeditions) ko daraj kiya, jise unhone baad mein publish kiya. Iske alawa, unke kai works, jinmein ek self-portrait shamil hai, aaj bhi London ki National Portrait Gallery mein pradarshit hain.

Atkinson _Calcutta Gazette_ ke editor bhi the, jo India mein sabse shuruati English bhasha ke akhbaron mein se ek tha aur naye _Government Gazette_ ke editor aur baad mein Press ke Superintendent bane. Ek scholar aur linguist ke roop mein, woh oriental bhashaon mein nipun bane aur baad mein Fort William College mein Persian ke Deputy Professor ke roop mein seva ki. Woh Firdausi ke classic epic _Shah Nameh_ ka English prose aur verse mein anuvaad karne wale pehle vyakti the, jiske liye unhone Oriental Translation Fund ka gold medal jeeta. Usi saal unhone _Customs and Manners of the Women of Persia, and their domestic superstitions_ publish kiya, jise mool Persian manuscript se translate kiya gaya tha, sath hi Persian classics jaise _Laili and Majnun_ aur _Makhzan ul Asrar_ (the Treasury of Secrets) ke kai doosre anuvaad bhi. Woh apne aap mein ek pratibhashali kavi bhi the jinhone 21 saal ki umar mein apna pehla work publish kiya, _Rodolpho_ naam ka ek romance.

Anthropological, artistic aur linguistic interests ke alawa, 18th- aur 19th-century ki khoj ke sabse bade drivers mein se ek science tha. Alexander von Humboldt ko modern history mein sabse bade scholar-adventurers aur sarv-gun-sampanna naturalists mein se ek maana jata hai. ‘Aakhri bade vaigyanik generalist’ ke roop mein jaane jaane wale, aur ‘18th aur 19th centuries ke sabse bade ekrat karne wale vicharkon’ mein se ek, unhone vaastav mein botany, geology, anthropology, oceanography, zoology aur anatomy samet kai natural sciences mein mahatvapurna yogdan diya. Unki mukhya khojein Latin America mein thin, jahan unhone paanch saal ki avadhi mein vyapak yatra ki aur Russia mein, jahan unhone 9,000-mile ki exploratory trek ki. Unki yatraon ke dauran unke vaigyanik kaam ko 21 saalon mein volumes ke ek bade set mein compile aur publish kiya gaya, vishesh roop se unka 5-volume karya, _Kosmos_ (1845), jisne vaigyanik gyaan ki vividh shakhaon ko ekrat (unify) karne ki koshish ki. Ek shauqeen linguist, unhone un vividh ilaqon ki bhashaon ka bhi adhyayan kiya jahan unhone yatra ki.

English explorer Francis Galton un kuch scientists mein se ek the jinhone in situ (ek hi jagah) apne adhyayan karne ke bajaye duniya bhar mein vyapak yatraayein kin. Unhone ek bestseller _The Art of Travel_ bhi likhi. Unhone meteorology (anticyclone aur pehle popular mausam nakshe), statistics (regression aur correlation), psychology (synaesthesia), biology (heredity ki prakriti aur kriya-vidhi) aur forensics (fingerprints) samet kai vaigyanik vishayon mein antar-drishti (insights) haasil karne aur ashadharan safaltaayein (breakthroughs) haasil karne ke liye alag-alag sanskritik aur bhaugolik vatavaranon mein apne observations ka upayog kiya. Galton Britain mein ek badhte hue eugenics movement ke pioneer the jisne white Caucasian race ki bauddhika shreshthta (intellectual superiority) ko sabit karne ke liye science ka upayog karne ki koshish ki.

20th century ki shuruat ne bahupakshiyata (multilateralism) ka janm aur League of Nations ka gathan dekha. 'Global statesman' ki ek nayi nasal ubharne lagi. Norway ne, doosre Scandinavian deshon ki tarah, ek kutneetijak roop se tatasth (diplomatically neutral) desh ke roop mein pratishtha jeeti. Yahi wajah ho sakti hai ki Norwegian Fridtjof Nansen ko League of Nations High Commissioner for Refugees niyukt kiya gaya tha. Unhe First World War aur usse jude sangharshon ke visthapit peediton (displaced victims) ki taraf se unke kaam ke liye Nobel Peace Prize se sammaanit kiya gaya tha. Jin pehalon (initiatives) ki unhone shuruat ki unmein be-watan (stateless) vyaktiyon ke liye ‘Nansen passport’ shamil tha, jo 50 se zyada deshon dwara maanyata-prapt ek praman-patra tha. Lekin isse pehle, unhone ek athlete, zoologist, neurologist, oceanographer, historian, travel writer, Arctic explorer aur diplomat ke roop mein ek vishisht roop se vividh zindagi ka netritva kiya. 18 saal ki umar mein unhone world one-mile skating record toda, aur agle saal national cross-country skiing championship jeeti, ek aisa kaarnama jise woh 11 baad ke mauqon par dohrayenge.

Professional sports se retire hone par, unhone painting ke apne junoon ko chhod diya aur zoology padha, Bergen Museum ke zoological department mein ek curator bane. Unhone lower marine creatures ke central nervous system par research kiya aur papers publish kiye aur baad mein Royal Frederick University mein zoology ke professor bane. Nansen ka vaigyanik anusandhan (scientific research) phir zoology se oceanography mein switch ho gaya. Unhe Royal Geographical Society dwara 16th century ke shuruat tak uttari ilaqon (northern regions) ki khoj ka 2-volume ka itihaas produce karne ke liye commission kiya gaya tha, jise 1911 mein _Nord i Tåkeheimen_ (In Northern Mists) ke roop mein publish kiya gaya tha. Woh ek explorer the jo do Arctic expeditions par gaye: pehla jab woh 27 saal ke the, jab unhone Greenland ke inland ice ke pehle ski crossing ka netritva kiya; aur phir paanch saal baad jab unhone polar ship _Fram_ ke sath Polar Sea par yatra ki (86°14’ ki record uttari akshansh tak pahonchte hue). Ek diplomat ke roop mein, unhone 1905 mein Norway aur Sweden ke beech union ke vighatan (dissolution) mein ek mahatvapurna bhoomika nibhayi, aur baad mein London mein Norway ke pehle Ambassador niyukt kiye gaye, jahan unhone Norway ki azaad sthiti ka kamyabi ke sath samjhauta (negotiate) kiya.

## Scientists (VAIGYANIK)

Charles Murray dwara manav kamyabi (human accomplishment) par kiye gaye vyapak adhyayan ke anusar, itihaas ke 20 sabse prabhavshali vaigyanikon mein se 15 vaastav mein polymaths the. Is baat ko psychologist Robert Root-Bernstein ke Nobel Prize-winning vaigyanikon par kiye gaye adhyayan dwara aur bal mila, jisne unmein se ek chonkane wali sankhya ko polymathic paaya. Aur yeh modern West ke baahar ke vaigyanik itihaas par vichar kiye bina hai.

Sinologist Frederick Mote ke anusar, Shen Kuo ‘poore Chinese scientific history mein shayad sabse chashp/drashtavya (interesting) charitra’ the. Unhone Sung Shih ki bauddhika aur sanskritik bahumukhta ko sabse behtareen dhang se murt roop diya, mathematics, astronomy, anatomy, geology, economics aur optics samet kai sciences ke sath-sath civil aur mechanical engineering mein yogdan diya. Ek engineer aur inventor ke roop mein, unhone ek armillary sphere, ek water clock, aur ek bronze gnomon (ek pointer jiski chhaya dopahar ke samay ko batati hai) viksit kiya. Woh vaastav mein yeh khojne wale pehle vyakti the ki compass seedhe north ki taraf point nahi karta, balki vaastav mein magnetic north pole ki taraf point karta hai. Ek mathematician ke roop mein, unhone 'Go' board ki samasya par vichar kiya jismein 19×19 lines hoti hain, jo 361 intersections deti hain. Unhe Astronomy Bureau ka Director banaya gaya, aur naye samraat ke liye ek naya calendar viksit karne ke sath-sath grahon ki gati (planetary motion) par observations karne ka prabhar diya gaya. Phir unhe Imperial Academy mein Finance Commissioner niyukt kiya gaya aur supply aur demand ke siddhant, keematon ka purvanuman (forecasting prices) lagane ke tareeqon, currency supply, price controls aur market intervention par unke lekhon ne unhe apne samay ke sabse pramukh arthshastriyon (economists) mein se ek ke roop mein pakka kiya.

Lekin woh ek prasiddh statesman aur cultural icon bhi the jinhone ek bade kavi, painter aur musician ke roop mein pratishtha jeeti. Jabki science aur arts mein Shen ka zyadatar yogdan ek practitioner ke roop mein tha, woh aakhirkar apne saare gyaan ko _Brush Talks_ (_Dream Pool Essays_) ke naam se jaane jaane wale ek encyclopaedic treatise mein synthesise karenge, jismein mathematics, music, art criticism, astronomy, calendars, cartography, geology, optics aur medicine ke tatva shamil the. Ek polymathic masterpiece, ise Sung China ke sabse mahatvapurna bauddhika kaamon (intellectual works) mein se ek ke roop mein jaana jata tha.

Chinese science ke mukhya Western chronicler, Joseph Needham ne kaha ki Chinese log ‘ek samagra vaigyanik vishwa-drishti (holistic scientific worldview) aur entities ke samanjasyapoorn, anukramik rishton (harmonious, hierarchical relationships)’ par dhyan kendrit karte the, jisne ek sath, aapas mein jude dhang se kai praakritik ghatnaon (natural phenomena) ki samajh ko pramanit kiya. Sixteenth- aur seventeenth-century China ka Ming period natural sciences se sambandhit apne encyclopaedic scholarship ke liye prasiddh tha. Bureaucrat Li Shizhen traditional Chinese medicine, herbology aur acupuncture mein ek pioneer the, lekin unhone apne _Bencao Gangmu_ ke zariya ek zabardast vaigyanik vidwatta ka pradarshan bhi kiya, jismein botany, zoology, mineralogy aur metallurgy jaise vividh vishayon par 1,892 entries shamil hain. Ming China ke hi Song Yingxing ne ab tak ke sabse pehle vyapak science encyclopaedias mein se ek, _Tiangong Kaiwu_ produce kiya. Yadyapi China ke paas encyclopaedias produce karne ka ek lamba itihaas tha, _Tiangong Kaiwu_ isne anokha hai ki ismein koi references nahi lagte, aur iske bajaye Yingxing dwara poori tarah se apne gyaan aur anubhavon se likhe jaane ka daava kiya jata hai. Yeh Yingxing ko ek mamooli encyclopaedist se zyada aur kai sciences mein ek mahatvapurna yogdankarta banata hai.

Islam ki sthapna ne shuruat mein kai theologian-scientists ka uday dekha, jinhone srishti (creation) aur uske rachiyata (creator) ko samajhna barabar mahatvapurna samjha. In scholars ne apni jaanch karte waqt vaigyanik seemaon ko kharij kar diya, aur Islam ke pehle do sadiyon mein Ja‘far Al-Sadiq, Ja̅bir ibn Hayya̅n, Al-Khwarizmi, Ibn Ishaq, Ibn Qurrah aur Al Jahiz jaise log mathematics, astronomy, anatomy aur scientia (ya 'ilm) ke doosre pehluon ke interdisciplinary scholars bane. Muslims ke beech vaigyanik polymathy ki taraf yeh jhukav aage bhi jaari raha. Yeh do sadiyon baad usi ilaqe mein Ibn al-Haytham dwara prateekit kiya gaya tha, jo physics, biology, astronomy aur mathematics samet science ki vividh shakhaon ki jaanch karne ke liye vaigyanik jaanch ki ek nayi pranali — scientific method — ko sthapit aur upayog karne wale pehle vaigyanikon mein se ek bane. Al-Haytham optics aur visual perception par apne kaam ke liye prasiddh hain, lekin unhone physics, anatomy, astronomy, engineering, mathematics, medicine, ophthalmology aur psychology ke kshetron mein bhi mahatvapurna yogdan diya. Kuch sadiyon baad Ottoman scholar Takiyuddin ne science aur engineering ke sabhi pehluon par likha, jinmein astronomy, physics, medicine, optics, mathematics aur mechanical engineering (jaise clock-making) shamil hain. Unhone in vishayon par 90 se zyada kitaabein likhin. Yadyapi inmein se kuch hi bachi hain, unke vicharon ne zaroor vividh vaigyanik vishayon mein theoretical aur experimental research mein ek naye daur ka aagaz kiya.

Vaigyanikon ke beech polymathy Renaissance ke baad se England mein aam thi. Isaac Newton ne vaastav mein science par jitna likha usse zyada occult theology par likha. Phir bhi, science mein unki uplabdhiyan aur yogdan daraj itihaas mein kisi bhi vaigyanik jitne vividh aur prabhavshali hain. Ek mathematician ke roop mein unhone differential aur integral calculus viksit kiya (Leibniz ke sath shrey baante hue), generalised binomial theorem ka pradarshan kiya, kisi function ke roots ka andaza lagane ke tareeqe ko racha aur power series ke adhyayan mein yogdan diya. Unhone practically physics ke vishay ka (kam se kam iski Western samajh mein) nirmaan apne _Principles of Natural Philosophy_ (jise aamtaur par _Principia_ kaha jata hai) ke zariya kiya, jismein unhone universal gravitation aur gati ke teen kanoonon (three laws of motion) ka varnan kiya aur heliocentrism ke baare mein aakhri shankaon ko hata diya. Unhone pehla practical reflecting telescope banaya aur is observation ke aadhar par rangon ka ek siddhant viksit kiya ki ek prism white light ko un kai rangon mein todta hai jo visible spectrum banate hain. Unhone cooling ka ek empirical law bhi racha aur sound ki speed ka adhyayan kiya. Newton ke samkalin Robert Hooke, jinhe historian Allan Chapman dwara ‘England’s Leonardo’ kaha gaya, ne bhi astronomy aur gravitation, aerodynamics, mechanical engineering, combustion, psychology aur microscopy samet kai vaigyanik vishayon mein yogdan diya. Unhone 1666 ki aag ke baad London mein mahatvapurna imaraton ko design karte waqt ek architect ke roop mein apni kalaatmak pratibha (artistic talent) ka samarthan karne ke liye sciences mein apni vividh mahaarat ka upayog kiya.

Enlightenment ke dauran academia mein specialisation ke badhte prabhav ke baavjood, kuch intellectuals ne dhare ke vipreet kaam kiya. Thomas Young, jinhe unke biographer dwara ‘sab kuch jaan-ne wala aakhri vyakti’ (the last man to know everything) bataya gaya tha, ne apna khud ka medical practice sthapit kiya, physiology, optics aur physics par mahatvapurna works publish kiye, aur physics ke professor ke roop mein light ki wave theory ko viksit kiya. Shayad aur bhi chashp/drashtavya, woh ek polyglot the jinhone 400 se zyada bhashaon ki phonetics ka adhyayan kiya aur prasiddh roop se Rosetta Stone par Egyptian hieroglyphics ko decipher kiya (halanki ise poori tarah se baad mein Champollion dwara poora kiya gaya tha). Unhone musical instruments ko tune karne ka ek tareeqa bhi banaya, jise aaj Young Temperament ke roop mein jaana jata hai.

Vaigyanik anusandhan (scientific research) ki duniya par 19th century tak mardon ka dabddaba tha, jab Mary Somerville ko Society for the Diffusion of Useful Knowledge — ek aisa sangathan jo tezi se badhte padhne wale public ke liye vaigyanik gyaan ko zyada accessible banane ke liye geared tha — ke liye kuch jatil vaigyanik kaamon ka anuvaad karne ke liye kaha gaya tha. Apni aakarshak lekhan shaili (writing style) ke sath science ke kai kshetron ko popular banane ke baad, unhone apne khud ke vividh vaigyanik kaamon ko produce karna shuru kiya, jinmein _On the Connexion of the Physical Sciences_, _Physical Geography_ aur _Molecular and Microscopic Science_ shamil hain. Unhone mathematics ke kshetra ko bhi aage badhaya, algebra mein upayog kiye jaane wale shabd ‘variables’ ko racha. Woh Royal Astronomical Society mein shamil hone wali pehli mahilaon mein se ek banin aur unki mrityu par London ke _Morning Post_ dwara unhe ‘nineteenth-century science ki Rani’ (Queen of nineteenth-century science) kaha gaya.

Enlightenment ke dauran ‘natural history mein saare vichar ke pita’ (father of all thought in natural history) bina kisi vivad ke Comte de Buffon, Georges-Louis Leclerc the, jo us daur ke sabse bade vaigyanik encyclopaedia, _Histoire Naturelle_ ke lekhak the, jo nature ki vividh shakhaon ko ekrat karne wala ek 44-volume ka karya tha. Doosre kaamon mein, unhone probability theory mein differential aur integral calculus ko shamil kiya, timber ke gunon ka adhyayan kiya, solar system ki shuruat ki khoj ki aur monogenism ke mukhya samarthak bane. Swedish Enlightenment scientist Carl Linnaeus ko sciences mein badhte specialisation ke samay mein human anatomy, botany, zoology aur geology mein unke nirantar anusandhanon ke liye ek sarv-gun-sampanna naturalist ke roop mein jaana jata tha. Woh human, plant, animal aur mineral classification ke pioneers mein se ek banenge. Enlightenment ke dauran scientific polymaths ke doosre udaharanon mein German Herman von Helmholtz shamil hain, jo ek ashadharan doctor, physicist aur psychologist the, jo aakhirkar 20th century ke pramukh vaigyanik darshanikon (philosophers of science) mein se ek bane.

‘Serial inventors’ aise vividh devices design aur create karte hain jo ek se zyada vaigyanik vishayon mein unki kshamta, ya shreshthta ko pradarshit karte hain. Ek classic (aur shayad sabse pehla) udaharan Ancient Greek inventor Archimedes hain, jinke ek astronomer, physicist, mathematician aur engineer ke roop mein pratibha ne unhe science ke itihaas mein kuch sabse mahatvapurna aavishkar karne ki anumati di. Unhone ek infinite series ke summation ke sath parabola ke arc ke neeche ke kshetraphal (area) ki ganana karne ke liye exhaustion ke tareeqe ka upayog kiya, aur pi ka ek behadd sahit andaza diya. Unhone apne naam wale spiral, surfaces of revolution ke volumes ke liye formulae aur bahut bade sankhyaon ko vyakt karne ke liye ek chatur pranali ko bhi paribhashit kiya. Unhone sabit kiya ki sphere ke paas cylinder ke volume aur surface area ka do-tihaai (two thirds) hota hai (isne base ko shamil karte hue), aur ise apni mathematical uplabdhiyon mein sabse bada mana. Physics mein unki pragatiyon mein hydrostatics, statics ki buniyaad aur lever ke siddhant ki vyakhya shamil hai. Unhe navinaatmak machines ko design karne ka shrey diya jata hai, jinmein siege engines aur screw pump shamil hain jo unka naam dharak hain.

Ninth-century ke genius Abbas ibn Firnas ek abhyas-karta doctor (physician) the jinhone engineering, physics, biology, astronomy aur chemistry ke kshetron mein ek inventor ke roop mein ek ashadharan roop se vividh career ki shuruat ki. Unhone _Al-Maqata_ naam ki ek water clock design ki, colourless glass ke nirmaan ka ek tareeqa racha, vividh glass planispheres ka aavishkar kiya, corrective lenses ya ‘reading stones’ banaye, rings ki ek shrinkhla viksit ki jiska upayog grahon aur taaron ki gatiyon ka anukaran (simulate) karne ke liye kiya ja sakta tha, ek prakar ka metronome produce kiya aur rock crystal ko kaatne ki ek aisi prakriya viksit ki jisne Spain ko quartz ko katne ke liye Egypt export karna band karne ki anumati di. Unka mukhya (jabki West mein kam jaana-maana) aavishkar, halanki, udaan mein ek prayog ke liye vishesh roop se design kiye gaye pankhon (wings) ka ek set tha. Kai sroton ke anusar — kuch doosron ki tulna mein zyada vishwasniya — woh pankhon ka upayog karke glide karne mein kamyab rahe, lekin tail mechanism ki kami ke kaaran landing ka galat andaza laga baithe, jisse unki peeth mein chot aayi. Yeh manav udaan ka sabse pehla daraj prayas jaana jata hai.

## Artists (KALAKAR)

Ziryab ne 9th century mein lagbhag akele hi Andalusian aesthetic culture (saundaryatmak sanskriti) mein kranti la di. Ek yuva musician ke roop mein, unki pratibha ne unhe Al-Andalus ke caliph se ek pratishthit amantran dilaya jahan unhone singers aur musicians ko train karne ke liye schools khole, aur musical instruments ko introduced kiya — vishesh roop se Persian lute, jo Spanish guitar bana — saath hi Persia aur Mesopotamia ke geet, dhun aur nritya (dances) jo baad mein, Gypsy prabhav ke saath milkar, prasiddh Spanish flamenco mein badal gaye. Ek musical theorist aur composer ke roop mein, unhone paramparik rachna (traditional composition) ke metrical aur rhythmical parameters ko phir se vyavasthit kiya aur muwashshah, zajal aur nawbah jaise abhivyakti ke naye tareeqe banaye.

Cordoba mein Abdar-Rahman II ke darbar mein hi Ziryab ne ek fashion icon, ek trendsetter aur gastronome (swadishth khane ka parakhdar) ke roop mein phalna-phoolna shuru kiya. Woh ek adarsh darbari (model courtier) bane, aur darbar mein logon ko style aur aachaar-vichaar mein train karna shuru kiya — 16th century mein Italian Renaissance ke dauran Baldesare Castiglione dwara apni _Book of the Courtier_ likhne se sadiyon pehle. Unhone naye hairstyles, pakwanon ki recipes, dress sense, hygiene care, samajik aacharan (social manner) aur aahaar ki aadat, teen-course ka khana (three-course meal), tablecloth, fine dining, mausami fashion, crystal glassware aur chess aur polo jaise khel introduced kiye. Ziryab ki rachnatmak pratibha ne darbar ki sanskriti ke kai pehluon ko sameta.

Early modern period se pehle 'arts' manav sikhne ke poore kshetra (entire spectrum) ko sandarbhit karta tha. Europe mein inmein saat Liberal Arts (grammar, rhetoric, logic, arithmetic, geometry, music aur astronomy) shamil the, jo sabse shuruati universities mein padhaye jaate the, aur kai Mechanical Arts (jaise weaving, masonry, soldiery, hunting, commerce aur cooking), jo guilds mein padhaye jaate the. Europe mein Renaissance ke baad se, lekin duniya bhar mein kahin aur isse pehle, arts ko un 'rachnatmak abhivyakti ke kaamon, jo kaushal aur kalpana ke saath banaye gaye hain, jinaka ashadharan saundaryatmak mulya hai' par dhyan kendrit karne ke liye phir se paribhashit kiya gaya hai. Aaj arts mein visual art (painting, sculpture, photography), performing art (music, cinema, theatre) aur literary art (poetry, novels aur plays) shamil hain. Kuch creative polymaths in vividh kalaroopon (art forms) ke andar ek ashadharan bahumukhta (versatility) pradarshit karte hain. Yeh wahi hain jinhe hum tab sandarbhit karte hain jab hum kalakar ki aatma (artist's soul) ki baat karte hain.

Yeh Italian Renaissance ki ek paribhashit visheshata ban gayi, jisne Uomo Universale ya ‘Renaissance Men’ ko janm diya jaise Michelangelo, jo apne samay ke sabse prasiddh painters, poets, sculptors aur architects mein se ek the. Ek sculptor ke roop mein, unhone _Pietà_ aur _David_ samet kai masterpieces produce kiye, aur ek painter ke roop mein Western art ke itihaas mein fresco mein do sabse prabhavshali works banaye: Rome mein Sistine Chapel ki ceiling par Genesis ke drishya aur altar wall par _The Last Judgment_. Ek architect ke roop mein, Michelangelo ne Laurentian Library mein Mannerist style ki shuruat ki aur St Peter’s Basilica ke architect bane, plan ko badalte hue aur uske western end ko phir se design karte hue. Ek kavi ke roop mein, Michelangelo ne prasiddh epigrams aur sonnets ki ek shrinkhla likhi, jinmein se har ek un logon se inspired aur samarpit thi jinhe unhone apni zindagi ke alag-alag charanon mein pyaar kiya aur chaha.

Ziryab aur Michelangelo ki tarah, sabse bade artists sanskritik andolan-on (cultural movements) ko paribhashit aur shuru karte hain. Woh aisa na sirf ek art form ko balki poore vatavaran (milieu) ko prabhavit karke karte hain. Mário de Andrade Brazilian art, music aur literature mein modernism ke pioneer the aur 1930s ke São Paulo mein avant-garde movement ke ek mukhya vyakti the. Unki kavita _Paulicéia Desvairada_ aur novel _Macunaíma_ ne Brazil mein modernism ki shuruat ki aur ismein adivasi Brazilian lok vishay (indigenous Brazilian folk themes) shamil the. Unki photographs unke travel journal ‘The Apprentice Tourist’ mein publish hui thin aur unhone modernist movement se kaafi dhyan khincha. Andrade ek pramukh art critic aur historian bhi the aur country mein ethnomusicology par focus karne wale pehle music scholars mein se ek the. Visual, literary aur performing arts mein apne yogdan ke zariya, Andrade ko ek poore artist ke roop mein dekha jata hai; ek aisa jisne ek sanskritik andolan ko shuru kiya aur use murt roop diya.

Yahi baat India ke ek artistic genius ke liye bhi kahi ja sakti hai. Unke biographer Andrew Robinson dwara ‘myriad-minded man’ (anant dimaag wala aadmi) ke roop mein aankit Rabindranath Tagore ek kavi, composer, painter, novelist aur playwright the. Lekin shayad usse bhi zyada mahatvapurna, woh ek bahumukhi intellectual aur political activist the jinka ek rashtravadi, aupniveshik-virodhi sanskriti ke prasaar mein mahatva aaj bhi mahsoos kiya jata hai. Law ki padhai karte hue London mein ek chhote daur ke baad, Tagore apni family estate mein ekant mein kavita aur chhoti kahaniyan likhne ke liye India laute. Is ‘Sadhana’ daur (1891–1895) ke dauran unhone teen-volume _Galpaguchchha_ mein maujood aadhe se zyada kahaniyan likhin, jo khud 84 kahaniyon ka ek samooh hai. Unhone aath novels aur chaar novellas aur kai prasiddh plays jaise _Dak Ghar_ (Post Office), _Chandalika_ (Untouchable Girl), aur _Raktakaravi_ (Red Oleanders) bhi likhe. Unhone apne zyadatar kaam ka English free verse mein anuvaad kiya aur 1913 mein Literature ke liye Nobel Prize jeeta, pehle Asian Nobel laureate bane.

Unki kavita ka kai international roop se prasiddh composers par gehra prabhav pada, jinhone unki kai kavitaon ko sangeet mein badla. Tagore ne khud lagbhag 2,230 geet rache, jo samuhik roop se Bengali culture ka ek abhinna hissa ban gaye hain. Woh itihaas mein do alag-alag deshon ke national anthems ke liye scores likhne aur compose karne wale akele composer bhi bane: 1911 mein India ke liye _Jana Gana Mana_ aur Bangladesh ke liye _Amar Shonar Bangla_ (halanki pehle racha gaya tha, ise baad mein 1971 mein aazadi par Bangladesh dwara upayog kiya jayega). Iske alawa, unki kai kavitaon aur doosre lekhon ka upayog lyrics ke roop mein kiya gaya hai aur woh samuhik roop se ‘Rabindra Sangeet’ branded sangeet ki ek nayi genre banate hain.

60 saal ki umar mein, Tagore ne drawing aur painting shuru ki. Unki vividh kala (eclectic art), jo oil aur chalk pastels se lekar paper par ink tak pheli thi, ne duniya bhar ke art critics ka dhyan khincha, jisse woh Europe mein kai kamyab exhibitions aayojit kar sake. Ek artistic polymath hone ke alawa, Tagore India ke ek historian, ek spiritualist jinhone God ki prakriti par lectures likhe aur diye aur ek linguist the jinhone Bengali grammar par likha. Tagore ka mukhya philosophical work, _Sadhana: Realisation of the Self_, ne insaan ko nature se phir se jodne ki koshish ki.

Sanskritiyan khud ek artist se ashadharan roop se bahumukhi (versatile) hone ki maang karti hain. Udaharan ke liye, West Africa ka Griot — jo 15th century ke phalte-phoolte Malian Empire se paida hua tha — aamtaur par ek musician, poet, singer, dancer, storyteller aur artist ke sath-sath ek historian bhi hota tha. Aaj bhi, un African artists ke liye jo rachnatmakta (creativity) ke prati ek adivasi drishtikon rakhte hain, kai art forms ka sanshleshan (synthesis) kriya ka ek praakritik kram hai. South African artist Pitika Ntuli ne apni kitaab _Storms of the Heart_ mein is tapan (yearning) ko vyakt kiya:

> Mere desh mein, aur Swaziland mein, jo mera apnaya hua desh hai, art forms ka milan, ek kavi, painter, sculptor, musician, actor, sab ek mein hona, sirf ek aam baat ho sakti hai. Ceremonies, rituals, saare art forms ko milate hain, taaki . . . cross-fertilization ki anumati mil sake. . . . Britain pahonchkar maine khud ko ek sath alag-alag khandon mein jeete hue, ya aadha-jeete hue paaya. Har khand poori tarah se band lagta tha. Har ek itna ghotan-bhara vyaktigat.

18th aur 19th centuries mein European Romanticism ne prasiddh roop se art ka upayog us cheez ke virodh ke liye ek shaktishali auzar ke roop mein kiya jise unhone Enlightenment ka atyadhik bauddhika tarkvaad (excessive intellectual rationalism) kaha tha. Parinaamswarup isne kuch adbhut roop se bahumukhi artists produce kiye. Is daur ki kuch ullekhniya mahila polymaths mein se ek German Romanticist Bettina von Arnim thin, jinhone ek musician, singer, sculptor, draughtswoman aur novelist ke roop mein shreshthta haasil ki. Woh Goethe ki dost aur samkalin thin, aur halanki unhe ek eccentric (sanki) ke roop mein jaana jata tha (kohi bhi female polymath us samay aisi hi hoti), arts mein unki bahumukhi chamak ne German Romantics ki prateek sarvabhoomikta (hallmark universality) ko darshaya. Unke German samkalin Richard Wagner ko sabse behtareen dhang se us composer ke roop mein jaana jata hai jisne _Ride of the Valkyries_ aur _Tristan und Isolde_ jaise bade pieces produce kiye. Lekin kam jaana-maana yeh hai ki woh ek theatre director aur art theorist bhi the jinke Universal Art (_Gesamtkunstwerk_) ke idea ne poetic, visual, musical aur dramatic arts ko synthesise karke opera mein kranti la di taaki ek 'total work of art' produce kiya ja sake.

19th century ke aakhri hisse mein motion pictures ya film ke aavishkar aur prasaar ne visual, performing aur literary arts mein sarv-gun-sampanna pratibha rakhne wale un artists ke liye ek poora naya khel ka maidaan khol diya. Frenchman Jean Cocteau, shayad 20th century ke sabse bahumukhi artist, early se mid-1900s mein avant-garde culture ke apne prasaar ke liye prasiddh the. Ek vilakshan writer, unhone 50 se zyada published poetic works produce kiye aur lagbhag 20 samikshak-dwara-saraha-gaye plays likhe aur chaar mukhya films direct kin, jinmein se chaar — _Beauty and the Beast_ aur ‘Orphic Trilogy’ — jinmein unhone khud acting ki, French cinema mein poetic consciousness ke sabse behtareen udaharan bane. Visual arts mein Cocteau ka yogdan barabar prabhavshali tha, jo pencil, crayon, pastel, paint ya pen-and-ink drawings se lekar posters, wall decorations, lithographs, ceramics aur tapestries tak ek kaafi graphic aur plastic range dikhata tha. Woh mid-twentieth-century France ke sabse zyada dikhawati celebrities mein se ek the, aur unhone ballet, stamp design aur sculpture mein bhi haath aazmaya. Isi nasal ke Italian Pier Paolo Pasolini the, jinhone isi tarah filmmaking, painting aur literature mein shreshthta haasil ki. Woh bhi ek kishor ke roop mein ek published kavi bane, aur uske turant baad apna vivadaaspad novel _Ragazzi di Vita_ likha, jisne us samay ke Italian establishment ko aakroshit kiya, lekin tab se critics dwara saraha gaya hai. Unhone baad mein kai kavitaayein, kahaniyan aur essays likhe, saath hi chhah plays — jinmein se zyadatar ne us samay ke dharmik aur rajneetik status quo ko chunauti di. Film mein unka kaam kam vivadaaspad nahi tha. Ek screenwriter aur director ke roop mein, unki films _Accattone_ (1961), _Ro.Go.Pa.G._ (1963) aur _The Gospel According to St. Matthew_ (1964) ko eeshninda-purna (blasphemous) aur aakrosh-janak maana gaya, lekin phir bhi samikshak-dwara tareef mili. Unhone 50 se zyada films aur documentaries ke liye screenplays likhe, aur unmein se kuch mein acting bhi ki aur music bhi compose kiya. Iske alawa, unka visual art — vishisht roop se styled drawings, paintings aur sketches — duniya bhar mein pradarshit kiya gaya hai.

Gordon Parks America mein pehle jaane-maane black artistic polymaths mein se ek the. Unhone apne career ki shuruat _Vogue_ aur _Life_ magazines jaise high-profile publications ke sath-sath Office of War Information ke liye ek photo-journalist ke roop mein ki, jismein fashion, sports, theatre, gareebi aur nasli bhedbhav se lekar Malcolm X, Muhammad Ali aur Barbra Streisand jaise logon ke portraits tak ke vishayon ko cover kiya gaya, saath hi photography manuals publish kiye gaye. Phir woh Hollywood ke pehle mukhya black film director bane, aur unki 1971 ki film, _Shaft_, ne blaxploitation films ki ek shrinkhla ko janm diya. Parks ek behtareen musician aur ek swayam-sikhay-hue jazz pianist bhi the. Unhone _Concerto for Piano and Orchestra_ (1953) aur _Tree Symphony_ (1967) ke saath-saath apni movie _Shaft_ ke liye score aur civil rights leader Martin Luther King ko samarpit ek ballet compose kiya. Parks ke lekhan sangrah (writing repertoire) mein novels, poetry aur autobiography shamil hain, saath hi photographic instructional manuals aur filmmaking books bhi.

Unke biographer Andrew Robinson (jinhone Thomas Young aur Rabindranath Tagore jaise doosre polymaths ke liye bhi biographies likhin hain) ke anusar, Satyajit Ray ‘aaj tak ke sabse bahumukhi rachiye’ (by far the most versatile creator) the jinhe woh jaante hain. Cinema ke itihaas ke sabse bade filmmakers mein se ek, woh apni har film ke liye ‘screenplay solo likhte the, har actor ko vyaktigat roop se cast karte the, apne khud ke sets aur costumes design karte the, camera operate karte the, har frame ko edit karte the, apna khud ka music compose karte the, aur yahan tak ki apne khud ke posters bhi banate the.’ Film banane ki prakriya ke vividh pehluon ko synthesise karne ki isi kshamta ne unhe 1950s mein _Apu Trilogy_ jaise masterpieces aur doosre classics banane ki anumati di jinne aakhirkar 1992 mein unki mrityu se pehle lifetime achievement ke liye ek Oscar dilaya.

Abbas Kiarostami ne art aur design ka adhyayan kiya aur 1960s mein Iranian advertising industry mein ek painter, designer aur posters ke illustrator ke roop mein apne career ki shuruat ki. Ek cinematic genius (_Koker Trilogy_, 1987–1994) maane jaane wale, Kiarostami ne 1970s se lekar ab tak kai TV commercials ke alawa, shorts aur documentaries samet 40 se zyada films direct kin. Kiarostami film banane ke sabhi pehluon mein seedhe taur par shamil rahe: ek screenwriter, film editor, art director aur producer ke roop mein, jabki credit titles aur publicity material bhi design karte the. Kiarostami ek prasiddh kavi bhi the, jinaki 200 se zyada kavitaon ka ek dvibhashi sangrah, _Walking with the Wind_, Harvard University Press dwara publish kiya gaya tha. Woh ek prasiddh photographer bhi the, jinhone apni _Untitled Photographs_ publish ki, jo 25 saal ki avadhi mein unke hometown Tehran ke barf ke drishyon (snow landscapes) ko pradarshit karne wali 30 se zyada photographs ka sangrah tha.

## Entrepreneurs (UDYAMI / VYAPARI)

Benjamin Franklin ne American history ke is naye daur ki aavishkaratmak aatma (inventive spirit) ko murt roop (personified) diya. Kam umar mein hi woh ek kamyab newspaper editor aur printer bane. Unhone *Pennsylvania Chronicle* aur *The Pennsylvania Gazette* jaise satirical (vyangya-atmak) papers publish kiye, lekin woh unka *Poor Richard’s Almanack* tha — jismein unhone prasiddh roop se kahavaton (proverbs) aur neeti-kathaon (parables) ka yogdan diya — jisne unhe ameer banaya. Unhone ‘junto club’ ki sthapna ki, jo vaigyanik aur darshanik jaanch ke maamlon ke sath-sath aam aatm-sudhar (general self-improvement) par charcha karne ke liye intellectuals ka ek group ya society thi.

Franklin ne ek scientist ke roop mein ek badhti hui international pratishtha bhi haasil ki. Theory ke mukable science ke praktikal application se zyada chintit, woh electricity mein apne prayogon (experiments) aur apni kai aavishkaron ke liye prasiddh bane, jinmein lightning rod, bifocals, Franklin stove aur ek carriage odometer shamil hain. Unhone climatology, oceanography aur demography mein bhi safaltaayein (breakthroughs) haasil kin.

American Revolution ke baad unhone Declaration of Independence aur U.S. Constitution ko draft karne mein madad ki, aur Revolutionary War ke ant ka prateek, 1783 Treaty of Paris ko negotiate kiya. Apni vyapak European yatraon aur European intellectual community mein sampark ke kaaran, woh pehle se hi America ke Founding Fathers mein sabse zyada internationally prasiddh the, baad mein ek kushal diplomat aur sakaratmak Franco-American rishton ke vikas mein ek mukhya vyakti bane. Unhone baad mein America ke pehle postmaster-general aur Pennsylvania ke Governor ke roop mein kai public positions mein seva ki. Unhone Philadelphia ki police force, fire department, subscription library aur American Philosophical Society (jiske woh President banenge) jaise kai sangathanon (organisations) aur sansthaon ki sthapna ki.

Woh 30 se zyada intellectual clubs aur societies ke sadasya the aur ek shauqeen hobbyist: ek yogy musician aur composer (aur glass harmonica ke inventor) aur American colonies mein pehle jaane-maane chess player. Ek writer ke roop mein unhone ek autobiography aur science aur moral philosophy se lekar chess aur gulam-pratha (slavery) tak ke vishayon par kai patra (letters) likhe.

Ek kamyab sangathan (jaise ki ek corporation, charity, academic institution ya multinational body) ko manage karne ke liye uske vividh pehluon ke prabhavshali milan (effective amalgamation) ki zaroorat hoti hai. 'Organisation' shabd 'organs' ka ek derivative hai, jo sujhatta hai ki yeh alag-alag karya-pranaliyon aur visheshataon wale kai organs wale sharir ke samaan hai. Sharir ka mukhya (head) har organ ke andaruni kaamon ke sath-sath poore sharir ke anukooltam (optimal) karya mein uski bhoomika ko jaan-ne ke liye zimmedar hai. Sabse behtareen business leaders woh hain jo apne sangathan ke alag-alag hisson mein ek majboot gyaan aur shamilat darshate hain, jismein finance, law aur communications se lekar technology aur trade ke sath-sath product aur sector gyaan tak ke kshetra shamil ho sakte hain.

Ek prabhavshali dhang se synthesise karne wala businessman ek mamooli manager se zyada hota hai. Woh business ke har aayaam (dimension) mein achhi tarah se nipun hota hai aur isliye samajhta hai ki inmein se har ek aayaam poore corporate puzzle mein sabse behtareen dhang se kaise fit baitha hai. Koi Apple ke founder Steve Jobs ko aise business leader ke ek shaktishali udaharan ke roop mein quote kar sakta hai. Unke paas corporation ke sabhi pehluon ki gehri pakad thi, jo technical engineering aur artistic design se lekar marketing aur finance tak pheli thi. In vibhagon (divisions) ke unke prabhavshali sanshleshan (synthesis) aur jaisa ki unhone kaha tha, ‘dots ko jodne’ ki kshamta ne 21st century ke sabse navinaatmak (innovative), kamyab aur prabhavshali corporations mein se ek ke nirmaan aur vikas ki anumati di.

American entrepreneur aur industrialist Thomas Edison ab tak ke sabse prachur inventors (prolific inventors) mein se ek the, jinke paas Europe mein kai ke alawa 1,093 U.S. patents the. Woh business aur entrepreneurship mein barabar adbhut kamyabi haasil karne wale pehle world-class inventors mein se bhi ek the. Unhone kai researchers, scientists aur inventors ko kaam par rakha, aur jabki har aavishkar mein unki vyaktigat shamilat par sawaal uthaye gaye hain, unhe un zyadatar, agar sabhi nahi, projects ko direct aur supervise karne ke liye jaana jata hai jinne unhe patents dilaye. Unke aavishkar electrical, chemical aur mechanical engineering se sambandhit the, aur kshetra mein vividh aur prakriti se krantikari the. Inmein phonograph, light bulb (jo vaastav mein upayog karne yogya lighting mechanism produce karne ke pehle ke ad hoc prayason par banaya gaya tha), pehla commercially available fluoroscope (ek aasi machine jo radiographs lene ke liye X-rays ka upayog karti hai), stock ticker (sabse shuruati digital electronic communications medium, jo telegraph lines par stock price information transmit karta tha), kinetograph (motion picture camera ka ek shuruati roop) aur kinetoscope (ek motion picture exhibition device) shamil hain.

Howard Hughes apne samay ke sabse bahumukhi businessmen mein se ek the. Unhe apne pita se ek sampatti (fortune) virasat mein mili lekin unhone 18 saal ki umar mein film production mein jaane ka faisla kiya. Unka directorial debut *Hell’s Angels* apne samay ki sabse mehangi movies mein se ek tha aur isne unhe Hollywood mein badi tareef dilayi. Productions ki ek shrinkhla ke baad (jinmein Academy Award winners shamil hain), Hughes ne udane ke apne junoon ko aage badhane ka faisla kiya (woh *Hell’s Angels* ki shooting ke dauran ude aur crash hue), aur prasiddh roop se kai aviation records set kiye, jinmein duniya ka chakkar lagane (circumnavigating the globe) ka record shamil hai. Unhone Trans World Airlines mein ek mukhya hissedari bhi kharidi aur U.S. government dwara kai aircraft banane ke liye commission kiye gaye. Apni holding company, Summa Corporation ke zariya, Hughes ne aerospace aur defence, electronics, mass media, manufacturing, hospitality, petroleum drilling aur oilfield services, consulting, entertainment aur mining jaise vividh industries ke kai projects mein invest karna shuru kiya. Woh vishesh roop se ek kamyab real estate investor the, aur aakhirkar Las Vegas mein zyadatar mukhya property ke maalik bane. Science mein unki jaari ruchi ne unhe duniya bhar mein prasiddh Howard Hughes Medical Institute sthapit karne ke liye prerit kiya.

Entrepreneurship beshak modern America tak hi seemit nahi hai. Lekin U.S. modern capitalism aur industry ka murt roop raha hai, jisne entrepreneurship aur innovation ki sanskriti ko badhava diya hai. Zyadatar sabse bade industrialists aur entrepreneurs American hone ki pravritti rakhte hain — aur woh bhi vaigyanik backgrounds wale jo jaante the ki latest technological advancements ko kaise popular (aur monetise) kiya jaye. Franklin, Edison, Hughes aur Jobs ke sath, yeh baat nahi hai ki unhone badi sampatti ik इकट्ठा ki jo unhe itna prabhavshali banati hai. Yeh haqeeqat hai ki woh visionaries the, jinhone apni business activity ke zariya status quo ko badal diya, market mein naye ideas aur products laaye, aur aisa karke duniya ko gehre roop se badal diya.

## Humanitarians (INSAANIYAT KE SEVAK / MANAVTAVADI)

Albert Schweitzer, modern philosophy ke itihaas mein ek uncha sthaan rakhne wali hasti (towering figure), ne un sabhi kshetron mein mahatvapurna bauddhika aur sanskritik yogdan (intellectual and cultural contributions) diya jismein unhone kaam kiya: philosophy, music, medicine, theology aur humanitarianism. Yadyapi Schweitzer ki soch par kuch hadd tak Indian philosophy ka prabhav tha (jis par unhone ek kitaab bhi likhi), woh bhi mukhya roop se Christian framework ke tehat kaam karte the. Unhone Kaiser-Wilhelm-Universität of Straßburg mein Protestant theology ka adhyayan kiya aur Sorbonne mein *The Religious Philosophy of Kant* par apni Ph.D. dissertation poori ki, jiske baad woh Straßbourg ke church Saint-Nicolas mein ek deacon bane. Theology mein apni licentiate poori karne ke sath, unhe ek curate ke roop mein niyukt (ordained) kiya gaya aur agle saal woh Theological College of Saint Thomas ke provisional Principal bane. 1906 mein unhone *Geschichte der Leben-Jesu-Forschung* publish kiya jiska sabse pehle William Montgomery dwara English mein anuvaad kiya gaya tha aur 1910 mein *The Quest for the Historical Jesus* ke roop mein publish kiya gaya tha.

Mukhya roop se insaaniyat ke prati apne dharmik kartavya (religious duty) se prerit hokar, Schweitzer 37 saal ki umar mein Doctorate in Medicine haasil karne ke baad ek doctor (physician) bane. Woh Paris Missionary Society mein shamil hue aur Lambaréné (ab Gabon) mein ek hospital sthapit kiya, jahan First World War ke dauran kai bimariyon ka ilaj kiya. Woh ek prasiddh musical scholar bhi the jinhone Bach ke sangeet ka adhyayan kiya aur German Organ reform movement (*Orgelbewegung*) ko prabhavit kiya. Universal ethical philosophy ke liye Schweitzer ki jaari khoj unke ‘Reverence of Life’ ke nirupan (formulation) mein charam par pahonchi, ek aisa concept jisne unhe Nobel Peace Prize dilaya. Mool roop se zindagi ka ek samarthan (affirmation) aur manav chetna par ek focus, ise unke is quote dwara sankshipt kiya gaya hai: ‘Main wahi zindagi hoon jo jeena chahti hai, aur main us zindagi ke beech astitva mein hoon jo jeena chahti hai.’ Is prize ke barabar haqdaar Africa mein European colonialism ke khilaf unki sakriyata (activism) bhi thi.

Kuch log doosron ki madad karne ko hi apna kaam banate hain. Yeh ‘humanitarians’ ek pukaar (calling), vulnerable (kamzor) logon ki suraksha karne ki ek zimmedari mahsoos karte hain. Woh aisa kai tareeqon se karte hain; aur yeh adbhut hai ki unmein se kitne polymaths the. Florence Nightingale, jo 20th century ki shuruat mein rahin, Crimean war ke dauran ek nurse ke roop mein apne kaam ke liye ek national hero banin, jahan woh ghayal sainikon ke liye medical conditions mein bade sudhar karte hue ‘Lady with the Lamp’ ke roop mein jaani gayi. Unhone baad mein London ke St Thomas’s Hospital mein apne nursing school ki sthapna ke sath professional nursing ki buniyaad rakhi — jo duniya ka pehla dharmanirpeksh (secular) nursing school tha. Us samay ke hisaab se ek ashadharan roop se achhi padhi-likhi mahila, Nightingale ek vilakshan mathematician thin jinhone statistical data ke graphical presentation ko popular banane mein madad ki aur unhe pie chart ke ek aise roop ko viksit karne ka shrey diya jata hai jise ab Nightingale Rose diagram ke roop mein jaana jata hai. Woh Royal Statistical Society ki pehli mahila member chuni gayi thin aur baad mein American Statistical Association ki ek honorary member banin.

Woh ek vivadaaspad theologian ke roop mein bhi ubharin, aur unki theodicy *Suggestions for Thought* ne burai ki samasya ko samdhanya aur universal reconciliation (sarvabhoomik mel-milap) ke idea ko badhava diya. Woh ek ashadharan roop se bahumukhi writer thin, jinhone medicine, feminism, human development, theology, mysticism, mathematics aur doosre kshetron mein yogdan diya. Unke lekhon ko unki mrityu ke baad 16-volume *Collected Works of Florence Nightingale* ke roop mein publish kiya gaya tha.

Teen baar Nobel Peace Prize Nominee rahe Russian Nicholas Roerich bhi South Asia mein esoterism ke Agni Yoga School ki sthapna karke duniya ke sabse pramukh theosophists mein se ek bane. Yeh 20th century ki shuruat mein arts aur humanities mein ek ashadharan roop se vividh career ke baad hua. Woh 7,000 se zyada paintings aur drawings ke output ke sath apni peedhi ke pramukh oil painters mein se ek bane. Woh World of Art Society ke Chairman, Imperial Society for the Encouragement of the Arts ke director aur Asia, Europe aur North America mein apni yatraon ke dauran kai arts aur culture institutions ke founder the. Woh ek pramukh stage aur costume designer bhi bane, jinhone *Ballet Russes*, *Prince Igor* aur *The Rite of Spring* jaise bade paimane ke productions par kaam kiya, aur London mein Covent Garden Theatre ke sath-sath Chicago Opera ke liye ek stage designer ke roop mein kaam kiya. Unhone 1917 ki krantiyon se pehle aur baad mein kai chhoti kahaniyan aur kavitaayein likhi thin, jinmein prasiddh *Flowers of Morya* cycle shamil hai. Ek architect ke roop mein unhone purani Russian architecture ke sanrakshan (preservation) ka aagrah kiya aur Russia mein pehla Buddhist temple design kiya. Roerich ek shauqeen archaeologist bhi the jinhone expeditions par vyapak yatra ki.

1900s ki shuruat mein, do preranadaayak hastiyon ne kshetron ki ek vividhta mein apne kaam ke zariya duniya ke alag-alag hisson mein utpeedit samudayon (oppressed communities) ko sashakt banaya. Australia mein, Aboriginal polymath David Ngunaitponi ne ek scientist, engineer, inventor, mythologist, short-story writer aur activist ke roop mein shreshthta haasil ki, aise samay mein jab aborigines aise role models dhoondhne ke liye sangharsh kar rahe the jinhone dikhaya ki woh aise kaarname poore karne mein kisi bhi white man ke jitne hi kshamatawan the. Unka shuruati career vividh tha. Woh ek boot maker ke apprentice the aur unhe mission organist ke roop mein niyukt kiya gaya tha, aur phir unhone ek bookkeeper ke roop mein kaam kiya. Unke aavishkaron mein ek anti-gravitational device, ek multiradial wheel aur ek sheep-shearing hand-piece, perpetual motion machine, centrifugal motor aur ek mechanical propulsion device shamil the. Pre-World War I helicopter ka unka design boomerang par aadharit tha aur unhone light ke polarisation par research kiya. Woh ballistics par ek maanyata-prapt adhikari bhi the. Lekin beshak, unhe in kaarnamon ke liye saraha nahi jayega. Woh Aboriginal rights ke liye ek sakht abhiyan-karta bane aur unki paramparik kahaniyan, jo 1929 mein *Native Legends* aur phir baad mein *Legendary Tales of the Australian Aborigines* ke roop mein publish hui thin, ne na sirf videshiyon ko indigenous culture ke baare mein shikshit karne mein ek badi bhoomika nibhayi, balki, shayad usse bhi zyada mahatvapurna, aboriginal culture ki garima (dignity) ko bahal kiya aur aborigines ko us virasat se phir se jodne ka mauqa diya jise aupniveshikon (colonists) dwara kamzor kar diya gaya tha.

Ngunaitponi ki tarah, George W. Carver 19th-century ke African American context mein ek manavtavadi (humanitarian) ke sath-sath ek prachur inventor (prolific inventor) the. Unhone shuruat mein college mein sangeet ka adhyayan kiya, lekin phir ek prasiddh painter bane. Ek poori tarah se aatm-nirbhar operator, woh apne khud ke paints banate the; school ke dauran kaam karte hue woh apne kapde khud seete, bunte aur crochet karte the; unhone baskets aur rugs bunna aur woodwork karna shuru kiya; unhone botany aur agriculture mein is hadd tak mahaarat haasil ki ki unhone dono mein moolyavan yogdan diya. Lekin woh ek aadaraniya sports teacher, aur ek dharmik vakta (orator) aur educator bhi the jinhone kamzor logon ko prerit kiya aur nasli sadbhav (racial harmony) ko badhava diya. Ek scientist aur inventor ke roop mein woh vishesh roop se peanut ke liye banaye gaye apne kai upayogikatanon ke liye jaane jaate the. Unhone jo kai doosre products ka aavishkar kiya unmein plastics, paints, dyes aur yahan tak ki ek prakar ka gasoline bhi shamil tha.

Itihaas ke sabse prabhavshali vyaktiyon ki ek badi sankhya ka polymaths hona simply ek aisa fact hai jise ignore karna bahut mushkil hai. Nezahualcóyotl, Akbar aur Churchill ab tak ke sabse prasiddh statesmen mein se hain. Confucius, Aristotle, Maimonides aur Ibn Sina dwara viksit ki gayi philosophies ka modern psyche par gehra prabhav pada hai. European Renaissance Leonardo, Bacon aur Copernicus ke kalaatmak (artistic) aur vaigyanik breakthroughs ke bina waisa nahi hota. Tagore, Diop aur Rizal ki sanskritik aur rajneetik vision ne imperial world order ko ghutno par lane mein madad ki. Johnson, Robeson aur Angelou United States mein utpeediton ki sanskritik akhandta (cultural integrity) ko bahal karne mein sahayak the. Goethe aur Steiner ke aakarshak vichar education, science, aur culture jaise vividh kshetron mein ghusna jaari rakhte hain. Yeh list anant hai. Yeh jaan-ne ke baad ki aise vyakti — jinki aam visheshata yeh thi ki woh bina kisi shak ke polymaths the — ka samaj par itna prabhav hai, parents, teachers, governments aur employers abhi bhi ek-aayami (one-dimensional), monomathic specialists ko banane par zor kyun dete hain? Pehle yeh samjhe bina ki specialisation ka yeh cult kaise aur kyun haavi hai, hum ise door karne ki koshish nahi kar sakte.

# Chapter 4: The Cult of Specialisation 

*(CHAPTER 4: SPECIALISATION KA CULT)*

Fast-forward to the twenty-first century. London ke Canary Wharf building ke stylish par frantic (bhag-daud bhare) environs (maahol) mein, meri mulakat Zack se hui, jo ek dashak pehle mere high school ka ek saathi tha. Woh neeche aane wali escalator par tha, impeccably dressed (behtareen kapdon mein tayaar), uske kandhe jhuke hue the, aankhein thakawat se jhuki hui thin, ek haath uske trouser ki pocket mein tha aur doosra uske backpack ko aadha bheenze hue tha. Jaise hi maine use neeche ke floor se pehchan liya, maine use surprise karne ke irade se uski escalator ke foot (neeche) par hi rukne ka faisla kiya. Mere paas ek flashback ke liye samay tha.

Har school mein ek ya do Zack hote hain. Woh type jo curriculum ke har subject mein chamakta hai, mind-blowing artwork produce karta hai, alag-alag musical instruments aasaani se bajata hai, school plays mein lead roles leta hai aur ek se zyada sports teams ki captaincy karta hai. Zack woh archetypical (adarsh) schoolboy all-rounder tha jisse sabhi ko uchi umeedein thin. Ek dashak se bhi kam samay baad, City mein escalator ke bottom par wapas, Zack mujhe schoolboy stardom ke baad se apni life ke baare mein batata hai.

Usne ek achhi university se economics padha, uske baad accounting aur finance mein master’s degree ki, jiske baad usne ek professional financial qualification complete ki aur luxury goods market par focus karne wala ek derivatives researcher banne ke liye City mein ek investment bank join kiya. Yeh poori tarah saaf nahi tha ki woh din mein solah (sixteen) ghante kaam karne par embarrassed (sharminda) tha ya proud (garv) tha. Yeh sab kaafi impressive lag raha tha; lekin hamari conversation se ek baat ne mujhe strike kiya. Zack ne apne saare numerous (kai saare) doosre talents aur interests—sport, art, music, science, drama, literature, languages, academia aur current affairs—sabhi se samjhauta (compromise) kar liya tha taaki woh kisi aasi cheez par micro-focus kar sake jo saaf taur par uske lifelong passion ya dream se bahut door thi. Yeh compromise inadvertent (unjaane mein hua) tha. Indeed, derivative researching zyadatar kisi average job ke mukable ek bada earner tha; lekin kisi tarah mujhe shakk tha ki wahi akela reason tha. Uski apparent success (saaf dikhti kamyabi) par use congratulate karne ke baad, main yeh sochte hue ghar ki taraf nikal gaya ki Zack ke liye cheezein is tarah kyun hui thin.

Yeh jald hi crystal clear (sheeshe ki tarah saaf) ho gaya. Zack ki kahani koi un-common nahi thi; yeh darasal norm (aam riwaaj) thi. Hamara Western system—us poori duniya ke majority ke sath jo iski nakal karne par tuli hui lagti hai—jaise-jaise hum bade hote hain humein imperceptibly (bina pata chale) 'specialise' karne par majboor karta hai. For example Britain: 10 GCSEs, chaar A Levels, ek bachelor’s degree, ek zyada specialised masters degree, ek aur bhi zyada specialised job, jiske andar har kisi ko apni khud ki speciality dhoondhne aur use nikharna ke liye aur encourage kiya jata hai—itna zyada ki koi bhi additional pursuits (baahari shauk) is speciality se samjhauta karne ka risk badha dete hain. Yeh ek raasta hai; doosra aur bhi zyada be-reham (merciless) hai. Limited ya bina kisi qualifications ke school chhodo, ek 'trade' dhoondho, usse related ek job dhoondho, usme competence (kusalta) haasil karein aur uske baad poori life survival aur stability ke liye uspar nirbhar rahein. Chahe kitni bhi dhalan wali (steep) kyun na ho, aisa lagta hai ki hamari lives pyramids ki shakal le leti hain. Is pyramid ke top par baithkar, hum aksar secure (surakshit) hote hain lekin shaayad hi kabhi fulfilled (santusht) hote hain.

Sadly (afsos ki baat hai), hum ek khatarnak bhram (**perilous illusion**) ke tehat jee rahe hain. Humein yeh maanne ke liye program kiya gaya hai ki life ke kisi ek tukde wale aspect (**fragmented aspect**) par lifelong, exclusive dedication hi sach, identity, ya yahan tak ki rozi-roti (**livelihood**) paane ka akela tareeqa hai. Yeh ek myth (jhooth) hai, agar kabhi koi raha ho toh. Hum yeh samajhne mein fail ho jaate hain ki duniya ke is ek aspect ko uske parivaar se toda ya phada gaya hai aur ise saaf aur sakht seemaon (**stringent boundaries**) ke sath apni khud ki ek duniya ke roop mein package kar diya gaya hai. Agar yeh 'field' hamare upar thopa nahi jata (jaisa ki duniya bhar ke zyadatar logon ke liye hai) toh kisi ek ko chunne, usme rehne aur jitna jaldi ho sake uski seemaon ke andar khud ko band kar lene ki zaroorat toh zaroor thopi jaati hai. Humein kaun majboor karta hai? Parents, educational institutions, employers, governments ya yahan tak ki society khud, jo life ke saare areas mein fragmentation (tukde) aur hyper-specialisation ko banaye rakhne ke liye evolve hui hai.

'Invisible hand' (adishya haath) ki tarah, yeh apne aap mein ek force (taqat) ban gaya hai, jise un logon dwara promote aur sustain kiya jata hai jinki yeh sabse zyada seva karta hai. Is sense mein, hyper-specialisation ek ideology (vichardhara) ban gaya hai, aur waise hi masses (aam janta) tak 'cheezon ko karne ke saaf tareeqe' ke roop mein phelaya gaya hai. Being aur thinking ke is purane ho chuke mode se khud ko azaad (**emancipate**) karne ke liye, pehle is baat ke prati conscious (jagruk) hona important hai ki hum pehli jagah par aisi hyper-specialised society kaise aur kyun bane.

## The Evolution of Specialisation (SPECIALISATION KA VIKAS)

## Are we wired to be ‘specialists’? (Kya hum ‘specialists’ banne ke liye wired hain?)

Kya hum ‘specialists’ banne ke liye bane (wired) hain? Kai vaigyanik yeh maante hain ki manav dimaag (human brain) ek aise tareeqe se viksit hua hai jo thriving (samriddh hone) ke bajaye surviving (jeevit rehne) par dhyan kendrit karta hai. Aisa karte hue, isne ek aisa mechanism viksit kiya hai jiske zariya duniya ki woh sabhi cheezein jo turant ke survival se seedhe taur par judi nahi hain, unhe automatic roop se sochne ki prakriya se baahar kar diya jata hai. ‘Dimaag ek aisi machine hai jise khud ko samajhne ke liye nahi, balki survive karne ke liye assemble kiya gaya hai,’ biologist aur ‘Darwin ke uttaradhikari’ E.O. Wilson zor dete hain. ‘Kyunki yeh do uddeshya mool roop se alag hain, science se factual knowledge ki madad ke bina mann duniya ko sirf chhote tukdon mein dekhta hai. Yeh duniya ke un hisson par spotlight daalta hai jinhe use agle din tak jeevit rehne ke liye jaan-na zaroori hai, aur baaki ko andhere ke hawale kar deta hai.’ Yeh ishara karta hai ki specialise karna vaastav mein ek aantarik sangyanatmak pravritti (innate cognitive tendency) hai. Lekin yeh sirf aadhi kahani hai — shabdash: (literally).

Aisa microfocus mukhya roop se dimaag ke left hemisphere ka kaam hai, jo linear, reductive soch ke liye zimmedar hai (right hemisphere ke mukable, jo intuitive, creative aur holistic soch ke liye zimmedar hai). Social systems jo left-brain thinking ko badhava dete hain, isliye reductive, narrow-focused specialisation ki ek sanskriti viksit karte hain. Psychiatrist aur philosopher Iain McGilchrist ke anusar, ‘left hemisphere thinking ki aadat-en sanskriti mein khud ko jaari rakhenge jise banane mein woh madad karte hain, jisse ek positive feedback loop paida hota hai. Mujhe lagta hai ki hum ab wahin hain.’ Woh kehte hain ki dimaag ke left hemisphere aur ek ghatna ke roop mein specialisation ke beech ka rishta ‘jaatil aur aapas mein ek-doosre ko majboot karne wala, prakriti mein chakrayi (circular in nature) hai.’ Jis hadd tak left-brain thinking ko apnaya gaya hai aur logon ke education, kaam aur aam jeevan ke prati drishtikon ko prabhavit karne ki anumati di gayi hai, woh samajik, shaikshanik aur vaicharik karakon (social, educational and ideological factors) ki vividhta dwara tay hota hai.

Specialisation ka, beshak, samaj mein apna ek sthaan hai. Ibn Khaldun ne ek vyavasthit samajik sangathan (orderly social organisation) ki zaroorat par zor diya aur dekha ki ek vyakti, jise khilana aur surakshit rakhna zaroori hai, akele us khane ko haasil karne, process karne aur uski raksha karne ke liye sab kuch nahi kar sakta. Vividh karya — auzar banana, khana process karna aur ityaadi — jo aapas mein labhdayak ban jaate hain, isliye ek aabadi ke beech ek aise tareeqe se baante jaate hain jo samaj ko kaam (function) ke anusar khandit (segments) karta hai. Yeh aapas mein nirbharta (interdependence) aur isliye samajik ekta (social cohesion) paida karta hai. Samaj ka yeh zaroori khandikaran (segmentation) hi hai — ek prakar ka functionalism — jisne shuruati insaanon mein specialise karne ki pravritti paida ki.

Tab se, halanki, kuch samajik pranalion aur ideologically ne aisi paristhitiyan aur ek aisi sanskriti paida ki hai jo un logon ke hiton ko surakshit karne ke liye specialisation ko majboot karti hai jinki yeh seva karti hai. Inmein European feudalism (kisaan aur landlord dono ke liye), African aur Pacific tribalism (specialist tribal clans ka matlab tha ki aapka trade aapke clan aur vanshavali ke anusar pehle se tay tha), Indian caste system (vyaktiyon ko unka 'jeevan ka uddeshya' virasat mein mila, jisne badle mein unke peshe ko tay kiya) aur sabse haal hi mein Western industrialisation, bureaucracy aur corporate aur academic specialisation ('division of labour', jo baad mein poori duniya mein phael gaya jise West ne gulam/colonise banaya) shamil hain.

Modern specialisation ki darshanik shuruat (philosophical origins) post-Enlightenment Western intellectual paradigm mein paayi ja sakti hai jisne gyaan ki vyapakta ko poore roop mein amanageable samjha. Isliye padhai ke prati ek behadd critical, reductionist drishtikon, jiski shuruat French philosopher René Descartes ne ki thi, ne intellectual specialisation ki taraf jhukav ko aage badhaya, jisne pehle ke daur se polymath ki prakriti ko badal diya. Is baat ko Diderot ki *Encyclopédie* ke 'gens de lettres' article mein swikar kiya gaya tha, jisne ghoshna ki, *‘la science universelle n’est plus à la portée de l’homme’* (Universal knowledge ab insaan ki pahonch mein nahi hai). Isne us baat ko chinhit kiya jise historian Peter Burke ‘har kshetra ke gyaan se kai kshetron ke gyaan tak [polymath ka] ek bauddhika peeche hatna (intellectual retreat)’ sandarbhit karte hain. Charles van Doren ne apni *History of Knowledge* mein Enlightenment ke dauran specialisation ke uday ke liye ek aur vyakhya ka sujhaav diya, jiske baare mein unhone kaha ki yeh Renaissance ke baad polymathy se moh-bhang (disillusionment) se juda tha.

> Renaissance ka kamyab ‘Renaissance Men’ produce karne mein asafal rehna bina notice kiye nahi gaya. Agar Leonardo, Pico, Bacon jaise log aur kai doosre lagbhag utne hi prasiddh log har cheez ke baare mein sab kuch jaan-ne ke apne maane gaye sapne mein kamyab nahi ho sake, toh chhote mardon ko koshish karne ki dharana nahi banani chahiye. Vikalp swayam-spasht (self-evident) ho gaya: ek kshetra mein mahaarat haasil karein jabki doosre apne kshetron mein mahaarat haasil karein. Poora karne mein bahut aasan, is raaste ne ek zyada aaramdayak shaikshanik samuday (academic community) ki taraf badhaya. Ab ek kshetra mein ek adhikari ko sirf apne kshetra ke experts ke sath muqabla karne ki zaroorat thi.

Is sthiti ki saaf nirarthakta (apparent futility) ne kai intellectuals ko ek niche area ka daava karne ke liye prerit kiya jise unhone mahsoos kiya ki yeh hi ekmatra tareeqa tha jisse woh pehchan (identity), uddeshya (purpose) — aur kai mamlon mein ek pesha (vocation) ka ahasas viksit kar sakte the. Is haqeeqat ne, iske parinaamswarup ubharne wale vishayon aur up-vishayon (disciplines and sub-disciplines) ki sankhya mein ghatatmak badhotari (exponential rise) ke sath milkar, use paida kiya jise ab 'knowledge ka crisis' ya 'information anxiety' kaha jata hai. Asal mein Enlightenment ke mukhya bauddhika projects mein se ek 'information ko order, codify aur classify karna' tha, jisne encyclopaedias ke uday ko badhava diya.

1800s tak intellectual specialisation is tarah ek haqeeqat ban gaya aur gyaan ke alag-alag vishay universities mein academic departments ke roop mein sansthagath (institutionalised) kiye ja rahe the. 'Discipline' shabd — jo control ya restraint ki sainy dharana (military notion) se import kiya gaya tha — academic context mein ab sakhti se roke gaye bauddhika kshetron (tightly restrained intellectual domains) ko sandarbhit karne ke liye upayog kiya jaane laga. Knowledge management ka yeh model gulam banaye gaye ilaqon mein export kiya ja raha tha (sanskriti, shiksha pranali, shasan tantra aur pūñjīvādī arthvyavastha ki tarah hi), jahan yeh tezi se norm ban raha tha. Europe mein, amateur societies aur specialised journals ke uday ne, 19th century mein universities ke research centres ke sath-sath teaching institutions mein badlav ke sath milkar is andolan ko aur majboot kiya. Knowledge ka khandikaran (compartmentalisation) poore zoor-shor par tha; jismein har khand par ek ivory tower priest dwara monopoly ki ja rahi thi.

Jaise 18th century ke ant ne Europe aur North America mein intellectual specialisation ki shuruat ko chinhit kiya, 19th century ke ant ne duniya bhar mein professional ya occupational specialisation ki shuruat ko chinhit kiya. Is ghatna ke vikas ko do shaktishali sansthaon ki sthapna dwara utprerit (catalysed) kiya gaya tha: government bureaucracy aur corporation. Yeh kramashah imperialism aur industrialisation dwara laye gaye the; dono ne labor ke division par nirbhar ek dhancha laagu kiya; aur dono ko Western (European aur phir American) hegemonic system dwara vishwa स्तर par phelaya gaya tha jisne, Marx ke shabdon mein, 'apni khud ki chhavi ke baad ek duniya' (a world after its own image) banane ki koshish ki.

Pramukh vicharkon, vishesh roop se Franz Kafka, Karl Marx aur Max Weber jaise logon ne state bureaucrats aur industrial workers ke specialisation (aur isliye 'dehumanisation') par prasiddh roop se tippani ki. Marx ne bureaucracy aur corporation ko tulnatmak pratiroop (comparable counterparts) ke roop mein dekha — ek hi sansthagath dharana ke state aur private versions — aur afsos ke sath tippani ki ki unhone 'doctor, lawyer, priest, poet, science ke aadmi ko apne paid wage-labourers mein badal diya.' Unki Alienation Theory ne samjaya ki kaise utpadan prakriya (production process) ki specialised aur dohrayi jaane wali prakriti workers ko 'aatmik aur bhaautik roop se ek machine ki sthiti mein depressed' hone ka kaaran banti hai. Specialisation ka kaaran, unhone tarka diya, labor ke division ki ek neeti thi, jo factory se aage phael gayi aur ek samajik norm ban gayi.

> Division of labour na sirf aarthik, balki samaj ke har doosre kshetra par seva karta hai, aur har jagah men ko specialise aur sort karne ke us sabhi ko gherne wale system ki buniyaad rakhta hai, doosri saari kshamtaon ki keemat par ek aadmi mein ek hi kshamta ka vikas.

Weber, jinhone theoretical roop se sangathan ke sabse kushal roop ke roop mein bureaucracy ka paksh liya, ne phir bhi vyakti par iske seemit karne wale prabhav ko pehchana. 'Specialised kaam tak seemit hona, insaan ki Faustian universality ke tyaag ke sath jise yeh aahwan karta hai,' unhone kaha 'modern duniya mein kisi bhi moolyavan kaam ki ek shart hai.' Europe (vishesh roop se British) colonialism ke duniya bhar mein phaelne ke sath, bureaucracy ka yeh system (aur is tarah iska division of labour) zyadatar colonies mein export kiya gaya tha, sabse vishesh roop se India, Kenya, Malaysia, South Africa, Australia aur beshak, Americas mein. Jabki specialised bureaucracy ka yeh roop 19th aur 20th centuries mein vishesh roop se ek European (aur isliye ek vyapak aupniveshik) ghatna tha, hum jaante hain ki kahin aur aur itihaas ke alag-alag points par state bureaucracies ke sath hamesha aisa nahi tha. Praacheen tribal chiefdoms aur Chinese imperial dynasties ke bureaucrats, udaharan ke liye, generalists ke roop mein prasiddh the aur unmein se kai, jaise Zhang Heng, Shen Kuo aur Su Song, vaastav mein ullekhniya polymaths the.

19th century ke ant mein modern corporation ke uday ne kaam ki prakriti aur manav jeevan-shaili par aur isliye polymath ke bhagya par gehra prabhav daala. Shuruat mein Royal charters ke sath private entities ke roop mein sthapit, woh tezi se trading aur investing organisations ban gaye, jo 14th Amendment (mool roop se African American mukt gulamon ke adhikaaron ki raksha ke liye design kiya gaya) ke baad, vyakti ke adhikaaron ko dharan kar chuke the aur shareholder returns ko kisi bhi doosri cheez se upar rakhne ke liye kanooni roop se bandhe the. Unhone 20th century ki shuruat mein viksit nayi krantikari technologies ko commercialise kiya, jaise telephone, phonograph, electricity aur aviation, aur mass production ka ek system banaya — jise 1913 mein Ford mass production car plant ki sthapna dwara prateekit kiya gaya aur Frederick Taylor dwara shuru kiye gaye labor management ke 'Efficiency School' dwara suvidhajanak banaya gaya — jisne labor ke mechanisation aur en masse specialisation ke uday mein ek naye daur ka aagaz kiya.

Ise shaikshanik pranali (educational system) dwara samarthan diya gaya tha, jisne in aarthik taqaton dwara zaroori resources ki poorti ki aur is tarah ek specialised samaj ko suvidhajanak banane aur banaye rakhne ke liye mukhya ban gaya. Europe ke post-industrialisation education model se behtar iska koi pradarshan nahi hai. 20th century se pehle, vishesh roop se Europe aur United States mein lekin kahin aur bhi, formal education kuch chune hue logon ka visheshadhikar (privilege) thi. Industrial Revolution ke baad, industrial production ki zarooraton ko poora karne ke liye ek mass education system introduced kiya gaya tha. Durbhagya se, halanki, is vyapak shiksha ki aalgaav-kaari (alienating) prakriti bauddhika ekta (intellectual unity), sanshleshan (synthesis) aur isliye samajh ki keemat par aayi.

Curriculum ko factory labourers produce karne ke liye design kiya gaya tha jo zyada se zyada instruction manuals padh sakein, aur production line ke ek vishesh kshetra par specialise kar sakein. Isliye vishayon ko compartmentalised kiya gaya tha aur ek doosre se alag padhaya jata tha, jismein students ko khud factory conveyor belt par products ki tarah treat kiya jata tha, jo process ke dauran alag-alag points par incremental input praapt karte the. Bachhon ko 'curriculum subjects' ke roop mein package kiye gaye gyaan ke in jebon ke beech sambandhon ko dekhne ke liye encourage nahi kiya gaya tha. British philosopher of education Alfred North Whitehead, jo industrialised Britain ke charam ke dauran rahe, ne pehchana ki shiksha ka yeh drishtikon bachhon ko har vishay ki prasangikta (relevance) ko samajhne se rok raha tha — unke apne jeevan ke liye bhi, lekin vyapak duniya ke liye bhi. Unki chinta ko niche diye gaye gadyansh mein sabse behtareen dhang se sankshipt kiya gaya tha:

> Is ekal ekta (single unity) ke bajaye, hum bachhon ko Algebra offer karte hain, jisse kuch nahi nikalta; Geometry, jisse kuch nahi nikalta; Science, jisse kuch nahi nikalta; History, jisse kuch nahi nikalta; ek do Bhashayein, jinpar kabhi mahaarat haasil nahi hui; aur aakhir mein, sabse ubaao, Literature, jise Shakespeare ke naatakon dwara pratinidhitv kiya jata hai, philological notes aur plot ya character ke sankshipt vishleshan ke sath jise mool roop se yaad mein samarpit kiya jana hai. Kya aisi list ke baare mein kaha ja sakta hai ki yeh Life ka pratinidhitv karti hai, jaisa ki ise jeevit rehne ke beech mein jaana jata hai? Iske baare mein jo sabse achha kaha ja sakta hai woh yeh hai ki yeh vishay-soochi (table of contents) ki ek tezi hai jise koi devta dimaag mein chala sakta hai jabki woh ek duniya banane ki soch raha tha, aur abhi tak yeh tay nahi kiya hai ki ise ek sath kaise rakha jaye.

20th century ke mod tak, phir, duniya ke teen sabse prabhavshali sansthaon — academia, government aur corporation — har ek ne labor ke sakht division ko apna liya tha, jeevan ke har kshetra mein hyper-specialisation ki ek nayi sanskriti sthapit ki thi; ek aisa jhukav jo aaj tak norm ban jayega.

Kai millenniums mein socio-psychological conditioning ki is mehnat-bhari prakriya ke kaaran, ab logon ke dimaag mein jaankari ke ghatatmak guna (exponential multiplication) aur 'kaam' ki ek vishesh dharana ke sath milkar, humne ek atyadhik specialised samaj viksit kiya hai jo logon ko lagbhag vishesh roop se unke 'trade', 'occupation', ya 'field' dwara pehchanne par tula hua lagta hai. Aaj lagbhag kisi bhi samajik ya peshevar gathering mein, log iske aadhar par decision/judgments banane ke liye ubaal rahe hain. Us decisive sawaal tak pahonchne ki ek automatic majboori hoti hai: ‘Toh, aap kya karte hain?’ Aur woh ek seedhe jawab ki umeed karte hain.

Psychologically, hum kisi ko ‘an electrician-musician-mother-of-six who was once a physician-athlete with a love for poetry’ ke bajaye ‘electrician’ ke roop mein sandarbhit karna zyada aasan paate hain. Yeh alag-alag samajon mein surnames ke banne ke tareeqe mein pratibimbit hota hai. Britain mein, udaharan ke liye, kai modern surnames (jaise Carpenter, Mason, Taylor, Harper, Smith, Piper, aur ityaadi) medieval feudalism ki virasat hain jahan logon ko unke trade ya occupation dwara sandarbhit kiya jata tha, jaisa ki India mein bhi tha (Bandukwala, ‘hathiyaron wala banda’, ya Lightwalla, ‘bijli wala banda’, aur ityaadi) aur kahin aur bhi. Stigmatisation ek sangyanatmak pravritti (cognitive tendency) hai, jo aksar prevailing socioeconomic system dwara ek samajik samjhoute (social convention) mein sansthagath ki jaati hai.

Toh ek baar jab kisi vyakti se koi kalank/stigma jud jata hai, toh us vyakti ke liye duniya ko yeh samjhana behadd mushkil ho jata hai ki uski zindagi ke doosre, barabar abhinna pehlu bhi hain. Asal mein use aksar khud ko samjhane mein musibat hoti hai. Aaj log doosron ko dibbon (boxes) mein rakhne ke liye majboor mahsoos karte hain, jinhe woh phir sakhti se band karte hain aur saaf taur par label karte hain. Label kiye gaye logon ke liye (jo hum sab mool roop se ban jaate hain) aavantit dibbe (allocated box) se baahar nikalna aur doosre mein pravesh karne ki koshish karna behadd mushkil ho jata hai. Yahan tak ki agar koi aisa karne mein saksham hai, toh log apne labels ke sath bahut kanjoos hain; woh shayad hi ek se zyada jaari karte hain. Artistic polymath Billy Childish, ek prasiddh kavi, novelist, painter aur musician ne samaj dwara unhe ek dhabbe/pigeonhole mein daalne ki jaari koshishon ke baare mein baat ki:

> Main ek loose cannon hoon. Press ke anusar main pehle ek musician hua karta tha. Ab mujhe musician banne ki anumati nahi hai, mujhe ek artist banna padega. Main ek 'purva musician' hoon bhale hi maine sirf pichle saal hi 7 albums produce kiye hon! British Art Show mere paintings ko andar nahi aane dega, ise meri poems hona padega. Aur theek usi samay meri poetry ko art ke roop mein rakha gaya tha, Poetry Society ne poocha ki kya woh mere magazine ke cover ke roop mein mere paintings ka upayog kar sakte hain! Art world mujhe ek musician ke roop mein dekhega, music world mujhe ek painter samjhega — yeh aisa tha, 'aap ise rakh lo . . . hum nahi chahte ki woh hamare aangan mein peshab kare.'

Yeh rukawat sabhi multitalented ya multi-ambitioned logon ko samna karni padti hai, chahe woh fashion models hon jo acting mein transition karne ki koshish karte hain, singers jo ek business venture shuru karte hain, ya jab scientists art ya literature mein apna haath aazmate hain. Publishers ek writer ko ek nishchit prakar ke roop mein 'brand' karna chahte hain aur ise encourage karenge; art dealers chahte hain ki artists 'unke' style mein kaam karna jaari rakhein taaki pehchanne yogya ban sakein; investors chahenge ki unke entrepreneurs 'apne sector se tike rahein.' Sirf ek novelist hona hi kaafi specialised nahi hai; aapko ek genre-specific novelist hona chahiye jo ek vishesh style se tika rahe. Stephen King ke kadd ke bahumukhi lekhakon ne pigeonhole na hone ki koshish mein kai chhadm-naamon (pseudonyms) ke tehat likha hai. Employers khud multitalented logon ko hire karne ke liye bahut nervous hote hain.

Yeh pravritti, jo dimaag ki schematic organisation process ka kaaran aur utpad (product) dono hai, is dharana par aadharit hai ki (kam se kam ek maana gaya) ekagrachitt focus (single-minded focus) behtar utpadakta (productivity), kushalta (efficiency) aur isliye bade aarthik inaam ke barabar hai. Isliye samaj ka ek nirantar khandikaran (compartmentalisation) ek duschakra (vicious circle) paida karta hai, jismein har koi apne khud ke 'field bubble' mein rehta hai — lawyers, investment bankers, athletes aur musicians har ek ya toh doosre se irshya karte hain ya unhe neecha dikhate hain; koi bhi doosre se relate nahi kar sakta. Har koi apne field ko jeeta hai, khata hai, sota hai, peeta hai, yeh unke jaan-ne aur hone ka tareeqa ban jata hai, yeh unhe aur unki chetna ko paribhashit karna shuru kar deta hai aur woh har ek apni khud ki bhasha, apna jargon, samajik ghera, yahan tak ki apna mazaak ka ahasas viksit karte hain.

Is bubble-creation ko predictive analytics ki nayi technology dwara aur badhava diya ja raha hai, jise lagbhag sabhi online businesses dwara ek prakar ki 'digital colonialism' sthapit karne mein niyukt kiya gaya hai. Hum digital aur social media platforms ke zariya apne vyavhaar ke baare mein jaankari ke dher dene mein khush hain, lekin badle mein hamara data ik इकट्ठा kiya ja raha hai aur aakhirkar un algorithms ko rachne ke liye upayog kiya ja raha hai jo humein batate hain ki hum kya chahte hain ya zaroorat hai hamare apne instincts se bhi tezi se. YouTube ko hi lein, udaharan ke liye — agar aap signed in hain toh sirf aapke maujooda dekhne ki aadatton se jude videos hi recommend kiye jaate hain. Isi tarah, Amazon sirf aapke maujooda interests se jude books hi recommend karta hai (aapki khoj aur khareedne ki aadatton dwara pradarshit).

Machine learning par aadharit yeh jatil algorithms kushal recommendation ke ek system ka bhram de sakte hain, lekin vaastav mein advertisers lagatar apne 'target market' ki talash mein rehte hain aur isliye maang karenge ki digital marketplaces aur social media platforms ke paas ek saaf taur par shrenibaddh grahak aadhar (categorised customer base) ho taaki woh ek behadd targeted phir bhi alag vigyapan abhiyan (advertising campaign) racha sakein. Aisa karne mein, hamari digital upasthiti (digital presence) ko bhi pigeonhole kiya ja raha hai. Toh jabki hum daava karte hain ki duniya hamari ungliyon par hai, hum vaastav mein jo kuch bhi karte hain woh yeh hai ki khud ko isse band kar lete hain. Yeh Big Data aur psychographics ka daur hai, jo Enlightenment project ki tarah, jaankari ki badi matra mein vyavastha aur codification lane ka ek prayas hai, aur is tarah gyaan (aur vyaktiyon) ko suvidhajanak dibbon (boxes) mein rakh raha hai.
