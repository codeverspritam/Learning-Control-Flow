# Book 1

### 1

Main kal Ariston ke bete Glaucon ke sath Piraeus gaya tha, goddess [327] se prayer karne ke liye.

### 2

Saath hi main festival dekhna chahta tha, yeh dekhne ke liye ki woh ise kaise conduct (aayojit) karenge, kyunki yeh pehli baar tha jab ise celebrate kiya ja raha tha.

### 3

Athenians ki parade mujhe bahut excellent lagi, aur Thracians dwara lagaya gaya show bhi utna hi impressive (prabhavshali) tha, maine socha. Humne apni prayers offer kin, festival dekha, aur phir town [b] ki taraf apni wapsi ki journey shuru ki. Hum pehle se hi apne ghar ke raste par the jab humein Cephalus ke bete Polemarchus ne dekha. Usne apne slave ko hamare peeche daudne aur humse unka intazar karne ke liye bolne ko kaha. Slave ne peeche se mere cloak ko khincha, aur kaha, ‘Polemarchus kehta hai ki aap unka intazar karein.’ Main peeche muda, aur usse poocha ki uska master kahan hai.

‘Woh wahan hain,’ usne kaha, ‘aapke peeche aa rahe hain. Unka intazar karein.’

‘Hum karenge,’ Glaucon ne kaha.

Kuch hi moments mein Polemarchus Glaucon ke bhai Adeimantus, Nicias ke bete Niceratus, aur kuch doosron [c] ke sath hamare paas pahoncha. Woh zaroor procession (juloos) dekh rahe the, zaahir taur par. Aur Polemarchus ne kaha, ‘Aisa lagta hai ki aap sabhi city ki taraf wapas ja rahe hain, Socrates. Aap ruk nahi rahe hain, phir?’

‘Yeh ek kaafi achha guess (anuman) hai,’ maine jawab diya.

‘Kya aap dekh rahe hain ki hum kitne log hain?’ usne poocha.

‘Haan.’

‘Well, phir,’ usne kaha, ‘aapko ya toh in sabhi logon par jeet haasil karni hogi, ya phir yahan rukna hoga.’

‘Ek aur sambhavna (possibility) hai,’ maine kaha. ‘Hum aapko persuade (raazi) kar sakte hain ki aap humein jaane dein.’

‘Aur kya aap sach mein sochte hain ki aap humein persuade kar sakte hain,’ usne kaha, ‘agar humne sunne se hi inkaar kar diya?’

‘Beshak nahi,’ Glaucon ne kaha.

‘Us case mein, apna decision is assumption (dharana) par banayein ki hum sunne nahi wale hain.’

‘Kya aapne torch race ke baare mein nahi suna?’ Adeimantus ne joda [328]. ‘Aaj shaam ko, horseback (ghodesawari) par, goddess ke sammaan mein?’

‘Horseback par?’ maine kaha. ‘Yeh toh kuch naya hai. Kya aapka matlab horseback par ek relay race se hai, ek doosre ko torches pass karte hue?’

‘Haan,’ Polemarchus ne kaha. ‘Aur woh ek all-night ceremony bhi karne wale hain, jo dekhne laayak honi chahiye. Hum dinner ke baad baahar ja sakte hain aur ise dekh sakte hain [b]. Wahan bahut se naye log honge. Hum unke sath kuch samay bitayein sakte hain, aur unse baat kar sakte hain. Please ruko. Please “haan” kaho.’

‘Aisa lagta hai ki humein rukna padega,’ Glaucon ne kaha.

‘Agar yeh aapka decision hai,’ maine kaha, ‘toh hum rukenge.’

Toh hum Polemarchus ke ghar wapas gaye, jahan humein Polemarchus ke bhai Lysias aur Euthydemus mile – saath hi Chalcedon ke Thrasymachus, Paeania deme

### 4

ke Charmantides, aur Aristonymus ke bete Cleitophon. Wahan, ghar mein, Polemarchus ke pita Cephalus [c] bhi the. Mujhe unhe dekhe hue bahut samay ho gaya tha, aur maine unhe kaafi boorha paaya. Woh ek garland (mala) pehne hue the, aur ek cushioned stool par baithe the. Woh abhi courtyard mein ek sacrifice conduct (aayojan) karke hate the.

### 5

Unke charon taraf stools ka ek circle tha, toh hum unke sath baith gaye.

Jaise hi unhone mujhe dekha, Cephalus ne mera welcome (swagat) karna shuru kiya. ‘Aap Piraeus mein humse milne aksar nahi aate, Socrates,’ usne kaha. ‘Aapko aana chahiye, halanki. Agar main abhi bhi itna strong (majboot) hota ki bina kisi kathinai ke town tak ki journey kar sakta, toh aapko yahan aane ki koi zaroorat nahi hoti [d]. Hum aapke paas aate. Lekin jaisi sthitiyan hain, aapko zyada aksar aana chahiye. Main aapko assure (aashwast) kar sakta hoon, apne liye bolte hue, ki jitna body ke pleasures fade (dheeme) hote hain, utna hi conversation (baatchit) ke liye kisi ki desire (ichha) aur taste badhta hai. Toh please in naye mardon ke sath kuch samay bitayein. Yahan aayein aur humse milein. Humein apne doston ke roop mein dekhein – apne family ke roop mein bhi.’

‘Badi khushi ke sath, Cephalus,’ maine jawab diya [e]. ‘Mujhe bahut boorhe logon se baat karna pasand hai. Yeh aisa hai jaise woh us raste par humse bahut aage hain jispar humein bhi shayad travel (yatra) karna padega. Mujhe lagta hai ki humein unse seekhna chahiye ki rasta kaisa hai – kya yeh steep (dhalandar) aur rough (khardura) hai, ya gentle (saamanya) aur easy (aasan). Vishesh roop se, main yeh sunna bahut pasand karunga ki yeh aapko kaisa lagta hai, ab jabki aap vaastav mein jeevan ke us samay par pahonch gaye hain jise poets “old age, the threshold (dehleez)” kehte hain.

### 6

Ispar aapki kya report hai? Kya aap ise jeevan ka ek kathin samay kahenge?’

‘Main aapko sateek roop se bataunga ki yeh mujhe kaisa lagta hai, Socrates [329]. Hamara ek group hai jo kaafi aksar milta hai. Hum sabhi lagbhag ek hi umar ke hain, toh hum purani proverb (kahavat) ke shabdon ka palan kar rahe hain.

### 7

Jab hum milte hain, toh unmein se zyadatar complain (shikayat) karna shuru kar dete hain; woh kehte hain ki woh un cheezon ko miss karte hain jinhe woh jawani mein enjoy karte the, aur woh apne sexual exploits (kaarnamon), apne drinking, apne feasting (daawaton), aur un pleasures se judi har cheez ko recall (yaad) karte hain. Woh upset ho jaate hain, jaise ki unhe koi bada loss (nuksan) hua ho – jaise tab unhone ek shaandar jeevan jiya tha, jabki ab woh zinda hi nahi hain [b]. Unmein se kuch old age ke prati unke families dwara dikhaye gaye respect ki kami ke baare mein bhi complain karte hain, aur is heading ke tehat woh old age ke khilaf grievances (shikayaton) ki ek litany (lambi list) recite karte hain. Mujhe lagta hai ki woh galat jagah par blame (dosha) laga rahe hain, Socrates. Agar old age blame ke laayak hoti, toh na sirf maine old age ke baare mein waisa hi mahsoos kiya hota, balki har us vyakti ne bhi kiya hota jo kabhi is umar tak pahoncha hai. Aur phir bhi main kai aise logon se mila hoon jo aise nahi hain – sabse vishesh roop se poet Sophocles. Main wahan ek baar tha jab kisi ne unse poocha, “Aapki sex life kaisi hai, Sophocles [c]? Kya aap abhi bhi kisi mahila se love karne ke capable (saksham) hain?”

“Is baare mein baat mat karo, mere achhe sir,” Sophocles ka jawab tha. “Yeh sabse bade relief (raahat) ke sath hai ki main isse escape (bach) gaya hoon. Kisi bhayanak aur frenzied (paagal) master se escape karne ki tarah.” Maine us samay ise ek achha jawab socha tha, aur main abhi bhi ise ek achha jawab manta hoon. Old age kul milakar us tarah ki cheezon se badi shanti aur aazadi ka samay hai.

‘Jab hamari appetites (bhookh/ichhaayein) fade hoti hain, aur humpar apni grip (pakad) dheeli karti hain, toh jo hota hai woh sateek roop se wahi hai jiski Sophocles baat kar rahe the [d]. Yeh paagal masters ke ek group se ek aakhri release (mukti) hai. Isme aur aapke family ke sath aapke relations (sambandhon) mein, sirf ek hi cheez responsible (zimmedar) hai, aur woh old age nahi, balki aapka character (charitra) hai. Un logon ke liye jo civilised (sabhya) aur contented (santusht) hain, toh old age bhi sirf ek halka burden (bojh) hai. Warna – un logon ke liye jo aise nahi hain – old age aur youth dono se nipatna kathin sabit hota hai.’

Main unke kahe se bahut impressed (prabhavit) hua, aur main chahta tha ki woh bolna jaari rakhein [e]. Toh maine unhe aage prompted (uksaaya) kiya: ‘Mujhe shak hai ki zyadatar log aappar vishwas nahi karte, Cephalus, jab aap yeh kehte hain. Woh sochte hain ki yeh aapka character nahi hai jo old age ko aapke liye aasan banata hai, balki yeh tathya hai ki aapke paas bahut paisa hai. Ameer logon ke paas, woh kehte hain, kai consolations (dilasa) hote hain.’

‘Aap sahi hain,’ usne kaha. ‘Woh mujhpar vishwas nahi karte. Aur jo woh kehte hain usme kuch sachai hai. Lekin utni sachai nahi hai jitna woh sochte hain. Themistocles ka prasiddh kehna yahan bahut point par hai [330]. Seriphus ke ek aadmi ne unke baare mein disparaging (apmaan-janak) remarks karna shuru kar diya, aur unhe batana ki unki shohrat unke apne merits (gona) ke kaaran nahi, balki unke city ke kaaran thi. Themistocles ka jawab tha ki yadyapi woh khud kabhi prasiddh nahi hue hote agar woh Seriphus mein paida hue hote, na hi doosra aadmi hua hota agar woh Athens mein paida hua hota. Wahi un logon par laagu hota hai jo ameer nahi hain, aur jo old age ko sehna kathin paate hain. Garibi mein, sahi temperament (swabhav) bhi old age ko poori tarah aasan nahi paayega, jabki galat temperament, daulat ki madad se bhi, kabhi khud ke sath shanti mein nahi rahega.’

‘Kya aapko apni zyadatar daulat inheritance (virasat) mein mili thi, Cephalus?’ maine poocha. ‘Ya iska zyadatar hissa waha paisa hai jo aapne khud banaya, apni inheritance ke upar?’

‘Kya maine isme joda, Socrates [b]? Jab paise banane ki baat aati hai, toh main apne grandfather aur apne father ke beech kahin hoon. Mere grandfather – mere namesake (hamnaam) – ko lagbhag utni hi daulat virasat mein mili thi jitni ab mere paas hai, aur unhone ise kai guna badhaya. Mere father Lysanias ne ise abhi jitna hai usse bhi kam kar diya. Main khush rahunga agar main in ladkon ke liye virasat mein mile paise se kam nahi, balki thoda sa zyada chhod sakoon.’

‘Mera poochhne ka kaaran,’ maine kaha, ‘yeh hai ki aap mujhe kabhi paise ke vishesh roop se shauqeen nahi lage [c]. Aur yeh aam taur par un logon ka attitude (ravaiya) hota hai jinhone ise khud nahi banaya hai. Zyadatar logon ki tulna mein, self-made men apne paise ke do-guna shauqeen hote hain. Jinhone daulat banayi hai woh sabse pehle apne paise ke prati devoted (samarpit) hote hain kyunki yeh unki apni creation (rachna) hai – jaise poets apni poems se pyaar karte hain, ya fathers apne bachhon se pyaar karte hain – aur doosri jagah par iske liye ki woh iske sath kya kar sakte hain, kisi bhi doosre ki tarah. Yeh unhe bahut poor company (kharab sangati) banata hai, kyunki woh paise ke alawa kisi bhi cheez mein koi value (mulya) nahi dekh sakte.’

‘Aap sahi hain,’ usne kaha.

‘Haan,’ maine kaha [d]. ‘Lekin mere paas aapke liye ek aur prashn hai. Aap kya kahenge ki sabse bada benefit (faayda) kya hai jo aapne badi daulat ke apne kabze se haasil kiya hai?’

‘Ek jise kai log vishwas karne ke prati inclined (jhuke) nahi ho sakte, agar main unhe bataoon. Lekin aap mere shabdon par vishwas kar sakte hain, Socrates, ki jab aap apni maut ke vichar ka samna karte hain, toh aap un cheezon ke baare mein darr aur anxiety (chinta) se visited (ghere) hote hain jinhone aapko pehle kabhi pareshan nahi kiya tha. Hades mein kya hota hai iske baare mein batayi gayi kahaniyan, ki jo koi bhi yahan unjust (a-nyaypoorn) hai use wahan iska bhugtan karna padega – kahaniyan jinpar aap ek baar hanse the – aapke dimaag ko pareshan karna shuru kar deti hain [e]. Aap sochte hain ki kya woh sach ho sakti hain. Aap khud ke liye us duniya ko dekhna shuru karte hain, ya toh old age ki infirmity (kamzori) ke zariya, ya kyunki aap pehle se hi kisi tarah se iske paas hain. Achanak aap suspicion (shak) aur darr se bhar jaate hain; aap calculating (hisaab) aur considering (vichar) karna shuru karte hain ki kya aapne kisi ke sath kisi bhi tarah ka injustice (a-nyay) kiya hai. Aur agar aapko apni zindagi mein injustice ke kai acts milte hain, toh aap aadhi raat ko panic (ghabrahat) mein jaagte rehte hain, jaise bachhe karte hain [331]. Aap apprehension (darr) ki sthiti mein jeete hain. Iske vipreet, jis vyakti ke conscience (anta:karan) par kuch nahi hai, uske paas achhi aur pleasant (sukhad) umeedon hoti hain – uski old age ke liye ek nurse, jaise Pindar ise rakhte hain. Unhone iske liye sateek shabd paaye, Socrates, jab unhone kaha ki jo koi bhi apni zindagi righteousness (dharmikta) aur purity (pavitrata) mein jeeta hai woh paayega ki

> Sweet hope, old age’s nurse, which chiefly guides
> Men’s wayward minds, accompanies his heart
> And so protects him.

### 8

Woh sahi hain – isse zyada sahi nahi ho sakte the. Aur yahi wajah hai ki main paise ke kabze ko sabse bada importance (mahatva) deta hoon [b]. Har kisi ke liye nahi, balki achhe character ke logon ke liye. Agar aap logon ko defraud (dhokha) karne, ya unse jhooth bolne se bachna chahte hain, chahe kitne bhi reluctantly (ani chha se), ya apni denadariyon ko chukaane mein asafal hone ke baad terror (darr) ki sthiti mein neeche ki duniya mein jaane se – chahe kisi god ko sacrifices hon, ya kisi man ko paisa – toh paise ka kabza is end (lakshya) mein koi chhote paimane par yogdan nahi deta hai. Beshak iske kai doosre upayog bhi hain, lekin ek cheez ko doosri ke khilaf weigh (tole) karte hue main ise kisi bhi samajh wale vyakti ki aankhon mein, paise ke sabse mahatvapurna upayogon mein se ek rate (dharadharit) karunga.’

‘Yeh shaandar dhang se kaha gaya hai, Cephalus,’ maine kaha [c]. ‘Lekin kyunki aapne justice ka vishay uthaya hai, kya hum spasht roop se keh sakte hain ki yeh truthfulness (sachai) hai, aur kisi bhi doosre se praapt ki gayi kisi bhi cheez ko wapas karna hai? Ya kya kabhi-kabhi in tareeqon se behave (vyavhaar) karna sahi hota hai, aur kabhi-kabhi galat? Main aapko ek udaharan deta hoon. Maan lijiye aapne kisi dost se kuch weapons (hathiyaar) udhaar liye jab woh apne sahi dimaag mein tha. Maan lijiye woh baad mein paagal ho gaya, aur phir unhe wapas maanga. Har koi sehamat hoga, main kalpana karta hoon, ki aapko unhe use wapas nahi dena chahiye, aur jo koi bhi unhe wapas deta hai – ya jo is sthiti mein kisi ke prati poori tarah se truthful hone ke liye bhi taiyar tha – woh sahi cheez nahi kar raha hoga.’

‘Correct (sahi),’ usne kaha [d].

‘Phir yeh justice ki definition (paribhasha) nahi hai – ki yeh sach bolna hai, aur jo aapko diya gaya hai use wapas karna hai.’

‘Haan, yeh hai, Socrates,’ Polemarchus ne interrupt (beech mein toka) kiya. ‘Kam se kam, yeh hai agar humein Simonides par vishwas karna hai.’

‘Main bas itna kehna chahunga,’ Cephalus ne beech mein kaha, ‘ki yeh woh jagah hai jahan main discussion aapko sonpta hoon. Samay ho gaya hai ki main sacrifices ke baare mein kuch karoon.’

‘Well, kya main Polemarchus, aapka heir (waaris) nahi hoon?’

‘Aap nischit roop se hain,’ usne ek hasi ke sath jawab diya, aur apne sacrifices ke liye chala gaya.

‘Toh mujhe batayein,’ maine kaha, ‘aap jinhone argument ko inherit (virasat mein paaya) kiya hai, Simonides justice ke baare mein kya kehta hai jise aap sochte hain ki sahi hai [e]?’

‘Ki har kisi ko woh chukaana just (nyaypoorn) hai jo uska baaki hai.

### 9

Wahi woh kehta hai, aur mujhe lagta hai ki woh sahi hai.’

‘Well,’ maine kaha, ‘Simonides ek samajhdar aur inspired (prerit) aadmi hai. Isse a-sehamat hona nischit roop se aasan nahi hai. Lekin is remark se uska aakhir kya matlab hai? Aap achhi tarah se jaan sakte hain, Polemarchus. Mujhe koi andaaza nahi hai. Woh zaahir taur par woh matlab nahi rakhta jiske baare mein hum abhi baat kar rahe the. Agar ek vyakti doosre ko safe keeping (suraksha) ke liye kuch deta hai, aur phir wapas maangta hai jab woh apne sahi dimaag mein nahi hota, toh Simonides ka matlab yeh nahi hai ki doosre vyakti ko ise use dena chahiye [332]. Aur phir bhi main kalpana karta hoon ki jo cheez safe keeping ke liye di gayi thi woh us vyakti ki baaki hai jisne ise diya tha, kya nahi hai?’

‘Haan.’

‘Us sthiti mein – jab koi apne dimaag se baahar ho jata hai, aur phir ise wapas maangta hai – kya ise wapas karna poori tarah se sawaal se baahar nahi hai?’

‘Haan, yeh hai.’

‘Aisa lagta hai ki Simonides ka matlab woh nahi hai, jab woh kehta hai ki jo baaki hai, ya due hai use wapas chukaana just hai.’

‘Nahi, yeh nischit roop se nahi hai,’ usne kaha. ‘Woh jo sochta hai ki doston ke kaaran due hai woh unka achha karna hai, nuksan nahi.’

‘Main samajhta hoon,’ maine jawab diya. ‘Agar ek vyakti doosre ko paisa wapas deta hai jo doosre ne use safe keeping ke liye diya tha, toh woh due nahi de raha hai agar uska wapas karna aur doosre ka ise receive (praapt) karna harmful (nuksandayak) hai, aur agar woh dono dost hain [b]. Kya aap aisa nahi sochte ki Simonides ka matlab yahi hai?’

‘Haan, yahi hai.’

‘Dushmanon (enemies) ke baare mein kya? Kya aapko unhe woh sab kuch dena chahiye jo vaastav mein unke kaaran due hai?’

‘Aapko nischit roop se dena chahiye,’ usne kaha. ‘Aur dushmanon ke beech jo due hai woh wohi hai jo appropriate (uchit) hai – kuch harmful.’

‘Simonides tab ek poet ke roop mein bol raha tha, zaahir taur par, aur justice ki apni definition ko disguise (chhipa) raha tha [c]. Uska jo matlab tha, aisa lagta hai, woh yeh tha ki justice kisi bhi individual (vyakti) ko wohi dena tha jo uske liye appropriate tha, lekin usne ise “jo baaki tha” kaha.’

‘Haan, uske matlab wahi raha hoga.’

‘Maan lijiye, phir, hum mein se kisi ne usse kaha hota: “Simonides, us art ya skill ko lein jise medicine kaha jata hai. Yeh kya deta hai jo due aur appropriate hai, aur yeh ise kisse deta hai?” Aapka kya khayal hai ki uska jawab kya hota?’

‘Obviously (jaahir taur par),’ usne jawab diya, ‘woh kehta ki yeh body ko drugs aur food aur drink deta hai.’

‘Aur cookery ki art? Yeh kya deti hai jo due aur appropriate hai, aur yeh ise kisse deti hai?’

‘Yeh cooked food ko flavour (swad) deti hai [d].’

‘Very well. Phir us art ya skill ke baare mein kya jise hum justice kahenge? Yeh kya deti hai, aur yeh ise kisse deti hai?’

‘Well, agar humein pehle ki definitions ka palan karna hai, Socrates, toh yeh doston aur dushmanon ko benefits (faayde) aur injuries (nuksan) deti hai.’

‘Kya uska matlab tab yeh hai ki apne doston ki madad karna aur apne dushmanon ko nuksan pahonchana justice hai?’

‘Mujhe aisa lagta hai.’

‘All right. Jab log unwell (bimar) hote hain, jab yeh sickness (bimari) aur health (swasthya) ka prashn hota hai, toh unki madad karne mein sabse behtar kaun hai agar woh dost hain aur unhe nuksan pahonchane mein agar woh dushman hain?’

‘Ek doctor.’

‘Aur jab woh samundar par hon [e]? Ek sea voyage (yatra) ke khatron ke beech unhe sabse behtar madad ya nuksan kaun pahonchaye sakta hai?’

‘Ek ship ka captain.’

‘Just man (nyaypoorn vyakti) ke baare mein kya? Kis activity mein, aur kis uddeshya ke liye, woh apne doston ke sath achha vyavhaar karne aur apne dushmanon ke sath बुरा vyavhaar karne mein sabse behtar saksham hai?’

‘War aur alliances mein, mujhe lagta hai.’

‘Very well. Ab, jab log ill (bimar) nahi hain, mere pyare Polemarchus, toh ek doctor unke kisi kaam ka nahi hai.’

‘True (sach).’

‘Aur jab woh samundar par nahi hain, toh ek ship ka captain unke kisi kaam ka nahi hai.’

‘Nahi.’

‘Kya iska matlab yeh hai ki just man unke kisi kaam ka nahi hai jab woh war mein nahi hain?’

‘Nahi, mujhe yakin hai ki aisa nahi hai.’

‘Justice peacetime (shanti ke samay) mein bhi kuch upayogi hai, phir?’

‘Haan, yeh hai [333].’

‘Lekin phir agriculture (kheti) bhi hai, kya nahi hai?’

‘Haan.’

‘Crops (fasalein) produce karne ke liye.’

‘Haan.’

‘Aur shoemaking?’

‘Haan, woh upayogi hai.’

‘Shoes produce karne ke liye, aap kahenge, zaahir taur par.’

‘Of course.’

‘Justice ke baare mein kya, phir? Jab aap kehte hain ki yeh peacetime mein upayogi hai, toh yeh kis kaam ke liye upayogi hai? Yeh kya produce karti hai?’

‘Contracts (anubandh), Socrates.’

‘Aur contracts se aapka matlab partnerships (sajhedari) hai, ya kuch aur?’

‘Mera matlab partnerships hai.’

‘All right [b]. Kya just man draughts

‘Shoe plays draughts woh zyada upayogi hoga.’

‘Aur jab bricklaying (eentein chinne), ya stone mein building banane ki baat aati hai, toh kya just man ek builder se zyada upayogi aur behtar partner hai?’

‘Of course nahi.’

‘Well, kis tarah ki partnership mein just man ek lyre player se behtar partner hai, jis tarah ek lyre player notes bajane mein behtar hai?’

‘Paisa shamil hone wali partnerships mein, mujhe lagta hai.’

‘Jab tak ki kisi chance se, Polemarchus, paise ko kisi upayog mein laane ka prashn na ho – agar aapko paise ki ek sum ke liye sanyukt roop se ek ghoda khareedna ya bechna hai [c]. Us case mein, main kalpana karta hoon, koi aisa vyakti jo ghodon ke baare mein jaanta hai zyada upayogi hai, kya woh nahi hai?’

‘Apparently.’

‘Aur ek ship khareedne ya bechna ke liye, aap ek shipbuilder ya ship ke captain ko chahenge.’

‘Aisa lagta hai.’

‘Kis sthiti mein, phir, silver ya gold ke sanyukt upayog ki zaroorat hone par, just man kisi bhi doosre se zyada upayogi hai?’

‘Jab paise ko deposit karne, aur ise safe rakhne ki zaroorat ho.’

‘Aapka matlab hai jab ise kisi upayog mein laane ki koi zaroorat na ho. Aap bas chahte hain ki yeh wahin rahe jahan yeh hai?’

‘Yeh sahi hai.’

‘Toh yeh tab hai jab paisa useless (bakar) hai ki justice isse nipatne ke liye upayogi hai [d]

### 11

?’

‘Aisa lagta hai.’

‘Aur ek pruning-knife? Jab aap ise safe rakhna chahte hain, toh justice upayogi hai, public life mein aur private life dono mein. Lekin jab aap ise upayog karna chahte hain, toh viticulture ki art woh hai jo aap chahte hain?’

‘Apparently.’

‘Aur kya aap ek shield ya ek lyre ke baare mein bhi wahi kehne wale hain? Ki justice tab upayogi hai jab aapko unhe safe rakhne aur unhe upayog na karne ki zaroorat hoti hai? Lekin jab aapko unhe upayog karne ki zaroorat hoti hai, toh aap soldier ki art aur music ki art chahte hain?’

‘Mujhe woh kehna padega.’

‘Aur sabhi doosre udaharanon mein, justice useless hai jab inmein se kisi ka upayog karne ki baat aati hai, aur sirf tabhi upayogi hai jab woh useless hain?’

‘Main aisa maanata hoon.’

‘Us case mein, mere dost, justice kisi bade mahatva ki nahi lag sakti hai, agar iska akela upayog tabhi hai jab cheezein useless hon [e]. Lekin aayein ek alag prashn ko dekhein. Ek fight mein – ek boxing match, shayad, ya kisi doosri tarah ki fight – kya woh vyakti jo blow (muka) deliver karne mein sabse chatur hai woh guard karne mein bhi sabse chatur nahi hai?’

‘Woh nischit roop se hai.’

‘Aur disease (bimari) ke sath? Kya woh vyakti jo isse guard karne mein chatur hai woh ise gupt roop se implant (faillane) karne mein bhi sabse chatur hai?’

‘Haan, mujhe aisa lagta hai.’

‘Aur warfare mein, jo aadmi military camp ki rakhwali karne mein achha hai woh deception (chhal) mein bhi achha hai [334]. Woh dushmanon ke plans chori kar sakta hai, ya chhipkar unke undertakings ko hara sakta hai.’

‘Certainly.’

‘Toh jab bhi koi kisi cheez ki rakhwali karne mein chatur hota hai, woh ise chori karne mein bhi chatur hoga.’

‘Aisa lagta hai.’

‘Toh agar just man paise ki dekhbhal karne mein chatur hai, toh woh ise chori karne mein bhi chatur hai.’

‘Well, argument wahi sujhave deta hai,’ usne kaha.

‘Phir just man, aisa lagta hai, ek tarah ka thief (chor) nikal aaya hai. Aap shayad Homer ke baare mein soch rahe hain [b]. Woh Autolycus, uski maa ki taraf se Odysseus ke grandfather ki tareef karta hai, aur kehta hai ki

> In swearing oaths and thieving he surpassed
> All men.

### 12

Justice, aapke aur Homer aur Simonides ke anusar, zaahir taur par chori karne ki ek tarah ki art hai – lekin apne doston ki madad karne aur apne dushmanon ko nuksan pahonchane ke drishtikon se. Kya aapne wahi nahi kaha tha?’

‘Nahi, maine nischit roop se nahi kaha,’ usne kaha. ‘Yadyapi personally, mujhe ab nahi pata ki main kya keh raha tha. Lekin ek cheez jo main abhi bhi sochta hoon, aur woh yeh hai ki justice apne doston ke sath achha vyavhaar karna aur apne dushmanon ke sath बुरा vyavhaar karna hai.’

‘Doston se aapka matlab un logon se hai jinhe har vyakti achha manta hai, ya woh jo vaastav mein achhe hain, bhale hi use iska ehsaas na ho [c]? Aur dushmanon ke sath bhi wahi?’

‘Sabhi sambhavnaon mein,’ usne jawab diya, ‘log unhe pasand karenge jinhe woh achha sochte hain, aur unhe na-pasand karenge jinhe woh kharab sochte hain.’

‘Aur kya log kabhi isme mistakes karte hain? Kya woh aksar logon ko achha sochte hain jab woh nahi hote, aur iska ulat?’

‘Haan, woh mistakes karte hain.’

‘Toh in logon ke liye, kya achhe unke dushman hain, aur kharab unke dost?’

‘Woh nischit roop se hain.’

‘Kya in logon ke liye phir bhi just hai, jab yeh hota hai, un logon ke sath achha vyavhaar karna jo kharab hain, aur achhon ke sath बुरा vyavhaar karna [d]?’

‘Aisa lagta hai.’

‘Aur achhe just hain. Woh us tarah ke log nahi hain jo galat karte hain.’

‘True.’

‘Toh aapke argument ke anusar un logon ko nuksan pahonchana just hai jo koi galat kaam nahi karte.’

‘Impossible, Socrates. Aisa lagta hai jaise argument achha nahi hai.’

‘Phir yeh sahi hona chahiye,’ maine kaha, ‘unjust ke sath ganda vyavhaar karna, aur just ke sath achha.’

‘Yeh behtar lagta hai.’

‘Us case mein, Polemarchus, aise kai log hain jinake liye yeh nikal aayega, agar logon ka unka judgment mistaken raha hai, ki apne doston ke sath ganda vyavhaar karna sahi hai, kyunki unke dost kharab hain – aur unke dushmanon ke sath achha, kyunki unke dushman achhe hain [e]. Un sthitiyon mein hum Simonides se quote ki gayi definition ke bilkul ulat kehna khatam karenge.’

‘Haan,’ usne kaha. ‘Yeh nischit roop se waisa nikal sakta hai. Aayein apni definition ko badlein. Hum shayad dost aur dushman ko sahi dhang se define nahi kar rahe hain.’

‘Hum unhe kaise define kar rahe hain, Polemarchus?’

‘Humne kaha tha ki jo vyakti achha lagta tha woh dost tha.’

‘Aur ab? Aap us definition ko kaise badalna chahte hain?’

‘Agar koi vyakti achha lagta bhi hai aur hai bhi, toh aayein use dost kahein [335]. Agar woh lagta hai, lekin vaastav mein nahi hai, toh aayein kahein ki woh dost lagta hai, lekin vaastav mein dost nahi hai. Aur wahi definition ek dushman par bhi laagu hone dein.’

‘Is definition par, aisa lagta hai, achha aadmi dost hoga, aur jo kharab hai woh dushman hoga.’

‘Haan.’

‘Kya aap chahte hain ki hum justice ki apni definition mein ek addition (jod) karein? Hamari pehli definition yeh thi ki ek dost ki madad karna aur ek dushman ko nuksan pahonchana just tha. Kya aap ab chahte hain ki hum isme jodein, aur kahein ki ek dost ki madad karna just hai agar woh achha hai, aur ek dushman ko nuksan pahonchana agar woh kharab hai?’

‘Haan,’ usne kaha, ‘mujhe lagta hai ki woh ek shaandar definition hogi [b].’

‘Lekin kya yeh sach mein ek just man ke swabhav mein hai,’ maine poocha, ‘duniya mein kisi ke bhi sath ganda vyavhaar karna?’

‘Yeh nischit roop se hai,’ usne kaha. ‘Use un logon ke sath ganda vyavhaar karna chahiye jo kharab hain – uske dushman.’

‘Agar aap kisi ghode ke sath ganda vyavhaar karte hain, toh kya woh behtar banta hai ya badtar?’

‘Worse (badtar).’

‘Kutton ko judge karne ke liye hum jis standard ka upayog karte hain usse badtar, ya ghodon ko judge karne ke liye hum jis standard ka upayog karte hain usse?’

‘Ghodon ko judge karne ke liye hum jis standard ka upayog karte hain.’

‘Aur kutto ka wahi? Agar aap unke sath ganda vyavhaar karte hain, toh woh kutto ko judge karne ke liye hamare dwara upayog kiye jaane wale standard se badtar ho jaate hain, ghodon ke nahi?’

‘Unhe hona padega.’

‘Manushyon ke baare mein kya, mere dost [c]? Kya humein, usi tareeqe se, kehna chahiye ki agar unke sath ganda vyavhaar kiya jata hai toh woh manav excellence (shreshthta) ko judge karne ke liye hamare dwara upayog kiye jaane wale standard se badtar ho jaate hain?’

‘Certainly.’

‘Lekin kya justice ek manav excellence nahi hai?’

### 13

‘Dobara, ise hona padega.’

‘Kis case mein, mere dost, manav jati ke sadasya jinake sath ganda vyavhaar kiya jata hai unhe aavashyak roop se zyada unjust banna padega.’

‘Aisa lagta hai.’

‘Kya musicians music ke saadhan dwara, logon ko unmusical banane mein saksham hain?’

‘Nahi, woh asambhav hai.’

‘Kya horsemen horsemanship ke saadhan dwara logon ko ghodon ke sath un-skilled bana sakte hain?’

‘Nahi.’

‘Aur kya just justice ke saadhan dwara logon ko unjust bana sakte hain [d]? Ya aam taur par, kya achha manav excellence ka upayog logon ko kharab banane ke liye kar sakta hai?’

‘Nahi, woh asambhav hai.’

‘Haan, kyunki yeh heat (garmi) ki property (gund) nahi hai, main maanata hoon, cheezon ko thanda karna. Yeh iske opposite ki property hai.’

‘Haan.’

‘Na hi yeh dryness (sookhe-pan) ki property hai cheezon ko geela karna, balki iske opposite ki.’

‘Haan.’

‘Aur yeh nischit roop se achhe ki property nahi hai nuksan pahonchana, ya logon ke sath ganda vyavhaar karna, balki iske opposite ki.’

‘Apparently.’

‘Aur just man achha hai?’

‘Haan.’

‘Us case mein, Polemarchus, yeh just man ki property nahi hai apne dost ya kisi doosre ke sath ganda vyavhaar karna. Yeh uske opposite, unjust man ki property hai.’

‘Mujhe lagta hai ki aap bilkul sahi hain, Socrates,’ usne kaha.

‘Toh agar koi kehta hai ki har kisi ko woh daitaa just hai jo uska due hai, aur agar uska isse matlab hai ki just man se jo due hai woh uske dushmanon ko nuksan hai, aur uske doston ko madad, toh jis kisi ne bhi yeh kaha woh ek samajhdar aadmi nahi tha [e]. Unhone jo kaha woh galat tha, kyunki humne saaf taur par dekha hai ki kisi bhi sthiti mein kisi ke sath ganda vyavhaar karna just nahi hai.’

‘Main sehamat hoon,’ usne kaha.

‘Kya hum weapons uthayenge, phir, aap aur main ek sath, agar koi daava karta hai ki Simonides, ya Bias, ya Pittacus, ya un samajhdar aur blessedly happy mardon mein se kisi doosre dwara yeh kaha gaya tha?’

‘Main nischit roop se karunga,’ usne kaha. ‘Main battle mein apna part play karne ke liye taiyar hoon.’

‘Kya aap jaante hain,’ maine poocha, ‘kaun mujhe lagta hai ki us kehne ke liye responsible tha ki apne doston ke sath achha vyavhaar karna, aur apne dushmanon ke sath ganda vyavhaar karna just hai [336]?’

‘Kaun?’

‘Mujhe lagta hai ki woh Periander, ya Perdiccas, ya Xerxes, ya Ismenias the Theban, ya koi aur ameer aadmi tha jisne socha ki uske paas badi taqat thi.’

‘Aap bilkul sahi hain,’ usne kaha.

‘Well, phir,’ maine kaha, ‘kyunki justice ki yeh definition – aur jo just hai uski – saaf taur par sahi nahi hai, iski kya doosri definition di ja sakti hai?’

Hamari baatchit ke beech mein bhi Thrasymachus ne baar-baar discussion ka control lene ki koshish ki thi, lekin har baar use hamare charon taraf baithe logon dwara roka gaya tha, jo ant tak discussion sunna chahte the [b]. Lekin jab hum argument mein is stopping-place par pahonche, jaise maine yeh prashn poocha, woh ab zyada samay tak silent rehne mein a-saksham tha. Usne khud ko gathered kiya aur humpar spring (jhadpata) kiya, jaise koi wild beast apne shikaar par. Polemarchus aur main alarmed aur dismayed (ghabra) gaye.

Oonchi aur saaf aawaz mein bolte hue, Thrasymachus ne kaha: ‘Yeh kya nonsense hai jo tum dono mein ghus gayi hai, Socrates [c]? Itne obliging kyun ho rahe ho? Ek doosre ko rasta kyun de rahe ho? Agar tum sach mein jaan-na chahte ho ki justice kya hai, toh simply prashn poochhna band karo, aur kisi doosre dwara diye gaye kisi bhi jawab ko galat sabit karke points score karna band karo. Tum achhi tarah se jaante ho ki jawab dene se prashn poochhna aasan hai. Aao, tum khud kuch jawab kyun nahi dete [d]? Humein batao ki tum kya kehte ho justice kya hai. Aur humein yeh mat batane lagna ki yeh woh hai jo zaroori hai, ya jo beneficial hai, ya jo advantageous hai, ya jo profitable hai, ya jo tumhare liye achha hai. Main us sabhi stuff ko nahi loonga. Nahi. Humein batao please, kaafi saaf taur par, sateek roop se tumhara kya matlab hai.’

Main is intervention (hakshep) se dismayed tha. Maine use dekha, aur panic karne laga. Aur mujhe yakin hai, agar maine wolf (bhediye) ko use mere dekhne se pehle na dekha hota, toh main dumb (be-aawaz) ho gaya hota.

### 14

Jaisa ki yeh tha, halanki, maine vaastav mein use pehle dekha tha – us point par jahan woh discussion se infuriated (gussa) hona shuru hua tha [e]. Parinaamswarup, main jawab dene mein saksham tha. ‘Hamse gussa mat ho, Thrasymachus,’ maine kaha, kuch apprehension ke sath. ‘Agar Polemarchus aur main arguments ke hamare examination mein mistakes kar rahe hain, main aapko assure karta hoon ki hum unhe uddeshya se nahi kar rahe hain. Agar hum gold dhoondh rahe hote, toh hum khoj mein ek doosre ko jaan-boojhkar rasta nahi dete, aur is tarah ise paane ke apne chances ko nasht nahi karte. Toh kyunki hum vaastav mein jo dhoondh rahe hain woh justice hai, gold ki ek badi matra se zyada moolyavan cheez, aap kalpana nahi kar sakte ki hum itne stupid hain ki ek doosre ko concessions dein, aur ise jitna ho sake saaf taur par view mein lane ke liye dhrid-sankalpit na hon. Humpar vishwas karo, mere dost. Pareshani yeh hai ki humme ability (kshamta) ki kami hai [337]. Toh jab aap chatur log hamare prayason ko dekhte hain, toh pity (daya) vaastav mein annoyance (chidchidahat) se kahin zyada appropriate reaction hai.’

Yeh Thrasymachus se ek a-priya hasi laya. ‘Oh mere god,’ usne kaha, ‘main jaanta tha. Socrates ki irony. Maine iska prediction kiya tha. Maine in logon se kaha tha ki aap koi jawab dene se inkaar kar denge, ki aap modest hone ka naatak karenge, ki aap jawab dene se bachne ke liye kuch bhi karenge, agar koi aapase koi prashn poochhta hai.’

‘Aapka cleverness, Thrasymachus. Itna clever ki jaante hain ki kya hoga agar aap kisi se poochhte hain ki twelve kya tha, lekin phir use jawab dene se pehle ek warning dete hain: “Now look here, humein yeh mat batane lagna ki twelve twice six hai, ya three times four hai, ya six times two hai, ya four times three hai [b]. Main aapase us tarah ka koi nonsense nahi lene wala hoon.” Yeh aapko spasht tha, main kalpana karta hoon, ki agar aapne prashn ko us tareeqe se poocha, toh koi bhi iska jawab nahi de sakta tha. Maan lijiye jis vyakti se aap poochh rahe the usne objection (a आपत्ति) ki hoti: “Aapka kya matlab hai, Thrasymachus? Kya mujhe unmein se koi bhi jawab nahi dena hai jinhe aapne forbid (mna) kiya hai? Kya aap serious hain? Bhale hi unmein se ek vaastav mein sach ho? Kya mujhe aapko koi aisa jawab dena hai jo sach nahi hai? Ya kya?” Uske objection par aapka jawab kya hota?’

‘Oh, haan,’ usne kaha [c]. ‘Itni close analogy (samanta)!’

‘Mujhe nahi dikhta ki isme kya galat hai,’ maine kaha. ‘Lekin bhale hi yeh close na ho, yeh phir bhi lag sakti hai, prashn poochhe ja rahe vyakti ko. Kya aapsochte hain ki woh use us jawab ko dene se rokega jise woh sahi sochta hai, chahe hum use forbid karein ya nahi?’

‘Kya yeh wahi hai jo aap ab karne wale hain? Kya aap unmein se ek jawab dene wale hain jinhe maine aapko na dene ke liye kaha tha?’

‘Isne mujhe surprise nahi kiya,’ maine kaha, ‘agar reflection (vichar) par main us nishkarsh par pahoncha.’

‘Kya hoga agar main aapko justice ke baare mein ek aisa jawab doon jo un sabhi doosre jawabon se poori tarah alag hai, unse kahin behtar jawab [d]? Aap kya sochte hain ki aapka penalty (dand) kya hona chahiye?’

‘Well, obviously, kisi aise vyakti ke liye appropriate penalty jo nahi jaanta. Ise seekhna chahiye, main ise leta hoon, us vyakti se jo jaanta hai.’

‘Aap innocent hain,’ Thrasymachus ne kaha. ‘Nahi, aapko seekhne se zyada karna padega. Aapko mujhe kuch paisa bhi dena padega.’

‘Very well. Jaise hi mere paas koi hoga, main doonga.’

‘Aapke paas kuch hai,’ Glaucon ne kaha. ‘Agar yeh paisa hai jiske baare mein aap worried hain, Thrasymachus, aage badhein aur bolein. Hum sabhi Socrates ke liye pay karenge.’

‘Main shart lagaunga ki aap karenge,’ usne kaha [e]. ‘Socrates ko unki aam trick play karne ki anumati dene ke liye kuch bhi – khud prashn ka jawab na dena, balki kisi doosre ke jawab dene ka intazar karna, aur phir jo woh kehta hai use lena aur ise galat sabit karne ki koshish karna.’

‘Really, mere pyare fellow!’ maine kaha. ‘Koi kaise prashn ka jawab de sakta hai agar shuruat ke liye woh jawab nahi jaanta tha – jaanchne ke liye ise jaan-ne ka daava bhi nahi karta tha – aur uske upar, toh bhi maan lijiye ki uske paas vishay par koi idea tha, agar use kisi authority ke aadmi dwara un cheezon mein se koi bhi na kehne ke liye kaha gaya tha jo usne sochi thin [338]? Nahi, yeh aapke liye bolne ka kaafi zyada sense banata hai. Aap woh hain jo jawab jaan-ne aur kehne ke liye kuch hone ka daava karta hai. Toh please, mere liye ek favour ke roop mein, apne jawab ko apne tak mat rakhein. Yahan Glaucon aur doosron ko apne gyaan ka benefit dein.’

Is appeal ke baad, Glaucon aur baaki ne unse waisa hi karne ki bheekh maangi jaise maine kaha tha. Thrasymachus saaf taur par bolna chahta tha, us behtareen jawab ke liye credit haasil karne ke liye jise usne socha tha ki uske paas taiyar tha. Lekin usne argue karne ka naatak kiya, naatak kiya ki woh chahta tha ki main jawab dene wala banoon [b]. Aakhirkar woh sehamat ho gaya, kehte hue: ‘Wahan aapke liye Socrates ki wisdom hai. Woh khud koi teaching karne se inkaar karta hai, bas doosron se seekhte hue ghoomta hai, bina kisi dhanyavaad ke.’

‘Ki main doosron se seekhta hoon, Thrasymachus, sach hai. Lekin jab aap kehte hain ki main unhe koi dhanyavaad nahi deta, toh aap galat hain. Main apni taqat mein saari thanks deta hoon. Aur jo meri taqat mein hai woh kamyab tareef hai, kyunki mere paas paisa nahi hai. Main kitna enthusiastic ho sakta hoon, agar main kisi ke kahe ko approve karta hoon, toh aap pata lagane wale hain, jab aap apna jawab denge. Mujhe yakin hai ki yeh ek achha hoga.’

‘Ise suno, phir,’ usne kaha [c]. ‘Main kehta hoon ki justice simply wohi hai jo stronger ke liye achha hai. Well, wahan saari tareef kahan hai? Aap ise nahi dene wale hain, kya aap hain?’

‘Haan, main doonga – jaise hi main samajhta hoon ki aapka kya matlab hai. Vartaman mein main abhi bhi nahi jaanta. Stronger ke liye kya achha hai, aap kehte hain, just hai. Aapka isse kya matlab hai, Thrasymachus? Agar Polydamas all-in wrestler humse stronger hai, aur beef khana uski body ko build karne ke liye achha hai, toh aap zaahir taur par yeh matlab nahi rakhte ki yeh khana hamare liye bhi achha – aur right

– hai jo usse weaker hain [d].’

‘Socrates, aap contempt ke neeche hain. Aapne jo maine kaha use us tareeqe se liya jo mere matlab ko misrepresent karne ke liye sabse aasan banata hai.’

‘Bilkul nahi, mere dost. Lekin aapko mujhe zyada saaf taur par batana padega ki aapka kya matlab hai.’

‘All right,’ usne kaha. ‘Aapko zaroor pata hona chahiye ki kuch cities tyrannies hain, kuch democracies hain, aur doosre aristocracies?’

‘Of course.’

‘Aur har city mein control mein kya hai ruling power hai?’

‘Haan.’

‘Har ruling power apne khud ke achhe ke liye kanoon banati hai [e]. Ek democracy democratic laws banati hai, ek tyranny tyrannical laws, aur ityaadi. In kanoonon ko banane mein, woh yeh saaf karti hain ki unke liye, rulers ke liye kya achha hai, unke subjects ke liye kya just hai. Agar koi disobey karta hai, toh woh use kanoon todne aur unjust way mein kaam karne ke liye saja dete hain [339]. Mera wahi matlab hai, “mere dost,” jab main kehta hoon ki sabhi cities mein ek hi cheez just hai, namatah ruling authority ke liye kya achha hai. Yeh, main ise leta hoon, jahan taqat nihit hai, aur parinaam yeh hai, kisi ke liye bhi jo ise sahi tareeqe se dekhta hai, ki ek hi cheez har jagah just hai – stronger ke liye kya achha hai.’

‘Ab main samajhta hoon ki aapka kya matlab hai,’ maine kaha, ‘yadyapi yeh sach hai ya nahi yeh dekha jaana baaki hai. Toh aapka jawab bhi, Thrasymachus, yeh hai ki kisi vyakti ke liye kya achha hai woh just hai, yadyapi woh ek aisa jawab tha jise aapne mujhe sakhti se na dene ke liye kaha tha. Lekin aap qualification jodte hain “stronger ke liye.”’

‘Ek trivial addition, aap keh sakte hain [b].’

‘Woh abhi tak saaf nahi hai. Yeh ek mahatvapurna ho sakta hai. Jo saaf hai woh yeh hai ki humein jaanch karni chahiye ki kya aap jo kehte hain woh sach hai. Aapki tarah, main sehamat hoon ki justice kisi vyakti ke liye achhi cheez hai, lekin jabki aap ise stronger ke liye kya achha hai ke roop mein qualify karte hain, mujhe itna yakin nahi hai. Humein prashn ki jaanch karni chahiye.’

‘Aage badho, phir. Iski jaanch karo.’

‘Main karunga,’ maine kaha. ‘Mujhe batayein, kya aap yeh bhi nahi kehte ki subjects ke liye apne rulers ka palan karna just hai?’

‘Main kehta hoon.’

‘Aur kya woh infallible (a-chook) hain, in sabhi cities mein rulers [c]? Ya kya woh mistakes karne ke capable hain?’

‘Woh nischit roop se, main kalpana karta hoon, mistakes karne ke capable hain.’

‘Toh jab woh kanoon enacting karne lagte hain, toh kya woh kuch correctly enact karte hain, lekin ek nishchit sankhya incorrectly?’

‘Meri raaye mein, haan.’

‘Aur “correctly” kanoon enact karna hai jo unke apne interest mein hain, aur “incorrectly” kanoon enact karna hai jo unke apne interest ke khilaf hain? Kya aapka wahi matlab hai?’

‘Haan.’

‘Lekin woh jo kuch bhi enact karte hain, unke subjects ko ise carry out karna chahiye, aur yeh justice hai?’

‘Of course.’

‘Us case mein, aapki definition ke anusar, stronger ke liye kya achha hai sirf wohi karna just nahi hai, balki iska opposite bhi, jo uske liye achha nahi hai [d].’

Unse jhaankte hue, usne andar dekha jo ek corpse (laash) lag rahi thi, insaan se badi, apne haath mein ek golden ring ke alawa kuch nahi pehne hue. Woh kehte hain ki usne ring hata di, aur baahar aa gaya.

‘Shepherds apni niyamit meetings mein se ek kar rahe the, taaki woh king ko flocks par apni monthly report de sakein. Aur woh aadmi bhi wahan pahonch gaya, ring pehne hue. Jaise hi woh unke baaki ke sath baitha, usne ring ki setting ko apni taraf, apni hatheli ke andar twist (modna) kar diya [360]. Jab usne yeh kiya, toh woh un logon ke liye invisible (a-drishya) ho gaya jo uske sath baithe the, aur unhone uske baare mein aise baat karna shuru kar diya jaise woh chala gaya ho. Woh amazed hua, aur ring ko phir se twist kiya, setting ko baahar ki taraf modte hue. Jaise hi usne aisa kiya, woh visible ho gaya. Jab usne yeh ehsaas kiya, toh usne ring ke sath experiment karna shuru kar diya, yeh dekhne ke liye ki kya isme yeh taqat thi. Aur usne paaya ki yeh waisa hi tha. Jab usne setting ko andar ki taraf moda, toh woh invisible ho gaya; jab usne ise baahar ki taraf moda, toh woh visible ho gaya. Ek baar jab usne ise sthapit kar liya, toh usne king ko report dene walon mein se ek hone ka intazam karne mein koi samay nahi khoya [b]. Jab woh wahan pahoncha, toh usne king ki patni ko seduce kiya, uske sath king ke khilaf plot kiya, use maar diya aur power par kabza kar liya.

‘Imagine karein ki wahan us tarah ke do rings the, aur ki just man ne ek pehna tha, jabki unjust man ne doosra pehna tha. Log sochte hain ki koi bhi justice ki bounds ke andar rehne ke liye sufficiently iron-willed nahi hoga. Koi bhi khud ko doosron ki possessions se apne haath door rakhne, aur unse door rehne ke liye nahi la sakta tha, agar woh market-place mein bina kisi doosre vichar ke jo kuch bhi pasand karta use lene ke liye free hota, ya logon ke gharon mein jaane aur kisi ke bhi sath sone ke liye jise woh pasand karta; ya agar woh kisi ko bhi maar sakta ya prison se release kar sakta jise usne chuna, aur aam taur par mardon ke beech ek god ki tarah acting karta ghoom sakta [c]. Agar woh is tarah behave karta, toh just man unjust se alag kaam nahi kar raha hota. Dono ek hi raste ka palan kar rahe hote.

‘Yeh ek majboot argument hai, aap keh sakte hain, is daave ke liye ki koi bhi voluntarily (apni marzi se) just nahi hai, balki sirf compulsion (majboori) ke tehat. Justice ko individuals ke liye ek achhi cheez nahi socha jata hai, kyunki jahan bhi koi sochta hai ki woh wrong kar sakta hai, woh wrong karta hai [d]. Har aadmi injustice ko justice se individual ke liye kahin zyada profitable manta hai. Aur woh is sochne ke liye sahi hoga, is drishtikon ko samne rakhne wale vyakti ke anusar. Jo koi bhi maine varnit ki hai us tarah ki aazadi ke kabze mein aaya, aur phir kabhi kuch galat karne se inkaar kiya, aur doosron ki possessions par ek ungli bhi nahi rakhi, use observers dwara sabse pathetic (dayaneey) aur brainless creatures ke roop mein dekha jayega – yadyapi of course public mein woh uski tareef karenge, ek doosre se jhooth bolte hue wronged hone ke apne darr ke kaaran.

‘Mera us daave ke baare mein kehna bas itna hi hai [e]. Jahan tak vicharadhin logon ke lives ke beech chunav ka sawaal hai, akela tareeqa jisse hum ise properly kar sakte hain woh hai completely just man ko completely unjust man ke sath contrast (tulna) karke. Hum unki tulna kaise karenge? Is tarah. Hum na toh unjust man ki injustice se aur na hi just man ki justice se kuch ghatayenge. Hum maanenge ki har ek apne vishesh tareeqe se behave karne ka ek perfect example hai. Toh shuruat ke liye aayein unjust man ke vyavhaar ko kisi profession ke ek skilled practitioner ke jaisa banayein. Ek sach mein achha ship ka captain ya doctor, udaharan ke liye, apni skill ke exercise mein uske beech antar kar sakta hai jo feasible nahi hai aur jo feasible hai [361]. Woh uski koshish karta hai jo feasible hai, aur usse bachta hai jo feasible nahi hai. Isse bhi zyada, agar woh kahin koi galat move banata hai, toh woh ise correct karne ke capable hai. Hamare unjust man ke sath aisa hi ho sakta hai. Aayein maan lein, agar woh sach mein unjust hone wala hai, ki woh sahi tareeqe se apne wrongdoings ke baare mein jata hai, aur isse bach nikalta hai. Jo pakda jata hai use incompetent ke roop mein dekha jana hai, kyunki perfect injustice mein just lagna shamil hai jab aap nahi hote. Humein completely unjust man ko, phir, sabse complete injustice ke sath credit dena chahiye. Us vyakti ko jo sabse bade wrongs karta hai humein deny nahi karna chahiye – vaastav mein, humein grant karna chahiye – justice ke liye sabse bade reputation ka enjoyment [b]. Agar woh koi galat move banata hai, toh humein use ise sahi karne ki ability allow karni chahiye. Woh persuasion ka upayog karne ke capable hona chahiye – taaki agar uske wrongdoings ka koi sabhoot uske khilaf laya jata hai, toh woh apna raasta baat karke nikal sake – lekin jahan force ki zaroorat ho wahan force ka upayog karne ke bhi capable, apne sahas aur taqat par, aur doston aur daulat ke kabze par nirbhar karte hue.

‘Yeh unjust man ka hamara model hai. Uske bagal mein aayein apne kalpanik just man ko rakhein, ek saral aur aadaraniya aadmi jo chahta hai, Aeschylus ke shabdon mein, good lagna nahi, balki good hona.
2
Humein use appearance se deprive (vanchit) karna chahiye, kyunki agar woh just lagta hai, toh justice ki appearance uske liye recognition (pehchan) aur rewards layegi, aur phir yeh saaf nahi hoga ki just hone ka uska motive justice ke liye ek desire thi ya rewards aur recognition ke liye desire [c]. Toh humein use justice ke alawa har cheez se strip (nagga) karna chahiye; humein use ek aisi sthiti mein rakhna chahiye jo hamare pehle ke udaharan ke opposite ho. Kuch bhi galat na karne ke baavjood, uske paas injustice ke liye sabse badtar sambhavid reputation honi chahiye. Phir, agar yeh disgrace aur iske consequences se unaffected rehta hai, toh uski justice ki purity ko aag mein test kiya gaya hoga. Use apni zindagi is tarah jeene dein, bina kisi badlav ke, apni maut ke din tak, unjust lagte hue yadyapi vaastav mein just hote hue [d]. Is tarah woh dono extreme – ek justice ka, doosra injustice ka – haasil kar sakte hain, aur judgment kiya ja sakta hai, unmein se kaun zyada happy hai.’

‘Help!’ maine kaha. ‘Yeh ek kaafi vigorous job hai jo aapne kiya hai, mere pyare Glaucon, judgment ke liye unhe taiyar karne ke liye hamare har ek contestant ko saaf karte hue. Kisi statue ko scouring (ragadna) karne ki tarah.’

‘Maine apna sabse behtareen prayas kiya hai,’ usne kaha. ‘Aur agar unki dono sthitiyan waise hi hain jaise maine varnit ki hain, toh yeh hamare liye pare nahi hona chahiye, main kalpana karta hoon, us tarah ki zindagi ka poora vivran dena jo unmein se har ek ka intazar karti hai [e]. Toh mujhe ab wahi karna chahiye. Aur agar meri bhasha thodi crude aur uncivilised hai, Socrates, toh yeh mat kalpana karein ki yeh main bol raha hoon. Nahi, yeh woh log hain jo justice ke muqable injustice ki sifarish karte hain. Woh daava karenge ki is sthiti mein just man ko whipped (chabuk maara) kiya jayega aur rack par rakha jayega, chains mein phenka jayega aur uski aankhein burn out kar di jaayengi [362]. Aakhirkar, in sabhi injuries ke baad, use crucified (sooli par chadhaya) kiya jayega, aur ehsaas hoga ki jis mahatvapurna cheez ka aim karna hai woh just hona nahi, balki just lagna hai.
3
Toh Aeschylus ne jo kaha woh unjust man ka kahin zyada accurate description nikalta hai, jo unjust lagna nahi chahta, balki unjust hona chahta hai, appearances aur public opinion ko santusht karne ki koshish karne ke bajaye reality ke sampark mein apni zindagi jeete hue,

> In his mind enjoying the deep furrow’s fruit,
> From which good counsel grows [b].
> 4

‘Pehle sthaan par, woh kahenge, woh apne city mein ek ruler ho sakta hai, justice ke apne reputation ke kaaran; doosre, woh jahan pasand karta hai wahan shaadi kar sakta hai, apni betiyon ki shaadi unse karva sakta hai jinhe woh chunta hai, aur kisi ke bhi sath contracts aur partnerships bana sakta hai jiske sath woh chahta hai. Is sabke alawa woh khud ko ek ameer aadmi banana aasan paata hai, kyunki uske paas unjust way mein kaam karne ke baare mein koi compunction (jhijhak) nahi hai. Yahi kaaran hai, woh kehte hain, woh political aur legal disputes mein kamyab hota hai – public aur private dono – aur kyun woh apne dushmanon ko outdo karta hai [c]. Unhe outdo karke woh ameer banta hai, aur apne doston ki madad kar sakta hai aur apne dushmanon ko nuksan pahoncha sakta hai. Woh gods ko poori aur generous sacrifices aur offerings kar sakta hai, aur just man se kahin behtar gods aur manushyata ke us hisse ki seva karne mein saksham hai jise woh seva karne ke liye chunta hai. Parinaamswarup, woh daava karte hain, woh sabhi sambhavnaon mein just man se zyada gods ka favourite hone ki sambhavna rakhta hai. Yeh woh tareeqe hain, Socrates, jinme woh kehte hain ki unjust man gods aur men dono se, just man se behtar deal paata hai.’

Jab Glaucon ne khatam kiya, toh main jawab dene ke liye poori tarah taiyar tha [d]. Lekin uske bhai Adeimantus ne intervene kiya. ‘Mujhe umeed hai ki aap yeh nahi sochte, Socrates,’ usne kaha, ‘ki woh unke case ka poora hissa hai.’

‘Kyun? Aur kya hai?’ maine poocha.

‘Humne us hisse ko chhod diya hai,’ usne kaha, ‘jise shamil karne ki sabse zyada zaroorat hai.’

‘Well,’ maine kaha, ‘brother ko brother ke sath khada hona chahiye, jaise ki kehna jata hai.
5
Har tareeqe se shamil hoiye, aur uski sahayata ke liye aayein, agar usne kuch bhi chhod diya hai – yadyapi jahan tak mera sawaal hai, usne jo kaha woh bhi mujhe throw (pareshan) karne ke liye kaafi tha, aur mujhe justice ke defence mein aane ke a-saksham banane ke liye.’

‘Nonsense,’ usne kaha [e]. ‘Aapko is second instalment ko bhi sunna chahiye. Mujhe jo lagta hai ki Glaucon chahta hai use zyada saaf banane ke liye, humein uske contrary (vipreet) arguments se guzarna chahiye – woh jo justice ki sifarish karte hain aur injustice ki criticism karte hain [363]. Fathers apne beton ko salah dete hue, aur woh sabhi jo doosron ke liye responsible hain, unhe just hone ke liye encourage karte hain – isliye nahi, main ise leta hoon, kyunki woh justice ko apne aap mein value karte hain, balki kyunki woh isse milne wale approval (anumodan) ko value karte hain. Agar woh just lagte hain, woh argue karte hain, toh yeh reputation unke liye public office, marriage aur un sabhi benefits ko layegi jinhe Glaucon ne abhi enumerate kiya hai, jinhe just man achha maana jaane se haasil karta hai. Aur yeh sab kuch nahi hai jo unhe reputation ke benefits ke baare mein kehna hai. Ek baar jab woh gods ke approval ko jodna shuru karte hain, toh unke paas pious (pavitra) ko offer karne ke liye rewards ki prachurta hoti hai – gods ke gifts, woh kehte hain [b]. Admirable Hesiod aur Homer
6
wahi cheez kehte hain. Hesiod kehta hai ki just ke liye, gods oak trees ko banate hain

> Bear acorns on their lofty tops, and bees
> Beneath, on lower branches. Weight of wool
> Burdens their fleecy sheep.

Aur isi tarah ke kai doosre benefits.
7
Homer lagbhag wahi kehta hai:

> Or like some worthy king who, fearing god,
> Supports the right. For him the rich dark earth
> Bears wheat and barley, while with fruit his trees
> Bow down [c]. Unfailingly his flocks bear lambs.
> For him the sea yields fish.
> 8

Musaeus aur uska beta just ko gods se zyada exciting kind ke rewards receive karvate hain.
9
Unke account mein, woh unhe Hades le jaate hain, unhe baithate hain, aur pious ke liye ek party organise karte hain [d]. Woh unhe crown karte hain, aur unhe poora samay drunk (nashil) hone mein bitane dete hain, perpetual drunkenness (lagatar nashe) ko human goodness ke liye sabse finest reward maante hue. Doosre phir gods se aise rewards grant karte hain jo inase bhi zyada extensive hain. Woh kehte hain ki bachhon ke bachhe aur descendants ki ek tribe pious man, us aadmi ki posterity (santati) hain jo apni oaths ko rakhta hai. Yeh, aur is jaisa kuch aur, wahi hai jo woh justice ki praise mein kehte hain. Jahan tak impious aur unjust ka sawaal hai, woh unhe Hades mein, kisi prakar ke mud (keechad) mein dafan karte hain [e]. Woh unse ek sieve (chalni) mein paani carry karvate hain;
10
aur woh unhe alive hone par bhi disgrace mein laate hain. Woh unjust par Glaucon ki un just logon ke liye penalties ki list thopte hain jinake paas unjust hone ka reputation hai; yeh sabhi penalties hain jinhe woh soch sakte hain. Yeh, phir, unka recommendation aur criticism hai do lives mein se har ek ka.

‘Iske alawa, Socrates, aapko justice aur injustice ke baare mein baat karne ke ek aur aam tareeqe ko account mein lena chahiye – everyday speech mein aur poets mein dono [364]. Self-discipline aur justice ki apni praise mein, woh sabhi ek aawaz mein gaate hain. Woh unhe ek good ke roop mein dekhte hain, lekin ek aise ke roop mein jo kathin aur laborious (mehnat-sadhya) hai, jabki self-indulgence aur injustice pleasant aur follow karne mein aasan hain; woh sirf reputation mein jo woh laate hain, aur convention dwara shameful hain. Woh kehte hain ki zyadatar part ke liye unjust actions just wale se zyada profitable hain. Woh wicked ko congratulate karne mein kaafi happy hain, agar unke paas daulat hai aur power exercise karte hain, aur public life aur private life dono mein unhe respect pay karte hain [b]. Doosron se woh despise (nafrat) karte hain aur ignore karte hain – unmein se koi bhi jo kamzor aur gareeb hain – yadyapi woh swikar karte hain ki woh wicked se behtar log hain. Halanki, is vishay par sabhi mein sabse ullekhniya statements gods ke human goodness ke prati attitude ke baare mein hain. Woh kehte hain ki gods kai achhe logon ko unhappiness aur ek wretched (dandniya) life dete hain, jabki unke opposites ko woh ek aisi life dete hain jo kaafi alag hai. Mendicant priests aur seers ameer aadmi ke darwaze par dastak dete hain, aur use convince karne ki koshish karte hain ki unke paas ek power hai, jise gods dwara sacrifices aur incantations ke badle mein unhe bestowed kiya gaya hai, feasting ke delights ka upayog karke uske ya uske purvajon dwara kiye gaye kisi bhi wrong ko sahi karne ke liye [c].
11
Aur ki agar koi kisi dushman ko nuksan pahonchana chahta hai, toh ek chhote charge ke liye woh charms aur spells se just aur unjust dono ko equally injure kar sakte hain. Woh kehte hain ki woh gods ko apne liye kaam karne ke liye persuade kar sakte hain. In sabhi daavon ke liye woh poets ko witnesses ke roop mein bulate hain. Kuch unhe wrongdoing ki aasaani par quote karte hain.

> There is much wickedness; it is never hard
> To make that choice [d]. The way is smooth, the goal
> Lies near at hand. Virtue is out of reach
> Without much toil. That is the gods’ decree.
> 12

Yeh ek lamba, uphill road hai. Doosre, mard gods ko kaise prabhavit kar sakte hain is baare mein baat karte hue, Homer ko witness ke roop mein bulate hain, uske is daave ke sath ki

> Even the gods themselves
> Will hear our prayers. Men who do wrong, and sin,
> Can thus dissuade them from their purposes
> With fair entreaty or with sacrifice, With incense or the fat of offered meat [e].
> 13

Woh Musaeus aur Orpheus dwara books ka ek host samne laate hain, Selene aur Muses ke bachhe, aisa woh daava karte hain. Yeh wahi hain jo unke sacrificial rituals ko govern karte hain, aur woh cities ke sath-sath individuals ko persuade karte hain ki sacrifices aur pleasurable amusements alive hone walon aur pass away hone walon dono ke liye injustice se release aur purification jeet sakte hain [365]. Rites se guzarna, woh ise kehte hain, jo humein afterlife mein evils se release kar sakta hai. Aur agar hum sacrifice nahi karte hain, toh bhayanak cheezein hamara intazar karti hain.

‘Yeh nature aur force hai, Socrates, un sabhi cheezon ka jo goodness aur wickedness ke baare mein kahi jaati hain, aur mardon aur gods dwara unpar rakha gaya mulya. Hum kya sochte hain ki unka naye logon ke dimaag par kya effect hota hai jab woh unhe sunte hain – able wale, woh jo, jaise ki, opinion se opinion tak flitting karne ke capable hain, isbaare mein jaankari ik इकट्ठा karte hue ki kis tarah ka vyakti banna hai, aur kis raste jana hai, sabse behtareen sambhavid life jeene ke liye [b]? Ek naya aadmi khud se poochh sakta hai, Pindar ke shabdon ka upayog karte hue, “Sabse uchi wall kaise chadhein? Kya justice madad karegi? Ya devious deception?”
14
Aur is tarah citadel (kile) ki suraksha mein, apni maut tak apni life jeeo? Poets dwaara judge karne ke liye, agar main just hoon bina just lagte hue bhi, toh main isse kathinai aur spasht nuksan ke alawa kuch umeed nahi kar sakta. Agar main unjust hoon, lekin justice ke liye ek reputation haasil kar li hai, toh mujhe ek shaandar life ka vaada kiya gaya hai [c]. Isliye, kyunki “Appearance,” jaise ki samajhdar mardon ne mujhe ishara kiya hai, “truth ko overpower karti hai” aur happiness ko control karti hai,
15
mujhe apna saara dhyan uspar modna chahiye. Mujhe apne charon taraf goodness ki ek exact likeness khinchani chahiye, ek front aur façade ke roop mein, apne peeche wise Archilochus ki crafty aur subtle fox ko laate hue.
16

‘“Iske sath pareshani,” koi kahega, “yeh hai ki evil hona aur hamesha ke liye isse bach nikalna kathin hai.” “Well,” hum kahenge, “kuch bhi bada kabhi aasan nahi tha [d]. Lekin agar hum happy hone wale hain, toh humein wahan follow karna chahiye jahan hamare argument ka trail humein le jata hai. Aur isse bach nikalne ke liye, hum gupt clubs aur societies banayenge,
17
aur persuasion ke teachers hain humein assembly aur lawcourts ki wisdom dene ke liye. Unki madad se hum kabhi-kabhi persuasion ka upayog karenge, aur doosre samay force ka, aur is tarah iske liye pay kiye bina top par aayenge.”

‘“Lekin gods ke khilaf stealth ya force ka upayog karna asambhav hai.” “Well, agar gods astitva mein nahi hain, ya agar woh mardon mein bilkul bhi interested nahi hain, toh humein badle mein jo hum karte hain use gupt rakhne mein interested kyun hona chahiye [e]? Agar woh astitva mein hain, aur mardon mein interested hain, toh hamari unki akeli gyaan ya hearsay custom aur poets se aati hai jo gods ke family histories ke baare mein gaate hain. Lekin yeh wohi writers hain jo humein batate hain ki gods ke nature mein ‘fair entreaty aur sacrifice ke sath’ moved aur won over hona hai.
18
Humein ya toh poets dwara kiye gaye dono daavon par vishwās karna chahiye ya unmein se kisi par nahi. Aur agar hum unpar vishwās karte hain, toh sabse behtareen policy unjust way mein kaam karna hoga, aur proceeds ka upayog sacrifices ke liye pay karne ke liye karna hoga [366]. Agar hum just way mein kaam karte hain, toh hum gods dwara saja se bachenge, lekin injustice ke rewards bhi khoyenge, jabki agar hum unjust hain toh hum rewards paayenge, aur prayers ke saadhan dwara jab hum mark ko overstep karte hain aur wrong karte hain toh hum gods ko bina kisi penalty ke humein chhodne ke liye persuade kar sakte hain.”

‘“Ah, lekin humein agli duniya mein pay karna padega – ya toh hum khud ya hamare descendants – un wrongs ke liye jo hum yahan karte hain.” “Aisa nahi hai, mere dost,” woh kahega, ek calculating air ke sath [b]. “Mystic rites mein, aur absolution dene wale gods mein badi taqat hai. Aisa sabse bade cities kehte hain, aur gods ke bachhe, woh jo gods ke poets aur mouthpieces banate hain; woh humein assure karte hain ki yeh cheezein aisi hain.”

‘Phir hamare liye sabse complete injustice ke muqable justice ko chunne ke liye kya kaaran bacha hai? Agar hamare paas counterfeit respectability ke sath coupled injustice ho sakti hai, toh hum gods aur men dono ke sath hamare dealings mein apne khud ke inclinations ko follow kar rahe honge, hamari lifetime mein aur hamari maut ke baad bhi. Yeh zyadatar logon ki aur experts ki raaye hai. In sabhi arguments ki roshni mein, Socrates, kya kisi bhi personality ki force, kisi bhi financial resources, kisi bhi physical strength ya family connections wale vyakti ko justice ka respect karne ke liye taiyar hone ke liye induce kar sakta hai, bajaye iske ki jab woh ise recommend hote hue sunta hai toh hanse [c]? Agar koi dikha sakta hai ki jo humne kaha hai woh galat hai, aur poori tarah santusht hai ki justice ek achhi cheez hai, toh main kalpana karta hoon ki woh unjust ke prati bahut forgiving hai, aur unpar gussa nahi hota. Woh jaanta hai ki un logon ke alawa jo injustice ke prati ek tarah ke divine aversion ke sath paida hue hain, ya jo isse refrain karne ke liye gyaan haasil karte hain, koi bhi sach mein just nahi hona chahta [d]. Log cowardice, ya old age, ya kisi doosri tarah ki kamzori ke parinaamswarup, aur ise practice karne ki a-kshamta se injustice ki ninda karte hain. Yeh kaafi spasht hai. Is tarah ke logon mein se ek jis minute power ki position mein aata hai, woh turant jitna ho sake utna unjust way mein kaam karna shuru kar deta hai.

‘Is sabka kaaran simply woh observation hai jisne hum dono ko aap par in lambi speeches ko thopne ke liye prompt kiya, Socrates. Yeh yeh hai. Aapke jaise logon ki koi kami nahi hai, mere shaandar dost, jo justice ke supporters hone ka daava karte hain, early days ke heroes se shuru karte hue, jinake shabd hum tak pahonche hain, right up to vartaman din ke logon tak [e]. Aapme se kisi ne bhi kabhi bhi injustice ki ninda nahi ki hai ya justice ki sifarish nahi ki hai sivaay un reputation, prestige aur rewards ke terms mein jo woh laate hain. Kisi ne bhi abhi tak, chahe poetry mein ya private discussion mein, unmein se har ek ka itself mein sufficiently detailed account nahi diya hai, jab yeh ise dharan karne wale vyakti ki soul mein apni taqat ke sath maujood hoti hai, gods ya men dwara undetected. Kisi ne nahi dikhaya hai ki injustice un evils mein sabse bada hai jo soul ke andar hain, ya ki justice sabse bada good hai [367]. Agar wahi hota jo aap sabhi shuruat se hi keh rahe the, aur agar aap humein hamare sabse shuruati saalon se persuade kar rahe hote, toh hum ab ek doosre par nazar nahi rakh rahe hote, injustice ke khilaf guard karne ke liye. Har aadmi khud par nazar rakh raha hota, darrte hue ki wrong karke woh apne abode ko share karne ke liye evils mein sabse bade ko admit na kar le.

‘Yeh, Socrates, aur shayad isse bhi zyada, woh hai jo Thrasymachus, ya us maamle ke liye koi aur, justice aur injustice ke vishay par keh sakta hai [b]. Woh har ek ko galat value assign karte hain – ek gross mistake, mere drishtikon mein. Kaaran – aur main aapke sath poori tarah khula rahunga – kyun maine unki position ko jitna ho sake utna vigorously set out kiya hai woh yeh hai ki main aapase opposite drishtikon sunna chahta hoon. Sirf argument dwara humein yeh mat demonstrate karein ki justice injustice se zyada powerful koi cheez hai.
