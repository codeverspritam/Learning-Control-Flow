## **INTRODUCTION (Parichay)**

Judith Kruger ka art school jane ka koi plan nahi tha. Wo pachees (25) saal se zyada waqt se ek successful product designer thin, home furnishings aur giftware mein kaam karti thin, jinke market mein saikdon products the. Unke designs Bloomingdale's, Crate & Barrel, aur kai dusre bade (big-name) retailers par beche jate the. Sabse zyada, unhe ceramics ke sath kaam karna pasand tha. Ek baar established (kamyab) hone ke baad, unhone apni khud ki ceramic tableware (bartano) ki line shuru karne ka faisla kiya. Unhone apni manufacturing Thailand mein set karne ka faisla kiya, aur wo nayi manufacturing locations aur factories dhoondhne ke liye wahan do baar ud kar (flew) gayin.

Dusre visit par, unke ghar wapas aane ke lambe safar (journey) mein Japan ka ek stopover shamil tha. Unhone Japan ki purani capital (rajdhani) Kyoto mein kuch din bitane ka faisla kiya, jo apni traditional culture aur art ke liye jana jata hai. Wo wood block galleries ke liye jani jane wali chhoti sadkon se guzar rahi thin; unka plan kuch prints kharidne ka tha. Kyoto mein apne aakhiri din, unhe sadak ke us paar ek chhoti si gallery ki jhalak (glimpse) mili. Usne unhe pukara: "Mujhe wahan andar jana chahiye." Kruger ne mujhe bataya, "Isne meri zindagi badal di."

The gallery *nihonga* bech rahi thi, jo Japanese painting ka ek traditional (paramparik) form hai (dekhein figure 0.1). Kruger ne isey pehle kabhi nahi dekha tha. Unhe gallery owner se pata chala ki nihonga artists ko apne paints khud banane padte hain. Yeh paints ajeeb (unusual) hote hain: inke pigments (rang) pise hue (crushed) keemti pattharon, mitti, aur oyster shells (seepiyo) se banaye jate hain. Unhe fine (baarik) se lekar coarse (mote) tak alag-alag size ke particles (kanno) mein peesa jata hai. Pise hue powder ko animal-hide (janwar ki khaal) glue ke sath milakar paint banaya jata hai. Judith is baat se dang (fascinated) thin ki pattharon se paint banana mumkin (possible) tha. Bachpan mein, wo patthar ikattha karti thin, aur unki issi rock-collecting hobby ne unke ceramics ke junoon (passion) ko aag (fuel) di thi.

Kruger ne apna successful design business jaari rakha jabki unhone American nihonga painter ke roop mein apni nayi pehchan (identity) bhi develop ki. Aakhir-kaar unhone apni in dono identities ko milane (merge) ka faisla kiya. Unhone colored tableware ki ek nayi line design ki, jisme unhone clay (mitti) ko unhi ground stone pigments (pise pattharon ke rang) ke sath milaya (blending) jo wo nihonga ke liye istemal kar rahi thin. Ek Fortune 500 company ne is project ko support (backed) kiya aur unke kharche (expenses) uthaye. Unhone is product line ko develop karne ke liye paanch saal kaam kiya. Lekin jaise hi unhone retail outlets mein distribute (baantne) karne layaq tableware ki manufacturing poori ki, us company ne homewares business ko poori tarah chhod diya. Kruger ki tableware ki poori line Japan ke ek warehouse (godaam) mein padi reh gayi. Aaj tak, ise kabhi release nahi kiya gaya hai.

Main Kruger se Savannah College of Art and Design (SCAD) ke campus mein mila. Halaat ke is badkismat mod (unfortunate turn of events) ke theek baad, Kruger ne apna design business piche chhodne aur full-time nihonga painting karne ka faisla kiya tha. Wo Savannah shift ho gayin aur fine arts mein master's (MFA) ke liye padhai shuru ki, jo ki ek advanced degree program hai jisme full-time do saal lagte hain. Lekin art school mein kuch an-socha (unexpected) hua: ek personal transformation (niji badlaav). "Mujhe ehsaas hua ki mera kaam asal mein nihonga ke baare mein nahi tha. Main medium (material) ko apni paintings banane de rahi thi." Ab wo apni creative practice ke baare mein alag tareeqe se sochti thin. "Mera kaam ab kahin zyada gahra (richer) hai kyunki isme ek maqsad (intention) hai." Unhone "eco-friendly art" banana shuru kiya jo unke kaam ko local environment se jodta hai. Unhone apni practice ko "insaaniyat (humankind) aur mile-jule ilaqo (mingled terrain) ke layering (parton/layers)" ke roop mein bayan kiya. Art school mein do saal ke baad bhi, unhone kisi ek creative raste (path) par pakka faisla (settled) nahi kiya tha. Unka kaam lagatar badalta/nikharta (evolve) raha: "Yeh sab bas khul रहा hai (unfolding), yeh sab bas aage badh raha hai." Haal hi mein, unki creative practice mein kuch naya hona shuru hua tha: "Kisi wajah se, yeh ghaas (grasses) aur daldal (marshes) mere kaam mein dikhne (showing up) lage hain." Unhe pakka nahi pata iska kya matlab hai, lekin wo dekh sakti hain ki isme potential (sambhavna/dam) hai aur ise aur explore karna (khojna) layaq hai.

Kruger jaisa koi insaan art ya design school kyun jata hai? Aakhir-kaar, wo do baar successful ho chuki thin—pehle, ek tableware designer ke taur par; dusra, ek nihonga painter ke taur par. Wo pehle se hi janti thin ki zyada-tar kisi bhi dusre American se behtar nihonga kaise kiya jata hai. Unhe iske liye art school ki zaroorat nahi thi. Art school ne unhe kuch bahut zyada keemti (valuable) cheez di—*unhone dekhna (see) seekha*. Pichle paragraph mein, Kruger ne aise shabdon ka istemal kiya hai jo aapne pehle shayad nahi sune honge agar aap artist ya designer nahi hain, aise shabd jaise *intention* (maqsad), *unfolding* (khulna/samne aana), *showing up in my work* (mere kaam mein dikhna) aur *potential* (sambhavna). Yeh un cheezon mein se hain jo aap art aur design school mein seekhte hain, aur yeh kitab aapko batati hai ki yeh kya hain aur aap inhe kaise seekhte hain.

Kruger ka art school ka tajurba (experience) aam hai, yeh mujhe Professor Carol Fabricatore se pata chala, jo New York ki ek illustrator hain, jo New York City mein School of the Visual Arts (SVA) mein MFA students ko padhati hain. Do saal ka MFA program ek bada commitment hai. Students apna dusra saal ek ahum (significant) body of work (kaam ka set) banate hue guzarte hain jise unke dusre saal ke aakhir mein ek public show mein display kiya jayega. Fabricatore ke students apni bees (20s) aur tees (30s) ki umar mein hote hain. Kaiyon ka pehle se hi New York mein illustrators ke taur par career hota hai. Kruger ki tarah, kuch mash-hoor (well-known) aur kamyaab hote hain. Wo wapas art school mein kyun aaye hain? "Unhe aur chahiye. Wo badlaav chahte hain. Wo risk lena chahte hain. Lekin unke liye yeh kehna asaan hai; phir, unhe yeh karna bhi padta hai. Mujhe unke liye ise chote hisson mein baant kar asaan banana padta hai (break it down). Main kehti hoon, 'Apne style ko darwaze par chhod do aur buri drawings banane ke liye taiyar raho (open to making bad drawings).'"

Kruger un das (10) MFA students mein se ek thin jinka maine interview liya jab wo art ya design mein apni do saal ki degrees poori kar rahe the. Un sabne mujhe bataya ki unhone wo cheezein seekhi thin jinki unhone umeed (expect) nahi ki thi, aisi cheezein jo unhe pata hi nahi thi ki unhe nahi pata (couldn't have known that they didn't know). Unhone sochne ke naye tareeqe, dekhne ke naye tareeqe, aur materials ke sath kaam karne ke naye tareeqe seekhe. Unme se koi bhi wo art nahi kar raha tha jo unhone shuru karte waqt soch tha ki wo karenge. Unka kaam kai baar badla—themes, intentions (maqsad), aur images. Yeh isliye nahi tha kyunki wo confuse the, ya isliye ki professors ne unhe dhamkakar (bullied) kuch alag karne ko kaha tha, ya isliye ki wo commercial success (paise wali kamyabi) ke piche bhag rahe the. Art aur design school mein, ek nayi creative practice personal growth, naye discoveries (khoj), aur transformation (badlaav) se ubhar kar (emerges) aati hai.

Yeh kitab un professional artists aur designers ki kahaniyon se bhari hai jo logon ko yeh sikhate hain ki dekhna kaise hai. Yeh professors students ko personal transformation ke ek aise process se guide karte hain jahan wo dekhne, sochne, aur banane ke naye tareeqe seekhte hain. Jin artists aur designers ka maine interview liya, unki dual (dohri) identities (pehchan) hai creatives aur educators (padhane walon) dono ke roop mein. Unke paas ausatan (average) lagbhag bees (20) saal ka teaching experience hai, aur unhone United States ke top schools mein padhaya hai; list is kitab ke shuru mein hai. Unme se koi bhi sirf ek teacher nahi hai; wo sabse pehle aur sabse zaroori, kaam karne wale creative professionals hain. Artists ke paas dealers, gallery representation, aur museum shows hote hain. Designers aur architects ke paas top corporations aur media outlets se contracts hote hain. In top universities mein se kisi mein teaching job pane ke liye, aapko ek rock star, ek successful professional creative hona padta hai. Aapka CV clients, gallery shows, aur museum collections se bhara hoga. Yeh kitab is baare mein hai ki artists aur designers kaise padhate hain, lekin yeh successful artists aur designers ki creative practices ke baare mein bhi hai—ki wo kaise dekhte hain, sochte hain, aur banate hain.

Aap un professors ki baatein sunenge jinhone pure United States mein padhaya hai—kul milakar pachaas (50) se zyada institutions mein. In colleges ki alag-alag reputations hain, alag-alag curricular emphases (padhai ke focus) hain, aur students ki ginti (numbers) bhi alag hai. Unme se kuch badi research universities par based hain, jaise St. Louis ki Washington University mein Sam Fox School of Design & Visual Arts ya Chapel Hill mein University of North Carolina ki Department of Art. Dusre azaad (independent) institutions hain jaise School of the Art Institute of Chicago ya New York ka School of Visual Arts. Agar maine koi cheez sirf ek university mein dekhi aur kisi aur mein nahi, toh maine use is kitab mein nahi rakha.

Maine art aur design dono ke professors ka interview liya, bees (20) se zyada alag-alag disciplines mein, painting aur sculpture se lekar typeface design aur architecture tak. Agar ek art professor ne koi baat kahi lekin kisi design professor ne wahi baat nahi kahi, toh maine use is kitab mein shamil nahi kiya. Mera maqsad visual arts ke andruni nichod (underlying essence) ko pehchan-na tha. Aap jo kuch bhi padhenge wo art aur design dono ke kai disciplines mein saajha (shared) kiya jata hai. Zahir hai, Art aur design alag-alag hain. Lekin yeh kitab us baare mein hai jo in sabhi disciplines mein aam (shared) hai. Main isey *the studio model* kehta hoon: aaj ki art aur design education ki saajha practices. *Learning to See* mein, main aapko classroom ke darwaze ke paar le jata hoon taaki yeh raaz khol (reveal) sakun ki log dekhna kaise seekhte hain.

### **Learning to See (Dekhna Seekhna)**

Dekhna seekhna be-tahasha (incredibly) mushkil hai. Aapko ek samajhdar (wise) guide ki zaroorat hoti hai, ek successful aur talented artist ya designer jo janta ho ki kaise dekhna hai—koi jo yeh pata lagane mein aapki madad kar sake ki aapko kahan jane ki zaroorat hai aur wahan tak pahunchne mein aapki madad kar sake. High school se naye-naye nikle students, jo bachelor of fine arts (BFA) program shuru kar rahe hote hain, unhe nahi pata hota ki unhe kya nahi pata (don't know what they don't know). Apni tees (30s) ki umar mein MFA program shuru karne wale successful professionals bhi hamesha nahi jante. Jab aap dekhna nahi jante, toh aap yeh tasavvur (envision) bhi nahi kar sakte ki ek baar dekh lene ke baad yeh kaisa hoga. Shuruaat karne wale students, chahe wo athara (18) saal ke hon ya adhtees (38) saal ke, nahi jante ki unka kaun sa personal transformation unka intezar kar raha hai. Jaise hi maine desh bhar ke art aur design schools mein professors ka interview liya, yeh theme baar-baar samne aayi:

*I teach students how to see. (Main students ko dekhna sikhata hoon.)*

Mujhe yeh jaan kar hairani hui ki yeh sabhi professors ek hi tareeqe se padhate hain. Zahir hai, har field mein farq hain—alag techniques, alag materials, alag itihas (histories). Har art aur design discipline ek alag medium ke sath kaam karta hai aur uski apni special techniques hoti hain; aapko wo techniques seekhni padti hain. Lekin art aur design school ka *asli (real)* maqsad yeh nahi hai. Sabse zaroori baat, professors wo gehra (profound) knowledge sikha rahe hain jo art *aur* design ki buniyaad (foundational core) mein chhipa hai—dekhna, sochna, aur banana. Sam Fox School mein padhane wale ek mash-hoor illustrator John Hendrix ne mujhe bataya:

*Zyada-tar log, jab wo itni achi tarah se banayi gayi kisi drawing ko dekhte hain, toh unhe wo choices nahi dikhti jo banate waqt li gayi thin. Drawing ke liye dekhna (seeing) hath wale hisse (hand part) se kahin zyada zaroori hai. Zyada-tar log hath wale hisse par dhyan lagate (obsess) hain, lekin mujhe sach mein lagta hai ki koi bhi mechanically (machinery dhang se) draw karna seekh sakta hai. Dekhna aur edit karna hi sach mein mushkil hai. Isme practice lagti hai. Isme cheezon ko lambe waqt tak dekhne ki zaroorat padti hai.*

### **Why Go to Art School? (Art School Kyun Jayein?)**

SVA mein Steve DeFrank ki Foundations class mein khud ko tasavvur (picture) karein. DeFrank ek murtikar (sculptor) hain jinka kaam chalees (40) shows mein display kiya ja chuka hai aur kai museums ke permanent collections mein hai. Yeh pehle saal ka pehla semester hai aur aap athara (18) saal ke undergraduate hain. Aap itne bade star ke sath ek hi kamre mein hone se excited aur thoda dare (intimidated) hue hain. Aapke sabhi professors DeFrank jaise hi honge: successful artists aur designers, jo New York mein kaam kar rahe hain, jo United States ki creative capital hai. DeFrank students ko unka pehla assignment dekar class shuru karte hain.

*Pehle assignment mein, jise "Corny Contours" kaha jata hai, unhe paper par paanch (5) hathon ki ek cross-contour drawing banani hoti hai. Main cross contours ki slides dikhata hoon. Main ek video dikhata hoon. Main unhe art history se cross contours ke behtareen examples dikhata hoon. Main unhe purane (past) students ke kaamon ke examples dikhata hoon aur main unhe jaan-bujh kar chunta (curate) hoon taaki unhe buri tarah dara saku (scare the shit out of them), theek hai na? Yeh school ka dusra hafta hai. Wo mujhse kehte hain, Steve, kisi ne nahi bataya tha ki yeh itna mushkil hone wala hai. Aur main kehta hoon haan, art school aasan nahi hai.*

Amy Honchell ek nationally known artist hain jinki paintings aur sculptures pachees (25) gallery shows mein lag chuki hain. Unke kaam ka pachaas (50) magazine articles aur exhibition catalogs mein review kiya gaya hai. Wo School of the Art Institute of Chicago (SAIC) ke MFA program mein bees saal se zyada waqt se padha rahi hain, jo desh ke sabse mash-hoor (legendary) art aur design schools mein se ek hai. Unhone mujhe MFA program ke graduate students ke baare mein yeh bataya:

*Graduate students bahut talented aate hain; unke paas badiya portfolios hote hain. Unme se bahuton ko undergraduates ki tulna mein koi bhi risk lene, ya kisi limit (boundaries) ko aage badhane mein bahut zyada mushkil hoti hai, kyunki unhe lagta hai ki yahan aane ke baad fail hone se darr lagta hai. Main aksar yeh conversation karti hoon jahan main kehti hoon, "Aap yahan challenges ka samna karne (to be challenged) aur yeh sawal poochne aur in aisi conversations mein shamil hone ke liye aaye hain jo asahaj (uncomfortable) hain ya jinke baare mein aap anjaan (unfamiliar) hain. Aap ghar par reh kar isey muft (for free) mein, apne tareeqe se kar sakte the. Lekin aapne wo nahi chuna. Aap yeh experience karna chahte the. Aapko sochna hoga ki yeh sach mein kya hai aur aap iska sabse zyada fayda kaise utha sakte hain."*

Art aur design school mehnge (expensive) ho sakte hain. Lisa Bulawsky ek printmaker hain aur Washington University in St. Louis (WashU) mein ek research-based printmaking workshop, Island Press ki director hain. Unka kaam poore United States mein exhibit (pradarshit) ho chuka hai. Unka kaam Belgium ki Royal Academy of Fine Art jaise collections mein shamil hai. Unhone art schools mein pachees saal se zyada waqt tak padhaya hai. Maine unse poocha, aap logon ko kaise convinced karte hain (manate hain) ki itni saari fees (tuition) dena worth it (faydemand) hai? Jab kisi student ke parents puchte hain ki mera bacha itne saare paiso ke badle kya seekh raha hai, toh aap kya kehti hain? "Hum is sawal ka jawab bahut dete hain. Mujhe laga ki yeh jawab maine banaya tha, lekin maine dusre logon ko bhi yeh kehte hue suna hai. Ek art education sabse behtareen education hai jo aap university mein paa sakte hain, kyunki aap seekhte hain ki be-tahasha resource-ful (halaat se nipatne wala) kaise ban-na hai, aatm-nirbhar (self-reliant) kaise ban-na hai, aur duniya ke baare mein aur aap isme kya yogdaan (contribute) dete hain iske baare mein critically (gehrai se) kaise sochna hai."

Maine art aur design schools mein kya hota hai iski study karne mein dus saal se zyada waqt bitaya hai. Maine lagbhag ek sau (100) professors ka interview liya. Main unki classes mein yeh dekhne gaya ki wo students ke sath kaise baat karte hain. In professors ne yeh dhoondh liya hai ki students ko naye tareeqon se dekhna aur sochna kaise sikhaya jaye. Lekin in professors ne padhana kaise hai iski classes nahi lin. Wo sabse pehle aur sabse zaroori, artists aur designers hain. Unhone job par rehte hue padhana seekha. Aur bees saal baad, wo isme be-tahasha ache (damned good) hain. Main ek learning scientist hoon aur main is baat ki tasdeeq (attest) kar sakta hoon ki wo jo kar rahe hain wo is baat par latest science ke sath poori tarah align (mel khata) hai ki log kaise seekhte hain. Is kitab mein aap jo padhte hain wo, buniyaadi taur par (at root), bas ek achi teaching hai, baat khatam (period).

### **Personal Transformation (Niji Badlaav)**

University of North Carolina mein padhane wali photographer aur printmaker Beth Grabowski kehti hain:

*Sabhi instructors ka goal (maqsad) students ko cognitive dissonance (dimaagi kashmakash) ki us jagah le jana hai jahan unhe pehle ki sochi hui sabhi baaton ke baare mein yakin nahi rehta. Yeh hone ke liye ek darawni (scary) jagah hai. Lekin agar aap logon ko batate hain, "Theek hai. Yeh darawna hone wala hai." Aur aapko bas itna kehna hai, "Yeh darawna hone wala hai aur us darr ko mere sath le lo aur jano ki wo wahan hoga." Aapko isay man-na (acknowledge) hoga taaki yeh aapko apahij (debilitating) na kar de.*

Sculptor (murtikar) Ron Leax ne WashU mein tees saal se zyada waqt tak padhaya hai. Wo mujhe ek chaar-hafte ke sculpture assignment ke baare mein bata rahe hain jo wo apni class Three-Dimensional Design mein students ko dete hain (dekhein figure 0.4). Yeh us class mein hai jise core ya foundations kaha jata hai—jise first-year students lete hain, bilkul Steve DeFrank ke course ki tarah. Leax unhe rozana istemal hone wale (everyday) chote objects se bhari ek shelf ke paas le jate hain: nimbu (lemon), seashell (seep), fish vertebrae (machli ki haddi), lily pad. "Maine un objects ko isliye chuna kyunki wo massive (bhari) rehte hain, buniyaadi taur par volumetric (jinke volume ko mehsoos kiya ja sake) hote hain, aur unki kabiliyat ke andar hote hain." Har student ek ko chunta hai, aur unka project iski ek murti (sculpture) banana hota hai, paanch guna badi. Us badi ki gayi (scaled-up) sculpture mein us object ki textured surface (khurdura-pan) bhi shamil hona chahiye, jise bhi usi anupat (proportionately) mein bada kiya gaya ho. Ek student ne nimbu (lemon) chuna tha. Ek minute ke liye tasavvur karein ki ek nimbu kaisa dikhta hai. Kya aap uski surface ki texture dekh rahe hain? Shayad nahi. Yahan tak ki agar aap apni aankhein sikodte (squint) hain aur apne dekhe gaye aakhiri nimbu ke baare mein bahut zor lagakar sochte hain, toh bhi aap shayad yeh tasavvur (envision) nahi kar sakte ki surface kaisi dikhti thi (dekhein figure 0.5). Students bhi textures nahi dekhte—jab tak unhe nimbu ko paanch guna bada nahi banana padta.

### **Part I: What You Learn (Aap Kya Seekhte Hain)**

Artists aur designers ne banane, sochne, aur dekhne ke ek anokhe aur shandaar (powerful) tareeqe mein maharat (mastered) hasil kar li hai. Yahi ek successful creative professional ki pehchaan (defines) hai. Art aur design students creative practice mein shagird (apprentices) hote hain. Wo top art aur design schools mein isliye aate hain kyunki yeh professors art aur design ki duniya ke stars hain. Yeh kitab is baare mein hai ki students art aur design school mein kya seekhte hain, lekin darasal yeh is baare mein hai ki professional creatives kaise sochte hain aur kaam karte hain. Professors successful professional artists aur designers hote hain jo jante hain ki lagatar (repeatedly aur consistently) create kaise karna hai. Jab wo college classroom mein aate hain, toh unka goal students ko ussi tarah dekhna aur sochna sikhana hota hai jaise wo karte hain.

Art aur design practice ka core (buniyaad) kaam karne ka ek khas tareeqa hai jise students art school shuru karte waqt nahi jante, aur—agar wo kadi mehnat karein toh—khatam karte waqt jante hain. Professional level par ki jane wali creative practice asaan nahi hai. Aur isey seekhna bhi asaan nahi hai. Art aur design students zyada-tar college majors se zyada kadi mehnat karte hain, yahan tak ki sciences mein hone wale "mushkil (hard)" majors se bhi zyada. Aap art aur design ko jaldi nahi seekh sakte. Kya aapko sach mein canvas par paint karna ya poster design aur print karna seekhne ke liye do se chaar saal chahiye? Bilkul (Absolutely). Professors ka kehna hai ki isme kam se kam do saal lagte hain, aur kuch ka toh yahan tak kehna hai ki chaar saal bhi shayad hi kafi hon (barely enough).

Part I ke paanch chapters professional artists aur designers ki creative practice ko describe karte hain. Zyada-tar professionals ko is baare mein baat nahi karni padti ki wo kya karte hain; unhe hosh-o-hawaas mein (consciously) apni practice ka analysis nahi karna padta. Lekin students ko dekhna sikhane ke liye, aapko jo pata hai use samajhna aur lafzon mein bayaan (articulate) karna hota hai. Bees saal padhane ke baad, yeh artists aur designers is baat par hosh-o-hawaas mein gaur (reflect) kar pate hain ki wo kaise create karte hain. Yahi wajah hai ki is kitab ke interviews itne zabardast (compelling) hain. Jab yeh professors is baare mein baat karte hain ki wo kya padhate hain, toh wo is baare mein baat kar rahe hote hain ki wo professional creatives ke taur par kya karte hain. Wo apni teaching practice ke baare mein bahut articulate (saaf dhang se bolne wale) hain. Part I mein paanch chapters hain jo is baare mein hain ki artists aur designers kaise sochte, dekhte, aur banate hain:

1. Process: Ek iterative (baar-baar dohrane wale), bhatakne wale (wandering), aur soche-samjhe (deliberate) process ke zariye apna rasta bun-na (weaving).
2. Materials: Ek lagatar chalte hue dialogue mein materials ke sath kaam karna.
3. Emergence (Ubhar kar aana): Nayi sambhavnaon ko an-sochi (unexpectedly) tarah se ubharne (emerge) ka mauka dena.
4. Failure (Nakaami): Dead ends (band raston) aur failures (nakaamiyon) ki umeed rakhna aur unhe manage karna.
5. Seeing (Dekhna): Ek aisi jagah jana jiska aapne tasavvur (imagined) bhi nahi kiya tha aur kuch aisa dekhna jo aapne pehle nahi dekha tha.

### **Chapter 1. Process**

**Creativity ek process hai. Yeh iterative (baar-baar hone wala) hai. Yeh jazz music jaisa hai. Aap ek line banate hain aur aap us line par respond karte hain. Aap kuch aisa banate hain jo aap seedhe raste par chal kar (linear way) nahi bana sakte the.**

John Hendrix, illustrator, the Sam Fox School

Art aur design dono mein, institutions aur disciplines ki ek badi range mein, professors ek creative practice, kaam karne ka ek anokha tareeqa sikhate hain. Yeh process un sabhi art aur design disciplines mein universal (poori duniya mein paaya jane wala) hai jinki maine study ki hai. Beshak (Of course), Art design se alag hai. Lekin yeh kitab un farq ko kinare rakh kar unki commonality (ek jaisi baaton) par focus karti hai. Jab hum art aur design mein common (saajha) baaton ko nikalte (extract) hain, toh humein creative practice ka nichod (essence) milta hai.

Professionals ke liye, creativity sirf ache ideas hone ke baare mein nahi hai. Artists aur designers inspiration (prerna) ya vision par depend nahi karte. Bahut se log WashU mein ek visual artist aur professor Cheryl Wassenaar ki tarah baatein kahenge: "Isse koi farq nahi padta ki aapka shuruati (initial) idea kya hai. Agar aap process se guzarte hain, toh aap bahut se ideas ko acha bana sakte hain." Unka kaam nationally aur internationally, pachaas (50) se zyada group aur solo (akele) exhibitions mein dikhaya gaya hai.

Create karna shuru karne ke liye aapko kisi idea ka intezar karne ki zaroorat nahi hai. Process pehle aata hai aur phir ideas ubhar (emerge) aate hain.

### **Chapter 2. Materials**

**Ideas cheezein banane se aate hain. Khojne (discovery) ka process darasal in cheezon ko apne hathon se karne (having hands on) ke baare mein hai, bajaye iske ki yeh maan liya jaye (assuming) ki idea banane wala hissa (ideation part) sach mein cheezein banane wale hisse se alag hai. Banana hi sochna (Making is thinking) hai. Main apne students se kuch bhi karwane ki koshish karta hoon taaki wo kaam karna shuru karein, isse pehle ki unhe lage ki unke paas ek poori tarah se tayyar (fully formulated) idea hona chahiye. Idea materials ke sath asal engagement (judav) se generate hota hai.**

Ken Botnick, book designer, New York mein Red Ozier Press ke co-proprietor, Penland School of Crafts ke purane executive director, aur Sam Fox School mein retired professor

**Yeh janne ke liye ki aap kya draw karne wale hain, aapko drawing shuru karni padegi.**

Picasso

Dekhna seekhne ke liye, aapko materials ke sath judna (engage) hoga aur kaam karna hoga. Dekhna (Seeing) sochne ka ek aisa tareeqa hai jo duniya mein maujood (embodied) hai. Art aur design school mein, aap apne dimaag (head) se bahar nikalkar materials, banane (making), aur craft ki duniya mein ana seekhte hain. Agar aap bahut zyada soch rahe hain, toh aap shayad create nahi kar rahe hain. Creativity aapas mein judi (embodied) hui hai, jo dimaag, hath, aur aankh ko ek sath laati hai—sochna, banana, aur dekhna. Aap is baare mein soch kar create karna nahi seekh sakte ki isey kaise karna hai; aapko ise materials ke sath kaam karke seekhna hoga. Kai cases mein, process bahut saadgi se shuru hota hai: pencil aur paper ke sath.

### **Chapter 3. Emergence (Ubhar Kar Aana)**

**Banate (making) waqt, mujhe is baat ke liye khula (open) rehna chahiye ki kaam kahan ja raha hai. Main apni marzi (will) thop sakta hoon aur painting se wahi karwa sakta hoon jo main chahta hoon, ya main isey khud aage badhne (evolving) de sakta hoon aur yeh nahi janne ki azaadi de sakta hoon ki yeh kahan ja raha hai. Phir aap baithte hain aur kaam ko dekhte hain, aur yeh samajhne (figure out) ki koshish karte hain ki kya hua.**

Steve DeFrank, murtikar (sculptor), School of Visual Arts

Artists aur designers ke paas sirf ideas *hote* nahi hain; wo aisi practices mein shamil hote hain jinke zariye ideas *samne aate (emerge)* hain. Jab aap dekhna jante hain, toh aap dekh sakte hain ki aapke kaam mein kya hua hai. Bhale hi aapne yeh kiya ho, yeh kisi tarah aapse alag (separately) hota hai, aur aap is sambhavna ke liye alert (satark) rehna jante hain. Kabhi-kabhi yeh wahi hota hai jo aap karne ka irada (intending) rakh rahe the, lekin aksar kaam aapko hairan (surprises) kar deta hai aur kuch aisi keemti (valuable) cheez offer karta hai jiski aapko umeed nahi thi.

Students abhi tak yeh nahi jante ki aisi creative practice mein kaise shamil (engage) hona hai jahan unhe yeh na pata ho ki yeh kahan ja raha hai. Yeh shuru mein darawna (frightening) aur nirash karne wala (frustrating) hota hai. Aapko nahi pata ki yeh acha hone wala hai ya nahi aur aapko nahi pata ki yeh pata lagane mein kitna waqt lagega. Yeh tajurba professional creative practice ke core (buniyaad) mein hai; yeh art aur design school mein aapki seekhi jane wali sabse zaroori cheezon mein se ek hai.

Students kaam karne ke ek improvisational (mauqe par banane wale) process mein shamil (engage) hona seekhte hain jahan aap nahi jante ki yeh kahan ja raha hai, aur aap nahi jante ki yeh kab khatam hoga. Aap yeh vishwas (trust) karna seekhte hain ki ideas process se ubhar kar (emerge) aayenge. SCAD mein graphic design padhane wali Rhonda Arntsen apne students se kehti hain: "Chalo khatam karne ke liye shuru nahi karte (Let's not start to finish). Main har naye project (exploration) ki shuruat mein apne students se kehti hoon. Hum isliye shuru nahi kar rahe hain, ki isey khatam kar dein."

### **Chapter 4. Failure (Nakaami)**

**Aap kuch karne ki koshish karte hain, aur raste mein aap jo galtiyan (mistakes) karte hain wahi aapke kaam ko anokha (unique) aur alag (different) banati hain. Aur aap uska fayda uthate (capitalize) hain aur aage badhte hain. Yeh kaam karne ka bas ek bahut hi alag tarah ka tareeqa hai.**

Richard Mehl, ek graphic designer aur School of Visual Arts, New York City mein professor

Isse pehle ki aap dekhna seekhein, aapko surprises aur accidents (haadse) galtiyon (mistakes) ki tarah lagte hain. Ek baar jab aap dekhna seekh jate hain, toh an-sochi (unexpected) cheezein hone par aap excited ho jate hain. Students aksar fauran notice nahi karte jab unhone us se kuch alag kiya hota hai jo unhone karne ka irada kiya tha; professors unhe in an-soche (unexpected) badlaavon se dekhne aur seekhne mein guide karte hain. Ache artists aur designers nakaamiyon (failures) ko hote hue saaf taur par dekh sakte hain. Wo unhe tezi se dekhte hain aur process ko aage badhane ke liye unka faydemand dhang se (productively) istemal karte hain.

Ek improvisational (mauqe par kuch banane wale) process mein, aap yeh jane bina actions (kadam) lete hain ki aage kya hone wala hai. Aap process ko guide karte hain, lekin aap process ko control nahi karte. Aap apne materials ke sath control share karte hain. Kabhi-kabhi, aap jo irada karte hain wo nahi hota. Aap kuch waqt ke liye ek raste (path) par chalte hain aur phir aap ek aise point par pahunch jate hain jahan se aap aage nahi badh sakte. Ek baar jab aap dekh sakte hain, toh aap behtar samajh pate hain ki ab is raste mein aage koi potential (sambhavna) nahi bachi hai.

Art aur design school ke dauran students ko jo failures (nakaamiyan) milti hain, wo unhe sikhate hain ki apni khud ki creative practice mein failure ke zariye aage kaise badhna (grow) hai. WashU mein illustration padhane wale John Hendrix ne mujhe bataya: "Jab tak wo pehle fail nahi hote tab tak wo is baat (point) ko nahi samajhte—unhe ehsaas hota hai ki unhone kya galti ki, aur kyun ki."

### **Chapter 5. Seeing (Dekhna)**

**Mere assignments unke cheezon ko dekhne ka tareeqa badal dete hain. Yeh unhe cheezon ko alag dhang se dekhne ki kabiliyat (ability) de raha hai. Mere liye, iska poora matlab yahi hai (that's what it's all about).**

Savannah College of Art and Design ke ek first-year foundations professor

Ek artist ya designer ki tarah dekhne ka matlab apne conscious intentions (soche-samjhe iradon) aur aapke kaam se lagatar nikalne wale an-soche surprises ke beech balance (santulan) banana hai. Aap sirf drawing poori karne ke irade (intent) se paper par nishan (marks) nahi bana rahe hote hain. Aap exactly nahi jante ki kya hoga ya aap kab apna kaam khatam karenge. Lekin aap is process ko samne aate (unfolding) dekh sakte hain. Bhale hi aapko poori tarah samajh nahi aata ki kya ho raha hai, lekin aap bas utna zaroor samajh lete hain jitni zaroorat hoti hai. Aap jo kar rahe hain use aise dekh rahe hote hain jaise koi aur ise kar raha ho. Aapke actions (kaamon) ka nateeja kuch aisa hota hai jiska aapne pehle se irada (intend) nahi kiya tha. Aap hairan (surprised) honge, aur phir aap yeh samajhne (figure out) ki koshish karenge ki aisa kyun hua. Hairani ki baat yeh hai (Paradoxically) ki, agar aap yeh jane bina kaam karna nahi jante ki aap kya kar rahe hain, toh shayad aap hairan hona (be surprised) bhi nahi jante.

Dekhna seekhna aasan nahi hai. SCAD mein padhane wali ek architect Julie Varland ne mujhe bataya, "Yeh Mount Everest ki unchai wali ek khayi (cliff) hai." Dekhna seekhna ek niji badlaav (personal transformation) hai.

### **Part II: Project Assignments (Project Assignments)**

Sabhi art aur design classes mein, students dhyan se design kiye gaye project assignments par kaam karte hain. Har professor apne khud ke projects develop karta hai. Wo pedagogical art (padhane ki kala) ke works hain, jinhe ussi creative process ke zariye develop kiya gaya hai jo yeh artists aur designers apni professional practice mein istemal karte hain—iterative (baar-baar dohrane wala) aur emergent (ubhar kar aane wala), jiske raste mein dead ends (band raste) aur failures aate hain. Halaanki har project alag hota hai, is baat ke piche ek buniyaadi logic (tark) hota hai ki wo itne ache se kaam kyun karte hain, aur part II ke chapters 6 aur 7 samjhate hain ki ek acha art ya design project kya hota hai.

#### **Ek baar jab aap isey dekh lete hain, toh aap isey dekhna band nahi kar sakte.**

Haven Lin-Kirk, University of Southern California's Roski School of Art and Design mein designer aur professor

Sabhi students ko ek hi project assignment diya jata hai, aur unke deliverables (jama karwane wali cheezein) aur deadlines ek hi hoti hain. Lekin yeh projects kisi math problem ko solve karne ya Roman civilization ke baare mein essay likhne jaisa nahi hote. Studio assignments structured hote hain, lekin wo bahut zyada creative freedom (azaadi) dete hain. Problem ko kaise approach karna hai yeh saaf nahi hota. Ek acha solution kaisa dikhega yeh bhi clear nahi hota. Ek ache assignment ke sath, har student ko problem ke liye apni khud ki approach banani (formulate) padti hai aur apna khud ka creative solution develop karna padta hai.

Kara Rooney, jo SVA aur Rhode Island School of Design (RISD) dono mein padhati hain, unhone sabhi professors ki taraf se bolte (spoke for all) hue mujhe bataya, "Main jo bhi assignments istemal karti hoon wo mere khud ke banaye hue hain. Wo meri studio practice se aate hain. Maine pedagogy (padhane ke tareeqo) par koi courses nahi liye hain." Rooney New York City mein ek multidisciplinary (kai fields mein kaam karne wali) artist hain. Wo installation performance, video photography, painting, aur sculpture ke sath kaam karti hain. Jin logon ka bhi maine interview liya, unki tarah unhone bhi padhana kaise hai, is par kabhi koi class nahi li. Jo cheez unhe ek behtareen professor banati hai wo yeh hai ki wo ek successful creative professional hain, *aur* unhone seekh liya hai ki students ko unki apni creative practice mein successful hona kaise sikhaya jaye. Professors apne projects ko kaise design karna hai, is baare mein sochne mein bahut waqt lagate hain. Wo apne teaching ke saalon mein ise baar-baar dohrate (iterate) hain, har baar assignment mein mamuli badlav (tweaking) karte hain. Mere interviews mein, unhone explain (samjhaya) kiya ki assignment ka har hissa (element) is tarah se kyun likha gaya hai.

### **Chapter 6. Constraints (Rukawatein / Haddein)**

**Rukaawatein/Haddein (Constraints) darasal aapko azaad karti hain. Jaise ek nadi jo ek patle raste se behti hai, yeh unki learning ko tez kar deti hai. Ek ache project mein bahut sakht (severe) rukawatein (constraints) hoti hain.**

John Hendrix, illustrator, the Sam Fox School

Achi tarah se design ki gayi assignment constraints (rukawatein) shandaar hoti hain. Yeh students ko un purani aadaton se dur le jati hain jo creative practice ke sath unke judne (engagement) mein rukawat dalti hain. Bina constraints ke, students aksar kharab (poor) kaam generate karte hain. Achi tarah se design kiye gaye assignments, jinme bahut dhyan se chuni gayi constraints hoti hain, students ko dekhna seekhne mein guide karte hain.

* Assignment designs uncertainty (un-dekhe) ke darr ko kam karte hain.
* Assignment designs students ko cliché (ghise-pite) kaam generate karne se rokte hain.
* Assignment designs students ko wo karne se rokte hain jiske sath wo pehle se hi aaramdayak (comfortable) hain.
* Assignment designs students ko assignment mein jald-baazi karne se rokte hain.
* Assignment designs students ka dhyan course ke diye gaye learning outcomes (kya seekhne ko milega) par lagate hain.
* Assignment designs students ko pehle se idea banaye rakhne se rokte hain; wo students ko ek aise process mein guide karte hain jahan ideas kaam se ubhar kar (emerge) aate hain.

Hum aksar creativity ko khule-pan (openness), azaadi (freedom), aur rukaawaton ke na hone (absence of limitations) se jodte (associate) hain. Lekin aap bina constraints (hadon) ke dekhna nahi seekh sakte. Jab assignment khatam hota hai, aur zyada-tar students kharab (poor) kaam laate hain, toh yeh aksar professor ki galti hoti hai; aisa isliye hota hai kyunki constraints poori tarah sahi nahi thin. Constraint aur flexibility (lachile-pan) ke beech sahi balance banana aasan nahi hai. Talented professors is baat par dhyan dete (reflect) hain ki kya kaam nahi kiya aur kyun, aur phir wo jante hain ki agli baar kya revise (theek) karna hai.

### **Chapter 7. Ambiguity (Uljhan)**

**Art banane ke dauran, kabhi-kabhi aap yeh bhi nahi jante ki aap kahan ja rahe hain. Aap bas is an-dekhe (invisible) goal ki taraf badh rahe hote hain. Aapke paas waqayi mein kho jane (being lost) ke kabil hone, ya yeh baat qubool karne ka mizaj (disposition) hona chahiye ki aapko agay kya ho ga iska ilm nahi hai. Bahut se students ke liye, isey apnana bahut mushkil hota hai—future ka na pata hona, yeh na pata hona ki wo kahan ja rahe hain, aakhiri nateeje (end result) ka na pata hona, aur is baat ka na pata hona ki unhe 'A' grade milega ya nahi.**

Catherine Rosamond, School of Visual Arts

Ambiguity (Uljhan/Saaf samajh na aana) darawni (frightening) hoti hai. High school se abhi-abhi nikle students iske aadi (used to) nahi hote. Ambiguity creative process ke liye zaroori hai, lekin students abhi tak itna theek se dekhna nahi jante ki wo is uljhan (ambiguity) se guzar kar ek faydemand rasta dhoondh sakein. Jab professional artists aur designers create karte hain, toh wo is process mein aane wale us pal ka swagat (welcome) karte hain jahan: wo nahi jante ki wo kahan ja rahe hain, wo nahi jante ki wo kya kar rahe hain, lekin unhe vishwas (trust) hota hai ki kuch behtareen samne aayega (emerge). Students ne abhi tak kisi successful process mein involve (engage) hona nahi seekha hai, aur wo abhi tak process par trust karna nahi jante. Uljhan (Ambiguity) se anxiety (ghabrahat) hoti hai. Wo frustrate (nirash) ho jate hain. Professor unke liye wahan maujood hain, is stage se guzarne mein unhe dekhna seekhne ke liye guide (rah dikhane) karne ke intezar mein.

Uncertainty (kisi baat ka pakka na hona) aur anxiety ke samne, kuch students shuru mein hi ek acha idea select karne ki jald-baazi (rush) karte hain aur phir us idea par poori tezi (efficiently) se kaam karne (execute) mein lag jate hain. Yeh anxiety ko kam karta hai kyunki yeh ambiguity ko hata deta hai, lekin yeh creative learning ke potential ko block kar deta hai. Creative process linear (seedhe raste wala) nahi hota, aur yeh behtareen (great) ideas par aadharit nahi hota. Dekhna seekhne ke liye, aapko us baat par dhyan dena (attentive) hoga ki *abhi (right now)* kya ho raha hai, na ki pehle se tay kiye gaye nateeje (predetermined outcome) ke kisi seedhe raste (direct path) ke baare mein sochna. Outcome (nateeje) par focus karna aapko yeh dekhne se rokta hai ki jab aap kaam karte hain toh darasal kya ho raha hota hai.

### **Part III: The Studio Critique (Studio Critique)**

In do chapters mein, aapko is baat ki gehri samajh (deep dive) milegi ki studio critique mein kya hota hai. Aap classroom mein hone wali batchit ki aisi kahaniyan padhenge jo is baat ka nichod (essence) batati hain ki sabhi studio classes mein kya hota hai. Maine architecture, communication design, graphic design, illustration, interior design, painting, product design, aur sculpture ki studio classes visit ki. Critique (raye dene ka amal) wo jagah hai jahan project assignment ek jeeti-jagti practice (living practice) ke roop mein samne aata hai. Zyada-tar studio classes mein, ek project ko poora hone mein kai hafte lagte hain—kuch classes mein, poora semester—aur har student ko lagbhag har hafte ya do hafte mein ek baar critique (raye) ka samna karna padta hai. Students critique mein apna pura kiya hua (finished) kaam pesh nahi karte; iske bajaye, unhe sketches aur prototypes dikhane aur un challenges ke baare mein khul kar (openly) baat karne ke liye invite kiya jata hai jinka wo samna kar rahe hain.

Kyunki sabhi students ek jaise hi creative challenge ka samna karte hain, wo dusre students ke critiques sun kar seekhte hain. Dusre student ke kaam ko saaf taur par dekhna apne khud ke kaam ko dekhne se zyada asaan hota hai. Figure 0.10 mein, Rhonda Arntsen (SCAD) bai (left) taraf baithe student ke kaam ka critique (tajziya) kar rahi hain. Table uske sketches se dhaki hui hai; samne rakhe (foreground) uske laptop par bhi kaam dikh raha hai. Aap dekh sakte hain ki baki students dhyan se (closely) sun aur dekh rahe hain. Jab aap dusre students ke critiques dekhte hain, toh aap dekhte hain ki kisi creative challenge ko approach (deal) karne aur solve karne ke kai tareeqe hote hain. Kyunki baki students bhi unhi challenges ka samna karte hain, un dusre solutions (hallo) ko dekhne se aapki apne kaam ko dekhne ki kabiliyat (ability) mein izaafa (contributes) hota hai.

Kara Rooney ne kaha:

*Unke liye yeh dekhna acha hota hai ki har insaan dwara instructions (hidayaton) ka kitna alag matlab (interpreted) nikala jata hai. Yeh dhoondhne aur explore karne (khoj) ke is process ka ek hissa hai. Jo aap apne samne dekhte hain, jo bahut clear (black and white) lagta hai, ya seedha (straightforward) lagta hai, zaroori nahi ki wo waisa hi ho. Yeh group critique mein hamesha bahut si faydemand (fruitful) conversation paida karta hai. Hum critique mein bahut waqt bitate hain jahan unhe bolne ke liye majboor (forced) kiya jata hai.*

### **Chapter 8. Thinking (Sochna)**

**Class ka maksad koi cheez banana nahi hai, balki yeh hai ki jab aap bana rahe hote hain toh aap kaisa sochte hain. Aap studio mein faisle (decisions) kaise lete hain. Jo cheez wo banate hain wo sirf ek zariya (carrier) hai, jo unke khayalon ko samne lata (manifests) hai. Mujhe is baat se koi parwah nahi hai ki wo kya banate hain.**

Ron Leax, sculptor, Washington University in St. Louis

Professors critique mein *bahut zyada* bolte hain. Dusre students bhi wahi karte hain; critique ke goals mein se ek goal sabhi students ko dekhna seekhne mein madad karna hai, aur iski shuruat sochna aur baat karna seekhne se hoti hai. Lekin students abhi tak yeh nahi jante ki sochna kaise hai. Professor ek professional creative hote hain aur ek expert creative thinker (sochne wale) hote hain. Wo students ko kisi artist ya designer ki tarah sochna seekhne mein guide karte hain.

Professors studio mein bahut bolte hain, lekin wo create karne ke baare mein lectures (bhashan) nahi dete. Wo students ko yeh nahi batate ki kya karna hai, aur wo students ko yeh bhi nahi batate ki unhe unka kaam pasand aaya ya nahi. Wo poori saavdhani (scrupulously) se aisa karne se bachte hain. Iske bajaye, unki baatein ek aisi performance (kala ka display) hai jo students ko yeh dikhati (reveals) hai ki ek expert creator banate (creating) waqt kaisa sochta hai. Professor sochne ki is performance mein shamil (engages) hote hain. Dusre shabdon mein, wo awaaz nikal kar sochte hain (think out loud), lekin ek aise dhyan se design kiye gaye tareeqe se jise main *studio talk* kehta hoon. Aap kisi student ko yeh nahi bata sakte ki kaisa sochna hai; aapko out loud soch kar thinking ko model (dikha) karna hota hai. Professor ki baton ko dhyan se (closely) analyse (gour) karke, hum dekh sakte hain ki unke bolne ka tareeqa—wo kya kehte hain yeh nahi, balki wo ise kaise kehte hain—wo creative thinking ko kaise model karta (darshata) hai.

**Creative hone ka matlab hai uljhan bhari (ambiguity) duniya mein kaam karna janna.**

Douglas Dowd, graphic design, Washington University in St. Louis

Ek professor yeh bata sakta hai ki kya koi student apne kaam ko clearly (saaf taur par) dekh sakta hai, is baat se ki wo student apne kaam ke baare mein kaisa bolta hai. Jaise-jaise students professor ke sochne ke tareeqe ko apne andar apnate (internalize) hain, yeh unki creative practice ko aage ki taraf drive (push) karta hai.

### **Chapter 9. Dialogue (Baat-cheet)**

Sabse behtareen ideas creativity ke dialogue (batchit) se nikal kar aate (emerge) hain. Creativity *hoti hai*; isey hone ke liye *force (majboor)* nahi kiya jata. Artist aur uske kaam ke beech kisi dialogue ke bina, koi creativity nahi hai. Studio classes mein, students is dialogue mein hissa lena (participate) seekhte hain. Creativity is practice se ubhar kar aati (emerges) hai, creator ke dimaag se nahi. Creativity ke dialogue mein ek kamyaab hissa lene wala (participant) ban-ne ke liye, aapko yeh dekhne ke qabil hona chahiye ki kaam kya keh raha hai, yeh dekhna ki yeh aapko kya offer kar raha hai. Yeh dekhna ki yeh kahan kamyaab ho raha aur kahan nahi. Kaam ko ek apna wajood rakhne wali (autonomous) cheez ki tarah dekhna.

Students abhi tak nahi jante ki apne kaam ke sath is tareeqe se interact (pesh ana) kaise karein. Bhale hi student ne kaam banaya ho, professor yeh behtar dekh leta hai ki kaam kya keh raha hai. Studio talk mein, professor student ko samjhata (translates) hai ki kaam kya keh raha hai. Jab aap dekhne ke kabil ho jate hain, toh aap apne khulte hue (unfolding) kaam ke sath ek dialogue mein involve (engage) hone ke liye taiyar ho jate hain, ek aisa dialogue jo har pal chalta rehta hai (flows from moment to moment). Aap dekh sakte hain ki kaam kya kar raha hai, jaise ki iska apna khud ka ek dimaag (mind) ho, aur phir aap us par respond karte hain. Improvisational (mauqe par banane wale) process ki apni khud ki ek energy hoti hai. Control mein rehne ke bajaye, aap control share karte hain.

Is chapter mein, main aapko yeh dikhaunga ki professors students ke samne creativity ka dialogue kaise perform karte hain, taaki wo (students) observe kar sakein ki professors kaise dekhte hain.

### **You Can't Tell People What They Don't Know (Aap logon ko wo nahi bata sakte jo wo nahi jante)**

Ek graphic designer aur cartoonist, Douglas Dowd ne students ko naye tareeqe se color ko dekhna sikhane ke liye *Ugly Colors* naam ka ek bada (elaborate) aur multi-step assignment describe kiya (samjhaya).

*Sabse pehle, main unhe apne sabhi paints laane ko kehta hoon. Phir main unse kehta hoon, "Main chahta hoon ki aap jitna ho sake utna sabse bhadda (ugliest) color banayein. Main chahta hoon ki aap ulti jaisa (barfiest), sabse bhadda, aur ganda (nastiest) color mix karein." Aur wo ek tarah se haste (giggle) hain aur poochte hain, "Iska kya matlab hai, bhadda (ugly)?" aur main kehta hoon, "Khair, mujhe lagta hai jab aap ise dekhenge toh aap samajh jayenge. Mere liye ugly cheezein banayein." Toh wo wahi karte hain. Phir main unse unhe deewar par lagane ko kehta hoon, aur unse poochta hoon: "Hum in ugly colors ke baare mein kya keh sakte hain?" Wo aksar dark hote the. Toh: "Khair, kya aap kuch halke (lighter) ugly colors bana sakte hain?"*

*Phir main unhe ek khubsurat (pretty) color banane ko kehta hoon. Beshak, wo pretty color banane ka kaam turant (in no time) pura kar lete the. Zyada-tar wo seedha tube se nikala gaya hota tha. Koi mixing nahi hoti. Toh phir main unse apne pretty colors ki range ko badhane (expand) ke liye kehta hoon. Toh hum unhe dekhte the aur main poochta, "Ugly colors ko ugly, aur pretty colors ko pretty kya banata hai?" Aam taur par, jo cheezein unhone ugly banane ke liye ki hoti thin, unke sath unka engagement (judav) kahin zyada active hota tha. Unhone zyada colors mix kiye the, unme zyada complexity (pechidgi) thi, zyada subtlety (baarikiyan) thin.*

*Phir, agli class se pehle, maine unhe kaam karne ke liye ek color chunne ko kaha. Maine unse apni (color sheet) cheezon ko chote-chote chips mein katne aur unhe ek plastic bag (baggie) mein laane ko kaha. Toh phir main unse kehta, "Apna chip apni daayin taraf (right) wale insaan ko de do. Ab wo insaan aapke pehle color ko barbad (screw up) karne ke liye sabse ganda (nastiest) color chunega jo wo soch sakta hai." Phir aapko un dono colors ke sath ek sath kaam karna hota tha. Unhe dono ke beech pul (bridge) banane ke liye ek teesra color chunna padta tha, phir chautha (fourth), aur ek paanchva (fifth). Toh, mera padosi (neighbor) jaanbujh kar gusse wale irade (hostile intent) ke sath color do aur chaar chun raha hai.*

*Phir unhe isse ek color ki bunai (weaving) karni hoti thi. Wo har color mein paper ki strips (pattiyan) paint karte the aur sach mein unhe bun (weaving) dete the.*

Yeh assignment sunne mein bahut complex (pechida) lag raha tha, aur mujhe nahi pata tha ki iska maqsad (purpose) kya tha. Maine unse poocha, is assignment se aapka learning outcome (seekhne ka nateeja) kya hai?

*Main chahta hoon ki wo yeh samjhein ki wo dynamic (lagatar badalne wale) rishte kaun se hain jinke sath unhe engage (judna) karna hoga, aur kisi hadd tak (on some level) control karna hoga, taaki ek asardaar (effective) nateeja nikal sake.*

Ek designer na hone ke naate, main nahi samajh paya ki iska kya matlab tha. Maine unhe aur zayada explanation (samjhane) dene ke liye kaha (pressed). Maine sujhav (suggested) diya, "Kyun na ugly colors aur pretty colors ke kuch examples ke sath ek PowerPoint presentation se shuruat ki jaye?" Unka jawab tha:

*Agar aap unhe bas bata dete hain, ya ek PowerPoint presentation de dete hain, toh wo us knowledge ko haasil (possess) nahi kar payenge. Wo isey nahi janenge. Yeh unke dimaag ke ek taraf se jayega aur dusri taraf se nikal jayega. Logon ko cheezein batane ki asal mein koi keemat (value) nahi hoti. Main bahut saari explanation deta hoon, lekin wo hamesha aakhir mein hota hai. Phir wo kehte hain, "Aapne mujhe yeh do hafte pehle kyun nahi bataya? Maine apna itna waqt barbad kar diya." Wo beech mein gussa (pissed) ho jate hain.*

Is assignment ke aakhir mein, students is baat ko samajh jate hain (get it). Wo waqayi mein samajh jate hain ki dynamic relationships kya hain. Lekin assignment karne se pehle, unke paas yeh samajhne ka koi rasta nahi tha. Aakhir mein, wo ise dekh sakte hain. Lekin shuru karne se pehle ise dekh pana namumkin (impossible) tha.

Koi aapko dekhna kaise hai yeh bata raha ho usay sun kar aap dekhna nahi seekh sakte. Aap koi textbook padh kar ya YouTube video dekh kar dekhna nahi seekh sakte. Dekhna (Seeing) koi hosh mein (conscious), lafzo se bhara (verbal) knowledge nahi hai. Yeh practice ka knowledge hai—ek aisa knowledge jo duniya mein dhansa hua (embedded) hai. Dekhna seekhne ke liye, aapko process se guzarna hoga, assignment ke parameters (hudood/niyamon) aur professor ki wisdom (samajhdari) aur tajurbe (experience) dwara guide hote hue.

### **All Art and Design Schools Teach This Way (Sabhi Art aur Design Schools Isi Tarah Padhate Hain)**

Studio model ka istemal sabhi art aur design schools mein sabhi professors karte hain. Sabhi professors ek hi tareeqe se padhana kaise seekh gaye? Unme se kisi ne bhi art padhana sikhane ki class nahi li; unhone galtiyan kar-karke (through trial and error) khud ko sikhaya. Claudia Hart, jo School of the Art Institute of Chicago mein virtual reality aur 3D simulation technologies padhati hain, unhone mujhe bataya, "Maine apna khud ka structure banaya hai. Maine apni khud ki pedagogy (padhane ka tareeqa) develop ki hai, aur mujhe is par garv (proud) hai." John Hendrix ne mujhe bataya, "Instructors aapas mein is tarah ke sawalon ke baare mein baat nahi karte jaise aap pooch rahe hain. Wo is tareeqe se analytic (jaanch-partal karne wale) nahi hain."

Jab maine 2010 mein professors ka interview lena shuru kiya tha, toh main Washington University in St. Louis (WashU) mein psychology ka professor tha. Mere office se thodi door (short walk) par hi Sam Fox School of Design & Visual Arts tha. WashU mein, art aur design school university ke mashhoor medical school ke saaye (overshadowed) mein chhup jata hai. Kai undergraduate students pre-med track (medical ki padhai se pehle ki degree) par hote hain, aur sciences ka bhari bojh uthate hain. Lekin art aur design school ko bhi bahut izzat (highly regarded) di jati hai, aur wo students yahan BFA karne aate hain. Kabhi-kabhi (Occasionally), koi pre-med student art ya design class ko ek elective (apni pasand ka subject) ke taur par chunta hai. Unhe lagta hai ki isme asani se 'A' grade mil jayega; wo hairan hone wale hote hain (in for a surprise).

### **The Unity of Art and Design (Art aur Design ka Eka)**

**Art aur design padhane ke beech kai ek jaisi baatein (similarities) hain. Hum potential (sambhavna/qabiliyat) ko bahar nikal (unleashing) rahe hain. Yeh ek tarah ka creative confidence hai jahan aap yeh mante (believe) hain ki kuch mumkin (possible) hai. Yeh us be-chaini (uncomfortable) mein sahaj (comfortable) mehsoos karne ki kabiliyat par bharosa (confidence) hai. Wo uljhan (ambiguity) aur pechidgi (complexity). Logon ko agency (azaadi/taqat) dena, kisi ko khud ki qabiliyat pehchanne (self-actualize) mein madad karna.**

Allan Chochinov, industrial design, School of Visual Arts

Creativity ki research karne wale do tarah ki creativity ki baat karte hain: *domain-general (jo sab jagah laagu ho)* aur *domain-specific (jo kisi khas field mein ho)*. Domain-specific creativity kisi ek chhote (narrow) hisse ki creativity hoti hai, jaise shayri (poetry) ya biomechanical design. Bahut si research batati hai ki creativity domain-specific hoti hai, jo ki aam samajh (common sense) jaisa lagta hai; sirf isliye ki aap ek creative kavi (poet) hain, iska matlab yeh nahi hai ki aap ek creative biomechanical engineer bhi banenge. Lekin research yeh bhi batati hai ki creative log sochne aur kaam karne ke kuch aam (common) tareeqe share karte hain. Yeh domain-general creativity hai; yeh aapke dwara kiye jane wale har kaam par laagu (applies) hoti hai. Ek sculptor bahut si aisi cheezein kar sakta hai jo ek graphic designer nahi kar sakta, aur ek typeface designer bahut si aisi cheezein kar sakta hai jo ek architect nahi kar sakta. Lekin yeh kitab in farq (differences) ke baare mein nahi hai; yeh un cheezon ke baare mein hai jo artists aur designers mein aam (share) hain, wo beliefs aur practices jo domain-general hain.

2021 mein, main Beth Grabowski ke sath baitha, jo ek printmaker hain aur University of North Carolina in Chapel Hill mein art padhati hain. Aapne unki class figure 0.9 mein dekhi hai. Maine unhe kuch videos dikhaye jo maine dusri universities ki design classes mein banaye the. Maine unhe sabse pehle WashU mein Heather Corcoran ki communication design class dikhayi, jahan Corcoran ek student ke do posters ka tajziya (critiquing) kar rahi thin. Wo possibilities (sambhavnaein) bata rahi thin aur student ke liye explore karne ke liye naye issues ko pehchan (identifying) rahi thin (aap iske baare mein chapter 9 mein padhenge). Grabowski ne kaha ki unki apni art classes mein student critique ki unki khud ki approach bilkul aisi hi (very similar) thi. Bhale hi wo ek artist hain aur Corcoran ek designer hain, dono ek hi tareeqe (similar ways) se padhate hain.

Maine Steve Heller ka interview liya, jo SVA mein padhate hain aur ek behtareen (prolific) design educator hain, jinhone 200 se zyada kitabein likhi hain. Maine unke sath in 71 themes ko share kiya; maine unhe bataya ki yeh un sabhi art aur design professors par laagu hote hain jinka maine interview liya tha. Wo hairan nahi hue: "Ikeesvi sadi (twenty-first-century) ke is daur mein, mujhe sach mein art aur design ke beech koi bada farq nahi lagta."

### **Don't Say “Creativity”! ("Creativity" Mat Kaho!)**

Dashko (decades) ka teaching experience rakhne wale SCAD ke ek photography professor ne mujhe us waqt ke baare mein bataya jab wo art school mein the, kafi arsa pehle (long ago). Apne ek assignment ke dauran, unhe ek nayi photography technique mil gayi (stumbled on). Wo itne excited the ki wo garv (proudly) ke sath apne professor ko batane gaye, jo ek jane-mane mashhoor (well-known established) photographer the. Unke professor ne ek kagaz ke tukde par kuch numbers jaldi se likhe (scribbled), unhe diya, aur kaha "library jao aur is call number ko dekho."

Wo chal kar library gaye, shelf dhoondhi, aur kitab nikali. Unhe pata chala ki yeh unke professor ne hi likhi thi, wo bhi tees (30) saal pehle. Topic kya tha? *Wahi technique jiske baare mein unhe laga tha ki unhone ise ijad (invented) kiya tha.*

Photographer ne mujhe yeh kahani is baat ko sabit karne ke liye sunayi ki art aur design mein originality (kuch naya ya apna) naam ki koi cheez nahi hoti. John Hendrix (illustration, WashU) ne mujhe bataya: "Main kabhi nahi kahunga ki mera kaam original hai." Amy Thompson (book arts, WashU): "Mujhe nahi lagta ki art mein ab koi bhi original ideas bache hain." Professors students ke samne "originality (naye-pan)" shabd ka istemal karne se bachte hain; ek se zyada logon ne toh ise "khatarnak (dangerous)" bhi kaha.

Judith Kruger aur un dusre MFA students ke baare mein sochein jinka maine interview liya tha. Agar aapne art school mein BFA karne mein chaar saal aur phir MFA karne mein do saal aur bitaye hote, aur aap ek artist ke taur par apna professional career shuru karne wale hote, toh kya aap yeh dawa (claim) nahi karna chahte ki aapka kaam original tha? In students ne aisa nahi kiya. Unhone poori tarah se (explicitly) aur zor de kar (vehemently) inkar kiya ki unka kaam original tha. Aur yeh wo log hain jinka career goal ek professional artist ban-na hai.

Is project ko shuru karne ke liye, maine professors ke liye sawalon ki ek list taiyar ki. Mera pehla interview, 2010 mein, SCAD painting professor Sandra Reed ke sath tha. Maine is sawal se shuruat ki, "Aap students ko creative ban-na kaise sikhati hain?" Wo tameez-dar (polite) thin, lekin yeh saaf tha ki wo is sawal se asahaj (not comfortable) thin:

*Tum jante ho, yeh kuch aisa hai ki, tum jante ho, mujhe nahi pata. Mujhe nahi pata. Um . . . main isey is tarah nahi kahungi.*

Ek saal baad, WashU mein, maine ek printmaker Lisa Bulawsky se poocha ki wo creativity kaise sikhati hain. Unka jawab zyada direct (seedha) tha:

*Mera iska sabse pehla andaruni (gut) jawab yeh hai ki aap nahi sikhate. Aap mujhse kuch aisi cheez pooch rahe hain jiske baare mein main kabhi baat nahi karungi. Aap unhe sikhate hain ki materials aur processes ka istemal kaise karna hai aur phir aap unhe critically (gehrai se) sochna sikhate hain.*

Kuch hafton baad, maine sculptor Ron Leax (WashU) se poocha, "Aap kisi student ke kaam mein creativity ko kaise define (bayan) karenge?" Unhone kaha:

*Oh, yaar. Main isse utni door, utni tezi se bhagunga jitna... Main kahunga, uh, yeh kaise hota hai aur yeh kya hai, mujhe nahi pata. Dekho, aapko kisi se bhi yeh nikalwana bahut mushkil (really hard time) lagega... zyada-tar artists is baare mein baat hi nahi karna chahte.*

Zyada-tar artists "creativity" shabd ke sath comfortable nahi hain, aur sabne inkar (denied) kiya ki wo isey sikha rahe hain.

* Julie Varland, ek architect jo SCAD mein padhati hain, students ke aas-paas "creativity" shabd ka istemal karne se dhyan rakhti (careful) hain. "Agar main 'creativity' shabd ka istemal karti hoon, toh students udte huye gubbaro (floating balloons) ki tarah ho jate hain. Bina kisi disha ke (Directionless). Bina kisi guide ke (Guideless)." Jess Parris Westbrook SAIC mein kehte hain, "'Creativity' ek mushkil shabd hai. Mujhe isse hamesha paidaish (fertility), ya bhagwan se milne wali kisi sunehri roshni (golden light) ka khayal aata hai."
* Adam Trowbridge SAIC mein kehte hain, "Main kabhi 'creative' shabd ka istemal nahi karta." Catherine Rosamond SVA mein kehti hain, "Yeh ek aisa shabd hai jo mujhe confuse karta hai aur uljha deta (stumps me) hai. Kya main creativity sikha sakti hoon? Mujhe toh yeh bhi nahi pata ki yeh kya hoti hai. Mujhe yeh shabd pasand nahi hai."

Apne interviews shuru karne se pehle, is project ke liye mera temporary title (working title) *Learning How to Create (Banana Seekhna)* tha. Lekin sabko yeh bekar laga (hated it). CalArts ki Louise Sandhaus ne kaha: "'Creativity' mat kaho!" Unhone aage kaha: "Mujhe nahi pata ki kisi ko creative hona kaise sikhaya jaye." Dusre professors ne mujhe bataya ki "creativity" shabd hai:

*fetishistic (kisi aisi cheez se lagaav jise mantiq se na samjhaya ja sake)*
*weird (ajeeb)*
*jargony (kisi khass peshe ka bhari shabd)*
*vague (saaf tor par bayan na hone wala)*
*elusive (samajh se bahar)*

Apne interviews mein baad mein, kuch professors ne is baat ko maana (acknowledged) ki creativity unke kaam ka ek zaroori (important) hissa hai. Amy Thompson (WashU) ne mujhe bataya, "Agar hum creativity ko asaan (facilitating) nahi bana rahe hain, toh mujhe nahi lagta ki hum apna kaam kar rahe hain." Lekin Thompson inkar karti hain ki wo isey "sikha (teaching)" rahi hain. Yeh shabd unhe aam schools (traditional schooling) ki yaad dilata hai, jahan teacher students ko lecture deta hai, aur yahan aisa nahi ho raha hai. "Sikhane (teaching)" ke bajaye, professors kehte hain:

* "Main unhe creative hone ki ijazat (permission) de rahi hoon." (Amy Honchell, painter aur sculptor, School of the Art Institute of Chicago)
* "Wo pehle se hi creative hain. Main bas kuch darwaze kholne ki, kuch alag raste (hallways) dikhane ki koshish kar raha hoon jin par wo chal sakein." (Grant Shaffer, illustrator, School of Visual Arts)

Dusre professors kehte hain ki wo hain:
*Creativity ko badhane wale (Maximizing creativity)*
*Creativity ko prerit karne wale (Inspiring creativity)*
*Creativity ko aage badhane wale (Promoting creativity)*
*Creativity ki chingari jagane wale (Igniting creativity)*
*Creativity ke kabil banane wale (Enabling creativity)*
*Creativity ke liye mahoul taiyar karne wale (Setting the stage for creativity)*
*Creativity ke beej bone wale (Planting the seeds for creativity)*

Art aur design school mein, students creative hona seekhte hain. Lekin yeh popular media ya business ki kitabo mein chhapne wali aam (clichés) kahanuton wali creativity ke concept se alag hai. Professors achanak aane wali (sudden) prerna (inspiration) nahi sikhate; wo originality (naye-pan) nahi sikhate; wo behtareen ideas laana nahi sikhate. Shayad yeh kehna sabse acha hoga ki art aur design school duniya mein rehne ka ek tareeqa (a way of being in the world) sikhata hai. Students aisi creative practices seekhte hain jo unka zindagi bhar sath dengi (support karengi). Art aur design ka nichod (essence) dekhna (seeing), sochna (thinking), aur banana (making) hai. Ek baar jab aap dekhna seekh jate hain, toh creativity apne aap piche chali aati hai (follows).

Zyada-tar log, jab kisi painting ko dekhte hain, toh yeh nahi sochte ki ise banaya kaise gaya tha. Object par is focus ko art ke baare mein hone wali aalochnatmak charchaon (critical discourse) se aur badhava milta hai. Jab critics art ke baare mein baat karte hain, toh wo object ko aise discuss karte hain jaise isey waisa hi hona tha; jaise yeh hamesha se waisa hi tha. Critical reviews is baare mein baat nahi karte ki object alag kaise ho sakta tha, ya jo galtiyan chhupayi gayin, ya wo kai creative faisle jinpar artist ne gaur kiya aur phir unhe chhod diya. Hum shayad hi kabhi us creative process ke baare mein sochte hain jiska nateeja wo object tha ya un dusre raston (alternative paths) ke baare mein sochte hain jinse koi alag object ban sakta tha.

Jab aap dekhna seekh jate hain, toh aap jis kaam ko dekhte hain wo dynamic (badalta hua) aur flux (lagatar badlaav) mein hota hai, jo alag-alag visual forces ke hisab se badal raha hota hai. Jab aap dekhna jante hain, toh aap kaam ke piche dekhne ke qabil hote hain. Aap jante hain ki us chhupe hue process ko samne (uncover) kaise lana hai jiska nateeja wo object tha. Jab aap apne khud ke kaam mein is process mein shamil (engage) hona shuru karte hain, toh aap dusron ke kaamon ke piche ke process ko dekhne ke qabil ho jate hain.

Art aur design ke piche iterative aur improvisational (mauqe par banane wale) process mein shamil hone ke liye, yeh zaroori hai ki aap shuru karne se pehle apne idea ko develop karne mein bahut zyada waqt bitane se bachein. Agar aap ek achi tarah se tayaar kiye gaye (well-elaborated) vision ke sath shuru karte hain, aur phir apna waqt isey pura karne (executing) mein bitate hain, toh yeh ek linear (seedha) aur andaza lagane layaq (predictable) process hai. Lekin professional artists aur designers ek iterative andaz mein kaam karna pasand karte hain jo process ke dauran naye ideas ko ubharne (emerge) ka mauka deta hai. Students ko kisi ek idea par bahut jaldi ruk jane (settling) se bachane ke liye guide karte waqt, professors aise assignments design karte hain jo unhe idea se lekar execution (pura karne) tak ke ek linear raste par chalne se rokte hain. School of Visual Arts ke ek illustrator, Grant Shaffer, ek illustration class mein is do-hafte ke assignment ka istemal karte hain: Wo students se kisi fairy tale (pari katha) ke beech mein se sirf ek page ko illustrate karne ke liye kehte hain. Kisi pehle se maujood fairy tale ka nahi; unki apni banayi hui fairy tale ka. Halanki, wo page shuruat ya aakhir ka nahi ho sakta. Unhe kahani likhne ya kahani ko illustrate karne ki zaroorat nahi hai—wo sirf ek page banate hain. Yeh waisa hi hoga jaise aapne bacchon ki kitab se ek page faad liya ho; bas ek random page. Shaffer kehte hain, "Bahut se students ghabra jate hain (freeze) aur sochte hain, mujhe nahi pata kya likhna hai. Main kahani kaise soch sakta hoon?" Jab students pehli baar ek iterative improvisational process mein shamil hona seekh rahe hote hain toh unhe guidance ki zaroorat hoti hai, aur Shaffer unhe kuch steps ke zariye guide karte hain jo unhone saalo mein develop kiye hain.

*Sabse pehle, bees (20) shabd likho. Koi bhi shabd. Chhata (Umbrella), tidda (grasshopper), boombox, uh, blimp (hawa ka jahaz), mitti ka dhela (dirt clod), tum jante ho, khidki se bahar dekho, bas shabd likhna shuru karo. Phir main kahunga ki apne bees shabdon mein se teen shabdon par ghera (circle) lagao. Apni pasand ke teen shabdon par ghera lagao. Toh, tum grasshopper, blimp, aur boombox ko kaise mila (combine) sakte ho? Aur tum students ko yeh kehte hue dekh sakte ho, mujhe nahi pata, shayad grasshopper ud nahi sakta. Isliye wo ek blimp mein udta hai aur uske paas ek boombox hai, aur boombox padosiyon ko pareshan kar raha hai. Aur achanak tumhare paas ek grasshopper ki kahani hoti hai jise udna nahi aata, aur wo blimp mein udta hai. Aur phir mujhe lagta hai ki tum in ideas ko aage badhana (cultivating) shuru kar sakte ho.*

Is do-hafte ke assignment ke baad, Shaffer is tajurbe (experience) ke aadhar par ek dusra assignment dete hain. Is baar, wo students se ek classic children's tale, jaise "Little Red Riding Hood" ya "Rapunzel" lene ko kehte hain, aur phir isey naye dhang se tasavvur (reimagine) karne ko kehte hain. Wo unhe kehte hain:

*Lekin isey apna andaaz (spin) do, jaise 2082 mein Little Red Riding Hood. Agle hafte, hum apni khud ki kahani ke liye ek single page banate hain. Har koi kya soch kar aata hai yeh dekhna kafi mazedar (fun) hota hai. Mera ek student tha jisne ek billi aur ek aurat ki kahani banayi. Yeh aurat apni billi se bahut pyar karti thi. Billi usse pyar karti thi. Aur phir ek din aurat ka ek boyfriend ban gaya aur wo apni billi ke sath kam waqt bitane lagi, aur billi ne aurat se divorce ke liye case file kar diya, aur wo cat court gaye. Aur yeh ek ajeeb (crazy), pyari (lovely), aur anokhi kahani ban gayi. Mujhe acha lagta hai jab kahaniyan thodi atpati/khayali (surreal) ho jati hain.*

Ek acha assignment students ko ek iterative process ke zariye guide karne ke liye design kiya jata hai. Unhe guidance ki zaroorat hoti hai, kyunki jab wo is process mein shamil hona nahi jante, toh wo qudrati taur par us linear, idea-driven kaam karne ke tareeqe par wapas chale jate hain jiske sath unhone college shuru kiya tha. Ek acha assignment students ko is linear process ko follow karne se rokta hai. Shaffer apna assignment is tarah design karte hain ki students ek linear process ko poora karne (completion) ke liye kisi ek idea ka istemal nahi kar sakte.

Jab hum samne dikhne wale aur mukammal (finished) object par focus karte hain, toh yeh sochna natural hai ki us kaam ke piche ek hi vision ya idea tha. Kyunki aap kaam ko ek finished cheez ki tarah dekhte hain, aap yeh maan lete (assume) hain ki artist is par kaam shuru karne se pehle hi janta tha ki yeh kaisa hoga. Kaam pehle creator ke dimaag mein maujood tha aur phir medium ke materials ka istemal karke isey zinda kiya (brought to life) gaya. Yeh creativity ka *linear insight* (seedha aur achanak aane wala khayal) nazariya hai (dekhein figure 1.2). Is nazariye mein, idea ko poora karna (execution) bahut dilchasp nahi hota—yeh idea se lekar finished work tak ka ek seedha rasta hai. Creativity process ko shuru karti hai, lekin idea ko execute karne ke liye creativity ki zaroorat nahi hoti. Aur in creative ideas ko soch pane ki kabiliyat—yahi artists aur designers ko baaki hum logon se alag banati hai. Agar professors isi tarah ki creativity sikha rahe hain, toh wo students ko yeh sikha rahe honge ki zyada aur behtar ideas kaise laye jayen, aur un ideas ko unke chune hue medium mein wafadari (faithfully) aur kamyabi ke sath kaise execute kiya jaye.

Lekin, jin professors ka maine interview liya aur jinhe observe kiya, unke anusaar, linear insight model ek jhooth (myth) hai. Create karne ki asliyat kahin zyada pechida (complicated) hai aur isey seekhna zyada mushkil hai. Introduction mein, humne seekha ki "originality" aur "creativity" shabd khatarnak hain. Wo shabd students ko ek ache idea ke sath shuruat karne par hadd se zyada focus karne par majboor karte hain. Linear insight ka jhooth kam se kam do tareeqon se galat hai. Pehla, creative cheezein aksar bade ideas se nahi aati hain. Arny Nadler, ek murtikar (sculptor) jo Washington University in St. Louis (WashU) mein padhate hain, samjhate hain ki yeh myth khatarnak kyun hai:

*Ek masla (issue) jo creative hone ke raste mein aata hai wo yeh hai ki jab aap is sab ko plan karne ki koshish karte hain. Aap apne dimaag mein over-edit (hadd se zyada badlaav) karte hain. Aap khud se kehte hain, 'Khair, yeh ek bewaqoofi bhara idea hai, yeh acha nahi dikhega.' Lekin aap in pieces ki asliyat ko apne aap ko sabit (prove) karne ka mauka hi nahi de rahe hain ki wo kya ban sakte hain.*

Dusra, creative process linear (seedha) nahi hota. Iske bajaye, creative works ek aise pechide (complex) process se ubhar kar (emerge) aate hain jiska andaza nahi lagaya ja sakta aur jo improvisational (mauqe par banne wala) hota hai (dekhein figure 1.3). Bhale hi process bikhra hua (messy) aur unpredictable (jiska andaza na lagaya ja sake) hota hai, iska ek pechida aur khoobsurat structure hota hai jo hamesha asardaar art aur design ke kamo ki taraf le jata hai. Art aur design ke objects ko sach mein dekhne ke liye, aapko us process ko samajhna hoga jo unhe banata hai.

Figure 1.3 creative process ka wo complex model dikhata hai jise professors sikhane ki koshish karte hain. Yeh process kisi linear process se kahin zyada complex hai; isme maharat hasil karna (master) aur isey sikhana zyada mushkil hai. In dono models ke beech ke farq Table 1.1 mein diye gaye hain. Jin artists aur designers ka maine interview liya, unke dwara sikhaya jane wala creative process linear insight model se poori tarah alag hai. Artists aur designers ke kaam karne ka tareeqa linear nahi hota. Wo kaam karna shuru karte hain aur is baat par vishwas (trust) karte hain ki materials ke sath dialogue (batchit/tal-mel) se ideas samne aayenge. Rasta unpredictable aur nonlinear (tedha-medha) hota hai. Yeh materials ke sath ek improvisational (mauqe ke hisab se) samna (encounter) hai, materials aur artist ya designer ke beech ek dialogue. Agar galtiyan (mistakes), accidents, ya surprises samne aate hain, toh unhe aapke pehle se tay kiye gaye goal ki taraf jane wale raste ki rukawatein (speedbumps) nahi mana jata. Unhe process ko ek alag disha (direction) mein modne ke mauko (opportunities) ke taur par dekha jata hai.

Is tarah ki improvisational creativity mein maharat hasil karna is baat par nirbhar (depends) karta hai ki dekhna kaise hai.

* Aapko yeh dekhna aana chahiye ki jab koi idea ubhar kar aaye (emerges).
* Aapko har ubharne wale idea ko parakhna (evaluate) hoga aur tay karna hoga ki kin par aage kaam karna (pursue) faydemand hoga.
* Aapko yeh dekhna aana chahiye ki jab aapse koi galti ho, aur aapko yeh dekhne ke qabil hona chahiye ki aap us galti ka istemal ek nayi faydemand disha (productive direction) mein mudne ke liye kaise kar sakte hain.
* Aapko yeh dekhna aana chahiye ki materials kya kar rahe hain, wo aapse kya keh rahe hain, aur aap unke sath ek lagatar chalne wale dialogue mein jawab (respond) dene ke qabil hone chahiye.

Dekhne ki kabiliyat ke bina, improvisational process bina kisi manzil ke (aimlessly) bhatakta rehta hai. Aur jab aisa hota hai, toh students apne jane-pehchane (familiar) raste par wapas chale jate hain: linear insight model par. Dekhna jane bina us tareeqe se kaam karna mumkin hai. Jab students ko idea-execution model ka istemal karne se roka jata hai, toh wo shuru mein kho jate hain. Wo abhi tak nahi jante ki jab koi umeed-bhara (promising) idea samne aaye toh use notice kaise karna hai. Wo abhi tak nahi jante ki apne materials ko ek lagatar chalne wale dialogue mein apne partner ke taur par kaise dekhna hai.

Professors is baat ko samajhte hain. Wo students ko linear rasta lene se rokna chahte hain. Lekin, sath hi, wo yeh bhi samajhte hain ki students ko dekhna nahi aata. Wo apne tajurbe se jante hain ki students kamyaab nahi honge—kam se kam abhi toh nahi—agar wo ek nonlinear (tedha-medha) rasta apnane ki koshish karein. Iska hal (solution) *project assignment* hai. Shaffer ki tarah, professors students ko dhyan se design kiye gaye assignments dete hain jinme achi tarah se tayaar ki gayi constraints (rukawatein) hoti hain. Bina constraints ke, students kho jate hain aur unka koi maqsad nahi rehta; constraints ke sath, wo asani se ek iterative process ke guided version ko follow kar sakte hain. Ek baar jab wo is process mein maharat (master) hasil kar lete hain, toh unhe constraints ki zaroorat nahi rehti.

**Halanki aksar yeh mana jata hai ki artists, designers, aur creative thinkers 'eureka moments' (achanak idea aane ka pal) mehsoos karte hain jisme ek shandaar idea samne aata hai, lekin practice mein, creativity ek lamba process hai. Isme aksar banane wale (maker) ko kisi cheez ko baar-baar banana padta hai, aur har baar pichle iteration (dohraav) se seekhna padta hai.**

Eva Sutton, transmedia artist, Rhode Island School of Design

Professors ne yeh seekha hai ki students program mein is galatfehmi (misconception) ke sath aate hain ki creativity kaise kaam karti hai. Ek professor ne mujhe bataya, "Hum un athara (18) saal ke bacchon ke sath deal kar rahe hain jinke paas bahut hi romantic (khayali) ideas hote hain." Asardaar (effective) artists ban-ne ke liye students ko us nazariye ko piche chhodna padta hai. Figure 1.3 ke creative process mein, banayi gayi cheezein sirf behtareen ideas nahi hotin jinhe ek kabil craftsperson (karigar) ne ache se execute kiya ho. Iske bajaye, artworks ek soche-samjhe (deliberate) process se, an-soche (unpredictably) dhang se lekin lagatar (consistently) ubhar kar aate hain, jinki khasiyaton ko seekha ja sakta hai. Successful artists aur designers ke paas koi anokhi (unique) personality traits hona zaroori nahi hai.

Professors kehte hain ki wo kisi ko bhi successful creative works generate karna sikha sakte hain—shart yeh hai ki student mein utsukta (curiosity) aur lagan (dedication) ho aur wo zaroori waqt aur mehnat (work) lagane ke liye taiyar ho.

Art aur design schools mein, aap figure 1.3 wala process seekhte hain, wo process jo in programs mein padhane wale successful artists aur designers istemal karte hain. Iska learning outcome (seekhne ka nateeja) ek conscious (hosh-mand) aur soche-samjha (deliberate) process hai jisme critical thinking (gehri soch) aur awareness (jaagrukta), samajh (perceptiveness) aur gaur-o-fikar (reflection), aur koshish-bhari khoj (effortful exploration) shamil hai.

### **Iteration (Baar-baar dohrana) aur Improvisation (Mauqe par banana)**

**Aap isey shatranj (chess) ka khel keh sakte hain. Main canvas par ek chaal (move) chalta hoon aur phir yeh mujhe kuch batata hai. Main us par respond (jawab) karta hoon. Yeh ek dusre ke prati lagatar (constant) responses hain.**

Thurston Belmer, MFA student, painting, Washington University in St. Louis

Tobi Kahn ek painter aur sculptor hain jinhone 1985 se SVA mein padhaya hai. Wo is baat par thoda chidh (bristles) jate hain ki wo students ko "padha (teaching)" rahe hain. "Main asal mein unhe yeh nahi *sikhata* ki dekhna kaise hai. Main unhe *madad* karta hoon usko samajhne (interpret) mein jo wo dekh rahe hain." Wo yeh kaise karte hain? Shaffer ki tarah—structured project assignments ke sath. Apne pehle saal ke pehle semester mein, students ek painting ko structure karne ke liye zaroori tools seekhte hain—color theory, landscape imagery ko copy karna, aur still life. Dusre semester mein, wo apne students se teen paintings ki ek series banwate hain, ek structured process ka istemal karke jo unhe linear tareeqe se kaam karne ki unki instincts (fitrati aadat) se door le jata hai:

*Main unse kisi bhi aise subject par ek painting banane ko kehta hoon jo unhe dilchasp lage. Main unhe yeh nahi batata ki agla assignment kya hai. Phir, agle assignment ke liye, students pehli painting se ek hissa (element) chunte hain taaki pehli painting se alag ek nayi kahani suna sakein. Wo apni hi painting ko dekh rahe hote hain, aur phir wo uske aadhar par ek kahani bana rahe hote hain. Teesra assignment poori tarah se ek alag disha (different direction) mein ek painting banana hota hai.*

Wo iski ek misaal (example) dete hain:

*Maan lijiye ki yeh ek summer landscape ki painting hai. Aur yeh ek summer landscape ka behad khubsurat bayan (articulation) hai. Dusri painting is baare mein hai ki aapko us summer landscape mein kaisa mehsoos hua: Kya aapko bahut garmi lag rahi hai? Kya aapko paseena aa raha hai? Teesri painting mein, aap jo bhi wo tha uska ulta (opposite) kar rahe hain. Ya toh puri tarah shanti (pure calmness) ke baare mein ya vacation mood mein hone ke baare mein. Dusre shabdon mein, ek hi experience (tajurbe) ko, aap teen alag-alag tareeqon se mehsoos kar rahe hain. Aap unhe pehle se nahi bata sakte ki agla kadam kya hone wala hai kyunki tab wo aage ki sochne lagenge.*

Dekhna seekhne ka maqsad aapko is creative process mein hissa lene ke liye taiyar karna hai. SCAD ke ek professor ne mujhe bataya, "Yeh actions (kaamon) ki ek series hai. Aap nishan (marks) banate hain, aap sone jate hain, aap uthte hain, aap nishano ko dekhte hain aur aap un nishano par aage kaam karte hain (build on), aur aap is baare mein sochte hain, aur wapas jakar phir se koshish karte hain." Patricia Olynyk, ek artist jo WashU mein alag-alag disciplines (khetron) mein kaam karti hain, unhone kaha:

*Hum unke samne baithte hain aur unhe is baat ka deeply jaiza (critical analysis) lene ke process mein shamil karte hain ki kya kaam jo nateeja (generates) de raha hai wo wahi hai jo student apne content mein develop karne ki koshish kar raha hai. Kisi aise insaan ke liye jo art ki duniya mein kai saalo se hai, mujhe nahi lagta ki pehla iteration (dohraav) wo hota hai jo kaam kar jaye.*

Kevin Kaempf School of the Art Institute of Chicago (SAIC) mein first-year aur second-year foundations ko padhane ke baare mein batate hain: "Hamare saaf (explicit) learning objectives (maqsad) mein se ek yeh hai ki students yeh seekhein ki ek iterative process ke zariye kisi project ko develop kaise karna hai." Maine unse poocha, "Aapko kaise pata chalta hai ki unhone yeh seekh liya hai?"

*Jab wo isey hasil (achieve) kar lete hain toh yeh mere liye saaf hota hai kyunki iska saboot (evidence) maujood hota hai. Hum us process ke beech mein rukte hain aur dekhte hain ki unhone kya produce (banaya) kiya hai. Misaal ke taur par, kai baar unka paanch-hafte ka project hota hai, aur raste mein benchmarks (nishanat) hote hain, rukne ke pal hote hain, jahan hum critique dete hain ya student presentation deta hai. Kabhi-kabhi koi student har step poora nahi karta. Aakhir mein, unka project utna socha-samjha (thought through) ya mukammal (complete) nahi hota, aur unhe nahi pata hota ki kyun. Main unse kahunga, 'Yaad hai jab tumne koi bhi research poori nahi ki thi? Ya tumne material tests nahi kiye the? Yahi wajah hai ki tumhara project acha nahi hai—kyunki tum iterative process se nahi guzre.'*

Tasavvur (Imagine) karein ki aap athara (18) saal ke hain. Aap Rhode Island School of Design (RISD) mein apne pehle semester mein hain, jo duniya ke top art aur design schools mein se ek hai. Aap Spatial Dynamics (jagah aur movement ki class) le rahe hain, jo Kara Rooney dwara padhayi jane wali ek foundations class hai, jo ek interdisciplinary artist hain aur performance, sculpture, aur new media installation mein kaam karti hain. Wo aapko ek assignment poora karne ke liye teen hafte deti hain jise unhone design kiya hai aur jise wo *Multiples + Networks* kehti hain.

**Jab Brassaï ne Picasso se poocha ki kya painter ke ideas use ittefaq se (by chance) aate hain ya soche-samjhe design se, toh Picasso ne jawab diya, 'Mujhe koi idea nahi hai. Ideas sirf shuruati points hote hain. Main shayad hi kabhi unhe waise likh pata hoon jaise wo mere dimaag mein aate hain. Jaise hi main kaam karna shuru karta hoon, dusre ideas mere pen se ubharne (well up) lagte hain. Yeh janne ke liye ki aap kya draw karne wale hain, aapko draw karna shuru karna padega. Jab main khud ko ek khali paper ke samne pata hoon, toh mere dimaag mein hamesha yahi chal raha hota hai. Main apni marzi ke khilaf (in spite of myself) jo capture karta hoon wo mujhe mere apne ideas se zyada dilchasp lagta hai.'**

*Conversations with Picasso*, in Brassaï, 1999

Yeh chhah (6) steps students ko ek creative process ke zariye guide karte hain jisme iteration (dohraav) aur disha (direction) badalne ke mauke hote hain. Kyunki students ko har step par kuch deliver karna (dena) hota hai, isliye wo bas ek idea lekar project ko due date se pehli raat mein poora (all-nighter) nahi kar sakte. Assignment ko is tarah se structure karne ke liye students ko dheema (slow down) hona padta hai. Students se har kadam par unke kaam ke baare mein discuss karne ko kaha jata hai. Apne kaam ke baare mein gehrai se sochna (Reflecting) aur uske baare mein baat karna aksar unhe apni direction badalne par majboor karta hai. Unhe har step par alag-alag materials ke sath kaam karna padta hai; isse aise an-soche (unexpected) ideas ubharne (emerge) ke mauke milte hain jo materials se aate hain, aise ideas jo unhone soche bhi nahi hote agar unhone experiment karna shuru na kiya hota.

Students is assignment ke dauran bahut si technical skills seekhte hain. Wo research library ka istemal karna bhi seekhte hain. Lekin Rooney ka man-na hai ki sabse important learning outcome (seekhne ka nateeja) yeh hai ki dekhna kaise hai aur sochna kaise hai.

*Wo ek installation wali setting ke andar kaam karke (saman ko) bada (scaling up) kar rahe hain. Wo is baare mein soch rahe hain ki ek network kya banata hai, ek network ka kaam (functionality), uska tootna (breakdown). Misaal ke taur par, samajik niyamon (societal norms) ke lihaz se ek network kaise kaam karta hai? Hum is baare mein bahut research karte hain aur readings padhne ko dete hain ki is shabd 'network' ka kya matlab hai. Iske alawa, main unhe tees (30) alag-alag artists ke kaamon (practices) ki lagbhag dedh ghante ki ek slide lecture dikhati hoon jo sculpture aur installation, aur network ke idea ke sath bhi jude (engaged) hain. Yeh project kuch sach mein dilchasp results nikal kar (yields) lata hai. Main kya dhoondh rahi hoon is mamle mein kuch rules aur rukawatein (restrictions) hain. Lekin yeh students ko waqayi jis disha (direction) mein wo jana chahte hain wahan jane ki kafi azaadi deta hai.*

Kai art aur design schools mein saaf (explicit) learning goals hote hain jo faculty milkar develop karti hai. In goals mein aksar "iterative process seekhna" shamil hota hai. School of the Art Institute of Chicago mein, Adam Trowbridge ne mujhe apne Assessment Committee for Contemporary Practices (wo pehle foundation year ko yahi kehte hain) ke baare mein bataya.

*Ek cheez jis par sab sehmat the wo yeh thi ki hamein curriculum (syllabus) mein iteration (baar-baar karne ke process) ko promote (badhava dena) karna chahiye. Yahi wo cheez hai jo main dekhna chahta hoon. Beech ke (in-between) pieces kahan hain? Mujhe wo saare pieces dekhne hain jo beech mein bane the. Jo students kamyaab (successful) hote hain unke paas waqayi wo hota hai. Unse iteration karwana sach mein mushkil hai.*

### **Idea (Khayal) ka Khatra**

**Shuruat karne mein hichkichayein mat. Bas karein.**

Beth Grabowski apne art students ko University of North Carolina mein kehti hain

Jeffery Keedy ek asardaar (influential) type designer hain jinhone 1985 se Los Angeles mein California Institute of the Arts (CalArts) mein padhaya hai. Unka kaam New York ke Museum of Modern Art mein pradarshit (exhibited) kiya ja chuka hai. Wo Keedy Sans namak typeface ke creator hain. CalArts mein unki colleague, Louise Sandhaus ne mujhe bataya, "Jeff teachers ke teacher hain. Wo waqayi janamjaat hunarmand (gifted) hain." Keedy is myth (jhooth) ke sakht khilaf (extremely critical) hain ki acha design process ke shuru mein ek badiya idea aane par aadharit (based) hota hai:

*Mujhe chote students, first aur second years walon ki is aadat ko todna padta hai jisme wo sochne (thinking) ko banane (making) se, aur sochne ko kuch karne (doing) se alag rakhte hain. Zyada-tar logon ko yeh lagta hai ki aapke paas ek idea ya inspiration aati hai aur phir aap use poora (execute) karte hain. Aur is tarah aapke idea ya inspiration ki quality is baat ko tay karti (dictates) hai ki jo execute kiya gaya hai aur jo banaya gaya hai uski quality kya hogi. Agar aap ek tay shuda buniyad (premise) ke sath shuru karte hain, toh nateeja (outcome) andaza lagane layaq (predictable) hota hai. Yeh toh pehle se tay tha (foregone conclusion) ki yeh aisa dikhega kyunki aapki buniyad yeh thi. Jab bhi aap aisa karte hain, jab bhi aapke dimaag mein ek khayal hota hai ki ise kaisa hona chahiye aur phir aap use execute karte hain, toh yeh saaf zahir (obvious) ho jata hai.*

Hum mein se zyada-tar log, creativity ko ache ideas aane ke roop mein sochte hain. Mere apne discipline, psychology of creativity mein, mere kai colleagues is baat ki study karte hain ki jab kisi insaan ko koi naya idea aata hai toh uske dimaag mein kya hota hai. Yeh psychologists creativity ko *insight* (achanak samajh aane wale khayal) ke pal se jodte (associate) hain. Insight ka tasavvur (conception) Gestalt psychologists ne shuru kiya tha, jinhone tajweez (proposed) kiya tha ki insight dimaag ke achanak dobara structure (restructuring) hone ki wajah se hoti hai. Misaal ke taur par, psychologists ne is baat ki study ki hai ki conceptual (khayali) combinations aur analogies (tashbeeh/misaalo) ke zariye insights kaise paida (generated) hoti hain.

Keedy creativity ke is nazariye ko nakarte (rejects) hain.

*Ek cheez jo aap unhe dikhane (demonstrate) ki koshish kar rahe hote hain wo yeh hai ki common sense naam ki koi cheez nahi hoti. Yeh bas wahi rasta hai jis par aap chal kar aaye hain. Yahi aapne socha tha. Aur aapko lagta hai ki, yeh mujhe sabse zyada logical (tark-sangat), sabse asli (real) disha lagti hai. Khair, aisi paanch aur dishaein hain jo aap kar sakte the. Aur apne classmates ko dekhein. Unhone is par poori tarah se alag nazariya (take) apnaya. Kya wo galat hain? Nahi, wo nahi hain. Main unke samajhne ke liye ek context (sandarbh/mahoul) bana raha hoon. Yahan bahut, bahut si choices aur nazariye (points of view) hain. Cheezein kisi ek khaas tareeqe se kaam nahi kartin. Cheezein lagatar badal rahi (in flux) hain. Cheezein lagatar badalti hain, variables (hissay) lagatar ghoomte (move) hain.*

Keedy ne jo sabse zyada provoke (uksane) karne wali baatein kahin unme se ek yeh thi ki acha dikhne wala kaam paida (generate) karne ki khwahish seekhne mein ek rukawat (barrier) hai. Bahut se students shandaar technical skills ke sath art school aate hain. Aakhir-kaar, unhe admission lene ke liye apne high school ke kaam ka portfolio jama (submit) karna padta hai. Lekin jab aapko pata hai ki acha kaam kaise banaya jata hai, aur aapko pata hai ki yeh acha dikhta hai aur aapke professor ko bhi pata hai ki yeh acha dikhta hai, toh phir seekhne ke liye kya bacha hai? Aapko kyun badalna chahiye? Isliye kyunki agar aap wahi cheezein baar-baar karte rahenge, toh aap dekhna nahi seekh payenge, aur agle level par jane ke liye aapko dekhne ki zaroorat hoti hai. Keedy ke samne ek mushkil challenge hai: unhe in talented students ko convince (manana) karna padta hai ki bhale hi unka kaam acha dikhta ho, yeh abhi tak itna acha nahi hai. "Agar aap wahi karte rehte hain jisme aap pehle se hi ache hain, toh yeh dilchasp nahi hoga aur yeh unhe lamba waqt nahi chala (sustain) payega. Yeh ek bahut hi boring, an-interesting (gair dilchasp) wajood (existence) hai."

Desh ke us paar East Coast par, ek poori tarah se alag discipline mein kaam karte hue, artist Beth Grabowski bhi process par zor deti (emphasizes) hain aur idea wali myth (jhooth) ko nakarti (rejects) hain:

*Meri sabse badi shikayat, mere students ke sath meri sabse badi problem yeh soch (idea) hai ki idea pehle aata hai aur phir aap ise banate hain. Aur yeh soch ki banane (making) ki skill sirf ek aisi cheez hai jiska istemal aap is idea ko haqeeqat banane (realize) ke liye karte hain jo aapke dimaag mein poori tarah se ban chuka hai. Wo is idea ko kafi ehmiyat nahi dete ki idea apne aap mein badalta (evolves) hai aur asal mein banane (actual making) ke process se idhar-udhar dhakela (pushed around) jata hai.*

WashU mein art ki professor Cheryl Wassenaar ne bhi shuruati (initial) idea ki ehmiyat ko kam (discounted) bataya:

*Aap bahut se ideas ko acha bana sakte hain. Bahut kam aisa hota hai (very rarely) ki main koi idea sunu aur sochu ki, isme koi umeed (hope) nahi hai. Creativity sirf andhere mein teer marna (grasping at straws) aur achanak se kuch aisa bana lena nahi hai jo aapko acha lage. Yeh kisi idea par kaam karne aur use aage dhakelne, aur ise bada karne (expanding), aur ise nayi disha (redirecting) dene ka ek process hai, taaki aakhir mein aapke paas kuch aisa ho jo naya (inventive), sakht (rigorous), aur asardaar (effective) ho. Aap aisi choices kar rahe hain jo aap jo karne ki koshish kar rahe hain uske hisab se kafi soche-samjhe (deliberate) hain.*

Professors student ke kaam ko grade kaise karte hain? Wo sabhi kehte hain ki process final kaam se zyada zaroori hai aur wo process ko grade karte hain. Lekin, beshak, aap chahte hain ki final kaam acha dikhe, haina? Students chahte hain ki wo itna acha kaam banayein jise wo apne portfolio mein rakh sakein taaki unhe graduation ke baad ek achi job mil sake. Jab maine Douglas Dowd ka interview liya, jo WashU mein illustration aur communication design padhate hain, toh maine sawal kiya ki kya wo sach mein process ko grade karte hain ya darasal (in fact) final kaam ko grade karte hain: "Maan lijiye ek student deadline (due) se pehle wale weekend par ek behad shandaar (awesome) final kaam banata hai, aur usne process ka istemal nahi kiya. Aur maan lijiye aapko yeh pata tha. Kya kaam ke shandaar hone ke aadhar par unhe phir bhi 'A' milega?"

Dowd ne kaha:

*Process ka istemal kiye bina unke liye kuch aisa banana jo waqayi bemisaal (extraordinary) ho, asal mein mumkin nahi hai. Aisa kabhi nahi hota ki kisi student ke paas itna behtareen qudrati hunar (natural gift) ho ki use process se guzarne ki zaroorat hi na pade.*

### **Khatam karne ke liye shuru na karein (Don't Start to Finish)**

**Chalo khatam karne ke liye shuru nahi karte (Let's not start to finish). Main har naye project (exploration) ki shuruat mein apne students se yahi kehti hoon. Hum isliye shuru nahi kar rahe hain, ki isey khatam kar dein.**

Rhonda Arntsen, graphic designer, SCAD

Agar students dimaag mein pehle se maujood ek idea ke sath kaam karna shuru karte hain, toh unka kaam ek linear process ban jayega. Wo ek zyada kamyaab (successful) process ki iterative fitrat (nature) ka tajurba (experience) nahi le payenge. Shuruaat karne wale students mukhya taur par us final kaam ke baare mein sochte hain jo wo assignment ke aakhir mein banayenge. Finish line (manzil) ke baare mein sochna creativity ko maar deta hai aur iska nateeja gair-asardaar (ineffective) kaam hota hai. Iske bajaye unhe ek iterative exploratory (khoj bhare) process ko follow karna seekhna chahiye. SCAD mein ek painting professor, Sandra Reed ne mujhe bataya,

*Maine teaching ke saalon se yeh seekha hai ki ek naye (young) artist ko behtareen ideas aayenge aur wo apne dimaag ki aankhon (mind's eye) mein dekh lega ki final painting kaisi dikhti hai. Wo sochte hain, 'Mujhe theek se pata hai ki yeh kaisa dikhne wala hai!' Lekin jaise hi unka samna materials, ya scale, ya kaam ki asaliyaton (realities) se hota hai, wo kho jate hain. Wo bahut asani se nirash (discouraged) ho sakte hain.*

Introduction mein, maine Beth Grabowski ke project *Souvenir* ko bayan (describe) kiya tha, jo unki class Photo Printmaking ka tha. Students ek cyanotype image banate hain jise hum objects aur images kyun collect (jama) karte hain is bare me literary writings padhne ke baad inspire ho kar banaya jata hai. Project ke handout mein, wo students ko kehti hain: "Shuru karne se pehle kisi specific intent (maqsad) ya 'meaning (matlab)' ko poori tarah tay karne ke liye khud ko majboor (obliged) mehsoos na karein. Banana (making) aapke intution (andar ki aawaz) aur aapki samajhdari (intellect) ke beech ek conversation (baat-cheet) hai." Class mein, maine unhe ek student se kehte hue suna, "Process mein aapka idea badal sakta hai," aur unhone ek dusre student ko bataya, "Kaam aise tareeqon se jawab (responds) deta hai jiska aap pehle se andaza (preconceive) nahi laga sakte." Figure 1.5 fall 2021 semester mein Grabowski ki student Dacia Perez ka kaam hai.

Lekin, jaisa ki humne Introduction mein seekha tha, aap students ko bas yeh nahi bata sakte ki unhe kya janne ki zaroorat hai. Aap ise syllabus par rakhte hain, aur aap unhe class mein batate hain, aur phir bhi wo wahi karte hain jo wo pehle se karna jante hain. Yahi wajah hai ki professors Grabowski ke *Souvenir* aur Shaffer ki illustrated children's book jaise structured assignments develop karte hain.

### **Jab Aap Phas Jate Hain (When You Get Stuck)**

**Jab aap phas jate hain toh aap kya karte hain? Jab aapke paas ideas khatam ho jate hain toh aapke paas kya bachta hai? Aap kahan jate hain? Aap khud ko us gaddhay (hole) se kaise nikalte hain? Main waqayi unhe yahi sikhane ki koshish karti hoon.**

Kara Rooney, interdisciplinary artist, School of Visual Arts

Art aur design school ka maqsad students ko creativity ke ek soche-samjhe (deliberate) process mein hissa lena sikhana hai: unhe is jhooth (myth) se dur le jana ki unhe ek behtareen idea se inspire hone ki zaroorat hai, aur kaam karne ke ek aise process ki taraf le jana jahan ideas process se ubhar kar (emerge) aate hain. Is tarah, unke paas kabhi bhi ideas ki kami nahi hogi. Jab tak aap process ke zariye kaam karte rahenge, ideas ubharte rahenge. SCAD ke ek sculptor ne mujhe bataya ki unka maqsad students ko "intuitive (apne aap aane wali) creativity se deliberate (soche-samjhe) creativity mein switch karwana (badalna) hai. Kamyaab hone ke liye aapko ek deliberate creator banna hoga." WashU mein ek book designer, Amy Thompson, kehti hain ki wo students ko sikhati hain "apni creativity ke baare mein aur zyada deliberate kaise ban-na hai." Patricia Olynyk, jo WashU mein ek professor bhi hain aur pehle Graduate School of Art ki director reh chuki hain, unhone mujhe bataya, "Is program mein art banana un behad sakht (rigorous), critically engaged (soch-vichar wale) processes se aata hai jo hum apne students tak pahunchane ki koshish karte hain." Students seekhte hain ki ek methodology (tareeqa)—ek process—hai jo lagatar (consistently) ache results deta hai. Students us tareeqe se dekhna seekhte hain jo is process ko aage badhata (drive) hai.

Lekin kya yeh advice improvisation (mauqe par banane) ke roop mein creativity ke vision se takrati (conflict) nahi hai? Aap ek hi waqt mein deliberate (soche-samjhe) kaise ho sakte hain aur sath hi improvisations (mauqe par badlaavon) aur direction mein badlaavon ke liye khule (open) ho sakte hain, un-sochay (unexpected) ideas ke ubharne ke liye khule ho sakte hain? Yeh art aur design practice ka challenge hai. Ise kaise karna hai yeh seekhne ke liye professional artists aur designers ki guidance mein rozana ki practice mein shamil hona zaroori hai. Yahi wajah hai ki aap bas kisi ko yeh nahi bata sakte ki kisi artist ki tarah kaise sochna hai; unhe ise studio mein experience karna padta hai. Artists aur designers process mein hissa lene ke baare mein deliberate hote hain, lekin pehle se soche gaye (preconceived) goal ko pane ke baare mein deliberate nahi hote.

Ek painting class sirf painting ke baare mein nahi hoti. Painting karne ka amal (act) ek aisa tool hai jo aapki dekhne, sochne, aur banane ki kabiliyat ko aage badhata (drives) hai. Maine Reed ko apne students se yeh kehte hue suna:

*Aap jin paradigms (misalo) aur processes se guzar rahe hain, wo hain. Image ke baare mein sochne aur use approach karne ke tareeqe hain. Agar aap kabhi phas jate (get stuck) hain, toh aap is class ko yaad kar sakte hain, aur keh sakte hain, 'Mujhe us process se dobara guzarne do.'*

### **Anchor Point (Rukne/Sahare ki Jagah)**

**Kabhi-kabhi aapki pehli cheez sabse behtareen cheez hoti hai. Lekin aapko yeh tab tak nahi pata hota jab tak ki aap process se na guzrein.**

Lynn Tomaszewski, ek interdisciplinary artist aur SAIC mein graduate studies ki associate dean

Creative process kisi idea ke sath shuru nahi hota, lekin ise kahin se shuru hona padta hai. Aap final kaam par ya aap kahan ja rahe hain is par focus nahi karte. Lekin aapko *kisi cheez (something)* se shuruat karne ki zaroorat hoti hai. Yeh man-na mushkil hai ki yeh aapka aakhiri idea nahi hai aur process ko wahan se ajeeb tarah (unpredictably) behne (flow) dena mushkil hai. Pehla idea ek anchor (sahara) hai jo aapko ek khas space (jagah) ko explore karne mein guide karta hai. Lekin yeh ek tool hai, na ki aakhiri point (endpoint). Successful creators hamesha aage badhne, kisi idea ko piche chhodne ke liye taiyar rehte hain, jab kaam se kuch zyada behtar (more promising) nikal kar aata hai. RISD mein ek graphic designer, Anther Kiley ne mujhe bataya,

*Ek strategy jo achi tarah kaam karti hai, wo hai students ko project par shuruati koshish (initial pass) karne dena, assignment ke challenges ko poori tarah se (holistic way) apnaana, isse pehle ki unhe ek zyada structured (tarteeb-war) approach ke zariye guide kiya jaye.*

Student ne wo rasta chuna hai jiske baare mein wo jante hain—ek idea lekar aana aur phir tezi se aage badhna, bas apne original idea ko execute (pura) karne ki koshish karna. Lekin idea problem nahi hai. Pehla idea ek important anchor point hai, lekin yeh zaroori hai ki aap us pehle idea ke sath zyada der tak na rukein. Kiley unhe apne anchor point ko ek shuruat ki tarah istemal karne ke liye guide karte hain, na ki ek anjaam (end) ki tarah: "Phir main unhe poori tarah se isey (idea ko) tod kar (tearing it apart) aur zaruri taur par (essentially) unhe ek structured process se guzarne par majboor karke poori tarah se niraash (frustrate) kar dunga." Agar aap apne pehle idea se chipke (stick) rehte hain, toh aap process nahi seekhte. Jaise-jaise wo process se guzarte hain, "Wo dekhenge ki yeh saare hisse ek sath kaise fit hote hain. Agar unhone sirf apne concept ke baare mein socha hota, toh uske mukable aisi conversations kahin zyada deep (richer) aur faydemand (productive) hoti hain."

Kiley ke kai assignments is tarah design kiye gaye hain taaki is baat ki sambhavna zyada ho ki students kaam karne ke is process ko experience kar sakein. Kai projects mein, wo unhe shuruat mein hi ek rough sketch banane ko kehte hain. Shuruati sketch ek important anchor point hai: "Main waqayi chahta hoon ki students ke paas final cheez ke liye ek vision ho aur wo final kaam ke vision ki taraf kaam kar rahe hon. Mujhe nahi lagta ki yeh ek gahra (rich) process develop karne ke khilaf (counter) kaam karta hai." Wo is idea ke sath thoda kaam karte hain, lekin Kiley schedule ko is tarah design karte hain ki unke paas isey develop karne aur ise perfect dikhane ke liye kafi waqt nahi hota. Phir Kiley unhe apne sketch ko break down (todne) ke liye kehte hain "aur usse ek sach mein sakht (rigorous) system banane ke liye kehte hain. Ek poore (holistic) tareeqe se is sab ke sath jaddo-jehad (wrestled) karne ki wo pehli stage kaam ko ek kahin zyada behtar (sophisticated) level par develop hone deti hai."

Yeh soch-samajh kar (deliberate) kaam karne aur sath hi exploration (khoj) aur iteration (dohraav) ke liye khula (open) rehne ka ek paradox (virodhabhas/uljhan) hai. Yeh ek paradox hai jise master karne mein saalo lag jate hain. SAIC mein ek painter aur sculptor, Amy Honchell ne mujhe bataya:

*Har kisi ko shuruati point ke taur par kisi cheez se shuruat karni padti hai, bhale hi wo rasta jis par unhe lagta hai ki wo ja rahe hain A tak le jata ho, hum sach mein jante hain ki aakhir mein wo C tak pahunchenge taaki yeh sach mein kuch acha ban sake. Lekin agar unke paas wo shuruati anchor point nahi hai, toh unke liye bhatakna (aimless) aasan ho jata hai. Grounding point (shuruati point) process ke dauran poori tarah se discarded (kharij/phenk diya) ja sakta hai, lekin yeh kisi aisi boundary (hadd) ke hone jaisa hai jahan se aap us point par wapas echo-locate (awaaz ki madad se rasta dhoondhna) kar sakte hain jab tak ki aapko agla marker nahi mil jata.*

SCAD mein ek lighting designer, Esma Dengiz ne mujhe bataya, "Main us pehle hal (solution) par nahi rukti jo wo mere paas late hain." Wo students ko aage badhane mein madad karne ke liye unse sawal poochti hain, jaise:

*Okay. Toh iske baare mein kya khayal hai? Agar aap isey is tarah sochein toh kaisa rahega?" Main unke sochne ke tareeqe ko challenge kar rahi hoon, aur main yeh bahut karti hoon. Jab koi student mere paas ek solution lekar aata hai, toh main yeh nahi kehti, "Oh, yeh bahut acha hai," aur tumhara kaam ho gaya. Bhale hi yeh ek acha solution ho, main unhe batati hoon ki yeh acha kyun hai, lekin main hamesha isey behtar, aur zyada anokha (unique) banane ke liye kuch dhoondhti hoon.*

Wo aage kehti hain: "Lekin jab wo apne pehle ya dusre solution par hi atak (fixated) jate hain, toh wo aur kuch nahi dekh pate."

Kai artists aisa kaam banakar explore karna shuru karte hain jo shayad hum mein se zyada-tar logon ko confused ya be-matlab (meaningless) lage. Artist ko bhi shayad yeh aisa hi lage. Yeh process ka ek keemti (valuable) hissa ho sakta hai, agar yeh kisi khule (open-ended) exploration ki shuruat mein aata hai. Kabhi-kabhi chaos (afra-tafri) clarity (saaf dikhne) ko ubharne (emerge) ka mauka deta hai. WashU mein ek sculptor, Arny Nadler ne mujhe bataya:

*Artist ka stereotype (bani-banayi chhavi) yeh hai ki wo bas andar ja kar cheezon ko yahan wahan phenkta (throwing shit around) hai. Lekin sach yeh hai ki wo shayad shuru mein aisa hi karenge, lekin phir ek acha artist wapas aata hai aur peeche baith kar is saari cheezon ko dekhta hai aur kehta hai, 'Yeh exciting (mazedar) tha, lekin yeh do bekar (crap) hain. Yeh kachre (dumpster) mein jayenge. Lekin yahan kuch ho raha hai. Shayad wahan mujhe kuch kaam karna hoga; shayad yeh dusri cheezein jaise hain waise hi badiya hain. Shayad mujhe yahan is par (on this over here) aage kaam karna chahiye.'*

Dekhna seekhne ka matlab yeh hai ki jab aap apni cheezon ke bade se bikhrav (mess) ko dekh rahe hote hain, toh aap yeh notice kar pate hain ki iska kaun sa hissa aage badhane (pursuing) ke kabil hai. Kabhi-kabhi, shuruaat karne wale students ek aise point par pahunch jate hain jahan unhe nahi pata hota ki aage kaise badhna hai (how to continue). Wo us bikhrav (mess) ke paar nahi dekh pate. Unhone bahut saare sketches banaye hote hain, lekin unhe nahi pata hota ki aage kya karna hai. Jab koi student phas (stumped) jata hai, toh Nadler unhe kehte hain: "Shayad is piece mein koi aisi choti si chingaari (kernel) hai jo sach mein dilchasp hai, aur jiske sath tum aage badh sakte ho (run with). Dus mein se nau baar, us process mein kuch aisa hoga jo unhe spark (josh/idea) dega." Sabhi professors ki tarah, wo "creativity" shabd ke sath asahaj (uncomfortable) hain; unhone mujhe bataya, "Main unhe creative hona nahi sikha raha hoon." Iske bajaye, wo unhe sikha rahe hain "experiment (tajarba) kaise karein" aur "khud ko aisi position mein rakhein jahan nayi aur exciting cheezein ho sakein." Wo ek aisa process sikhate hain jo iterative, improvisational, aur exploratory hai.

MFA program mein full-time do saal lagte hain. Zyada-tar MFA students ke paas pehle se hi undergraduate BFA degree hoti hai, halaanki yeh zaroori (required) nahi hai jab tak ki unke paas kisi tarah ki bachelor's degree ho. Lekin unki undergraduate degree jo bhi ho, MFA program mein apply karne wale students se umeed ki jati hai ki wo apne kaam ka ek portfolio jama (submit) karein. Program ka sabse ahem nateeja (culmination) MFA thesis hota hai. Thesis project mein do hissay shamil hote hain: ek achi khasi body of work jise dusre saal ke aakhir mein aam logon ke liye display (publicly displayed) kiya jata hai, aur tees (30) se pachaas (50) pages ka ek scholarly (tahqeeqi) document jo theory aur dusre artists ke kaamon ke sath juda (engages) hota hai. Maine SCAD aur WashU ke das (10) MFA students se interview lene ka faisla kiya ki unhe apne thesis project ke ideas kaise mile. Unmein se ek Judith Kruger thin, jinse hum introduction ki shuruat mein mile the. Sabhi das ne mujhe bataya ki wo shuruat wali jagah se ek alag jagah par khatam hue the. Jab maine das interviews ka analysis poora kar liya, toh main SAIC mein painter aur sculptor Amy Honchell se mila. Maine in MFA students ke iterative processes ko describe kiya, aur maine unse poocha ki kya SAIC mein MFA students ko bhi yahi tajurba (experience) hota hai. Unhone kaha:

*Yeh kahani (narrative) bahut aam hai, aur sirf MFA level par hi nahi. Unhe un cheezon ke baare mein sochne mein madad karna jo kaam ke liye unke aam vision (nazariye) ka hissa nahi hain. Unhe thoda push karna, unhe challenge karna. ### **Na Janne ki Ahmiyat (The Importance of Not Knowing)**

**Artists shikari (hunters) aur kisano (farmers) ki tarah hote hain. Aap bahar jate hain aur cheezein dhoondhte hain.**

Richard Mehl, graphic design, School of Visual Arts

Ek illustrator, Carol Fabricatore, SVA mein MFA students ko padhati hain. Students ke liye unka goal hai "yeh vishwas (trust) karna seekhna ki exploration ka process aapko ek achi problem aur ek ache solution ki taraf le jayega, yahan tak ki tab bhi jab aapko nahi pata ki kya ho raha hai." Process par trust karne ka matlab hai ki aapko khud ko saaf taur par dekhne ki apni kabiliyat par bharosa (confident) hai, yeh dekhne ki ki kaam se kya nikal kar aa raha (emerging) hai, aur aap aage kahan ja sakte hain.

*Process par vishwas karne ka matlab khud ko sun-na hai, aapke liye kya mayne (important) rakhta hai use sun-na hai, aap kis cheez ke baare mein curious (utsuk) hain use samajhna aur uske piche jana hai. Bahut si alag-alag approaches, aap jo dekh rahe hain use dekhne aur draw karne ke alag-alag tareeqe aazmane ke liye taiyar (willing) rehna. Agar wo is process ke sath khud par vishwas kar sakein, mujh par vishwas kar sakein, toh aakhir mein wo aesa kaam banayenge jo behad asardaar (compelling) hoga. Finished kaam ke baare mein fikar karne ke pressure ko chhod (Let go) dein.*

Jab aapka dhyan is baat par hota hai ki aapka final kaam kaisa dikhega, toh aap un an-sochi (unexpected) cheezon ko nahi dekhenge jo hoti hain. Aap un possibilities (sambhavnaon) ko nahi dekh sakte jo samne aati (emerge) hain. Aapko sirf yeh dikhta hai ki aap apne shuruati idea ko haqeekat banane (realizing) mein kitna aage pahunch gaye hain. Aap kisi artist ya designer ki tarah nahi dekh rahe hain.

Bahut se students acha dikhne wala kaam banane par dhyan dete hain jise wo apne portfolio mein rakh sakein taaki unhe jobs milne mein madad mil sake. Fabricatore unhe is approach se door (away) le jati hain: "Yeh sabse bada tareeqa hai jisse aap apni drawing ko tabah (sabotage) kar sakte hain. Main isey rokne ke liye kuch assignments mein badlaav (tweak) karti hoon. Jab aap khud par wo pressure dalte hain, toh yeh isey maar deta hai. Mera ek student tha jise polished product (chamakte hue final kaam) ki parwah nahi thi, aur wo saal ke aakhir mein apni sketchbook seedhe art directors ke paas le gaya, aur use usi waqt (on the spot) hire kar liya gaya—uski drawings itni zinda (alive) aur tazi (fresh) aur behtareen (amazing) thin." Student ki rough sketches (drafty sketches) ne uski apne khud ke kaam ko saaf taur par dekhne ki kabiliyat dikhayi (demonstrated). Dekhne ke qabil hone ke liye aapko great-looking kaam banane ki zaroorat nahi hai. Lekin great-looking kaam banane ke liye aapko dekhne ke qabil hona padega.

WashU ke Sam Fox School mein, main Ron Leax ke sath unke first-year foundations students ki sculptures (murtion) ko examine (jaanch) karte waqt chal raha tha (tagging along). Wo ek student ki sculpture ko dekhne ke liye ruke aur yeh advice di: "Jo aap sabse pehle chahte the usse aage dekhein, aur dekhein aur samjhein ki aapko kya mila hai." Humne is assignment ke baare mein introduction mein padha tha: students ko Leax ke diye gaye ek set se ek chota object chun-na tha. Unka task ek bada, scaled-up version banana tha. Ek challenge yeh bhi tha ki surface texture (satah ka khurdurapan) ko bhi bada (scaled up) karna tha. Leax ne aise objects chune the jinki textures dilchasp hoti hain, jaise ek nimbu (lemon) ya lily pad. Is student ne ek chota seashell (seep) chuna tha. Student ko exploration (khoj) par zyada trust karne ke liye manane (convince) mein madad karne ke liye, unhone pichli class ke baad se student ki textured surface ke badalne (evolved) ke tareeqe par comment kiya, aur kaha, "Tumhe yeh process mein mila!"

**Carol Fabricatore aise students ke baare mein yeh kahani sunati hain jinhone ek behtareen mauka ganva diya (missed) kyunki wo abhi tak dekh nahi sakte the:**

### **Itefaqan (Serendipitous) Haadsa (Accident)**

Bahut se artists apne creative process mein ek aise pal (moment) ko bayan karte hain jab koi an-socha (unexpected) accident kaam ko aage badhata hai. Kisi tarah, unhe koi aisi image mil jati hai jise wo be-tahasha pur-kashish (compelling) paate hain. Yeh kisi dusre artist ke studio space mein ya kisi art gallery mein ya shehar ki kisi sadak par ho sakta hai. Ya yeh tab ho sakta hai jab wo apne khud ke studio mein kaam kar rahe hon, jab process ka nateeja (results) us cheez se alag ho jiska unhone irada (intended) kiya tha. Kabhi-kabhi, wo accident, wo surprise, unke original irade se kahin zyada asardaar (compelling) sabit hota hai. Jab aisa hota hai, toh aapko apne pichle idea ko piche chhodne ke liye taiyar rehna padta hai.

MFA students jinka maine interview liya un sabne accidents aur surprises ke baare mein kahaniyan sunayi. WashU mein ek MFA student, Lauren Cardenas ne mujhe yeh kahani sunayi:

*Jab main pehli baar yahan aayi, toh main har taraf bhatak (all over the place) rahi thi. Mere ideas mere kaam se mel (lining up) nahi kha rahe the. Main consumerism (chizein kharidne ki aadat) ke baare mein baat kar rahi thi, lekin jo kaam main kar rahi thi uska talluq ghar (home) ki pehchan (identity) se tha. Pehle semester mein, zahir hai (apparently) yeh aam baat hai—wo aapse struggle karne (flounder) ki umeed karte hain. Apne dusre semester mein, maine letter-pressed scrolls (lapatne wala kaghaz) ki ek series banayi aur maine scrolls ko lapeta aur unhe bottles mein daal diya.*

Maine usse poocha, "Tumhe yeh idea kaise mila?"

*Main 12-step program ke zariye depression ke sath aane wale daily affirmations (khud ko hosla dene wali baten) aur meditations (soch-vichar) ko explore kar rahi thi. Aur isse self-medication (khud-ilaji) samne (emerged) aayi; mujhe yeh idea apne ek dost se mila jo Barnes-Jewish Hospital mein kaam karta tha, jo mere liye yeh ampules [sealed glass capsules] laya tha, aur maine inka istemal ek pichle project mein kiya tha. Phir maine aur bahut se ampules mangwaye, lekin wo bina tops (dhakkan) ke aaye. Yeh bas ek khush-gawar itefaq (happy accident) tha, waqayi koi idea (insight) nahi tha. Main bas cheezein ikattha karne wali (packrat) hoon aur mujhe kisi cheez ka istemal na karna acha nahi lagta. Phir, maine socha ki yeh ampules logon ko dena aur unse unhe tudwana (break) dilchasp hoga. Maine chaudah (14) ampules ke sath ek Z-PAC jaisa banaya, har ek mein ek likha hua message andar tha, ampules mein, jinhe sile hue pouches (thailiyon) ke andar rakha gaya tha [dekhein figure 1.7].*

Yeh ek pechida (complex) artwork hai jo aisa lagta hai jaise ise pehle se dhyan se plan kiya gaya hoga. Na sirf material composition complex tha; isme ek gehra concept (conceptual depth) bhi tha. Lekin yeh ek improvisational aur unpredictable process se nikal kar samne (emerged) aya. Jab aap soche-samjhe (deliberate) process ko follow karte hain, toh aap acha kaam zaroor banayenge (generate). Final kaam aisa lagega jaise ise shuru se hi plan kiya gaya tha. Lekin artists aur designers jo dekhna jante hain, unhe pata hoga ki kaam ke piche ek chhipi hui history hoti hai.

Ek creative career ek rozana ki practice hai. Iski koi saaf shuruat nahi hoti aur koi saaf anjaam (end) nahi hota. Ideas lagatar ubhar (emerge) aate hain. Art aur design school ka ek maqsad students ko aakhiri manzil (endpoint) par focus karne se rokna hai aur unhe us process par focus karne ki taraf le jana hai jisme wo shamil (engage) hote hain. Agar aap process ko sahi kar lete hain, toh ache kaam apne aap nikal aayenge (emerge).

**Creativity ek process hai. Yeh iterative (baar-baar dohrane wala) hai. Yeh jazz music jaisa hai. Aap ek line banate hain aur aap us line par respond karte hain. Aap kuch aisa banate hain jo aap seedhe raste par chal kar (linear way) nahi bana sakte the.**

Object par is focus ko art ke baare mein hone wali aalochnatmak charchaon (critical discourse) se aur badhava milta hai. Jab critics art ke baare mein baat karte hain, toh wo object ko aise discuss karte hain jaise isey waisa hi hona tha; jaise yeh hamesha se waisa hi tha. Critical reviews is baare mein baat nahi karte ki object alag kaise ho sakta tha, ya jo galtiyan chhupayi gayin, ya wo kai creative faisle jinpar artist ne gaur kiya aur phir unhe chhod diya. Jab hamari nazar kisi aane wale concert ka advertisement karte hue ek behtareen poster par padti hai, ya hum kisi mashhoor architect dwara design ki gayi nayi building mein dakhil hote hain, toh hum iska samna ek tay shuda cheez (fixed thing) ke taur par karte hain. Hum shayad hi kabhi us creative process ke baare mein sochte hain jiska nateeja wo object tha ya un dusre raston (alternative paths) ke baare mein sochte hain jinse koi alag object ban sakta tha.

Jab aap dekhna seekh jate hain, toh aap jis kaam ko dekhte hain wo dynamic (badalta hua) aur flux (lagatar badlaav) mein hota hai, jo alag-alag visual forces ke hisab se badal raha hota hai. Jab aap dekhna jante hain, toh aap kaam ke piche dekhne ke qabil hote hain. Aap jante hain ki us chhupe hue process ko samne (uncover) kaise lana hai jiska nateeja wo object tha. Jab aap apne khud ke kaam mein is process mein shamil (engage) hona shuru karte hain, toh aap dusron ke kaamon ke piche ke process ko dekhne ke qabil ho jate hain.

Art aur design ke piche iterative aur improvisational (mauqe par banane wale) process mein shamil hone ke liye, yeh zaroori hai ki aap shuru karne se pehle apne idea ko develop karne mein bahut zyada waqt bitane se bachein. Agar aap ek achi tarah se tayaar kiye gaye (well-elaborated) vision ke sath shuru karte hain, aur phir apna waqt isey pura karne (executing) mein bitate hain, toh yeh ek linear (seedha) aur andaza lagane layaq (predictable) process hai. Lekin professional artists aur designers ek iterative andaz mein kaam karna pasand karte hain jo process ke dauran naye ideas ko ubharne (emerge) ka mauka deta hai. Students ko kisi ek idea par bahut jaldi ruk jane (settling) se bachane ke liye guide karte waqt, professors aise assignments design karte hain jo unhe idea se lekar execution (pura karne) tak ke ek linear raste par chalne se rokte hain. School of Visual Arts ke ek illustrator, Grant Shaffer, ek illustration class mein is do-hafte ke assignment ka istemal karte hain: Wo students se kisi fairy tale (pari katha) ke beech mein se sirf ek page ko illustrate karne ke liye kehte hain. Kisi pehle se maujood fairy tale ka nahi; unki apni banayi hui fairy tale ka. Halanki, wo page shuruat ya aakhir ka nahi ho sakta. Unhe kahani likhne ya kahani ko illustrate karne ki zaroorat nahi hai—wo sirf ek page banate hain. Yeh waisa hi hoga jaise aapne bacchon ki kitab se ek page faad liya ho; bas ek random page. Shaffer kehte hain, "Bahut se students ghabra jate hain (freeze) aur sochte hain, mujhe nahi pata kya likhna hai. Main kahani kaise soch sakta hoon?" Jab students pehli baar ek iterative improvisational process mein shamil hona seekh rahe hote hain toh unhe guidance ki zaroorat hoti hai, aur Shaffer unhe kuch steps ke zariye guide karte hain jo unhone saalo mein develop kiye hain.

*Sabse pehle, bees (20) shabd likho. Koi bhi shabd. Chhata (Umbrella), tidda (grasshopper), boombox, uh, blimp (hawa ka jahaz), mitti ka dhela (dirt clod), tum jante ho, khidki se bahar dekho, bas shabd likhna shuru karo. Phir main kahunga ki apne bees shabdon mein se teen shabdon par ghera (circle) lagao. Apni pasand ke teen shabdon par ghera lagao. Toh, tum grasshopper, blimp, aur boombox ko kaise mila (combine) sakte ho? Aur tum students ko yeh kehte hue dekh sakte ho, mujhe nahi pata, shayad grasshopper ud nahi sakta. Isliye wo ek blimp mein udta hai aur uske paas ek boombox hai, aur boombox padosiyon ko pareshan kar raha hai. Aur achanak tumhare paas ek grasshopper ki kahani hoti hai jise udna nahi aata, aur wo blimp mein udta hai. Aur phir mujhe lagta hai ki tum in ideas ko aage badhana (cultivating) shuru kar sakte ho.*

Is do-hafte ke assignment ke baad, Shaffer is tajurbe (experience) ke aadhar par ek dusra assignment dete hain. Is baar, wo students se ek classic children's tale, jaise "Little Red Riding Hood" ya "Rapunzel" lene ko kehte hain, aur phir isey naye dhang se tasavvur (reimagine) karne ko kehte hain. Wo unhe kehte hain:

*Lekin isey apna andaaz (spin) do, jaise 2082 mein Little Red Riding Hood. Agle hafte, hum apni khud ki kahani ke liye ek single page banate hain. Har koi kya soch kar aata hai yeh dekhna kafi mazedar (fun) hota hai. Mera ek student tha jisne ek billi aur ek aurat ki kahani banayi. Yeh aurat apni billi se bahut pyar karti thi. Billi usse pyar karti thi. Aur phir ek din aurat ka ek boyfriend ban gaya aur wo apni billi ke sath kam waqt bitane lagi, aur billi ne aurat se divorce ke liye case file kar diya, aur wo cat court gaye. Aur yeh ek ajeeb (crazy), pyari (lovely), aur anokhi kahani ban gayi. Mujhe acha lagta hai jab kahaniyan thodi atpati/khayali (surreal) ho jati hain.*

Ek acha assignment students ko ek iterative process ke zariye guide karne ke liye design kiya jata hai. Unhe guidance ki zaroorat hoti hai, kyunki jab wo is process mein shamil hona nahi jante, toh wo qudrati taur par us linear, idea-driven kaam karne ke tareeqe par wapas chale jate hain jiske sath unhone college shuru kiya tha. Ek acha assignment students ko is linear process ko follow karne se rokta hai. Shaffer apna assignment is tarah design karte hain ki students ek linear process ko poora karne (completion) ke liye kisi ek idea ka istemal nahi kar sakte.

Jab hum samne dikhne wale aur mukammal (finished) object par focus karte hain, toh yeh sochna natural hai ki us kaam ke piche ek hi vision ya idea tha. Kyunki aap kaam ko ek finished cheez ki tarah dekhte hain, aap yeh maan lete (assume) hain ki artist is par kaam shuru karne se pehle hi janta tha ki yeh kaisa hoga. Kaam pehle creator ke dimaag mein maujood tha aur phir medium ke materials ka istemal karke isey zinda kiya (brought to life) gaya. Yeh creativity ka *linear insight* (seedha aur achanak aane wala khayal) nazariya hai (dekhein figure 1.2). Is nazariye mein, idea ko poora karna (execution) bahut dilchasp nahi hota—yeh idea se lekar finished work tak ka ek seedha rasta hai. Creativity process ko shuru karti hai, lekin idea ko execute karne ke liye creativity ki zaroorat nahi hoti. Aur in creative ideas ko soch pane ki kabiliyat—yahi artists aur designers ko baaki hum logon se alag banati hai. Agar professors isi tarah ki creativity sikha rahe hain, toh wo students ko yeh sikha rahe honge ki zyada aur behtar ideas kaise laye jayen, aur un ideas ko unke chune hue medium mein wafadari (faithfully) aur kamyabi ke sath kaise execute kiya jaye.

Artists aur designers ke kaam karne ka tareeqa linear nahi hota. Wo kaam karna shuru karte hain aur is baat par vishwas (trust) karte hain ki materials ke sath dialogue (batchit/tal-mel) se ideas samne aayenge. Rasta unpredictable aur nonlinear (tedha-medha) hota hai. Yeh materials ke sath ek improvisational (mauqe ke hisab se) samna (encounter) hai, materials aur artist ya designer ke beech ek dialogue. Agar galtiyan (mistakes), accidents, ya surprises samne aate hain, toh unhe aapke pehle se tay kiye gaye goal ki taraf jane wale raste ki rukawatein (speedbumps) nahi mana jata. Unhe process ko ek alag disha (direction) mein modne ke mauko (opportunities) ke taur par dekha jata hai.

Is tarah ki improvisational creativity mein maharat hasil karna is baat par nirbhar (depends) karta hai ki dekhna kaise hai.

* Aapko yeh dekhna aana chahiye ki jab koi idea ubhar kar aaye (emerges).
* Aapko har ubharne wale idea ko parakhna (evaluate) hoga aur tay karna hoga ki kin par aage kaam karna (pursue) faydemand hoga.
* Aapko yeh dekhna aana chahiye ki jab aapse koi galti ho, aur aapko yeh dekhne ke qabil hona chahiye ki aap us galti ka istemal ek nayi faydemand disha (productive direction) mein mudne ke liye kaise kar sakte hain.
* Aapko yeh dekhna aana chahiye ki materials kya kar rahe hain, wo aapse kya keh rahe hain, aur aap unke sath ek lagatar chalne wale dialogue mein jawab (respond) dene ke qabil hone chahiye.

Jab students ko idea-execution model ka istemal karne se roka jata hai, toh wo shuru mein kho jate hain. Wo abhi tak nahi jante ki jab koi umeed-bhara (promising) idea samne aaye toh use notice kaise karna hai. Wo abhi tak nahi jante ki accidents aur mistakes mein chhipe potential (sambhavna) ko kaise dekhna hai. Wo abhi tak nahi jante ki apne materials ko ek lagatar chalne wale dialogue mein apne partner ke taur par kaise dekhna hai.

Professors is baat ko samajhte hain. Wo students ko linear rasta lene se rokna chahte hain. Lekin, sath hi, wo yeh bhi samajhte hain ki students ko dekhna nahi aata. Wo apne tajurbe se jante hain ki students kamyaab nahi honge—kam se kam abhi toh nahi—agar wo ek nonlinear (tedha-medha) rasta apnane ki koshish karein. Iska hal (solution) *project assignment* hai. Shaffer ki tarah, professors students ko dhyan se design kiye gaye assignments dete hain jinme achi tarah se tayaar ki gayi constraints (rukawatein) hoti hain. Bina constraints ke, students kho jate hain aur unka koi maqsad nahi rehta; constraints ke sath, wo asani se ek iterative process ke guided version ko follow kar sakte hain. Ek baar jab wo is process mein maharat (master) hasil kar lete hain, toh unhe constraints ki zaroorat nahi rehti.

**Halanki aksar yeh mana jata hai ki artists, designers, aur creative thinkers 'eureka moments' (achanak idea aane ka pal) mehsoos karte hain jisme ek shandaar idea samne aata hai, lekin practice mein, creativity ek lamba process hai. Isme aksar banane wale (maker) ko kisi cheez ko baar-baar banana padta hai, aur har baar pichle iteration (dohraav) se seekhna padta hai.**

Eva Sutton, transmedia artist, Rhode Island School of Design

Professors ne yeh seekha hai ki students program mein is galatfehmi (misconception) ke sath aate hain ki creativity kaise kaam karti hai. Ek professor ne mujhe bataya, "Hum un athara (18) saal ke bacchon ke sath deal kar rahe hain jinke paas bahut hi romantic (khayali) ideas hote hain." Asardaar (effective) artists ban-ne ke liye students ko us nazariye ko piche chhodna padta hai. Figure 1.3 ke creative process mein, banayi gayi cheezein sirf behtareen ideas nahi hotin jinhe ek kabil craftsperson (karigar) ne ache se execute kiya ho. Iske bajaye, artworks ek soche-samjhe (deliberate) process se, an-soche (unpredictably) dhang se lekin lagatar (consistently) ubhar kar aate hain, jinki khasiyaton ko seekha ja sakta hai. Successful artists aur designers ke paas koi anokhi (unique) personality traits hona zaroori nahi hai.

Professors kehte hain ki wo kisi ko bhi successful creative works generate karna sikha sakte hain—shart yeh hai ki student mein utsukta (curiosity) aur lagan (dedication) ho aur wo zaroori waqt aur mehnat (work) lagane ke liye taiyar ho.

Art aur design schools mein, aap figure 1.3 wala process seekhte hain, wo process jo in programs mein padhane wale successful artists aur designers istemal karte hain. Iska learning outcome (seekhne ka nateeja) ek conscious (hosh-mand) aur soche-samjha (deliberate) process hai jisme critical thinking (gehri soch) aur awareness (jaagrukta), samajh (perceptiveness) aur gaur-o-fikar (reflection), aur koshish-bhari khoj (effortful exploration) shamil hai.

##  Chapter 2 - MATERIALS (Saaman/Materials)

**Class ka maqsad koi cheez banana nahi hai, balki yeh hai ki jab aap bana rahe hote hain toh aap kaisa sochte hain. Aap studio mein faisle (decisions) kaise lete hain. Jo cheez wo banate hain wo sirf ek zariya (carrier) hai, jo unke khayalon ko samne lata (manifests) hai. Mujhe is baat se koi parwah nahi hai ki wo kya banate hain.**

Ron Leax, sculptor, Sam Fox School at Washington University in St. Louis

Art aur design ka nichod (essence) ek soche-samjhe (deliberate) process mein shamil hona hai jahan materials ke sath kaam karne se ideas ubhar (emerge) kar aate hain, dimaag aur haath ke beech ek dialogue (batchit) hoti hai. Agar aapka dimaag materials ke sath jude bina kaam karta hai, toh aapke dimaag mein aane wala har idea us jankari par aadharit (built) hoga jo pehle se hi aapke dimaag mein hai. Aap shayad shandaar (brilliant) ho sakte hain, aap apne discipline (field) mein expert ho sakte hain, lekin sabse zyada knowledgeable professional ke paas bhi aakhir-kaar ideas khatam ho jayenge. Ek professional artist ya designer banne ke liye, sirf ek shandaar cheez banana kafi nahi hai; aapko lagatar (consistently aur continuously) create karna padta hai. Art aur design school mein, aapko zindagi bhar ke creative kaam ke liye taiyar kiya ja raha hai. Zindagi bhar ki practice banane ke liye, aapko lagatar materials ke sath judna (engage) hota hai aur banane ke amal (act of making) se jankari (information) absorb karni (kheench leni) hoti hai.

Jeffery Keedy, ek typeface designer jo California Institute of the Arts (CalArts) mein padhate hain, unhone mujhe bataya:

*Yeh karne ke zariye sochna, sochne ke zariye karna hai. Yeh is baare mein hai ki ye dono kaise mile-jule (integrated) hain, bajaye us dusri aadat ke jise mujhe first aur second year ke chote students mein todna padta hai: sochne ko banane se alag rakhna aur sochne ko kuch karne se alag rakhna. Zyada-tar logon ko lagta hai ki aapke paas ek idea ya inspiration aati hai aur phir aap use poora (execute) karte hain. Aapke idea ya inspiration ki quality is baat ko tay karti (dictates) hai ki jo execute kiya gaya hai aur jo banaya gaya hai uski quality kya hogi. Lekin agar aap ek idea ke sath shuru karte hain, toh nateeja (outcome) andaza lagane layaq (predictable) hota hai.*

First aur second year ke students aksar assignment milte hi ek idea soch lete hain. Phir wo us idea ko ek linear (seedhe) process ke zariye execute karte hain. Keedy hamesha yeh bata sakte hain.

*Student mujhe apna kaam dikhata hai, aur main ise dekh sakta hoon. Main unhe batata hoon, "Yeh toh pehle se tay tha (foregone conclusion) ki yeh aisa dikhega kyunki aapki buniyad (premise) yeh thi, kya main sahi hoon?" Aur wo kehte hain, "Haan, aap sahi hain. Yahi meri exact buniyad thi." Jab bhi aap aisa karte hain, jab bhi aapke dimaag mein ek khayal hota hai ki ise kaisa hona chahiye aur phir aap use execute karte hain, toh yeh saaf zahir (obvious) ho jata hai.*

Agar aap ek achi tarah se develop kiye gaye idea ke sath shuru karte hain, toh nateeja predictable hota hai. Creative exploration (khoj) ke bina, kuch bhi naya ubhar kar (emerge) nahi aata. Keedy ki tarah, ek baar jab aap dekhna seekh jate hain, toh aap kisi kaam ko dekh sakte hain aur us process ko dekh sakte hain jo us tak lekar gaya.

**FIGURE 2.2** Ken Botnick. Diderot Project. Limited edition book. Book/paper. Letterpress printing. 2015.

Ken Botnick ek internationally jane-mane printmaker hain jinhone apni retirement (2020) tak Washington University in St. Louis (WashU) mein padhaya. Unka kaam duniya bhar ke collections mein shamil hai, jisme Library of Congress aur Getty Center shamil hain. Unhone Yale School of Design, City College of New York, aur Parsons School of Design mein padhaya hai. 1993 se 1997 tak, wo North Carolina ke pahadon (mountains) mein behad mashhoor (highly regarded) Penland School of Crafts ke executive director the. Botnick ne mujhe bataya:

*Ideas cheezein banane se aate hain. Students ke haath material par hote hain. Craft darasal material technique aur design sab ko ek sath milane ke baare mein hai. Craft ke khilaf yeh baat (rap) hai ki kabhi-kabhi yeh poori tarah technique ke baare mein ho sakta hai, aur isme koi ideas nahi hote. Lekin baat yeh hai ki aap technique sikhate hain, aap materials ko handle (sambhalna) karna sikhate hain, aur aap students ko waqayi basic exercises ki ek series se guzarne ki ijazat dete hain taaki ideas develop aur percolate (tapkna/panna) shuru ho sakein, aur unhe khoj (discovery) ke ek aise process mein shamil kiya ja sake jo asal mein hathon se kaam karne (having hands on) ke baare mein hai, bajaye iske ki yeh maan liya jaye ki idea sochne wala hissa (ideation part) banane wale hisse (making part) se alag hai.*

Shayad yeh hairani ki baat nahi hai ki Botnick craft ke kirdar (role) ki himayat (advocates) karte hain. Aakhir-kaar, unke Penland se gehre jude (strong links) hain, jo craft par apne focus ke liye mashhoor hai. Lekin Botnick jaise craft ke himayati ke liye bhi, art aur design sirf achi technique ke baare mein nahi hain; sikhana sirf discipline ki techniques ko sikhana nahi hai. Botnick jis wajah se craft ko ehmiyat (values) dete hain, wo yeh hai ki yeh aapko dekhne mein madad karta hai:

*Ideas asal mein banane (actual making) ki is jagah se aate hain. Banana hi sochna (Making is thinking) hai. Main apne students se kuch bhi karwane ki koshish karta hoon taaki wo kaam karna shuru karein, isse pehle ki unhe lage ki unke paas ek poori tarah se tayyar (fully formulated) idea hona chahiye. Idea materials ke sath asal engagement (judav) se generate hota hai.*

Materials ke sath kaam karne se aapko nayi cheezein dekhne aur naye ideas paane mein madad milti hai.

Creative tareeqe se banane mein action lena, dekhna, aur sochna shamil hai, jo kaam ke sath ek independent (azaad) wajood ke taur par ek dialogue hai. Materials ke sath kaam karne ki practice ka ek discipline (zabt) hota hai jo creative action se juda hota hai. Art aur design school mein aap yahi seekhte hain.

Creative process ek material object ke wajood (existence) par nirbhar (depends) karta hai, lekin hairani ki baat yeh hai (paradoxically), process us object ke baare mein nahi hai. WashU mein communication designer Heather Corcoran ne kaha, "Mujhe is illustration se koi sachchi parwah nahi hai. Mujhe is baat ki parwah hai ki aapne yeh khoj (investigation) kaise ki."

Leax ek sculptor hain; Botnick aur Corcoran designers hain. Discipline koi bhi ho isse farq nahi padta; sabhi professors ek aisa creative process sikhate hain jo material ki duniya se juda (grounded) hota hai.

### **Milk and Motherhood (Doodh aur Maatritva/Maa Banna)**

Jennifer Baker WashU mein MFA painting program ke apne dusre aur aakhiri saal mein thin. Har student apna dusra saal apni MFA thesis develop karne mein guzaarta hai—kaam ka ek mukammal (complete) set jo spring semester ke aakhir mein ek gallery show mein display kiya jata hai. Kaam ke alawa, students ko lagbhag tees (30) se pachaas (50) pages ka ek document likhna hota hai jo un concepts ko samjhata hai jo kaam ke piche ki buniyad hain aur yeh bhi ki yeh aaj ki art ki duniya (art world today) se kaise judta hai.

Zyada-tar MFA students ke paas pehle se hi art ya design mein chaar saal ki degree hoti hai. Jab tak wo graduate school shuru karte hain, unhe ab undergraduate saalon ke constrained (rukawaton wale) assignments ki zaroorat nahi hoti. Baker ke paas fine art mein BA tha aur apna MFA program shuru karne se pehle Chicago mein unki pehli solo exhibition ho chuki thi. Unhone iterative process mein maharat (mastered) haasil kar li thi; wo janti thin ki dekhna kaise hai. Wo janti thin ki materials ke sath dialogue mein kaise shamil (engage) hona hai. MFA program ke dusre saal tak, students art ki duniya, art history, aur contemporary social theories aur philosophers ke baare mein seekh chuke hotey hain. Unki written thesis unke kaam ke message aur motivation ko samjhati hai aur yeh batati hai ki yeh alag-alag theories aur usse jude artists se kaise relate karti hai.

Maine unke aakhiri saal ke fall semester mein Baker ka interview liya. Unka thesis show lagbhag chhah (6) mahine baad hone wala tha. Unhone mujhe motherhood (maa banne) mein apni dilchaspi aur ek medium (zariye) ke taur par milk (doodh) ke sath apne fascination (lagaav/kashish) ke baare mein bataya. Maine unse poocha: Unhe motherhood par apni thesis karne ka idea kaise aaya? Kyunki thesis ek bada (substantial) project hai, ek aakhiri bada kaam (capstone) jo ek professional career ki shuruat karta hai, mujhe laga ki MFA students fall semester ke shuru mein hi ek vision soch lete honge, taaki unke paas studio mein kaam karne aur apne creative vision ko execute (pura) karne ke liye poora academic saal ho.

Lekin Baker ke sath aisa nahi hua. Unhone apna process exploration (khoj), improvisation (mauqe par kuch banana), aur materials ke sath shuru kiya.

**FIGURE 2.3**

Washington University in St. Louis mein Jennifer Baker ke studio mein kiye gaye experiments aur sketches. 2011.

Apne pehle saal ke fall semester mein, wo seekh rahi thin ki shapes (forms) ko cast (dhalne) karne ke liye molds (sanche) kaise banaye jate hain. Unhone wax (mom) aur butter (makkhan) ke mixtures se sculptures (murtiyan) banane ke experiments kiye. Unhone mix ko bilkul sahi karne ki koshish mein kitchen mein ghanton bitaye. Sculptures ko cast karne ke liye, wo plaster se bhare molds ka istemal kar rahi thin jo unhone khud banaye the, isey pani ke sath milakar. Ek din, unki nazar (gaze) unke fridge ki sabse upari shelf par rakhe doodh (milk) ke dabbe par padi. Doodh ki kisi cheez ne unhe fascinate (aakarshit) kiya. Unhone yeh dekhne ka faisla kiya ki kya hota hai jab wo pani ki jagah doodh ke sath plaster banati hain. Unhone kuch waqt tak iska experiment kiya, aur jaldi hi unke studio mein milk-plaster ki sculptures ki ek series ban gayi. Agle iteration (dohraav) mein, unhone ek baar phir experiment kiya. Unhone in sculptures ko doodh ke bowls (katoriyon) mein bhigona (soaking) shuru kiya, "bas yeh dekhne ke liye ki kya hota hai... bas yeh dekhne ke liye ki kya yeh dekhne mein (visually) bilkul bhi dilchasp hai. Kisi wajah se, ek theme jo baar-baar samne (showing up) aa rahi thi wo thi mere doodh tapkane (dripping milk) ki." Unhone mujhe bataya:

*Mujhe samajh nahi aa raha tha ki main jo bana rahi hoon wo kyun bana rahi hoon. Mujhe pata tha ki is par aage kaam karna (pursuing) qabil-e-gaur tha kyunki log sach mein is par respond (jawab/dhyaan) de rahe the. Main kuch banane ke liye majboor (compelled) hoon aur mujhe poori tarah se nahi pata ki yeh kis baare mein hai.*

Apne pehle saal ke spring semester mein, Baker ne ek bookmaking (kitab banane ki) class li. Assignments mein se ek ke liye, unhone ek purani (used) kitab kharidi, aur unhone us kitab ko ek table par display kiya jiske upar doodh se bhara ek bag latak raha tha, jise is tarah design kiya gaya tha ki wo kitab par doodh tapkaye (drip). Yeh ek anchor point tha, wo beej (seed) jiski unhe apne ideas ko clear (crystallize) karne ke liye zaroorat thi. Unhone doodh ke tapakne (drip) ke liye ek bada apparatus (dhancha) banane ka faisla kiya. Unhone ek bada container banaya jo chhat (ceiling) se latakta tha aur doodh tapkata tha, "architecture se bahar nikalte huye (growing) ek tumor jaisa." Lekin—jaisa ki iterative process mein aam hai—"yeh poori tarah theek nahi baitha (didn't quite work)." Jab maine unka interview liya, unke dusre saal ki autumn (fall) mein, wo exploration aur iteration ke kai aur stages se guzar chuki thin, aur wo abhi bhi experiment kar rahi thin; unhone kaha, "Mujhe nahi pata ki meri thesis kya hone wali hai." Lekin wo pareshan nahi thin; unhone process par vishwas (trust) karna seekh liya tha.

Maine unse poocha ki kya unhone is vision (nazariye) ke sath shuruat ki thi ki kaam kahan ja raha hai. Kya iske piche koi theoretical framing (theory ka dhancha) ya social message tha? Unhone na kaha, aisa nahi hua tha. "Mere ideas mujhe kisi thos (concrete) tareeqe se ya bhasha (language) ke zariye nahi aate. Mujhe bas iska color aur iska material (materiality) pasand aaya, aur yahi wajah hai ki main bas iske sath kaam karti rahi."

Baker ne kuch waqt tak in possibilities (sambhavnaon) ke sath experiment kiya, aur aakhir-kaar unhe ehsaas hua ki kuch bhi dilchasp (interesting) nahi ho raha tha. Wo ek aur dead end (band raste) par pahunch chuki thin. Unhone plaster-milk ki saari sculptures phenk din aur doodh ki jagah sugar water (cheeni ka pani) tapkane ka experiment karna shuru kiya. Lekin wo baar-baar doodh par wapas aa rahi thin; unhe nahi pata tha kyun. Unhone apne dripping sugar water (tapakte sugar water) wale idea ko aur aage badhate hue (build on) ek paper bag ko doodh se bharne aur ek "printing plate" ke zariye kisi aur paper par doodh tapkane ka faisla kiya. Unhone is irade (intention) se is process ki video banayi ki wo is video ko apne gallery show ke dauran project karengi (chalayengi). Yeh bhi exploratory (khoj-bheen wala) tha: "Mujhe pakka nahi pata ki yeh kaam meri thesis hoga. Ho sakta hai meri thesis mein koi doodh (milk) ho hi na." Kai mahino baad, unki final thesis mein, waqayi, aese kaam shamil the jinme raw milk (kacha doodh) ka istemal kiya gaya tha.

Baker ki kahani is baat ki aam misaal (typical) hai ki artists aur designers kaise create karte hain. Wo process ki shuruat mein hi materials ke sath khelna (playing) shuru kar dete hain, unhe ideas aane se pehle hi, yahan tak ki unhe yeh pata hone se pehle bhi ki wo kya kar rahe hain. Jaise-jaise wo kaam karte hain, materials ke sath ek dialogue se ideas ubhar (emerge) aate hain. Band raston (Dead ends) ko dhyan se examine (parkha) kiya jata hai yeh dekhne ke liye ki jo kuch hua uski materiality (maddat/material ke roop) mein kya potential (sambhavna) chhipa ho sakta hai. Jin das (10) MFA students ka maine interview liya, wo sabhi is process se guzre.

New York ki School of Visual Arts (SVA) mein sculpture padhane wali Professor Kara Rooney ne kaha ki banane (making) ka process circular (gol) aur nonlinear (tedha-medha) hota hai; wo yahi umeed karti hain ki wo students ko sikhayengi. "Materials hamesha waisa nahi karte jaisa aap chahte hain aur aapko apna rasta (course) badalna padta hai. Yeh physical discovery (jismani/haath se ki gayi khoj) ka ek process hai." Arny Nadler, WashU mein ek sculptor aur professor, ne kaha, "Nayi aur creative cheezein karne se aati hain, uske baare mein sochne se nahi." Materials ke sath kaam karte waqt, students kaam ke sath ek dialogue mein involve (engage) hote hain jiska nateeja kaam ke lagatar iterations (dohraav) mein nikalta hai, jo ek ghoomte hue (spiraling) ya zigzag pattern mein hota hai.

Lekin Baker ke kaam ka matlab (*mean*) kya tha? Aakhir-kaar, unhe abhi bhi pachaas (50) pages ka ek document likhna tha. Baker ko nahi pata tha ki iska kya matlab hai jab tak ki milk sculptures us iterative process se nikal kar nahi aaye. Wo meaning (matlab) ke baare mein pareshan (worried) nahi thin; unhone process par vishwas (trust) karna seekh liya tha. Wo janti thin ki kaam ke process se meaning nikal kar aayega, aur unhe apni us kabiliyat par bharosa tha ki jab aisa hoga toh wo use dekh lengi. Baker ne mujhe samjhaya: "Doodh (Milk) definitely (zaroor) mere kaam ki un themes se bada hua hai jinka talluq ek maa hone, aur motherhood (maa ban-ne) ke sath mere rishte, aur meri maa ke sath, aur bas ek cheez ke taur par 'Maa (Mother)' se hai. Mujhe lagta hai ki doodh saaf taur par us daire (realm) mein hai. Meri written thesis (likhi hui thesis) ka object relations theory, aur mother-infant attachment (maa-bachhe ke lagav), aur mythology (pauranik kathaon) se bahut zyada lena-dena hone wala hai." Lekin jab hum unke process ko examine karte hain, toh hum dekh sakte hain ki unhone is vision ke sath shuruat nahi ki thi. Unhone yeh faisla karke shuruat nahi ki thi ki wo motherhood ki theme par kaam karengi (pursue). Motherhood doodh se, wax se, sugar water se, plaster se nikal kar aayi (emerged). Yeh creative process se nikli. Kaam ka matlab motherhood tha, lekin kaam pura hone se pehle yeh aesa nahi tha:

*Main ab yeh keh sakti hoon, lekin tab yeh itna thos (concrete) nahi tha. Kabhi-kabhi yeh cheezein bas nazar (appear) aati hain. Mujhe yaad nahi kis din main doodh ko apne studio mein aayi thi. Doodh ke bare mein, main nahi bata sakti ki kis din maine socha tha, "Oh, main doodh ka istemal karungi." Yeh bas un cheezon mein se ek hai jo ho gayi. Doodh ka idea tab background mein chal raha tha jab main dusri cheezon par kaam kar rahi thi.*

Meaning (matlab) ko kaam par baad mein (retroactively) laagu kiya jata hai. Process pehle aata hai, aur meaning baad mein aata hai.

Beshak, art ideas aur meaning ke sath juda hota hai. Artworks aksar ek message communicate (pahunchate) karte hain. Kai artists apne kaam se duniya ko badalna chahte hain. Lekin professors chahte hain ki aap pehle process se guzrein. Jab aap process se guzarte hain, toh aapko ek behtar idea, ek zyada asardaar (compelling) message, kuch aisa milta hai jiska duniya par zyada asar (impact) hoga. Aur professors apne experience se jante hain ki process se guzarne ke baad, shuruati (initial) idea badalne wala hai. Kuch students ke sath, yeh bahut dramai (dramatically) dhang se badlega. Pehle process aata hai, phir object, aur phir idea. Banane (making) ki taqat kisi pehle se soche gaye (preconceived) idea ko execute karna nahi hai; banane ki taqat naye ideas ko paida (generate) karna hai.

Jeff Pike, ek illustrator aur WashU mein professor—aur 1999 se 2008 tak School of Art ke dean—mujhe batate hain ki jab students unke paas koi bada idea, koi theoretical concept, ya koi zaroori social issue lekar aate hain toh wo unse kya kehte hain:

*Aapko us par aage kaam karna (pursue) chahiye aur usay visual (dekhne layaq) banana chahiye, taaki hum sab usay dekh sakein aur us par discuss kar sakein. Apne dimaag se bahar nikalna, aur apni andar ki aawazon (interior monologue) se bahar aana, aur isay paper par lana wakayi mushkil hai jahan yeh kaam aa sake (useful ho).*

Creativity ki research mein ek sadiyon purana sawal yeh hai ki, ideas kahan se aate hain? Psychologists aksar sochte hain ki ideas dimaag se aate hain; yahi wajah hai ki wo un mental processes ki study karte hain jo creativity ke piche hote hain. Lekin art aur design mein, ideas sirf dimaag se nahi ubharte (emerge); wo dimaag aur materials ke ek sath kaam karne se ubharte hain.

**"Agar main apne hathon ko busy kar leta hoon, toh mere dimaag ka ek hissa shift ho jata hai, aur ek chota sa rasta (opening) khul jata hai, aur koi cheez andar aa (flow in) sakti hai."**

### **Banane (Making) aur Sochne (Thinking) ke beech ka Dialogue**

**"Bhale hi banane wale ke dimaag mein koi shakal (form) ho, yeh wo shakal nahi hai jo kaam ko banati hai. Yeh materials ke sath judav (engagement) hai. Aur isliye agar hamein yeh samajhna hai ki cheezein kaise banti hain, toh hamein is judav par dhyan dena (attend) hoga."**

Tim Ingold, *Making*

Cheezein banana hi artists aur designers karte hain. Beshak, artist ban-ne ke liye aapko technique mein maharat (master) hasil karni hogi. Aapko wo techniques art aur design school mein seekhni hongi. Lekin professors apne kaam ko is tarah nahi dekhte. Jaisa ki Amy Honchell ne mujhe bataya, "Main aapko bunai (knit) karna sikha sakti hoon. Isse koi farq nahi padta. Main aapko bunai karna sikha sakti hoon. Lekin kya main aapko kuch dilchasp bun-na ya kuch aisa bun-na sikha sakti hoon jiski koi aur parwah (care) karega? Main yahi karne ki koshish kar rahi hoon." Jab Judith Kruger ne art school shuru kiya, toh wo pehle se hi janti thin ki nihonga kaise karte hain. Wo technique seekhne ke liye SCAD mein nahi thin. Wo dekhna seekhne aayi thin.

Art aur design school mein, aap dekhna aur sochna seekhte hain, lekin aap yeh lectures sun kar ya padh kar ya videos dekh kar nahi karte. Aap banana (making) ke zariye dekhna seekhte hain. Yahan WashU ke Ron Leax hain: "Agar aap yahan kisi bhi professor se baat karte hain, toh wo aapko batayenge ki wo technique nahi sikhate. Hum har waqt technique sikhate hain! Kyunki technique hi wo jagah hai jahan dusri cheezein zaahir (manifested) hoti hain, aur bina technique ke, aap zyada mushkil (elusive) cheezein nahi kar sakte." Aur Leax kuch behad mushkil (extremely elusive) cheezein sikhate hain; unke Studio Methods course mein, students autonomous agents, spontaneous organization, autocatalytic sets, phase transition states, matrix thinking, aur aisi hi bahut si cheezon ke baare mein seekhte hain.

Technique aur ideas, dekhna aur banana—yeh sab ek lagatar chalne wale logic (constant dialectic) mein ek sath judte hain.

SCAD mein ek sculpture professor apne foundations students ko yeh assignment dete hain:

*“Mere paas padhane ki ek strategy hai jo maine ijad (invent) ki hai, jo is baat par aadharit hai ki designers aur artists jo hasil (achieve) kar rahe hain wo kaise karte hain. Main ise 'materiality' (maaddat/asliyat) kehta hoon. Basically, aap material lete hain aur aap use uske aam context (mahaul/jagah) se hata (displace) dete hain. Yeh displacement (hata dena) material ke mayne (connotations) ko badal deta hai. Kuch materials ke mayne dusron se zyada hote hain, jaise khoon (blood), ya urine (peshab). Isay sikhana bahut aasan hai. Misaal ke taur par, keelon (nails) se ek hathoda (hammer) banayein. Yeh ek recipe ki tarah hai, isay steps ki ek series mein sikhaya ja sakta hai.”*

Yeh assignment sun-ne mein aisa lagta hai jaise yeh shayad bas ek aam craft (simple craft) ki baat ho. Keelon ke ek guchhe ko glue (gond) se chipka kar ek hathode ki shakal mein kaise banaya jaye, yeh samajhna; yeh kitna mushkil ho sakta hai? Lekin wo is assignment ka istemal kuch bahut hi complex concepts sikhane ke liye karte hain: "material performativity (material ka kaam karne ka dhang)" aur "transgressive art (hudood torne wala art)." Wo isay postmodern movement se jodte hain. "Ek baar jab unhe is se introduce (muta'rif) kara diya jata hai, toh wo iske sath aage badhte (grow) hain. Mujhe unhe material performativity ke zyada pechida (sophisticated) tareeqe sikhane ki bhi zaroorat nahi padti, kyunki wo bas iske sath ched-chad karke (messing with it), naye connection banane ke liye kisi cheez ko naye andaz me pesh karne (recontextualizing) ki buniyadi baat (basic premise) ke sath khilwad karke khud hi isay samajh jate hain." Materiality (maaddat) hi abstraction (khayaliyat) aur conceptual depth (khayalat ki gehrai) ko aage badhati hai.

### **Kisi Object (Cheez) se Poochne ke liye Do Sau (200) Baatein**

**"Agar aap physically apne hathon aur motor skills ko shamil (involved) kar lete hain, toh ek alag hi tarah ki learning hoti hai."**

Cheryl Wassenaar, Washington University in St. Louis

Cheryl Wassenaar ek visual artist hain jo painting, sculpture, aur design ki mili-juli (hybrid) practice mein text (lafzon) ke function (kaam) ko explore karti hain. Wo mile hue (found) aur commercial signage (boards) ke sath kaam karti hain, feki gayi lakdi (discarded wood) ko communication ke visual metaphors (dekhne wali misalon) mein badal (repurposing) deti hain jo modern painting aur contemporary advertising ki bhasha (language) se idea (borrow) leti hain. Unki Eco Art class mein, sabse zaroori concepts mein se ek *repurposing (kisi cheez ko kisi aur maqsad ke liye istemal karna)* hai. "Agar main bas itna keh doon, jao kuch dhoondho aur use repurpose karo, toh yeh bahut wasee (broad) ho jata hai, unhe nahi pata hota ki kya karna hai, unke paas kaam karne ke liye kafi buniyad (against) nahi hoti. Aapko thode framework (dhanche) ki zaroorat hoti hai; aapko shuru karne ke liye ek jagah chahiye." Framework ke liye, wo ek assignment ka istemal karti hain jo unhone saalon mein develop kiya hai jo shuru mein murtikar (sculptor) Tom Friedman ki "kisi object se poochne ke liye 200 sawalo" ki list (*Ingredients*, 1990) se inspired tha. Pehla step yeh hai ki students kisi flea market (kabadi bazaar) mein jayein aur koi aisi cheez kharidein jo unhe dekhne mein cool lage. Aisa karne ke liye, unhe apni dekhne ki kabiliyat, apne andar jhankne aur yeh samajhne ki practice (exercise) karni hogi ki unke liye kya dilchasp hai. Wo sabhi apne objects class mein laate hain. Agla step backward (peeche ki taraf) kaam karna aur khud se poochna hai, "Yeh cheez kya hai? Yeh mujhe kis cheez ki yaad dilati hai? Iski khasiyatein (attributes) kya hain? Yeh kaisa dikhta hai? Ise kaun istemal karta hai?" Phir wo wahan se ek concept (idea) ki taraf kaam karte hain.

*“Yeh kisi bhi cheez ko lene aur us se aur zyada jankari (information) nikalne (digging) ke kabil hone ki ek behtareen misaal hai, taaki aap is cheez ka istemal karne ke liye khud ko ek behtar situation mein rakh sakein, kyunki aap iski khasiyaton (attributes) aur iski history ke ehsaas aur iski, iski, har cheez ko explore (khoj) karte hain. Concept is object ko chunna (selecting) hai. Aap object ko dekh sakte hain aur aap isse jankari hasil (gain) kar sakte hain aur ek concept ki taraf apna rasta bana (work your way) sakte hain.”*

Kabhi-kabhi Wassenaar ke students pareshan (frustrated) ho jate hain aur unhe nahi pata hota ki kya karna hai. Wo yeh nahi samajh paate (figure out) ki object se concept tak kaise pahunchna hai. Wo kehti hain, "Mujhe yeh mat kaho ki tum is cheez se jankari (information) nahi nikal sakte!" Wo students ko unke object ko explore karne mein guide karti hain:

*"Phir, ek tarah se material ke andaaz mein, object ya material ko chhan-been (investigate) karo aur kaho, Jab main isey farsh par phekta hoon toh yeh kya karta hai? Jab main isey tukdon mein kaatta hoon toh yeh kya karta hai? Aap bas iske sath physically kaam karein aur aapko ideas aane lagenge. Kabhi-kabhi mujhe unke liye yeh likhna padta hai. Jaise, kaho, aap jo kar rahe hain uske sath kaun se verbs (kaam/fael) jude (associated) hain? Un verbs ke sath kaun se ideas jude hain? Taaki wo yeh jod (connect) saken ki cheezein kaisa mehsoos (feel) hoti hain aur wo kya kar rahi hain, aur wo kaisi dikhti hain aur unka mukin taur par (potentially) kya matlab ho sakta hai."*

Jab aap materiality (maaddat) se shuruat karte hain toh concepts behtar samajh aate hain. Jaisa ki Leax kehte hain, isse farq nahi padta ki object kya hai. Object sirf ek zariya (carrier) hai. ### **Sochna Band Karein, Banana Shuru Karein (Stop Thinking, Start Making)**

**"Materials ke sath pehli dafa (firsthand) judna, aur un baarikiyon (nuance), un chhoti-chhoti details (minutiae) ke sath ki materials kya kar sakte hain, wo aapke haath mein kaisa mehsoos (feel) hote hain, jo responses (jawab) aap unke sath pa sakte hain, tabhi dilchasp (exciting) cheezein hona shuru hoti hain. Main apne students ke liye yahi karne ki koshish kar raha hoon. Chahe aap painter hon ya print maker ya sculptor, ya designer, kuch bhi. Is physical activity ke zariye hi hum seekhte hain."**

Arny Nadler, Washington University in St. Louis mein 3D design padhane ke baare mein

Botnick ki tarah, Nadler "technique" ke bajaye "craft" shabd ko pasand (prefers) karte hain. Wo kehte hain, "Hum students ko badal (cloud) ko is tarah dikhne ke liye paint karna nahi sikhate," lekin "hum sikhate hain ki is art object ki physical maujoodgi (presence), aur yeh apne dekhne walon (audience) ke sath kaise interact karta hai, yeh is baat par zahir (manifest) hota hai ki isey kaise banaya gaya hai." Wo banane (making) se dekhne (seeing) mein badi asaani (seamlessly) se chale jate hain:

*"Kya main ek aesa nishan (mark) bana raha hoon jo conceptually (khayali taur par) aur physically (jismani taur par) sathee (superficial) hai, ya kya yeh waqayi mein ek mayne-khez (meaningful) tareeqe se samne aata (manifest) hai? Aur wahi craft hai. Mera matlab hai, wahi craft hai. Yeh khud ki aalochna (critique) karne ke qabil hone, aap jo karte hain aur dusre log jo karte hain use dhyan se dekhne, aur tez nazar rakhne wale (keen observers) ban-ne ki baat hai. Main apne students se kehta hoon ki as a culture (ek samaj ke taur par) hum sabhi dekhne ke mamle mein (visually) bahut, bahut aalsi (lazy) hain. Mera matlab hai hum hain. Aur artists aur designers ke taur par, hamein cheezon ko sach mein dhyan se dekhne (carefully) ke qabil hona chahiye. Ek artist ke taur par, main jo banata hoon use dekh kar keh sakta hoon, 'Tumhe pata hai kya? Yeh yeh kar raha hai, lekin main chahta hoon ki yeh aesa kare.' Aur isse bahut bada farq (difference) aane wala hai."*

Yeh mere liye Nadler se apna aam sawal poochne ka ek mauka tha: "Kya aap kahenge ki aap students ko creative hona sikha rahe hain?" Wo jawab dete hain:

*"Mujhe nahi lagta ki main unhe creative hona sikha raha hoon. Main unhe sikhane ki koshish karta hoon ki experiment kaise karein aur apne kaam ke zariye khud ko aisi positions mein kaise rakhein jahan unke aur unke kaam ke liye nayi aur exciting cheezein ho sakein. Aur umeed hai ki unhe un cheezon ko notice (dekhne) karne ke qabil banne ke liye attune (taiyar/dhyan dena) kar saku."*

Kya aapka matlab hai ki jab unke kaam mein kuch exciting hoga toh shayad wo isey notice na karein? Bilkul (Absolutely). Studio mein guidance ke bina,

*"Wo shayad isey notice na karein. Students aksar sochte hain ki unhe kuch sochna hai aur phir isey execute (pura) karna hai. Mujhe us creative cheez ke baare mein sochna hoga aur phir main use execute karunga. Aur baith kar kisi naye idea ke baare mein sochna, mujhe nahi lagta ki nayi creative cheezein dhoondhne ka yeh zaroori taur par sabse behtareen tareeqa hai. Mujhe lagta hai ki zyada-tar waqt, aap in cheezon ko karne (doing) ke zariye dhoondhte hain, inke baare mein sochne (thinking) ke zariye nahi. Beshak, jab aap kar rahe hote hain toh aap soch bhi rahe hotey hain, isliye main yeh nahi kahunga ki jab aap ise kar rahe hain, toh aap iske baare mein nahi soch rahe hain, lekin jab aap ise kar rahe hote hain, toh aap actively (poori tarah se) us activity (kaam) mein shamil (engaged) hote hain."*

*"Drawing asal mein yeh real physical cheez kya hai, iska sirf ek idea (khayal) hai. Agar aapke paas pehle physical (thos roop) nahi hai, toh aap isey nahi samajh payenge. Yeh kisi cookbook se khana banana seekhne jaisa hai lekin asal mein kabhi kitchen mein na jana."*

Nadler ke liye, student ko dekhne aur sochne ke liye guide karne mein object (cheez) behad zaroori (essential) hai:

*"Jab main design aur proportions (anupat/size ka hisab) ke baare mein padhata hoon, toh main kabhi is tarah nahi padhata, 'Okay, yeh Golden Rule hai. Cheezon ko is tarah proportioned (banaya) jana chahiye.' Wagera-wagera (Yada-yada-yada). Lekin kisi student ke sath visual relationships (dikhne wale rishte) aur proportion ke baare mein baat karna, bina kisi cheez ke aapke theek samne hue, lagbhag namumkin (impossible) hai."*

Art banane (artmaking) ke baare mein Nadler ka nazariya (view) us theory ka ek version hai jise main creativity ki *action theory* kehta hoon. Yeh theory creativity research ki ek buniyaadi (core) finding (natije) par aadharit hai: creative ideas kuch karne (acting) se ubharte hain, sochne se nahi. Creative ideas kaam karne ke process se nikal kar aate hain, aur yeh sirf art mein nahi hota: balki science, engineering, medicine, aur business innovation mein bhi hota hai. Aam taur par (More generally), *extended mind (faile hue dimaag)* par research hamein dikhati hai ki saari soch (thinking) deeply (gehrai se) physical movement (jismani harkat), duniya mein maujood hone, aur kaam karne se judi hui hai. Creativity ek aapas mein judi (embodied) dimaagi process (cognitive process) hai, aur professors students ko yeh seekhne mein guide karte hain ki art ka matlab duniya mein embodied action (apne jism aur haath se kaam karne) mein shamil hona hai.

### **Conceptual Art ke sath Problem**

Douglas Dowd, jo WashU mein ek communication designer aur graphic artist hain, Kenyon College mein ek undergraduate student the. Unhe artist banne mein dilchaspi thi, lekin wo art department se door rahe aur uski jagah history mein major kiya kyunki art department unke liye bahut zyada conceptual (khayali/theory par aadharit) tha. "Yeh ek tarah se waqt barbad karne (jerking off) jaisa lagta tha. Baad mein, mujhe ehsaas hua ki main ek maker (banane wala) tha. Mujhe cheezein banani thin. Mujhe ek aisa rasta dhoondhna tha (figure out) jisse main apni zindagi mein ek maker ke taur par aage badh saku (make progress). Jo experiences (tajurbe) mujhe huye the, unke aadhar par, sirf printmaking hi munasib (appropriate) lag raha tha. Yeh art disciplines mein sabse zyada sabko sath lekar chalne wala (democratic) hai. Toh maine printmaking mein apna MFA kiya."

**"Maine Paul Rand se seekha ki aap ek pencil uthate hain, aap draw karna shuru karte hain, aur turant (immediately) aapko ideas aane lagte hain."**

Richard Mehl, graphic design, SVA, asardaar (influential) midcentury designer Paul Rand ke baare mein kehte hue

Dowd ne apna poora career WashU mein bitaya hai; unhe wahan 1992 mein hire kiya gaya tha. Unke pehle assignment ke taur par, unhe core program—pehle aur dusre foundation saalon—ko lead karne ke liye kaha gaya tha.

*"Program ko sudhar (reform) ki bahut sakht zaroorat thi aur is sudhar ko lead karne mein madad karna mera kaam tha. 2D design ko sikhana apni mushkil ke liye badnam (notoriously difficult) hai. Wahi ek course tha jo sabse zyada kharab (screwed up) ho chuka tha. Maine kaha, iske liye mera naam likh do (write me up with that). Main isey padhaunga. Maine na toh isey kabhi padha tha aur na hi kabhi padhaya tha. Toh main gaya aur Bauhaus ko dekha. Aur maine un logon se baat ki jo wo courses padhate the jahan main graduate school gaya tha. Toh maine waisa karna shuru kar diya. Semester ke lagbhag ek-tihayi (a third) hisse ke baad, maine khud se kaha, wow. Yeh kaam nahi kar रहा hai. Wo bore ho chuke hain aur main bore ho chuka hoon. Yeh ek disaster (tabahi) hai!"*

Bauhaus school ek asardaar (influential) modernist pedagogy (padhane ka tareeqa) tha jiska focus haqeeqat ki buniyaadi shakalon (underlying forms of reality)—circles (gole), squares (chaukor), lines (lakiron)—par tha (iske baare mein chapter 5, "Seeing" mein aur zyada bataya gaya hai). Kai art aur design schools mein, pehle saal ki foundations (buniyad) ek Bauhaus approach par based hoti hai, isliye Dowd ke liye apne course ka design is tarah shuru karna natural tha. Unhe ehsaas hua ki problem yeh thi ki Bauhaus method ko ek aise tareeqe se padhaya ja raha tha jo ideas aur concepts par bahut zyada focus karta tha. Yeh unke ya unke students ke liye kaam nahi kar raha tha.

*"Jab aap drawing padha rahe hote hain, toh drawing ek physical (jism se hone wala) kaam (act) hai. Aapko cheezein karni padti hain (do stuff). Aapko material ko apne hisab se (manipulate) karna hota hai. Aap apne bartav (behave) ke zariye us cheez tak pahunchte hain, toh aap yeh drawing banate hain, jo shayad badiya (great) ho ya shayad bekar (sucks) ho, lekin aapne ise banaya aur ise produce (paida) karne mein aapka ek engaged experience (shamil hone ka tajurba) raha. Lekin jis tareeqe se 2D design padhaya ja raha tha, usme aisa kuch nahi tha. Yeh hadd se zyada analytical (jaanchne wala) tha. Isme kafi behavior (amal) nahi tha. Isme kafi engagement (judav) nahi tha."*

Printmaker Ken Botnick bhi us art ke khilaf (critical) hain jo concepts ya theories par aadharit hoti hai. Wo art ki duniya ke is movement ko "deskilling (hunar khatam karna)" kehte hain. Concepts par focus karne ki problem yeh hai ki yeh aapko creativity ke linear insight model ki taraf le jata hai, jahan idea pehle aata hai aur execution (us par kaam karna) baad mein. "Yeh kaam karne ka ek aisa tareeqa (working method) hai jahan aap baithte hain, aap ek aesa idea nichod (squeeze out) nikalte hain jo poori tarah se bana hua aata hai, aur khoobsurti is idea ki hoti hai jaise ki wo maujood ho, aur phir raste mein kahin (somewhere along the line) ise ek asli cheez mein dhal (formed) diya jata hai." Botnick creativity ke is model ko nakarte (rejects) hain. Jaisa ki humne chapter 1, "Process" mein dekha, yeh sirf ek myth (jhooth) nahi hai; yeh ek khatarnak myth hai. Yeh naye aane wale students ki galatfehmiyon (misconceptions) ko aur mazboot (reinforces) karta hai aur unki iterative process ko seekhne ki kabiliyat ko rok (blocks) deta hai. "Mera man-na hai ki craft (hunar/dastkari) ki practice ideas ko develop karne ka ek bahut hi fertile (upjau) tareeqa hai. Asal mein cheez ko banane ke mamle mein students ki taraf se ek waqayi hichkichahat (reticence) hoti hai."

Miya Osaki SVA mein social innovation MFA program ki buni yad rakhti hain aur use lead karti hain. Unka goal yeh sochna hai ki design social change (samajik badlaav) mein kaise yogdaan (contribute) de sakta hai. Isse zyada conceptual kisi cheez ka tasavvur karna mushkil hai. Jaisa ki unhone kaha, unke students ke "outputs (natije)" shayad "objects ya cheezein na hon. Yeh nayi soch (new thinking) ho sakti hai, yeh ek approach (nazariya) ho sakta hai." Aur phir bhi, is program mein bhi, unka kehna hai ki materials ke sath kaam karna behad zaroori (critical) hai. "Meri technique hamesha yahi rahi hai ki, jab tak aap kuch banayenge nahi, aapko pata nahi chalega. Hum is ke baare mein lambe waqt tak baat kar sakte hain, lekin iska tab tak koi matlab nahi hoga jab tak ki hum ise thos (tangible) na bana lein." Aur yaad rakhein (keep in mind) ki wo ek aisi field ke baare mein baat kar rahi hain jo objects se apna focus hatakar systems ki taraf le jane ki koshish kar rahi hai.

Ache ideas aane se pehle aapko craft (hunar) seekhna hoga. SCAD mein padhane wale ek painter, Stephen Thorpe ne kaha, "Aapko behtar karne ke kabil banane ke liye basic skills ki zaroorat hoti hai." Wassenaar apni 2D design ki core class ke baare mein kehti hain: "Unhe dekhne ke lihaz se (visually) wo karne ke qabil hone ke liye ek level ki technique ki zaroorat hoti hai jise karne ki disha (directing) unke ideas unhe de rahe hain. Main broad principles (wasii asool) sikha rahi hoon jinhe baad mein wo apne major (khas subject) mein apply karenge."

### **Banane (Making) aur Sochne (Thinking) ka Badhta Hua Ghoomta Rasta (Expanding Spiral)**

Professors technique ko sochne (thinking) aur concepts se alag (separately) nahi sikhate. Ache assignments ki yeh maang hoti hai ki students technique aur sochna dono ek sath seekhein. Students pehle technical skills nahi seekhte, aur sirf baad mein yeh nahi seekhte ki dekhna kaise hai aur create kaise karna hai. Wo dono seekhte hain, baar-baar dohrate huye (iteratively), ek sath (side by side), ek aage badhte huye chakkar (advancing spiral) mein. Yeh traditional school ki us soch (notion) ko ulat (upends) deta hai jahan aapko kisi course ko lene se pehle zaroori shart (prerequisites) poori karni hoti hain, yani aapko dekhne aur sochne jaisi higher-level skills seekhne ke liye taiyar hone se pehle technique mein apni buniyad (grounding) banani padti hai.

Zyada-tar fields (domains) mein maharat wale kaam (expert performance) ke liye bahut zyada baar-baar (repetitive) practice ki zaroorat hoti hai, aur zyada-tar knowledge-intensive (gayan se bhari) professions ke liye yeh zaroori hota hai ki facts aur skills ke ek bade hisse par maharat (mastered) hasil ki jaye. Art aur design ke professional schools mein—jahan creativity ko kisi bhi dusri discipline se zyada ehmiyat (valued) di jati hai—basic skills ko eham (essential) mana jata hai. Phir bhi skill seekhna (skill learning) dekhne aur sochne mein dhansa (embedded) hota hai. Skills ko aksar mauqa dekh kar (opportunistically) sikhaya jata hai, jab kisi student ke project kaam ke dauran sawal ya problems samne aate hain. Misaal ke taur par, SCAD mein Sandra Reed ki painting class mein, ek student ke paint kiye gaye portrait ke critique (tajziye) ne unhe class ko parchhai (shadow) aur background ki classic techniques ke baare mein batane ka mauka diya. Seekhane layaq in palon (teachable moments) par respond karne ke liye ek high degree skill aur tajurbe ki zaroorat hoti hai. Art aur design ke syllabi specific facts aur skills ke ird-gird structure nahi kiye jate, bhale hi unhe learning outcomes (seekhne ke nateeje) ke taur par bataya (stated) gaya ho. Instructors hamesha apne chahe jane wale (desired) learning outcomes ko dimaag mein rakhte hain aur unhe introduce (shamil) karne ke liye ache mauko (opportunities) ka intezar karte hain.

Creativity dohraav (repetition), craft (hunar), aur skill ke khilaf (opposed) nahi hai. Creativity unki maharat (mastery) par nirbhar (depends) karti hai. Main hairan (shocked) aur amused (khush) hua jab Steve Heller (SVA) ne mujhe bataya ki high school se sidhe (fresh out of) aane wale kai incoming undergraduates ko toh *ek ruler (scale)* ka istemal karna bhi nahi aata; aur unhe yeh nahi pata hota ki bina khud ko chot (hurting) pahunchaye cheezon ko kaatna (cut) kaise hai. Unki poori artistic zindagi computer par guzri hai. Heller ne kaha ki unhe yeh seekhne ki zaroorat hai ki "ideas kaise develop karein aur form ko sketch kaise karein, aur sketching aur agle step ke beech kya farq hai, jo ki concept ko poora karna (execution) ya haqeekat (realization) banana hai. Yeh sab us baat ka hissa hai jise main ek designer ya artist banne ke liye skills ki practice karna manta hoon. In cheezon ko seekhne aur ise baar-baar (over and over and over again) karne ke process se hi wo skills is hadd tak behtar (elevated) ho jate hain jahan aap confident ban jate hain, aur wo (skills) apki aadat (intuitive) ban jate hain." SCAD mein, Thorpe kuch aisa hi (similar) kehte hain: Foundations mein, unhe students ko bahut basic skills sikhani padti hain jinme *pencil ko kaise pakadna hai* yeh shamil hai. Computer ka unki artistic zindagiyon par dabdaba (dominated) raha hai. Aur phir bhi, ruler ka istemal kaise karna hai aur pencil ko kaise pakadna hai yeh sikhate waqt bhi, "hum sath hi sath (at the same time) un par concepts bhi daal (pushing) rahe hote hain."

### **Gaanth Baandhna (Tying Knots)**

**"Ideas mere material ka istemal shuru karne ke baad aaye. Kyunki maine bas iske sath khelna (playing) shuru kiya tha. Mujhe isme dilchaspi (interested) thi."**

Kathleen Perniciaro, MFA student, Washington University in St. Louis

Kathleen Perniciaro ne apne ek-saal lambe creative process ko Jennifer Baker ki doodh aur maatritva (motherhood) ki kahani se bahut milte-julte (similar) tareeqon se bayan kiya (described). Jab maine fall (patjhad) semester mein unke studio mein unka interview liya, tab unhone abhi tak yeh faisla nahi kiya tha ki wo apni spring thesis ke liye kya karne wali thin. Unhone apne iterative process ke teen alag-alag "moments (palon)" ko bayan kiya—ki ab tak unki themes kaise samne aayi (emerged) thin, wo themes jo unke artist statement mein likhi ja sakti thin.

*"Yeh un cheezon ko pehchan-ne (recognizing) ke baare mein hai jo aapke kaam mein baar-baar aati (occurring) hain. Past (maazi) mein mera kaam—mera matlab hai, ek purana (older) statement yeh hoga ki, mujhe mousam ki mari huyi (weathered) cheezon mein dilchaspi hai. Main past ki in cheezon, ya past ke bache huye hisson (remnants) ko, society (samaj) ke liye ek tarah se underdogs (dabe-kuchle) manti hoon. Aur main unhe unke asli function (kaam) se aage dhakelna (push) chahti thi. Main packaging, jaise tape, aur foil, aur gumball seed pods jaisi cheezon ka istemal kar rahi thi, unhe kisi nayi cheez se dobara jodne (reconnect) ki koshish kar rahi thi taaki wo usse aage ki zindagi ji sakein jiske liye unhe shuru mein (originally) banaya gaya tha. MFA program shuru karne ke kuch hi waqt baad, mujhe ehsaas hua ki main narrative (kissa) aur story ko shamil (incorporate) karna chahti thi."*

MFA program mein kuch waqt bitane ke baad, unhe ehsaas hua ki "kuch khas shapes baar-baar samne (coming up) aa rahi thin." Misaal ke taur par, wo wine ki purani bottles ke sath experiment kar rahi thin. Unhe ehsaas hua ki wo "asal mein time (waqt) ke baare mein baat kar rahi thin. Toh phir maine usay aage push karna shuru kiya aur phir wahan se badalna (change) shuru ho gaya." Jaise-jaise unhone time ke baare mein aage (further) socha, unhone khud se poocha, "Time ke baare mein aesi kya cheez hai jisme mujhe dilchaspi hai?" Unhe dheere-dheere ehsaas hua ki "yeh darasal time nahi tha, yeh wo cheezein thin jo hamein hamare aam routines se todti (break) hain."

Maine unse poocha, "Yeh kaise hua?"

*"Yahin par kaam aata hai. Mera kaam dekhna (observe karna) hai. Agar main un cheezon ko observe nahi kar sakti jo mere apne kaam mein ho rahi hain, toh mujhe sach mein nahi pata ki main kitni asardaar (effective) artist ban sakti hoon."*

Time par apne focus ke sath, apne 2009 ke first-year show mein, "Maine deewar par ghaas ki alag-alag pattiyan (blades of grass) lagayin (installed), aur maine isey apni timeline (waqt ki rekha) mana." Har patti (blade) ek second thi. "Phir maine yeh video projections banayi jo timeline par aaram (rested) kar rahi thin, lekin main ghaas par gaanth (knots) baandh rahi thi. Mujhe toh yeh bhi nahi pata ki main aisa kyun chahti thi. Maine ghaas par gaanth bandhne aur uski tasveerein lene mein kayi ghante bitaye."

Wo ghaas mein gaanth baandhne ke experiment karti rahin. Yeh unke liye dilchasp (interesting) tha, lekin unhe abhi tak yeh nahi pata tha ki kyun. Unhone ghaas aur time se hatne (shift) aur knots (gaantho) ke sath associations dhoondhne ka faisla kiya. Unhone fishing (machli pakadne wali) knots ke baare mein socha. Unhone Mark Twain dwara gehrai napne (measure depth) ke liye knots wali ek rassi upar kheenchne ke baare mein socha. "Aap jante hain na ki aap cartoons mein kuch yaad rakhne ke liye apni ungli par gaanth kaise baandhte the? Toh har baar jab maine koi gaanth baandhi, toh wo ek pal (moment), ek memory (yaad) ki nishandahi (signify) karti thi."

Show ke kuch hafton baad, wo yeh jaan kar dang (stunned) reh gayin ki unka idea ek aur artist ko pehle hi aa chuka tha! Wo galti se (accidentally) usi saal ke ek artist ke gallery show mein chali gayi jisne khud ki gaanth baandhte hue videos display ki thi. Wo childhood (bachpan ki) kahaniyon ko gaanthon se jod raha tha, jaise ek khargosh (rabbit) ki ped (tree) ke charo taraf jane aur ek hole mein koodne ki kahani, jo ek bowline knot baandhna yaad rakhne ka sikhaya jane wala tareeqa hai. Bhale hi Perniciaro ne khud se yeh idea nikala (came up with) tha, ab wo is kaam ko display nahi kar sakti thin kyunki aisa lagta ki unhone bas isey dusre artist se chura liya (stolen) hai. Unhone ise aaram se (in stride) liya: "Ek cheez jo aapko man-ni (accept) padti hai wo yeh hai ki *kuch bhi* original (naya/apna) nahi hai. Main sach mein is baat par yakeen karti hoon. Mujhe nahi lagta ki koi original idea hota hai."

Wo abhi bhi gaanthon (knots) se aakarshit (fascinated) thin; wo isey chhodne (give up) ke liye taiyar nahi thin. Unhone gaanth baandhne mein mahinon (months) bitaye. Jab hum unke studio mein mile, toh unhone mujhe string (dhage) ka ek tukda dikhaya aur mujhe bataya: "Main is dhage se aage nahi badh pa rahi (can't get past)." Unke sabhi pieces mein knots aur string hain. Lekin unhe abhi tak nahi pata kyun. "Yeh isliye hai kyunki maine isey abhi tak theek se (worked it out) poora nahi kiya hai. Maine abhi tak yeh nahi samajh payi (figured out) hoon ki iska poori tarah se matlab kya hai, wo metaphor (misaal) kya hai, aur jab main wahan pahunch jaungi, toh main materials ko badal dungi."

Phir unhone mujhe kuch aur dikhaya jo unhone banaya tha: Yeh ek rope maker (rassi banane ki machine) thi. Unhone isey isliye banaya taaki wo alag-alag tarah ke string, rope, aur knots ke sath aur zyada gehrai se (deeply) experiment kar sakein. "Shayad maine isey poori tarah se tay (worked out) nahi kiya hai, lekin yeh theek hai ki main isey kar rahi hoon aur main iski taraf kaam kar rahi hoon." Unhone mujhe ek kitab dikhayi jo unhone dhage (string) ko ink mein dalkar aur phir use page par dabakar (pressing) banayi thi, "kyunki mujhe wo material pasand hai." Unhe yeh bhi nahi pata tha ki yeh kahan ja raha hai.

**"Materials students ko lead karte hain (aage rah dikhate hain). Wo meri apni aawaz (voice) se zyada materials ko sunte hain."**

Sandra Reed, painting, SCAD

Perniciaro nahi janti ki wo kya kar rahi hain, yeh kahan khatam hoga, ya yeh kab khatam hoga. Yeh creativity ka wo iterative process hai jiska nateeja (leads to) asardaar art ubhar kar (emergence) samne aata hai. Yeh kehna theek (accurate) nahi hai ki wo bina kisi manzil ke bhatak rahi (wandering aimlessly) hain, halanki ek non-artist ko yeh waisa hi lag sakta hai. Yeh kehna theek nahi hai ki wo waqt barbad (wasting time) kar rahi hain, ya wo kho (lost) gayi hain, ya wo nahi janti ki art kaise banate hain. Asal mein, har ek MFA student jiska maine interview liya (kull 10), usne mujhe aisi hi ek kahani sunayi. Aur, jaisa ki hum mere professor interviews se jante hain, yahi wo process hai jo wo sikha rahe hain.

Artists aur designers ko apne materials se pyar (love) hota hai: Baker ko doodh se, Kruger ko mitti (dirt) se, Perniciaro ko gaantho (knots) se. Unhone ek rope machine tak bana li! Apne materials se pyar karne ka yahi matlab hota hai. Aapka pyar behad (boundless) hota hai. Jab wo iske baare mein baat kar rahe the toh main unki aawazon mein unka josh (excitement) sun sakta tha. Wo materials ke sath interact karne (kaam karne) se excited ho gaye the. Unhone concepts ya social movements ya trendy theories se zyada materials ke baare mein baat ki. Unhone kaam generate karne ki mechanics (kaam karne ke dhang) aur medium ke sath judne (engaging) ke process ka swagat (welcomed) kiya. Unka creative process materials ke sath kaam karne ki aadat (act) se chal (driven) raha tha. Isne mujhe typeface designer Ken Botnick ki yaad dila di, jo mujhe apne downtown loft mein apni 19th sadi (nineteenth-century) ki printing machinery ki collection fakhriya andaaz (proudly) mein dikha rahe the.

### **Computers ke Sath Problem**

Richard Mehl ek digital artist hain jo SVA mein padhate hain. Yale School of Art mein unki MFA thesis ek human-computer interface (insaan aur computer ka mel) thi. Wo apne khud ke kaam mein lagbhag poori tarah (exclusively) se computers ka istemal karte hain. Wo apne students ko class mein apne computers lane ke liye majboor (requires) karte hain, lekin wo is baat par bhi zor (insists) dete hain ki unke students haath se drawing karein: "Main jitna zyada computers ka istemal karta gaya, utna hi mujhe apne hathon se kaam karne ki tadap (ached) hone lagi. Yeh dono cheezein bahut alag (different) hain. Aap bahut alag tareeqe se sochte hain, aur aapko bahut hi alag ideas aate hain." Wo apne students ko materials ke sath kaam karne ke liye majboor (requires) karte hain. "Computer par jana ek bahut hi alag baat hai. Yeh unhe mehdood (limits) kar deta hai." Unki class kafi hadd tak computer-based hai; phir bhi, "haath se kaam karne ki skills (hand skills) bhi utni hi important hain. Apne hathon se kaam karna aapko ek insaani khasiyat (humanistic quality) ko apnane (embrace) ki ijazat deta hai." Wo pehle students ko ideas sochne ke liye computer par kaam karwayenge. Phir wo unhe haath se render (banate) karte hain, aur "us process mein, un mistakes (galtiyon) aur un cheezon ki humanistic (insaani) sambhavnaon (possibilities) ko apnate hain jo aapke hathon mein hoti hain. Hath ka kaam (handwork) waqayi acha hai. Yeh aapko us cheez ko dekhne ke liye encourage (prerit) karta hai jo ek mistake lag sakti hai, use ek positive cheez ke roop mein. Serendipity (Itefaqiya achi cheez mil jana). Aap imperfection (khot/nuks) ko ek aisi khasiyat ke roop mein dekhte hain jo negative hone ke bajaye bahut positive hoti hai."

Carol Fabricatore ek artist hain jo books, magazines, newspapers, aur design and advertising firms ke liye art produce karti hain. Wo 1994 se SVA mein narrative drawing padha rahi hain. Unhone mujhe bataya: "Wo iPad ya yahan tak ki apne phones ka istemal karne ke itne aadi (used to) ho chuke hain. Aur wo undo (piche jana) kar sakte hain, wapas ja sakte hain, correct (theek) kar sakte hain, us line ko perfect bana sakte hain. Mujhe unhe convince (samjhana) karna padta hai ki perfect na dikhna theek hai. Kuch students toh mujhe yahan tak batate hain ki unhe paper par draw karne se *darr (afraid)* lagta hai. Agar yeh ek mistake hai toh koi baat nahi. Aapko process par vishwas (trust) karna hoga. Aap isey hamesha baad mein scan kar sakte hain. Lekin aaj kal yeh baat manwana thoda mushkil (harder sell) hai. Unke paas ab digital jaisa control nahi raha."

Mehl aur Fabricatore ki tarah, kai professors apne students ko unke kuch ya sabhi assignments ke liye computer ka istemal karne nahi dete, bhale hi inmein se kai students apne poore career ke liye computers ka istemal karne wale hain. Unhe user interfaces ya computer games design karne ki jobs mil sakti hain. Job par, wo shayad ek tablet aur ek stylus ka istemal karke mock-ups aur thumbnails banayein. Aaj, students jab college aate hain toh wo software mein bahut skilled (mahir) hote hain. Kayo ne saalon se Adobe Creative Suite ka istemal kiya hota hai. Wo jante hain ki acha dikhne wali koi cheez kaise banani hai. Unhe yeh seekhne ke liye art school ki zaroorat nahi hai. Jab kisi assignment mein kaha jata hai ki unhe pencil aur paper ke sath ek sketch banana hai, toh koi na koi hamesha poochta hai ki kya wo computer ka istemal kar sakte hain. Ya, kya main tablet aur stylus ka istemal nahi kar sakta? Yeh toh drawing karne jaisa hi hai na? Ek book designer, Amy Thompson ko is sawal ki aadat hai: "Kayi baar, ek student poochega ki kya wo computer ka istemal kar sakte hain, aur main no (nahi) kahungi kyunki main sach mein chahti hoon ki wo maujood (available) materials ka istemal karein, apne hathon ka zyada istemal karein."

VA Ta

Computers process ko seekhne mein kai tareeqon se dakhlandazi (interfere) karte hain. Pehla, wo aapko process se bahut jaldi (too fast) guzarne dete hain, aur process tab zyada kamyaab (successful) hone ki sambhavna rakhta hai jab yeh dheema (slower) ho, jisme iterations (dohraav) aur mistakes bhari hon. Yahi wajah hai ki Botnick ne apne letterform design ke students ko patle paper ki jagah mote cardboard se letters kaatne ko kaha tha—isme zyada waqt lagta hai aur yeh pareshan karne wala (frustrating) hota hai, lekin yeh unhe dheema (slow down) kar deta hai. Douglas Dowd computer ke bajaye paper par kai assignments is wajah (reason) se dete hain:

*"Physical activity (Hath pair se kiya jane wala kaam), is haqeeqat ki yeh isme shamil (involved) hai, ise karne mein aapko thoda waqt lagta hai, iska matlab hai ki aapke paas jab yeh kaam ho raha ho (while it's happening) toh us par gaur karne (reflect) ke liye zyada waqt hota hai. Yeh aapko dheema (slows down) kar deta hai. Mujhe lagta hai ki seekhna (learning) is kaam (act) mein shamil (embedded) hai."*

Dusra, computers jo images generate karte hain wo bahut perfect (bina khot ki) hoti hain; aapke paas wo choti galtiyan (tiny mistakes) nahi hotin jo aksar ache ideas ki taraf le jati hain. Jeff Keedy—CalArts ke type designer—ne mujhe bataya ki jab students computer ka istemal karte hain, toh "yeh aam taur par acha (good) hota hai. Yeh problem ko solve kar deta hai. Isliye log aisa karte hain. Yahi wajah hai ki is pattern ko todna (break) itna mushkil hai." Maine unse poocha, agar yeh acha dikhta hai, toh students ko usi tareeqe se karte rehne kyun nahi diya jata? Unhone kaha, "kyunki yeh dilchasp (interesting) nahi hai aur yeh unhe lamba waqt nahi chalayega (won't sustain them). Yeh ek bahut hi boring, gair dilchasp wajood (existence) hai."

Teesra, jab aap computer ka istemal karte hain, toh is baat ki zyada sambhavna (more likely) hoti hai ki aap ek idea ke sath process shuru karein, aur phir ek linear (seedhe) process mein execution (us idea ko pura karne) ki taraf aage badhein. SCAD mein ek graphic designer, Rhonda Arntsen ne mujhe bataya: "Jab students ko ek assignment milta hai, toh unki aadat (tendency) hoti hai ki wo dheeme hone aur diye gaye ideas ke baare mein sochne, unhe draw karne, unhe likhne, utsukta (curiosity) ko develop hone ke liye waqt nikalne ke bajaye seedhe computer par jump kar jate hain." Phir, aap phir se usi khatarnak myth par wapas aate hain ki creativity ka matlab sirf ideas aana hai.

### **Jab Aap Phas Jayein, Toh Ek Dabba Banayein (When You're Stuck, Build a Box)**

Steve DeFrank SVA mein pachees (25) saalo se padha rahe hain. Wo first-year foundation drawing, first-year sculpture techniques, aur upperlevel drawing aur painting (teesre aur chauthe saal) padhate hain. Unhone ek assignment ko describe kiya jahan wo apne upper-level students ko space-time continuum (waqt aur jagah ki lagatar line) ko badalne (alter) ke liye kehte hain:

*"Students shuruat mein bahut zyada sochte hain (think too hard). Main open-ended assignments (khule assignments) deta hoon. Ek jo main apni upper-level painting class ke liye deta hoon wo hai 'space-time continuum (waqt aur jagah ki line) ko alter (badal) do.' Wo mujhe dekhte hain aur kehte hain, 'Mujhe nahi pata mujhe kya karna hai.' Aur main kehta hoon, 'Mujhe bhi nahi pata.' Wo jo chahein wo bana sakte hain, haina? Phir jab hum unke kaam ke baare mein baat karte hain, toh kai baar yeh space-time continuum ke baare mein nahi hota. Lekin isse koi farq nahi padta, hum bas wahan se aage badhte hain. Maine aisa sirf isliye kiya taaki unhe respond (jawab) dene ke liye kuch mil sake. Yeh aage zor lagane (push on) ke liye kuch hai."*

Jab is tarah ke open-ended assignments diye jate hain, toh students aksar phas (stuck) jate hain. Unhe nahi pata hota ki shuruat kaise karni hai, bhale hi assignment unhe kuch bhi karne ki ijazat (allows) deta ho jo wo karna chahte hain. Jis tareeqe se aap unstuck (phasne se bahr) hote hain, wo kuch physical (haath se chhune wali cheez) banana hai—bhale hi assignment kisi bahut hi abstract (khayali) cheez ke baare mein ho. Unhone aage kaha:

*"Main aksar advanced (senior) students ke sath bhi aisa hi karta hoon. Ek se zyada baar (More than once), mere paas ek sculpture student aaya jise koi idea nahi sujh raha tha. Wo frustrate (nirash) the. Main unhe kehta hoon, jao ek dabba (box) banao. Wo poochenge kyun. Main kahunga, bas ek dabba banao. Wo kehte hain (They're like), main koi dabba nahi banana chahta. Main kehta hoon, bas apni poori kabiliyat (best of your ability) se ise banao. Toh wo woodshop (lakdi ki dukan/jagah) mein jate hain aur lakdi kaat rahe hote hain. Lakdi ke tukde kat chuke hain, aur phir wo excited (khush) ho kar mere paas aate hain. Wo kehte hain Steve, jab main dabba bana raha tha, toh mujhe yeh idea aaya. Ab wo apne idea ke baare mein excited hain. Toh hum unke idea ke baare mein baat karte hain. Phir wo kehte hain, kya mujhe dabba banana zaroori hai? Aur main kehta hoon nahi, ab tum dabba banana band kar sakte ho."*

Isse koi farq nahi padta ki aap kahan se shuru karte hain; aapko bas shuru karna hota hai. Aap bas be-tarteeb dhang se (randomly) koi dictionary khol sakte hain, aankhein band karke kisi page par ishara (point) kar sakte hain, aur phir kuch aisa bana sakte hain jo us shabd (word) ko dikhaye (represents) jis par aapki ungli ruki ho. Yeh itna aasan ho sakta hai. Unstuck (phasne se bahar) hone ke liye, kuch banayein (make something).
